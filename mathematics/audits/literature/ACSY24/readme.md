## Selected introduction and scope observations

The original selected presentation reading, recorded 2026-09-10, covered Section 1 and the beginning of Section 1.1, published/PDF pp. 2–3.
The source begins from a recognizable mathematical question, identifies fixed and varying data, supplies a notation bridge before specialized statements and explains a consequence and the boundary of a result.
On p. 3 it distinguishes normal bounded generators from potentially nonnormal or unbounded rational outputs.
Normal inputs therefore do not assert normal outputs, and an admissible affiliated evaluation need not be bounded.
Its analytic affiliated rank, linked to Definition 2.26, differs from the ordinary Hilbert-space range dimension.
