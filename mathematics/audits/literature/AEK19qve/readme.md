# AEK19qve source and edition notes

## Yin

The work is Ajanki, Erdos and Kruger's *Quadratic vector equations on complex upper half-plane*, distinct from the AEK19 matrix-Dyson paper.
The inspected source is [arXiv:1506.05095v6](../../../literature/pdf/AEK19qve--arxiv-v6.pdf), revised 9 June 2020, 135 PDF pages.
The [complete supplied contents](../../../literature/contents/AEK19qve--arxiv-v6.md) are transcribed from PDF pp.2--3, printed ii--iii.

The bounded attribution comparison concerns A1--A2 at printed pp.6--7, Section 6.2 and Lemma 6.8/(6.28) at pp.50--51, and the (11.8) lead at pp.111--112.
Their PDF positions are respectively 9--10, 53--54 and 114--115.
Appendix A.4 starts at printed p.122/PDF p.125; its complete original proof has not been audited.
The [C_F0125 source comparison](../../../claims/Yin_C_F0125_v1.md#source-reuse-and-scope) records the actual zero-drift, purely imaginary-parameter scope and its distinction from the local ordered covariance formula.
No equality of preprint and published mathematical text or new proof dependency is asserted.
