# AK26: commutative covariance and spectral-radius boundaries

Source/version: Alt and Kruger, *Brown measures of deformed L-infinity-valued circular elements*.
The [canonical bibliography](../../../literature/references.bib) records Forum of Mathematics, Sigma 14 (2026), e75, DOI 10.1017/fms.2026.10227, checked against the publisher's Crossref deposit.
The retained [AK26.pdf](../../../literature/pdf/AK26.pdf) is arXiv:2409.15405v3, 29 April 2026, 52 pages.

On 2026-10-10, Section 2, equations (2.1)--(2.10), Assumptions A1--A2 and Remark 2.1 (printed/PDF pp.5--6), Theorem 2.2 (p.7), and Proposition 5.16 (p.22) were read directly.
Coverage state: **Read**, contextual comparison, self-review.
Proposition 5.16 was also rendered and checked visually.
The proofs and the classification of singularities were not audited.

The setting is a circular element over a commutative base with covariance given by a nonnegative integral kernel.
A1 is block-primitivity: the kernel is comparable to a primitive zero-one block pattern with every diagonal block present.
A2 is the data regularity condition expressed by divergence of Gamma_(a,s); Remark 2.1(ii) makes A2 automatic on a finite base.
Theorem 2.2 gives a bounded Brown density, its interior analyticity and the support closure.
Proposition 5.16(iii) identifies the sign of beta(z) with the sign of 1-r(S D_(|a-z|^-2)).

For a circular matrix-valued model with zero pseudo-covariance rho, diagonal a_0, and eta(x)=diag(sum_j s_ij x_jj), the positive map x -> (a_0-z)^{-1} eta(x) ((a_0-z)^{-1})^* annihilates the zero-diagonal subspace and restricts to D_z S on the diagonal subspace, where D_z=diag(|(a_0)_ii-z|^{-2}).
Since D_z is invertible away from spec(a_0), D_z S and S D_z are similar.
This is an elementary identification of the two spectral-radius tests, not a transfer of density or support conclusions without A1/A2.
Both rho=0 and the diagonal covariance structure are necessary for the stated overlap.
