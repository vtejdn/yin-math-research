## Statement and hypotheses

Let \(A\) be a \(*\)-subalgebra of \(\mathcal B(H)\), and let \(M\) be its weak-operator closure.
Theorem 2.3.1 states that the unit ball of \(A\) is strongly-operator dense in the unit ball of \(M\), and that the same assertion holds for the selfadjoint parts.

## Notation and conventions

The source distinguishes the weak-operator closure from the strong-operator topology used for approximation.
The unit-ball qualification is part of the statement.
