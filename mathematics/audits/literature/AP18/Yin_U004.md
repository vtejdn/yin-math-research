## Statement and use

For projections \(p,q\) in a factor with a faithful tracial state \(\tau\), the inequality \(\tau(p)\le\tau(q)\) is equivalent to Murray–von Neumann subequivalence of \(p\) to \(q\).
Equality of the two traces is equivalent to the existence of a partial isometry with initial projection \(p\) and final projection \(q\).
Applying the same statement to the complementary projections extends that partial isometry to a unitary carrying \(p\) to \(q\).
