### Inspected recurrence

For the monic single-variable free Appell family, the inspected primary passages give
\[
xA_n=A_{n+1}+\sum_{k=0}^n r_{n+1-k}A_k,
\]
where r_m are the free cumulants.
This is the exact recurrence used by the local exploration, rather than a theorem about arbitrary noncommuting finite-state expansions.

### Local comparison and applicability limit

For a nondegenerate selfadjoint orthogonal polynomial family, the three-term recurrence forces the coefficients of A_k with k<=n-2 to vanish.
Comparing with the displayed monic free Appell recurrence gives r_m=0 for every m>=3.
Thus the orthogonal free Appell family in this scalar comparison is shifted/scaled semicircular.
This is the original local recurrence comparison, not an all-page source audit or a classification of all possible residual models.
It leaves nonorthogonal expansions and more general finite-state recurrences available.
The observation is optional strategy evidence for the [Wick/residual exploration](../../../strategies/Yin_S_F0004.md#s_f0004); it is not a premise of the MSY23 main theorem or a new bounded-intersection result.
