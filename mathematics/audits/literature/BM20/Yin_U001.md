## Growth of the Hölder constant with the operator norm

For the [scalar word-chain application](../../../claims/Yin_C_F0195_v4.md#scalar-fisher), the proof developer inspected Theorem 1.1(i) on PDF p. 3, the iterative bounds on p. 13 and the coefficient formulae (3.10), (3.12) on p. 14 of the [same preprint](../../../literature/pdf/BM20--arxiv-v3.pdf).
For a fixed nonconstant selfadjoint scalar polynomial \(Q\) of degree \(d\), the Hölder exponent is \(2/[3(2^d-1)]\).
In (3.12) the dependence is on \(d\), the operator radius \(R\ge\max_j\|X_j\|\), the free Fisher information of \(X\), the coefficient norm \(\|Q\|_R\) and the leading weight \(\rho_R(Q)\).
For \(R\ge1\) the norm is bounded above and below by positive multiples of \(R^d\) and the leading weight is bounded away from zero.
Consequently a uniform bound on the free Fisher information gives a Hölder constant of at most polynomial growth in \(R\).
The application uses precisely this growth for a tuple with an arbitrary bounded deterministic background.
The inspection checked these statements and their application without re-auditing the source proof in full.
