# BYZ24: uniform density bounds under free circular addition

Source/version: Belinschi, Zhi Yin and Zhong, *The Brown measure of a sum of two free random variables, one of which is triangular elliptic*.
The [canonical bibliography](../../../literature/references.bib) records Advances in Mathematics 441 (2024), 109562, DOI 10.1016/j.aim.2024.109562.
The retained [BYZ24.pdf](../../../literature/pdf/BYZ24.pdf) is arXiv:2209.11823v3, 15 February 2024, 48 pages.

On 2026-10-10, Theorem 1.3 and the circular setting, Theorem 7.10 (printed/PDF pp.42--43), Lemma 7.11 and its bound (p.43), and the weak-limit argument at the start of the proof of Theorem 7.10 (p.45) were read.
PDF p.43 was also rendered and checked visually.
Coverage state: **Read**, contextual comparison, self-review; only the displayed uniform-bound-to-absolute-continuity deduction was checked, not the complete density proof.

For a circular c_t of variance t>0, scalar star-free from x_0, Lemma 7.11 gives 0<rho_epsilon(z)<1/(pi t) for every positive regularizer and every z.
Theorem 7.10 gives global absolute continuity and the upper bound 1/(pi t) for the limiting Brown density.
The argument on p.45 combines the bound with weak convergence and outer regularity of planar area.
The paper permits x_0 in the affiliated log-plus class, including bounded x_0 in a finite tracial algebra.

This shows what uniform control adds to a positive-regularizer density formula.
It does not assert that arbitrary matrix-coefficient semicircular pencils are scalar free circular perturbations, or that positivity of regularized densities alone excludes limiting singular measure.
The triangular-elliptic extension, unbounded subordination theory and remaining proofs were not reviewed.
No source prose was reused.
