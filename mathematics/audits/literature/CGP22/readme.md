# CGP22: quantitative GUE resolvent comparison

The retained [original preprint](../../../literature/pdf/CGP22--arxiv-v1.pdf) is arXiv:1912.04588v1, 10 December 2019, 40 pages.
The journal identity is recorded in the canonical bibliography.
Corollary 1.3 on p. 5 is the selected normalized resolvent comparison for [F0012's mesoscopic argument](../../../claims/Yin_C_F0186_v1.md#polynomial-application).
Its fixed matrix-amplification statement is also used in the [affine determinant extension](../../../claims/Yin_C_F0186_v2.md#logarithmic-tail), with the coefficient dimension held fixed.
The corollary allows arbitrary deterministic matrix variables and their adjoints inside a fixed selfadjoint formal polynomial, with normalized trace on the product dimension and error polynomial in the deterministic matrix norms.
It concerns GUE inputs; the separate [Sch05 affine real-Gaussian comparison](../Sch05/Yin_U001.md#goe-hermitization-specialization) supplies the currently inspected GOE interface.
Proposition 2.11 on p. 10, Proposition 4.4 on p. 30 and the real GUE coordinates on p. 31 were inspected for the [stronger convergence argument](../../../claims/Yin_C_F0186_v3.md#inputs).
These supply the stated norm-tail/moment comparison and global Gaussian Lipschitz concentration; the proof uses the global affine setting.
No full original-proof audit is supplied by this locator.
