## CR94-U001: necessity of state-family independence

Date: 2026-09-11.
Source: the registered [CR94 published article](../../../literature/pdf/CR94.pdf), Section 3.1 pp.524-525 and Section 4.2 pp.527-528.

For a nonzero element f of the free field over an infinite scalar field, a least-size representation f=uB^-1v with full affine B and scalar u,v has complex-linearly independent left and right families (uB^-1)_j and (B^-1v)_j when the field is C.
No invertibility of B's constant coefficient is required.

This is Lemma 4.5, p.527.
The polynomial-core research author and the dual-domain independent reviewer both inspected its complete short proof in the primary published version: a dependent state is made zero by a scalar basis change, and an invertible smaller row or column minor gives a smaller affine representation.
Proposition 4.7 supplies the converse, which is not used here.

Its analytical conclusions are new deductions, not assertions of CR94.
The article's scalar-extension, uniqueness and converse proofs remain outside this selected input.
