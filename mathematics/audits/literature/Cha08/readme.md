# Compact-support lower-envelope theorem

Theorems A.3 and A.4 on printed p. 20 of [Djalil Chafaï's single-author manuscript of 10 August 2008](https://hal.science/hal-00310528v1/file/cirmar.pdf#page=20) state the descent inequality and quasi-everywhere equality for weakly convergent probabilities on a common compact set.
The source attributes these deterministic results to Saff and Totik and uses the negative logarithmic kernel.
The [fixed-shift argument](../../../claims/Yin_C_F0348_v1.md#lower-envelope) changes that sign and verifies compact truncation for expected eigenvalue measures.
Inspection covered these statements and their domain.
In the [retained 25-page arXiv v1 manuscript of 11 August 2008](../../../literature/pdf/Cha08--arxiv-v1.pdf), Theorems A.3 and A.4 appear on PDF page 21 and use equation (27).
In the linked HAL manuscript of 10 August the corresponding equation is (26).
The [2012 journal paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00440-010-0336-1) is by Bordenave, Caputo and Chafaï and has a different appendix.
