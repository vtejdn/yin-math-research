# An Early Use of Rational Closure

## Coh71-U001

Source: [Coh71, published article scan](../../../literature/pdf/Coh71.pdf), Section 7, The matrix method, printed p.612 / PDF p.18.
Artifact identity is in the registry (private lookup: `../../../literature/registry.md#coh71-rational-closure-history`).

Date and inspection: 2026-10-11; direct visual reading for Yin, separately checked by the source-search agent and an additional authoring agent.
Reading coverage: Read.
The name and construction were checked for historical citation, not the article's proofs.

### Inspected content and scope

Theorem 7.1 states that for an admissible family of square matrices over a ring \(R\) and a homomorphism \(f:R\to S\) inverting them, the entries of the inverse matrices over S form a subring of \(S\).
The following paragraph specializes to a field \(S\), takes the family of all matrices over \(R\) whose images are invertible, and names the resulting subring the rational closure of \(R\) under \(f\).

This supports an early occurrence of the name and matrix-inverse construction.
It does not establish first historical use, and the field specialization in that paragraph is retained.
The general ambient-algebra definition is independently supported by [MSY23 Definition 4.7](../MSY23/Yin_U003_v1.md#definitions-47-and-410-two-closures-in-one-ambient-algebra) and [Coh85 Section 7.1](../Coh85/Yin_U001_v1.md#rational-closure-comparison).

No strictness assertion is attributed to the field specialization in Coh71.
The general difference between division closure and rational closure is stated in Coh85 Exercise 7.1.4 and MSY23 Section 4.3, pp.25--26; these passages were directly checked.

### Search boundary

The search began with the earlier Cohn works cited around matrix localization, then directly inspected the available primary articles and Coh85 Chapter 7.
Cohn's Inversive localization in noetherian rings (1973), Section 3, p.686, also explicitly recalls rational closure in the general homomorphism setting; its [academic-hosted scan](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/cohninv.pdf) was read as a corroborating search lead, not a retained proof input.
Coh85 Section 7.1, p.382, gives the general matrix-inverse definition.
The bounded search ended once the 1971 primary passage and the directly applicable later definitions supplied the requested historical reference and distinction.
