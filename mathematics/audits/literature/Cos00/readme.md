# Coste’s An Introduction to Semialgebraic Geometry

Coste lists the Pisa publication in 2000 in his [bibliography, entry B00a](https://perso.univ-rennes1.fr/michel.coste/articles.html).
The [Rennes text](https://perso.univ-rennes1.fr/michel.coste/polyens/SAG.pdf) is dated October 2002 on the cover.
The [local copy of that edition](../../../literature/pdf/Cos00--october-2002.pdf) comes from a [public teaching mirror](http://nicolas.patrois.free.fr/maths/agr%C3%A9gation/documents/semialgebraic-geometry-Coste.pdf).
It has 78 PDF pages, with its [supplied contents](../../../literature/contents/Cos00--october-2002.md) on PDF pp. 2–3.

## First-order formulas over the reals

Theorem 2.6 on printed p. 28 (PDF p. 29) allows parameters in the ordered field of real numbers.
It makes the set defined by any first-order formula in real variables semialgebraic.
The proof uses the projection theorem for existential quantifiers and complementation for universal quantifiers.
These are the operations used to select the resolvent branch and the maximum over a compact semialgebraic family in the [uniform singular-value count](../../../claims/Yin_C_F0195_v2.md#argument).
