# DM17: matrix-space rank regularity

The retained [published PDF](../../../literature/pdf/DM17.pdf) is Harm Derksen and Visu Makam, *Polynomial degree bounds for matrix semi-invariants*, Advances in Mathematics 310 (2017), 44--63, DOI 10.1016/j.aim.2017.01.018.
The author-hosted copy has 20 PDF pages; journal p. 49 is PDF p. 6.
The title, authors, journal identity and this page correspondence were checked in the original.

Definitions 2.1--2.2 and Proposition 2.3 on journal p. 49 were inspected for the [affine GUE determinant argument](../../../claims/Yin_C_F0186_v2.md#generic-rank).
The complete adopted square-blow-up definition and the exact rank-divisibility statement are retained in [C_F0186_v2](../../../claims/Yin_C_F0186_v2.md#matrix-space-rank).
The proposition is attributed there to Ivanyos, Qiao and Subrahmanyam; its original proof is an accepted external input, not newly audited here.
The application is over the infinite field of complex numbers and concerns the maximum rank of the matrix space.
It does not assert divisibility for the rank of every individual member.

The article has an organization paragraph in Section 1.5, not a supplied table of contents.
No whole-paper proof review or adoption of its invariant-degree bounds is recorded.
