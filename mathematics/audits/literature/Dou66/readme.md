# Dou66: bounded majorization and factorization

Source: Douglas, *On majorization, factorization, and range inclusion of operators on Hilbert space*, Proceedings of the AMS 17 (1966), 413-415, DOI 10.1090/S0002-9939-1966-0203464-1.
The selected [institution-hosted original scan](https://home.agh.edu.pl/~rudol/Operat/DouglasFactorizationLemma.pdf) has three pages and was read online.
The original online inspection retained no local PDF after a TLS validation failure at that mirror and HTTP403 at AMS.

On 2026-10-15, the acsy_to_full_domain agent read Theorem 1, printed pp.413-414, and its bounded majorization-to-factorization proof.
For bounded Hilbert-space operators A,B, the theorem equates Ran A subset Ran B, AA*<=lambda^2 BB* for some lambda, and A=BC with bounded C; the reduced factor has the corresponding optimal norm.
The relevant proof defines D(B*f)=A*f, obtains its bound from the inequality, extends D from the closed range, and takes its adjoint.
This exact proof step was checked, not the entire paper's unbounded-operator Theorem 2.

The source is attribution for the elementary factor construction reproduced in [C_F0142, Section 5](../../../claims/Yin_C_F0142_v1.md#5-the-independent-hilbert--schmidt-equation).
Substituting source A=h^(1/2), source B=(h^(1/2) S h^(1/2))^(1/2), and the bound S>=c gives the bounded factor there.
The complete local Hilbert--Schmidt range argument is additional mathematics and does not follow from source attribution alone.
No formal verification or full-paper audit is claimed.
