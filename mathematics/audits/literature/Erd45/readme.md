# Erdős on signed sums in an interval

<a id="interval-theorem"></a>
## Inspected journal edition

The [journal article](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9904-1945-08454-7) appeared in volume 51 of the Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society in 1945, pages 898–902.
The [five-page original](../../../literature/pdf/Erd45--journal.pdf) is the scan from the [Rényi Institute archive](https://www.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1945-04.pdf).
PDF page 1 is printed page 898.
The article supplies no contents list.

Theorem 1 on page 898 bounds the number of sign choices giving a real sum in an open interval of length two by the middle binomial coefficient when every coefficient has absolute value at least one.
The proof on that page reduces to positive coefficients and applies Sperner's theorem to the sets of positive signs.
[C194v32](../../../claims/Yin_C_F0194_v32.md#argument) uses this real scalar statement after projection and rescaling and supplies the permutation-chain count.
The inspection covered this statement and its antichain argument.
