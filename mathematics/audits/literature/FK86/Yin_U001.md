No visual-page or whole-paper audit is claimed.

Section 1.2 identifies all closed densely defined affiliated operators with the measurable operators when the trace is finite.
Sections 1.4–1.5 use closed sums and products and give a complete Hausdorff topological star-algebra.
Its zero neighborhoods require a projection \(p\) with small \(\tau(1-p)\) and small \(\|Tp\|\).
Thus multiplication and adjoint are continuous, and every measure-Cauchy sequence has a unique affiliated limit.

These established statements and their finite-tracial applicability are accepted; their original proofs are not re-audited.
The paper attributes the foundations to Nelson and Terp.
Yin_C_F0085 supplies its own two-tail support estimate to verify the Cauchy hypothesis.
That estimate is not imported from an unchecked rank-completion theorem.
