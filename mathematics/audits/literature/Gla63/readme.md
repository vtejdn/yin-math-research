# Glaeser on the regularity of a square root

The [retained French journal scan](../../../literature/pdf/Gla63.pdf) contains Georges Glaeser's 1963 article and an unnumbered cover.
Printed pp. 203–204 correspond to PDF pages 2–3. [Journal article](https://doi.org/10.5802/aif.146)

## Lemme I

On p. 203 Glaeser credits B. Malgrange with suggesting Lemme I.
The lemma assumes a strictly positive \(C^2\) function \(\phi\) on the whole real line with \(\phi''\le M\) for a constant \(M>0\).
It gives \(|(\sqrt{\phi})'|\le\sqrt{M/2}\).
The proof on p. 204 bounds \(\phi(x+h)\) from above by the quadratic Taylor expression with second-order coefficient \(M/2\), whose positivity for every real \(h\) yields \(|\phi'(x)|^2\le 2M\phi(x)\).
The statement, proof and credit were checked on 2026-11-14.
Application to a nonnegative function defined only locally requires localization and passage through possible zeros, for example by positive regularization.
The separate \(C^1\) conclusion on p. 203 assumes second-order flatness at the zeros.
