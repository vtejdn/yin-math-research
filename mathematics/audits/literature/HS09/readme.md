# HS09: spectral subspaces and invariant block masses

Source/version: Haagerup and Schultz, *Invariant subspaces for operators in a general II1-factor*, published article, 93 pages.
The [canonical bibliography](../../../literature/references.bib) own identity and acquisition; [HS09.pdf](../../../literature/pdf/HS09.pdf) is the retained Numdam article.

Coverage state: **Read**, contextual comparison, self-review.
Original proofs were not audited.

- Main Theorem 1.1, printed p.20 / PDF p.2, supplies a largest invariant subspace for a prescribed Borel set in a II1-factor.
  Its range projection has trace equal to the Brown measure of the set; the complementary compression has Brown measure concentrated outside the set.
- Introduction (1.3), printed p.22 / PDF p.4, discusses the Riesz idempotent when the circle lies in the resolvent set.
  Ordinary holomorphic functional calculus supplies the contour construction and its separated spectral mass.
  A deterministic eigenvalue-count specialization requires a separate zero-pseudo-covariance hypothesis rho=0.
- Proposition 6.5, printed p.69 / PDF p.51, gives the weighted decomposition of determinant and Brown measure for an invariant projection, with normalized corner traces and weights tau(P), 1-tau(P).
  The page was also rendered and inspected.
  The source permits a broader affiliated class, including bounded operators.

The full invariant-subspace theorem has a II1-factor hypothesis; no extension to every finite algebra was investigated.
A diagonal circular/zero block example can be computed directly from the trace logarithm; HS09 supplies the broader decomposition context.
