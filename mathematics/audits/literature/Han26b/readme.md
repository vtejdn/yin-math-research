## Yi Han, arXiv v1

Yi Han's *Universality of Brown measure convergence for non-Gaussian matrix polynomials with adjoints* is a public preliminary preprint.
The manuscript is dated 14 November 2026 and arXiv records the v1 submission on 16 November. [Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20657v1)
The printed page numbers agree with the PDF page numbers throughout the 43 pages. [PDF](../../../literature/pdf/Han26b.pdf)
The HTML carries the same section, theorem and equation labels, but its bibliography orders Anderson's paper and the Anderson–Guionnet–Zeitouni book differently from the PDF.
For Lemma 5.5.4 of the book, the PDF cites reference 3 and the HTML cites reference 2. [HTML](../../../literature/pdf/Han26b/source.html) · [Searchable contents](../../../literature/contents/Han26b/source.html.md)
