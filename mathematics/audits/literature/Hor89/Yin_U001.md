## Hor89-U001 — Theorem 2.4.14

Date: 2026-09-11.
Source: Lars Hormander, *Linear Functional Analysis: Lectures Fall Term 1988*, University of Lund, February 1989, [institutional PDF](https://lucris.lub.lu.se/ws/portalfiles/portal/124277769/HormanderLinearFunctionalAnalysis.pdf).
Public online teaching notes; no local artifact or full-book audit.
The title/date were read in the front matter by the dual-domain research agent; the coordinating agent independently checked the exact theorem and short proof through the primary indexed text, while direct full-PDF retrieval failed.
No printed-page locator is asserted.

Theorem 2.4.14 states that a separately continuous bilinear map from E times F into G is jointly continuous when E is locally convex and metrizable, F is Frechet, and G is locally convex.
Its Baire proof was read in that indexed primary passage.
In [Yin_C_F0104](../../../claims/Yin_C_F0104_v1.md#yin_c_f0104), the factors are a Banach von Neumann algebra, C-infinity of a compact interval, and a Hilbert space, so the hypotheses apply.
The consuming proof also writes the actual closed graphs and a direct finite-seminorm-order Baire argument.
This source is attribution and corroboration for that standard step; it supplies no unbounded-domain invariance or graph-core conclusion by itself.

The ordinary Frechet closed graph theorem and Baire category theorem remain standard functional-analysis inputs with their concrete applicability checked in the [historical Yin_C_F0104 review](../../reviews/Yin_R_F0114_v1.md#original-f0040-account).
