## Exact retained statement and proof-inspection depth

- **III.3.2(i), p. 136:** divisibility in a commutative ring with identity is equivalent to the reverse inclusion of the corresponding principal ideals.
- **III.3.9 and preceding polynomial example, p. 139:** a polynomial ring over a field is Euclidean, and a Euclidean ring is a principal ideal ring.
  The displayed proof of III.3.9 was read.
  The polynomial division algorithm invoked by its example is accepted at that standard source interface; no new full Chapter III audit is asserted.
- **III.3.11(i)-(ii), p. 140:** a generator of the ideal generated by finitely many elements is a greatest common divisor that is a linear combination of those elements; a principal ideal ring supplies such a generator.
  The statement, source proof sketch, and warning distinguishing a UFD gcd from a Bezout identity were read.
