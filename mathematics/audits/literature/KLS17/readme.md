# KLS17: source editions

<a id="journal-edition"></a>
## Yin: journal edition

The retained [KLS17.pdf](../../../literature/pdf/KLS17.pdf) is the published Documenta Mathematica edition, printed pp. 659--677, followed by the blank journal page 678.
The twenty-page file has the title, author list, received date 17 June 2016 and revised date 17 December 2016 of that edition.
The [publisher record](https://ems.press/journals/dm/articles/8965506) supplies DOI 10.4171/DM/575.
The [complete source audit](Yin_U001.md#kls17-u001) uses journal page and theorem numbers throughout and retains its unresolved G-theory interface.

The [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.09069v2) is a distinct typesetting with twenty-three PDF pages; its Theorem 3.9 corresponds to journal Theorem 3.7 and its Definition 3.8 to journal Definition 3.6.
This numbering correspondence does not establish complete agreement of the two proofs or transfer the journal audit to every preprint passage.
