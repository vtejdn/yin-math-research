# Free elliptic pencils and universal Hermitian full rank

In the [author manuscript](https://igorklep.github.io/files/regular_fun_23dec16.pdf), Definition 2.1 on p. 4 defines free ellipticity by successive restrictions to the kernel of the positive part of a constant left multiplier.
Proposition 2.4 on p. 5 treats the strong ellipticity case, and Theorem 2.6 on p. 7 characterizes universal full column rank on Hermitian tuples.
For a complex pencil of column size \(e\) in \(g\) variables the finite test bound is \((g+1)e^2\).
The [retained 22-page manuscript](../../../literature/pdf/KPV17--author.pdf) displays 30 April 2018 on its title page.
The [journal publication](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms.12030) appeared in 2017.
The [Wigner determinant application](../../../claims/Yin_C_F0186_v37.md#elliptic-definition) uses the precise rank criterion at the inspected manuscript locator.
