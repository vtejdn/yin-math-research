# KR12: projective symmetry claim and its use limitation

Source: Kumar--Rajpal, *Symmetry and quasi-centrality of the operator space projective tensor product*, Archiv der Mathematik 99 (2012), 519-529, DOI 10.1007/s00013-012-0462-3.
The publisher's identity was checked.
The actual inspected and retained artifact is [arXiv:1205.1679v1](../../../literature/pdf/KR12--arxiv-v1.pdf), 8 May 2012, ten pages.
No preprint/journal textual identity is asserted.

On 2026-10-15, the acsy_to_full_domain agent read the symmetry definition and Theorem 2.1 on p.2, the relevant proof on p.3, Lemma 2.3 on pp.3-4, and Theorem 2.4 with its proof on pp.4-5.
Theorem 2.4 asserts symmetry of the Banach projective tensor product of arbitrary C*-algebras.
It was considered as a possible source of a projective inverse for the particular selfadjoint coefficient Q+i with bounded h^-1.

The theorem was not adopted.
The inspected proof has a concrete interface concern: Lemma 2.3 treats a closed two-sided ideal, while the proof of Theorem 2.1 to which Theorem 2.4 refers uses proper modular left ideals.
This concern triggered the later-source check.
The primary [LSW24b-U001 correction](../LSW24b/Yin_U001.md#lsw24b-u001), directly inspected by root, explicitly corrects the universal assertion and gives the exact replacement: the Banach projective tensor product is symmetric if and only if one of the two C*-algebras is type I.
This resolves the general validity question against using KR12's universal symmetry statement.
It does not decide projective invertibility of the particular Q+i from the nonlinear exploration.
No full proof review or validity assertion for the rest of KR12 is made.

The preprint has section headings but no supplied table of contents.
The historical search and its mathematical effect are retained in the continuing nonlinear exploration; no result in C_F0142 depends on this claimed symmetry theorem.
