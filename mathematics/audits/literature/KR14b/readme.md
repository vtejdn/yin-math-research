## Yin

The retained [public preprint](../../../literature/pdf/KR14b--arxiv-v5.pdf) is Kirchberg and Rørdam's [arXiv:1209.5311v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/1209.5311v5), revised 7 June 2013.
Its [complete supplied contents](../../../literature/contents/KR14b--arxiv-v5.md) appear on PDF p. 1.

The work is distinct from Kassel and Reutenauer's zeta-function paper registered as [KR14](../KR14/readme.md).
