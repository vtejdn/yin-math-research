## Selected reading for the condition-A literature search

On 2026-10-16 the literature reader checked the primary version record, Section 1 on PDF pp.2--3, and Proposition 1.4 on p.5.
The paper initially uses the covariance-normalized quasi-free derivatives
\[
 \partial_i(A_j)=\varphi(A_jA_i)1\otimes1,
\]
and explains the conversion to usual conjugates through Nelson's normalization.
Proposition 1.4 supplies actual vacuum membership in the dual-adjoint domain.
These are source-setting and selected-statement checks, not a full proof audit.

The general vacuum state is nontracial, and individual generators need not be selfadjoint.
Consequently the whole q-Araki--Woods theorem is not a theorem about finite-tracial condition A as stated in the present question.
The tracial q-Gaussian specialization and any covariance change of coordinates must be identified explicitly before using that condition.
The exact normalization and actual-adjoint mechanisms remain potential reusable inputs; no general normal-tuple domain theorem is inferred.
