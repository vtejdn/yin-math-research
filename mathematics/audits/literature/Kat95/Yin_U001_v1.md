## Selected citation check

Checked 2026-10-13 at the user's request.
Problem III.5.18 is on printed p.166 / PDF p.189 and directly states that every closable operator of finite rank is bounded.
It is a problem statement, without an accompanying proof in the inspected passage.
The result applies in Banach spaces and does not require density for boundedness on the given domain.

For a densely defined closable finite-rank Hilbert-space operator, III, Section 2.2, printed p.145 / PDF p.168, provides the unique bounded extension to the closure of its domain.
This is the graph closure of the original operator.
Its range equals the original finite-dimensional range: continuity puts the extension range in that closed space, and the extension contains the original operator.
Thus the range and rank are preserved.
These are the elementary applicability consequences, distinguished from the literal Problem III.5.18 statement.
