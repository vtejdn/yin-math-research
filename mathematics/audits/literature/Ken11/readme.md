# Ken11: graph connections and the sparse Haar-pencil comparison

The inspected [primary original](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1001.4028v3) is Richard Kenyon, *Spanning forests and the vector bundle Laplacian*, arXiv:1001.4028v3, 8 December 2011.
Its title page identifies the publication as The Annals of Probability 39(5) (2011), 1983--2017, DOI 10.1214/10-AOP596.
The electronic reprint explains that its pagination and typographic details differ from the journal original.
The [retained original](../../../literature/pdf/Ken11.pdf) preserves that exact arXiv v3 edition.

Section 3.1 on PDF pp. 4--5 was inspected directly in the public original for graph connections, vertexwise gauge equivalence and cycle monodromy.
This supplies attribution for the graph-gauge viewpoint used in [the sparse Haar-pencil candidate](../../../claims/Yin_C_F0192_v1.md#graph-gauge).
The bipartite row/column recursion, conditional Haar argument and determinant limit are proved in that candidate; they are not asserted to be Kenyon's theorem.
No proof of the vector-bundle Laplacian determinant formula or whole-paper review was performed.
