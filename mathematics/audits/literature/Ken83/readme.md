# Kenne on presentations of direct products

## Yin's inspected edition

The [publisher's three-page article](../../../literature/pdf/Ken83.pdf) spans printed pages 131–133.
On [printed page 131, item (ii)](../../../literature/pdf/Ken83.pdf#page=1), Kenne records the presentation of \(A_5\) with generators of orders five and three and product of order two.
The [icosahedral rank argument](../../../claims/Yin_C_A0223_v1.md#statement) changes generators to an involution and an element of order three whose product has order five.
The article's later direct-product presentations are outside this use. [Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0004972700026186)
