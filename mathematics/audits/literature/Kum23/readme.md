## Selected reading for the condition-A literature search

On 2026-10-16 the literature reader checked the primary version record, the finite-dimensional construction in Theorem 3.4 on PDF p.10, and Corollary 3.5 near p.13.
The source constructs conjugate variables for mixed q-Araki--Woods generators and proves their membership in the generated von Neumann algebra.
The setting includes a real-symmetric parameter matrix with supremum of the absolute entries below one, an orthogonal action and its prescribed direct-sum decomposition.
Corollary 3.10's Lipschitz conclusion was located through the introduction, but its full proof was not inspected.

The general vacuum state is nontracial.
These concrete model results must therefore be separated from the finite-tracial general condition A.
A tracial specialization may supply examples, but this reading does not derive arbitrary-normal-tuple estimates or identify all canonical bounded-vector domains.
The inspection is selected statement and applicability reading, not a full source proof audit.
