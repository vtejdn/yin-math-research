# The Ore condition for lamplighter groups

## Preprint and proceedings editions

The chapter by Peter A. Linnell, Wolfgang Lück and Thomas Schick appeared in *High-Dimensional Manifold Topology*, pp. 315–321 (2003). [Proceedings DOI](https://doi.org/10.1142/9789812704443_0013)
The [second preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0202027v2) includes Warren Dicks' proof in Section 3.
The seven-page [local copy](../../../literature/pdf/LLS03--arxiv-v2.pdf) uses preprint pagination, with Theorem 11 on p. 5 and the end of its proof on p. 6. [Yin's source inspection](Yin_U001.md#lls03-u001)
