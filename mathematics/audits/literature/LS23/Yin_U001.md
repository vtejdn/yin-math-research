For bounded freely independent variables a_i and a regular linearization
\[
 (1-z^mP(a))^{-1}=u^t(I-z\sum_i C_i a_i)^{-1}v,
\]
the conditional expectation onto the algebra generated by a retained subset I is, for z near zero,
\[
 u^t(I-zH_J\sum_{i\in I}C_i a_i)^{-1}H_Jv,
 \qquad H_J=(I-z\sum_{j\in J}C_jF_j(z))^{-1}.
\]
The scalar matrices F_j satisfy the source's Boolean-transform fixed-point system, with the branch analytic at zero.
J is the complementary subset.
This is an accepted local analytic statement, not an arbitrary affiliated-rational evaluation theorem.

Application boundary: the matrix size stays finite after taking conditional expectation.
