## Accepted statement and correction

For C*-algebras A and B, their Banach-space projective tensor product is symmetric if and only if at least one factor is type I.
Symmetry means that the spectrum of every element of the form a* a is nonnegative.

The introduction explicitly corrects the universal symmetry assertion in [KR12](../../../literature/pdf/KR12--arxiv-v1.pdf), cited there as reference 35.
Example 1.2 embeds the nonsymmetric algebra l1(F2) isometrically as the diagonal closed star subalgebra of the projective tensor square of the reduced free-group C*-algebra.
Its norm calculation was checked; nonsymmetry of l1(F2) and inheritance by closed star subalgebras remain the source's accepted standard inputs.

## Effect on the nonlinear exploration
