# Commutation with a Generated von Neumann Algebra

<a id="module-commutation-search"></a>
## Selected source and applicability check

Date: 2026-10-14.
Question: whether commutation of a closed operator with an adjoint-closed subset of bounded operators extends to the von Neumann algebra generated by that subset.

Source: Zhe Liu, A double commutant theorem for Murray-von Neumann algebras, PNAS 109(20) (2012), 7676--7681, DOI 10.1073/pnas.1203754109.
Retained artifact: [Liu12--early-edition.pdf](../../../literature/pdf/Liu12--early-edition.pdf), PNAS Early Edition author copy.

### Verified source statement

Lemma 17, Section 2.2, Early Edition PDF p.3: if A is a closed operator on H and CA is contained in AC for every C in an adjoint-closed subset F of B(H), then BA is contained in AB for every B in W*(F).

The inclusion means that each bounded C preserves Dom A and A(C xi)=C(A xi) there.
No symmetry or normality of A is assumed in this statement.
The selected passage is a directly stated background result, without a separate proof.
Section 2 points generally to Kadison--Ringrose Sections 2.7 and 5.6; no exact book theorem number or independent inspection of those books is claimed.

### Applicability limits

The result assumes a closed operator and commutation, including domain invariance, for an adjoint-closed subset of B(H).
A closable operator or commutation with normal generators alone is not the literal hypothesis.
Passage to the closure and commutation with the adjoint generators require separate arguments.
Lemma 17 does not itself supply an adjoint-intertwining theorem for a bounded map from a von Neumann algebra to its L2 space.
