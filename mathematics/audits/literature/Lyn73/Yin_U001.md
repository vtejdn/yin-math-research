The accepted input is that the kernel of the canonical homomorphism from a finite free product of groups to their direct product is free, with finite index and finite rank when all factors are finite.
The source attributes the original cyclic-group result to Nielsen and states its extension to arbitrary group factors.
This is an attribution through Lyn73; Nielsen's original paper was not independently inspected.

Yin_C_F0089 applies it only to finitely many finite cyclic groups.
Their direct product is finite, so the resulting free subgroup has the precise finite-index, finite-rank properties needed by the separate operator-algebra transfer proof.
The source supplies no bounded-division-closure assertion; that is the project deduction.
