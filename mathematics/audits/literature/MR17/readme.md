# MR17: free Hilbert transforms and the remaining endpoint interface

The inspected source is Mei and Ricard, *Free Hilbert Transforms*, [arXiv:1605.02125v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.02125v4), revised 5 September 2016, with title page dated 6 September 2016, 25 pages.
The canonical bibliography records the published identity Duke Mathematical Journal 166(11) (2017), 2153–2182, DOI 10.1215/00127094-2017-0007, as stated on the arXiv source page.
The acquired [v4 PDF](../../../literature/pdf/MR17--arxiv-v4.pdf) has its own pagination; it was not compared page by page with the publisher version.

On 2026-10-15 the general ACSY-to-full-domain branch read the reduced-word and left/right projection definitions on printed/PDF pp.4–5, Theorem 3.5 on p.7, Corollary 3.12 on p.9, Theorem 3.17 on p.12 and the length-reduction statement Corollary 4.7 on p.16.
The introduction and proof openings were also read.
This is a statement/applicability inspection, not a complete proof audit.

Theorem 3.5 bounds the free Hilbert transforms formed from reduced-word first- or last-factor projections on Lp for 1<p<infinity, with coefficients in the center of the amalgamation algebra.
Corollary 3.12 gives the corresponding finite-p column estimates.
Theorem 3.17 supplies a length-independent free Rosenthal comparison, while Corollary 4.7's length reduction is stated for the free-group setting.
None of these displayed statements is an endpoint L-infinity contraction theorem for an arbitrary Hilbert kernel.

The search application in [D_F0003](../../../drafts/Yin_D_F0003.md#one-hidden-factor-actual-a-and-the-full-forward-domain) concerns coupled kernels that replace or collapse the final two free blocks, rather than merely multiplying a whole first/last-factor projection by a central scalar.
That operator has not been identified with a source theorem here.
The specific coupled estimate is now supplied by the direct Fock argument in [C_F0152](../../../claims/Yin_C_F0152_v1.md#argument), whose own selected decision governs its review status.
No MR17 result is used as a proof premise for that argument or C_F0150.
The source remains a discovery/reading lead, rather than a selected reusable theorem input.
