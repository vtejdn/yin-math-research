<a id="flatness-scope"></a>
## Selected source scope

Theorem 2.5 on [printed p. 78 / PDF p. 2](../../../literature/pdf/Mat16.pdf#page=2) gives ordinary \(\mathbb CG\)-module flatness of \(\mathcal N(G)\) for locally virtually cyclic groups.
Theorem 6.4 on [printed p. 89 / PDF p. 13](../../../literature/pdf/Mat16.pdf#page=13) gives the converse when \(G\) is torsion-free elementary amenable.
Their exact statements and hypotheses were checked on 2026-11-12; the complete proofs were not audited.
These statements distinguish ordinary flatness from dimension-flatness and do not replace a proof of weak exactness or assert ordinary flatness for all amenable groups.
This source is retained for that structural comparison, with no new general flatness classification inferred from its introduction.
