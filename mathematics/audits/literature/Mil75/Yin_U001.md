# The hyperbolic triangle group and its full lift

## Lifted triangle presentation

Let \(p,q,r\ge 2\) be integers with \(1/p+1/q+1/r<1\).
In the hyperbolic plane choose a triangle with angles \(\pi/p,\pi/q,\pi/r\).
Milnor's \(\Sigma(p,q,r)\) is the orientation-preserving subgroup of the group generated by the reflections in its sides.
Let \(G\to\operatorname{Isom}^{+}(\mathbb H^2)\) be the universal covering homomorphism.
Milnor defines \(\Gamma(p,q,r)\) as the full inverse image of \(\Sigma(p,q,r)\) in \(G\). [Mil75, §§2–3, pp. 179 and 185–186](../../../literature/pdf/Mil75.pdf#page=11)

With Milnor's orientation and ordering, let \(\gamma_1,\gamma_2,\gamma_3\) be the lifts along the one-parameter rotations about the vertices through \(2\pi/p,2\pi/q,2\pi/r\).
Lemma 3.1 gives the presentation
\[
\Gamma(p,q,r)=
\langle \gamma_1,\gamma_2,\gamma_3
\mid \gamma_1^p=\gamma_2^q=\gamma_3^r=\gamma_1\gamma_2\gamma_3\rangle .
\]
The common value \(c\) is the lift of a positive full rotation and generates the infinite cyclic central kernel of \(\Gamma(p,q,r)\to\Sigma(p,q,r)\). [Mil75, Lemma 3.1, pp. 186–189](../../../literature/pdf/Mil75.pdf#page=12)

The circle of rotations about one point induces an isomorphism on fundamental groups because the hyperbolic plane is contractible.
This identifies the full turn as a generator and fixes the powers of the three lifted rotations.
Their product stays in the discrete covering kernel as the triangle shrinks to a point, where the Euclidean angle sum gives one positive full turn.
For the abstractly presented group the common power is central, and quotienting by it gives the triangle-group presentation of Corollary 2.5.
The abstract cyclic subgroup it generates maps onto the infinite cyclic geometric kernel, hence isomorphically, which proves that the displayed relations present the full lift. [Mil75, Corollary 2.5, p. 182](../../../literature/pdf/Mil75.pdf#page=8) and [Lemma 3.1, pp. 187–189](../../../literature/pdf/Mil75.pdf#page=13)

The inspection covered the hyperbolic argument in Lemma 3.1 and the cited presentation of its quotient in the [1975 chapter](readme.md#yin-edition).
The identification of fundamental groups of Brieskorn manifolds in later sections is outside this inspection.
