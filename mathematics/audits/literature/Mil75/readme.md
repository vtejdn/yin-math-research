# Milnor's chapter on three-dimensional Brieskorn manifolds

<a id="yin-edition"></a>
## Yin's inspected edition

The [chapter scan](../../../literature/pdf/Mil75.pdf) begins with the article on printed p. 175 and ends with the author's note on printed p. 225, corresponding to PDF pp. 1–51.
The [2024 archive copy of the University of Edinburgh scan](https://web.archive.org/web/20240628091936id_/https://www.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/milnbries.pdf) preserves that pagination.
The [author's bibliography](https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/~jack/milnor-pub-2025.pdf) also gives pp. 175–225, whereas the [publisher's electronic chapter record](https://doi.org/10.1515/9781400881512-014) indexes pp. 175–226.

The [source unit on the lifted triangle group](Yin_U001.md#lifted-triangle-presentation) uses the statement of Lemma 3.1 on printed p. 186 and its hyperbolic proof through p. 189.
