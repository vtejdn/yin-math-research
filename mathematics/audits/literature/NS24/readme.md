# NS24: source editions

## Author manuscript

The [nine-page author manuscript](../../../literature/pdf/NS24--author.pdf) comes from [Pace Nielsen's public copy](https://mathdept.byu.edu/~pace/KaplanskyConjecture_web.pdf).
The [author's publication list](https://mathdept.byu.edu/~pace/research.html) gives volume 23, issue 8, article 2550050 (11 pages), 2024.
The [publisher's Crossref deposit](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1142/S0219498825500501) dates the online publication to October 6, 2023 and the print issue to 2024.

The [finite-set input in L_A0037](../../../ledger/Yin_L_A0037_v1.md#the-exterior-cyclic-covers) uses Theorem 1.4 on pp. 2–3 of the author manuscript.
For nonempty finite subsets of a torsion-free group, the absence of a uniquely represented product forces their sizes to sum to at least 16.
Section 2.2 on p. 5 describes the authors' exhaustive presentation search.
This use relies on their reported computation.

Section 4, p. 8, gives the fourteen defining equalities for the eight row and eight column elements in \(G_3\), their two-generator presentation, and the correspondence with the Section 3 tables.
The authors report that the group is torsion-free, all eight elements on either side are distinct, and there are exactly two fibers of size three while the rest have size two.
They also report the exclusion of a zero product over a domain on these supports and refer to [Soelberg's thesis](https://scholarsarchive.byu.edu/etd/6932/) for the computation.
The [four-equation proof](../../../claims/Yin_C_A0031_v1.md#argument) uses the paper's group and multiplicity results and checks three particular double fibers in a finite image. This calculation does not rerun the source's full presentation search.

## The subsequent domain result

NS24 ends §4 by asking about arbitrary domain coefficients for its three groups.
For commutative domains Moe Tabei gives the affirmative answer in [Theorem 1.1, p. 2 of the preprint](../../../literature/pdf/Tab26--arxiv-v1.pdf#page=2).
The proof uses virtual nilpotence of all three groups in Lemma 2.1 and the field-coefficient domain theorem of Kropholler, Linnell and Moody, then embeds the coefficient domain in its fraction field.
For \(G_3\), [Gardam, §4, p. 6](../../../literature/pdf/Gar24--arxiv-v2.pdf#page=6) identifies an integral Heisenberg subgroup of index sixteen in the displayed presentation.
Theorem 1.1, Lemma 2.1 and the stated deduction were inspected in Tabei's text, and the subgroup statement was checked in Gardam's paper.
The finite-index computations and the external group-ring theorem were used at their stated scope.
