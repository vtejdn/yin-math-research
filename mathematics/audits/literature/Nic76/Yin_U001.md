# Semiregularity modulo the Jacobson radical

## Nic76-U001

Theorem 2.9(1)–(2), printed p. 1114 (PDF p. 10) of the [inspected journal edition](readme.md#yin), characterizes a semiregular ring \(A\) by two requirements.
The quotient by its Jacobson radical \(J(A)\) must be von Neumann regular, and each idempotent in that quotient must be the image of an idempotent in \(A\).
The [formal ring calculation](../../../claims/Yin_C_A0147_v1.md#the-jacobson-radical-and-complete-deletion) identifies the radical and the quotient in its own setting.
The November 25, 2026 inspection covers this equivalence at its stated hypotheses and terminology.
