# Pel24-U001 on Hardy-projection commutators

The statement-level inspection covers Section 5.2, equation (5.1) and Theorem 5.3 on pp. 15–16, together with the convention \(B^s_p=B^s_{p,p}\) in Section 2.1. [Inspected edition](readme.md#yin-edition)

For \(\varphi\in L^2(\mathbb T)\), let \(M_\varphi\) be multiplication by \(\varphi\) on trigonometric polynomials and \(P_+\) the orthogonal projection from \(L^2(\mathbb T)\) onto \(H^2(\mathbb T)\).
Peller defines \(C_\varphi=[M_\varphi,P_+]\) on the trigonometric polynomials.
Theorem 5.3 gives, for \(0<p<\infty\),
\[
 C_\varphi\in S_p
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \varphi\in B^{1/p}_{p,p}(\mathbb T),
\]
where the operator-class condition means that \(C_\varphi\) extends to an operator in \(S_p\) on \(L^2(\mathbb T)\).
At \(p=1\), this identifies trace-class membership with the \(B^1_{1,1}(\mathbb T)\) condition on the full symbol \(\varphi\).
