## Pis12-U002

For a bounded linear map from a complex C*-algebra into a Hilbert space, Theorem 8.1 supplies two states on the source.
The squared output norm is at most the squared operator norm of the map times the sum of the first state's value on the input's adjoint times the input and the second state's value on the input times its adjoint.
Both terms have coefficient one.
The assertion gives neither normality relative to a prescribed representation nor a single state dominating columns.
The [omitted-coordinate averaging argument](../../../claims/Yin_C_F0276_v1.md#argument) establishes the extra normality and module conclusions in its application.
