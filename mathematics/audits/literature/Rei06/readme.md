Holger Reich's *L2-Betti numbers, Isomorphism Conjectures and Noncommutative Localization* is a chapter in Andrew Ranicki's edited *Noncommutative Localization in Algebra and Topology*, LMS Lecture Note Series 330, Cambridge University Press (2006), 103–142.
The [323-page electronic collection](../../../literature/pdf/Rei06--collection.pdf) identifies the editor, publisher series and 2006 edition on its title page.
Its [complete supplied contents](../../../literature/contents/Rei06--collection.md) occupy PDF pp.2–3, printed v–vi.

The exact passage used in the [Yin20 source comparison](../Yin20/Yin_U002.md#64-the-reich-strengthening-and-its-corrected-primary-locator) is Proposition 4.9 and the following unnumbered remark on printed p.122 / PDF p.132.
