# Sar42: critical values of differentiable maps

## Journal edition

The [canonical bibliography](../../../literature/references.bib) identifies Arthur Sard's 1942 article in the *Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society*, volume 48, pp. 883--890.
The [AMS journal PDF](https://www.ams.org/journals/bull/1942-48-12/S0002-9904-1942-07811-6/S0002-9904-1942-07811-6.pdf) is the eight-page edition used here, with the logical original at [Sar42.pdf](../../../literature/pdf/Sar42.pdf).
Printed pp. 883--890 correspond to PDF pp. 1--8.

The [regular-value statement and applicability inspection](Yin_U001.md#sar42-u001) records the precise source statements and limits of the check.
