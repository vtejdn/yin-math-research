## Statement and Applicable Scope

The contribution uses selfadjoint generators (p. 1272).
On p. 1273 it explicitly identifies the free difference quotient through

\[
\partial_j(Q)\#P_1=[D_j,Q],\qquad (a\otimes b)\#T=aTb.
\]

Here P_1 is the projection onto the unit vector 1 in L2(M).
It discusses the canonical contraction D_j=(id tensor tau) partial_j in examples where it is bounded.
That normalized contraction is not automatically an arbitrary bounded dual from Yin_C_F0001: for a general dual the vacuum formula includes Q D_j Omega, as checked in Yin_C_F0002.

This source is an explicit modern locator for the polynomial identity.
It is not imported as a theorem identifying all HS commutator domains with a minimal closed free difference quotient, and it does not state Yin_C_F0001 for normal generators.
The applicable normal-generator identity is established directly by the local word expansion.
