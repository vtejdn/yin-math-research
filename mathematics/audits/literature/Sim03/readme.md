# Sim03 source and inspection notes

## Yin

The inspected source is S. Simons, *A new version of the Hahn-Banach theorem*, Archiv der Mathematik 80 (2003), 630--646, DOI [10.1007/s00013-003-0500-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00013-003-0500-2).
The [author-hosted published text](../../../literature/pdf/Sim03--author.pdf) has 17 PDF pages and carries that journal identity on its first page; its public address is [HBT.pdf](https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~simons/preprints/HBT.pdf).
Theorem 2.4, printed p.634/PDF p.5, and its displayed proof were inspected for the finite-convex minimax statement.
It provides the standard abstract interface explained in [D_F0006](../../../drafts/Yin_D_F0006.md#what-a-fixed-positive-functional-proof-would-require), not a verbatim statement of that draft's algebraic (7)--(8).
The remainder of the article and its external foundations were not re-audited.
