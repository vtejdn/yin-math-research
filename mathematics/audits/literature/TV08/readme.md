# TV08: complex scalar inverse concentration

The selected [local original](../../../literature/pdf/TV08--arxiv-v5.pdf) is Tao and Vu, *Random matrices: the circular law*, arXiv:0708.2895v5, 29 February 2008, with 46 PDF pages.
The title, authors, version and date were checked in the original.
The public [arXiv version page](https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.2895v5) identifies this edition; the journal identity is also listed in NO15, reference [45].
This work is distinct from the repository's TV06, which is Tolsa and Verdera's Cauchy-transform paper.

The selected mathematical passages are Definition 2.2 on PDF pp. 6--7, Lemma 2.4 on pp. 7--8, Definition 3.1 on p. 10, and Theorem 3.2 on p. 11.
They concern a genuinely complex scalar random variable, its controlled second moment, phase normalization and a finite covering net for unit complex coefficient vectors.
The definitions, theorem and phase lemma were read directly, and the selected definition and theorem pages were visually checked; the inverse theorem's full proof was not audited.
The exact finite-net interface and its limits as a replacement for progression encoding are retained in [D_F0030](../../../drafts/Yin_D_F0030.md#tv08-interface).
