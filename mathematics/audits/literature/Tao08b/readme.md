# Tao's mean ergodic theorem lecture

Tao's [254A Lecture 8](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2008/01/30/254a-lecture-8-the-mean-ergodic-theorem/) of 30 January 2008 is retained as [original HTML](../../../literature/pdf/Tao08b/lecture-8.html).
Theorem 2, equation (5), gives norm convergence of the averages \(N^{-1}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}U^nv\) to the projection onto the fixed space of a unitary \(U\) on a separable Hilbert space.
For a single vector in an arbitrary Hilbert space, the closed span of \(U^nv\) for \(n\in\mathbb Z\) is a separable reducing subspace, and its fixed-space projection of \(v\) equals that in the whole space.
The average starting at one differs by \((U^Nv-v)/N\).
The statement and normalization were checked on 2026-11-14 for this application.
The lecture's proofs and quantitative variants were outside this inspection.
