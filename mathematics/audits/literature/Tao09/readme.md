# Tao's Fourier transform lecture

Tao's [245C Notes 2](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2009/04/06/the-fourier-transform/#rll) of 6 April 2009 are retained as [original HTML](../../../literature/pdf/Tao09/fourier-transform-20090406.html).
Exercise 12 states that the Fourier transform of an \(L^1\) function on a locally compact abelian group is continuous and vanishes at infinity on the dual group.
For the circle the dual group is \(\mathbb Z\), so the Fourier coefficients of an integrable density tend to zero as \(|n|\to\infty\).
The statement, its translation-continuity hint and its circle specialization were checked on 2026-11-14.
The exercise supplies a hint, without a worked proof.
