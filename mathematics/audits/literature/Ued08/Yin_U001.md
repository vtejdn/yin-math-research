## Ued08-U001

Section 2.1 and Proposition 2.1, pp. 2–4 in the [inspected arXiv v4 edition](readme.md#yin), describe the reduced HNN extension through an amalgamated free product of two matrix algebras.
In the group case write \(J=\langle V,t\mid tC_2t^{-1}=C_1\rangle\) with the two edge embeddings understood, and \(T=\operatorname{diag}(1,t^{-1})\).
The factors inside \(M_2(\mathcal N(J))\) are \(M_2(\mathcal N(V))\) and \(TM_2(\mathcal N(C_1))T^*\).
The common diagonal algebra has entries in \(\mathcal N(C_1)\) and \(\mathcal N(C_2)\), respectively.
