## Yin

The published paper is *Remarks on HNN extensions in operator algebras*.
The [arXiv v4 original](../../../literature/pdf/Ued08--arxiv-v4.pdf) is titled *A relationship between HNN extensions and amalgamated free products in operator algebras*.
Section 2.1 and Proposition 2.1, pp. 2–4 of that edition, identify a matrix amplification of a reduced HNN extension with an amalgamated free product.
The [graph-of-groups proof](../../../claims/Yin_C_A0145_v1.md#amalgams-and-the-hnn-matrix-construction) gives the particular embeddings, trace and corner for group von Neumann algebras. [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1215/ijm/1254143997)
