## Unitary multiplicative free Brownian motion

The source works in a tracial \(W^*\)-probability space \((M,\tau)\).
Its unitary multiplicative free Brownian motion starts at \(U(0)=1\), has stationary free left increments, and has laws \(\nu_t\) on \(\mathbb T\) with
\[
 \nu_0=\delta_1,\qquad \nu_t\boxtimes\nu_s=\nu_{t+s},
 \qquad S_{\nu_t}(z)=\exp(t(z+\tfrac12)).
\]
For \(0\le t_1<t_2\), \(U(t_2)U(t_1)^*\) has law \(\nu_{t_2-t_1}\) and is free from the earlier process.
The normalization gives \(\tau(U(t))=e^{-t/2}\).
Construction of this process, its moment formula and the \(O(\sqrt t)\) operator-norm increment bound are accepted from the source's references [1]–[3].

Proposition 1.4 compares any fixed alternating moment involving \(U(\varepsilon)^{\pm1}\) with the corresponding moment using a free variance-one semicircular variable and the expansion \(1-\varepsilon/2\pm\mathrm i\sqrt\varepsilon S\), with error \(O(\varepsilon^2)\).
The source checks first, second and third centered moments and controls terms of degree at least four by the norm increment bound.
The parity argument removes odd half-integer powers.
Constants depend on the fixed word and its bounded coefficients.

Proposition 1.6 proves absolute continuity of \(\nu_t\) for \(t>0\).
For normalized Haar measure and density \(p_t\),
\[
 \|p_t\|_\infty\le
 \max\{1+2\gamma,\ 2(1+(2\gamma)^{-1})^{1/2}t^{-1/2}\}
 \quad(\gamma>0).
\]
For \(0<\alpha<1/2\) and \(t\ge\alpha^{-1}\log(4/(1-2\alpha))\),
\[
 \|p_t-1\|_\infty\le 2e^{-\alpha t}.
\]
The checked proof bounds the real part of the Herglotz transform using its inverse equation, then uses the inverse branch on a small circle for the exponential estimate.
The first claim is for positive time; the point mass at time zero is excluded.
Corollary 1.7 transfers both estimates to the law of \(VU(t)\) when \(V\) is a unitary free from the process, using the cited analytic subordination theorem.
The original proof of that external subordination theorem was not inspected here.

## Liberation process

For unital subalgebras \(A,B\) and a unitary free Brownian motion free from their jointly generated algebra, let \(A*B\) denote their abstract free product over the scalars.
The liberation process is the family of homomorphisms
\[
 \rho_t(a)=U(t)aU(t)^*\quad(a\in A),\qquad
 \rho_t(b)=b\quad(b\in B).
\]
For \(C^*\)-algebras the source uses the full unital \(C^*\)-free product.
The trace laws tend to the free-product state as \(t\to\infty\); this is a distributional assertion, not convergence of generated von Neumann algebras.

Proposition 2.5 assumes that \(U\) is free from \(A\), \(\tau(U)\ne0\), and \(A\) contains a diffuse abelian unital von Neumann subalgebra.
It proves
\[
 W^*(A,UAU^*)=W^*(A,U).
\]
The proof selects trace-zero orthogonal symmetries \(v_j\in A\).
The uniformly bounded vectors \(v_jUv_jU^*-\tau(U)U^*\) are orthogonal in \(L^2\), hence weakly null.
Their weak limit recovers \(U^*\) in the generated algebra.
The nonzero-mean assumption is essential to this recovery argument.
The source's Section 2.4 explains why this finite-time recovery can disappear at the Haar limit.

## Free Markovianity

For unital von Neumann subalgebras \(A,B,C\subseteq M\), the sequence \(A,B,C\) is freely Markovian when \(A\) and \(C\) are free with amalgamation over \(B\) in \((M,E_B)\), equivalently after adjoining \(B\) to both end algebras.
For a process of unital \(*\)-homomorphisms, its past and future generated algebras must be free over the algebra generated by its value at the separating time.
These are Definitions 3.1–3.2.

Lemma 3.3 changes the amalgamation algebra from \(D\) to an intermediate \(B\): with \(D\subseteq B\subseteq A_1\), \(A\subseteq A_1\), \(A_1\) free from \(\Gamma\) over \(D\), and \(C\subseteq W^*(B,\Gamma)\), the sequence \(A,B,C\) is freely Markovian.
The centered alternating-word decomposition in the proof was checked, including its reduction by the outside \(B\)-coefficients.
Corollaries 3.4–3.6 apply this argument to additive independent increments and to liberation.
Lemmas 3.7–3.8 give
\[
 E_AE_BE_C=E_AE_C
\]
and its time-indexed version.
The proof uses the orthogonality of the two centered amalgamated free components.

## Section 4: a stated heuristic

Section 4 motivates the gradient and the integral definition of mutual free information by an anticipated entropy derivative and an anticipated entropy limit at infinite liberation time.
The paper explicitly states that the needed entropy properties were unproved and that this section is not a premise of later proofs.
In particular, the identity
\[
 i^*(X;Y)=\chi^*(X)+\chi^*(Y)-\chi^*(X,Y)
\]
is not established here in the generality suggested by the heuristic.
The derivation of the motivating factor \(1/2\), the commutator expression and the dependence on these assumptions were read.
This unit accepts no entropy identity merely from the heuristic.
