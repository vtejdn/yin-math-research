## Selected reading for the condition-A literature search

On 2026-10-16 the literature reader checked the primary version record, the source setting and Theorem 4.3 on PDF pp.22--23, including the opening convergence and pairing discussion.
The theorem concerns a finite-dimensional complex Hilbert space with a standard subspace and a compatible braided crossing-symmetric twist of norm below one.
It constructs the usual free-difference-quotient conjugate system for the specified generating basis.
The full combinatorial proof and subsequent factor-type and transport arguments were not audited.

The general Araki--Woods vacuum state need not be tracial.
The theorem belongs in the model and nontracial-extension branch of the literature collection; applicability to finite-tracial condition A requires checking the tracial specialization separately.
It does not supply a conclusion for arbitrary correlated normal tuples under only A.
