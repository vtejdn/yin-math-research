## Exact specialization

Let \(a_i\ge0\), on a finite orthonormal basis \(e_1,\ldots,e_d\), and define the commutative multiplication
\(e_ie_j=\delta_{ij}a_i e_i\), with coordinatewise conjugation as involution.
For \(a_i>0\), this is the Hilbert algebra from a one-point weight \(a_i^{-2}\), with normalized basis \(e_i=a_i p_i\).
A coordinate with \(a_i=0\) is the degenerate Gaussian summand allowed by Corollary 3.11.
The source realizes the operators

\[
X_i=\ell_i+\ell_i^*+a_i\ell_i\ell_i^*
\]

on the usual full Fock space.
The vacuum is cyclic and separating and its state on the generated von Neumann algebra is tracial.
Proposition 3.9 gives zero mixed free cumulants, \(\kappa_1(X_i)=0\), \(\kappa_n(X_i)=a_i^{n-2}\) for \(n\ge2\), with \(a_i=0\) interpreted as a standard semicircular coordinate.
For \(a_i>0\), \(a_i^{-1}X_i+a_i^{-2}\) is free Poisson of rate \(a_i^{-2}\) and jump one, by NS06's already checked cumulant convention.

Write \(\ell_w=\ell_{w_1}\cdots\ell_{w_n}\), \(\ell_\varnothing=I\), and let \(W_w\Omega=e_w\).
Proposition 3.6 specializes to

\[
W_w=\sum_{w=pq}\ell_p\ell_{q^{\mathrm{rev}}}^*
+\sum_{w=p i q}a_i\ell_{p i}\ell_{q^{\mathrm{rev}} i}^*.
\tag{Y1}
\]

The source's preceding recursion is
\(W_{iv}=X_iW_v-\delta_{i,v_1}(a_iW_v+W_{v_2\cdots v_n})\) for nonempty \(v\), with \(W_i=X_i\), \(W_\varnothing=I\).
It supplies the polynomial representatives, and the following paragraph gives \(W_w^*=W_{w^{\mathrm{rev}}}\).
The base case of the source's proof accidentally omits the preservation term when spelling out \(X(\xi_1)\); the displayed Proposition 3.6 and the definition include it.
Our base case uses \(W_i=\ell_i+\ell_i^*+a_i\ell_i\ell_i^*\) and the induction retains the preservation term.

The finite commutative version of (Y1) follows directly by substitution into that recursion using \(\ell_i^*\ell_j=\delta_{ij}I\).
It was also checked against the recursion by the exact finite computation linked in Yin_C_F0096.
This is a verification of this specialization and the harmless base-case correction; it does not certify every general modular identity in the source.

The project additionally proves the scalar homogeneous L2 estimate and the residual identity needed for rational boundedness in Yin_C_F0096.
Neither the all-rational theorem nor that commutator criterion is attributed to Yan25.
