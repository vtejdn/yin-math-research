# Six elementary generators in the presentation of the integral special linear group

## You13-U001

In Section 2.3, equation (1), printed p. 975 of the [journal edition](readme.md#yin), Young gives a finite presentation of \(\operatorname{SL}_p(\mathbb Z)\) on its elementary generators \(e_{ij}(1)\) for \(p\ge3\).
At \(p=3\) there are six generators.
Fixing that finite presentation fixes the one-vertex presentation complex and the group-ring supports of its first two cellular boundary matrices.
Quotient maps thus give matrices from the same finite list of words in every finite quotient, as used in the [finite subgroup average](../../../claims/Yin_C_A0153_v1.md#finite-subgroup-averages-of-bounded-realization-size).
The November 25, 2026 inspection verified the presentation statement and its range of \(p\).
