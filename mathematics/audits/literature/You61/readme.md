# You61: complex skew matrices under unitary congruence

## Yin

The selected original is the 1961 Canadian Journal of Mathematics publication identified by You61 in [references.bib](../../../literature/references.bib), retained as [You61--CJM.pdf](../../../literature/pdf/You61--CJM.pdf).
It has 11 pages corresponding to printed pp. 694-704.
The [publisher page](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/canadian-journal-of-mathematics/article/normal-form-for-a-matrix-under-the-unitary-congruence-group/964D0AA8DAC0CDB9079F04331B61859D) identifies the original volume and year as 13 (1961); its separate 20 November 2018 online date is not the paper's publication year.
The [publisher PDF](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/964D0AA8DAC0CDB9079F04331B61859D/S0008414X00013584a.pdf/div-class-title-a-normal-form-for-a-matrix-under-the-unitary-congruence-group-div.pdf) is the selected retained edition.

Printed p. 694 / PDF p. 1, introduction equations (2)-(3), was inspected in text and visually.
It states the complex transpose-skew unitary congruence form with positive two-by-two skew blocks and zero blocks, and explicitly records even rank.
This is the precise classical attribution used for paired positive singular values, and for even zero multiplicity in even dimension.
The original source itself describes the displayed form as classical; no earliest-priority claim is made.
The complete source proof and its general matrix normal-form extensions were not independently inspected.
The [working skew-pairing argument](../../../drafts/Yin_D_F0029.md#singular-pairing) supplies a separate complete antilinear proof, while [its source passage](../../../drafts/Yin_D_F0029.md#pairing-source) records the exact use.
No independent audit of the whole paper or new rolling source unit is implied.
