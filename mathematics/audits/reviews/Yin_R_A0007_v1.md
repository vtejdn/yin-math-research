## Claim finding

No substantive mathematical defect was found in the complete statement and argument of C_A0003 under its stated hypotheses.

The weighted energy calculation uses only bounded operators before passing to affiliated inverses.
The matched row gives the required orthogonality, positivity places the vector in the positive-part kernel, and the intersection hypothesis and nonzero constant column eliminate both kernel coordinates.
Finite trace then gives full unnormalized rank and affiliated invertibility, without a bounded-inverse or spectral-gap conclusion.
The separate invertibility premise on the denominator is correctly retained under the weaker intersection hypothesis, and the one-dimensional zero-denominator example verifies its necessity.

Schur elimination is valid in the actual affiliated matrix algebra, and the complete rational-closure interface places both the Schur value and its inverse in the stated closure.
It does not replace affiliated invertibility by bounded invertibility or assume that the closure is a division ring.

The bilateral-shift argument proves injectivity of every indicated cyclic difference, giving the exact cyclic kernel and its constant-projection trace.
The augmented coefficient-stack condition is exactly the claimed intersection test, and the term \(iK\) for arbitrary selfadjoint \(K\) requires no commutation assumption.

The graph proof correctly handles loops, parallel edges, isolated vertices, reversed traversal and scalar phases.
The tree parametrization and actual loop transports give the stated flatness criterion independently of the chosen spanning tree.
Both a nontrivial group label and an identity group label with nontrivial phase kill the root vector.
The flat-component isometry has trace-one range projection, proving the rectangular rank and kernel-dimension formulas.
The zero-flat-component conclusion and the specified coordinate border for one flat component follow, while the text correctly withholds a general arbitrary-border claim.

The coupled two-edge example has the asserted nonidentity loop and injective positive square.
Its cyclic specialization has smaller eigenvalue \(2-|1+z|\), confirming the absence of a necessary positive lower spectral bound.

## Exploration finding

No substantive mathematical defect was found in the complete current L_A0005, including the final positive-realization restriction.
Its positive certificate uses C_A0003 at precisely the hypotheses checked above.

The complementary-block elimination has the correct noncommuting factor order and zero Schur correction.
All three rank/nullity identities, the empty complementary matrix for \(N=1\), and the admissibility bounds for \(N\ge2\) follow.
Both scalar-test completions have the displayed inverses and norm bounds.
The first has the asserted uniform positive real part and an unmatched border; for selfadjoint \(a\), the second preserves the scalar test with a matched border and an indefinite selfadjoint denominator.
Passing to \(b^*b\) preserves the scalar kernel.

The positive-realization identity has the correct neighboring inverse cancellations in the affiliated algebra.
Its factorization as the adjoint of a closed affiliated column times that column proves the asserted positivity.
The Haar spectral distribution of a nontrivial regular group unitary excludes every scalar rotation from this positive class.
This proves the obstruction to universal exact-value-preserving conversion, while leaving the stated possibility of more general kernel-preserving transformations untouched.

The graph description uses the actual domain of \(L^{-1}v\), and its closedness and density arguments are valid.
The spectral-projection meet estimate proves density of the domain intersection in the scalar kernel, without asserting that the whole scalar kernel belongs to that domain.
The first-coordinate map and its polar decomposition therefore give the claimed equality of dimensions.
The same trace estimate gives the bounded graph submodules of arbitrarily close dimension, with no finite-propagation or discreteness consequence.

The free-group example has a boundedly invertible denominator, the displayed bordered matrix and exact kernel graph, and endpoint rank \(N\).
The weighted leaf-removal proof excludes every nonzero finite-support kernel vector.
The truncated geometric series gives the stated residual \(\sqrt{5}\,2^{-m-1}\) and norm lower bound.
The example consequently refutes unrestricted finite-support extraction in the precise family, without refuting a fractional-kernel-restricted extraction assertion or the Task.

The arc-compression argument proves strict positivity of the finite matrices and their complements, together with independence of every finite set of distinct translates.
Pair counting and Parseval give the exact trace-defect formula and the displayed logarithmic upper bound.
The spectral-counting estimate, including its maximum reciprocal-threshold constant, follows from the two scalar eigenvalue inequalities.
These statements allow arbitrary fractional limiting density despite integer finite counts.
The null-zero-set argument correctly excludes the arc projection from the actual cyclic rational closure, and the projection has infinite propagation.

The concluding limitations match the proved results.
In particular, replacing the denominator by its positive square changes the right border, and positive regularization alone supplies no endpoint continuity of kernel dimension.
Neither the examples nor the positive certificates settle the general forbidden interval.
