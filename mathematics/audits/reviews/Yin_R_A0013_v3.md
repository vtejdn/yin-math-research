For (1), expanding the summands cancels adjacent terms in their displayed noncommutative order, and multiplying by the already certified inverse of \(u=a-b\) gives the stated right-coefficient polynomial.
The identity \(b^ku=uy^k\) uses actual similarity, and \(w\) commutes with \(a,b,u\) because it is their common power.
Subtracting the two cube equations gives \(y\Delta+\Delta z+\Delta a=0\), which is exactly (3).
The equation \(z(z+a)=-a^2\) gives the displayed product of units for \(z+a\).
Expanding \((z+a)^2\) gives \(az\), and multiplying this equality by \(z+a\) on the right gives \((z+a)^3=-a^3=-w\).
Thus its two intertwining coefficients \(y\) and \(-z-a\) are actual units with the asserted common cube.
For a kernel projection \(p\), multiplying the affiliated identity by \(p\) yields \(\Delta(-z-a)p=0\); it supplies no injectivity statement on that projection.
The reused central-column Schur factors establish (4) without treating a nonzero rational difference as an original binomial.

Identity (5) expands to \(b(a-b)+(a-b)a=a^2-b^2\), with the correct cancellation order.
If the squares agree, multiplication by \(u^{-1}\) gives precisely \(y=-a\).
If they differ, \(a^2-b^2\) is a nonzero difference of actual group elements and therefore a cyclic-certified unit.
