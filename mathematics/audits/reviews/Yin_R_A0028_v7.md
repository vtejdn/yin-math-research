### Central compression and the complete allowed sets

For \(H:=\langle r,s\rangle\) with \(r^2=s^3=z\ne e\), z commutes with both generators and has infinite order.
Its regular spectral law is Haar, so every singleton spectral projection vanishes.
The kernel projection p commutes with bounded central functions of z, although no commutation of p with r or s is assumed.
Subgroup-coset decomposition preserves the dimension upon return to the original ambient group.

Write \(A:=psp\) and \(R:=prp\).
For the first operator, the kernel equation gives \(R=z(A-p)\), and its norm square gives \(A+A^*=p\).
The identity \(R^*=z^{-1}R\) then gives \(A^*-zA=(1-z)p\), hence \((1+z)A=zp\).
For the second operator, \(R=p-A^*\), its norm square again gives \(A+A^*=p\), and the same adjoint identity gives the same compression equation.
For either sign in the other two operators, \(R=\eta(A-zp)\), with \(\eta=\pm1\); the norm square and adjoint identity give the two equations displayed in v8, and elimination yields \((1+z)A=z^2p\).
The sign cancels from the calculation.

The cuts \(f_N:=1_{\{|1+z|\ge1/N\}}\) increase to one because the excluded point \(z=-1\) has zero projection.
All divisions on these cuts are bounded, so \(Af_N=z^j(1+z)^{-1}pf_N\) is justified without an everywhere-defined unbounded inverse.
Since \(s^2=zs^*\), compressing the finite three-root projections gives
\[
 t_k^{(j)}(\theta)
 =\frac13\left(1+\frac{\cos((j-5/6)\theta-2\pi k/3)}{\cos(\theta/2)}\right).
\]
The phases and complex conjugates agree with the displayed projection formula.
Positivity of \(pE_kp\), restricted to central negative-weight cuts and then passed to increasing limits, forces the kernel projection to vanish wherever a weight is negative.

The minimum of three cosines separated by \(2\pi/3\) is at most \(-1/2\), so nonnegative weights require \(|\theta|\le2\pi/3\).
For \(j=1\) and \(0\le\theta\le2\pi/3\), the required minimum comparison factors as
\[
 \cos(\theta/6+2\pi/3)+\cos(\theta/2)
 =2\cos(\theta/3+\pi/3)\cos(\pi/3-\theta/6).
\]
Its sign changes exactly at \(\theta=\pi/2\).
For \(j=2\), when \(0\le\theta\le4\pi/7\), it is
\[
 \cos(7\theta/6+2\pi/3)+\cos(\theta/2)
 =2\cos(5\theta/6+\pi/3)\cos(\theta/3+\pi/3).
\]
The signs give \([0,\pi/5]\) and \([\pi/2,4\pi/7]\) in this range.
On \([4\pi/7,2\pi/3]\), the minimum comparison instead is
\[
 \cos(7\theta/6)+\cos(\theta/2)
 =2\cos(5\theta/6)\cos(\theta/3),
\]
which holds exactly up to \(3\pi/5\).

Consequently the stated open allowed sets are exact after removal of their finitely many endpoints, and their Haar measures are \(1/2\) and \(3/10\).
