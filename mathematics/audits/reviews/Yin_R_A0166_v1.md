# Reviews of maximal-rank fibers, annihilator tests and two kernel constructions

## Maximal-rank fibers

At 2026-11-25T09:05 UTC a reviewer independent of proof development examined the full [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0132_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0132_v1.md#argument) and their stated inputs.
The reviewer received the fixed proof and scope without development discussion or prior findings.
The countable group gives separable fibers and each finite rational expression has a common conull domain of specialization.
The bounded range projection at the chosen fiber supplies Laurent polynomials over the finite von Neumann algebra, the exact setting of the [generic-rank theorem](../../claims/Yin_C_A0012_v2.md#exact-generic-rank-for-rectangular-laurent-matrices).
On its support the positive denominator is \(CC^*\), hence the recovered projection is the initial support of \(C\) even when it has nonclosed range.
Unnormalized trace gives exactly the loss of rank, also when it is zero or the chosen fiber has rank zero.
The reviewer checked descent for arbitrary affiliated constants, the reduction from rectangular group-ring kernels to scalar regularity, both finite-index applications and the height calculation for the cyclic extension.
The final statement explicitly confines the virtually abelian extension to the regularity equivalence and the argument identifies the subgroup and scalar trace.
At 09:22 UTC the same reviewer checked these clarifications and both directions of the further [relative commutant criterion](../../claims/Yin_C_A0132_v1.md#a-test-in-the-relative-commutant-of-a-cyclic-extension).
A nonzero Fourier coefficient off the subgroup has finite conjugacy orbit under that subgroup by square summability.
The finite orbit sum gives the converse, and the centralizer product has the indicated finite index by the height map even for an infinitely generated base.

## Scalar annihilators

At 2026-11-25T09:08 UTC a separate reviewer independent of proof development examined the entire [annihilator criterion](../../claims/Yin_C_A0133_v1.md#statement) and its [proof](../../claims/Yin_C_A0133_v1.md#argument) without development discussion or prior findings.
Properness of the affiliated matrix involution applies to the column with entries \(xz,y_i^*z\), including when its entries are unbounded.
Finite trace identifies a zero kernel with affiliated invertibility, so the positive sum supplies an actual inverse in the division closure.
The resulting formula gives the orthogonal kernel projection also for a unit, the zero element, an empty set of generators and nonclosed range.
The rectangular block argument proves the needed coherence property for a regular ring.
The [amenable localization theorem](../../claims/Yin_C_A0001_v2.md#rational-kernel-localization) applies to unbounded scalar rational elements and produces a group-ring annihilator using the separating trace vector.
These arguments prove each equivalence, and leave finite generation for unrestricted torsion-free amenable groups undecided.

## Polynomial relations and supported kernel generators

At 2026-11-25T09:05 UTC a further reviewer independent of the authors of both notes checked their full arguments, examples and the cited interfaces.
The [right polynomial formula](../../ledger/Yin_L_A0075_v1.md#a-formula-from-right-polynomial-relations) works with several initial coefficients and with infinite compressed spectral support.
In the free-group pencil the conjugates of the second generator give independent tree boundaries, forcing independence of the second coordinates in triangular order and then of the full sequence.
This excludes the polynomial method even for a rational corner.
Definability of a single rational expression at zero forces its measure limit to be rational, and the kernel graph supplies such an expression in the converse direction.
The [supported-kernel formula](../../ledger/Yin_L_A0076_v1.md#a-finite-set-of-kernel-generators-after-hilbert-space-closure) allows redundant generators, and Følner density gives its right-coherence corollary for amenable groups.
The second free-group pencil has zero group-ring kernel and positive Hilbert kernel by independent tree and graph computations.
At 09:08 UTC the reviewer of the annihilator criterion also checked the corrected existential choice of an injective generator matrix in the amenable domain case and the uniform order of scalar Gram pivots for the compressed resolvent.

## Coamenable subgroups

At 2026-11-25T09:22 UTC an independent reviewer examined the entire [coamenable-subgroup theorem](../../claims/Yin_C_A0134_v1.md#statement) and [proof](../../claims/Yin_C_A0134_v1.md#argument), without prior findings or development discussion.
Finite coset trace compression bounds the rank in the nonnormal case and the interval example attains the bound for every positive window size.
Injective projection of the window kernel onto its upper coordinates preserves dimension after closing the image, and the bounded lifted columns solve the affiliated equation by Schur elimination.
The reviewer also checked the first deficient Gram block for rectangular affiliated coefficients, the normalization of unbounded columns, and the separate propagation of detection and density from the respective hypotheses on the subgroup.
The scalar coherence consequence and the attainment formula hold at their stated scopes.

## Principal right ideals

At 2026-11-25T09:22 UTC a separate reviewer outside the earlier development examined the whole [principal-ideal investigation](../../ledger/Yin_L_A0077_v1.md#the-obstruction-for-one-principal-right-ideal).
The right-module exact sequence gives the displayed Tor quotient and the finite sum of left supports recovers the kernel projection by a scalar affiliated inverse.
The rank-density estimate handles unbounded inputs in \(p_xU\).
The reviewer checked detection at all matrix sizes in the distinguished-atom model, the part of the scalar kernel missed by those solutions, and both implications of the orthogonality criterion using the finite-length regular module.
The reviewer supplied the refinement without the distinguished atom, where rational kernels are dense and the scalar quotient is nonzero; this last construction was checked by the main researcher as a contribution of that reviewer.
