# Reviews of scalar kernel tests, elimination of columns and formal constant terms

Separate reviewers received the fixed statements, proofs and their directly cited mathematical inputs.
They did not receive the corresponding development discussions, author explanations or previous findings before making their initial assessments.
Each finding below concerns the indicated fixed version from November 25, 2026.

## Periodic scalar tests

The independent reviewer examined the whole [scalar encoding and specialization](../../claims/Yin_C_A0138_v1.md#statement) and its [proof](../../claims/Yin_C_A0138_v1.md#argument) at 10:18 UTC.
Finite-height solutions are dense after grouping a periodic sequence into copies of the infinite cyclic group, including for rectangular graph relations.
The reviewer checked the positive-measure cylinder transfer with bounded group von Neumann algebra coefficients on the readout, preservation of admissible inverse gates and descent of constant operators by a bounded left denominator.
On the repeated marker word the matrix units map to the ordinary units and the unit \(a_X+p_{\ker a_X}\) forces the exact target kernel.
The reviewer checked width one, the zero matrix, full column rank and the failure of the same periodic evaluation for infinite cyclic lamps.
The published comparison in Ara and Claramunt was checked at its periodic-orbit statement, including the identification of the relevant regular closure in the lamplighter case.
The reviewer spotted an edition discrepancy for the introductory theorem number.
The retained citation uses Theorem 4.11 and the full title of the published article.

## Elimination of columns

At 10:14 UTC an independent reviewer checked the [block elimination theorem](../../claims/Yin_C_A0139_v1.md#statement) and its complete [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0139_v1.md#argument).
On the two orthogonal supports the positive denominator recovers the inverse between supports.
The shear gives an equivalence of affiliated kernel modules, and the second positive denominator gives the bounded orthogonal projection even when the shear is unbounded or a Hilbert range is nonclosed.
The reviewer checked the dimensions and orientations of the right-module cellular boundaries, algebraic exactness in degree one, and the necessity as well as sufficiency of vanishing first reduced \(L^2\)-Betti number.

## Finite regular moments

At 10:20 UTC another independent reviewer checked the [marked tracial construction](../../claims/Yin_C_A0140_v1.md#statement) and its full [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0140_v1.md#argument).
The reviewer inspected published Theorem 4.1 of Arizmendi, Cébron, Speicher and Yin and Theorem 5.6 and Example 5.24 of Mai, Speicher and Yin at their points of use, together with the fixed Cayley and Schur construction.
The atomless comparison for unstarred polynomial matrices gives the required ranks for the mixed tuples.
The free product embeddings and two admissible bordered pencils give scalar injectivity and rank dominance, excluding the nonzero lower right corner from the division closure.
The centered free product grading and disjoint intervals in the Schreier basis give all stated trace agreements.
The witness matrix can change with the radius, and the free group's regular trace itself lies outside the scope of the example.

## Formal constant terms

An independent review at 10:17 UTC covered the [two formal identities](../../claims/Yin_C_A0141_v1.md#statement) and the full [proof](../../claims/Yin_C_A0141_v1.md#argument).
The finite Toeplitz truncation fixes the requested coefficient for every solution.
The reviewer verified both formal inverse identities in each leading positive compression, the unit Schur pivots and cancellation of the possible pole after multiplication by \(K^*\).
For descent the interval kernel theorem applies first to a square polynomial matrix and then to its rectangular graph relation.
Bounded multiplication by the left denominator extends the identity from finite-height solutions to the whole kernel, so central-character specialization applies to the constant operator obtained in the direct product.
The comparison with the earlier real-parameter criterion was verified at its stated scope.

## Fourier multipliers

At 10:21 UTC an independent reviewer checked both sections of the [Fourier multiplier investigation](../../ledger/Yin_L_A0080_v1.md#finite-dimensional-matrix-coefficients).
The amplified twist extends by unitary conjugation to affiliated operators and scalar Gram inversion recovers entries in the original division closure.
For the two-torus row the reviewer verified the rational phase, its graph projection, the strictly positive normalized density, the Fourier coefficient convention and both binomial sums.
The resulting diagonal square root rules out rationality after cancellation of any common diagonal divisor.
This gives an obstruction to one proposed method of extracting kernel entries.
