# Reviews of analytic specialization, free convolution atoms and ranks in finite models

Each whole-proof reviewer received the relevant fixed statement, argument and directly cited inputs.
No proof-development discussion, author explanation or previous review finding for the assigned result was supplied before the initial review.
The reviews concern the fixed versions recorded below on November 25, 2026.

## Free convolution atoms

The review of the [free-amalgam statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0142_v1.md#statement) and its [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0142_v1.md#argument) identified an initial overstatement about finite markings.
At 10:43 UTC the reviewer cleared the repaired distinction between regularity for countable groups and equation (1) for finitely generated groups.
The reviewer verified the two nonnegative rank defects at an edge projection, the equality conditions at every maximizer and the affiliated inverse on the direct sum of the two vertex supports.
The calculation works even for a nonclosed Hilbert range.
The nonnormal coamenable subgroup case uses translated cosets and freeness of the edge action; finite generation over the semisimple edge ring gives the entire expected support.
The reviewer checked graph compression and the reduction by word length, and inspected Theorems 3.2 and 3.5(vii) in the arXiv v2 text of Belinschi, Bercovici and Liu at their stated hypotheses.
The imported analytic foundations were accepted without a new proof audit.
A targeted review at 10:44 UTC verified that the support projection yields scalar expressions for the scaled formal resolvent with all gates defined at zero.
It checked every leading Gram block after the support projection had been computed and corrected the wording describing the inverse power series.

## Analytic specialization

At 10:49 UTC a separate independent reviewer cleared the full [analytic and formal specialization theorem](../../claims/Yin_C_A0143_v1.md#statement) and its [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0143_v1.md#argument).
The finite left and right truncation identities give convergence of the scaled inverse at each order in the measure topology.
The one-variable rank estimate supplies the exceptional sets for all gates and the bordered rank argument distinguishes exact vanishing from an asymptotic limit.
The reviewer checked both directions of the specialization map using finite-height graph relations and the first nonzero Laurent coefficient.
For formal Gram elimination, successive support corners strictly increase the order until the fixed positive diagonal term makes the remaining corner invertible.
Stabilization of the kernel of powers gives the affiliated decomposition used to compute the exact pole order, including order zero.
The reviewer checked the positive-probability lamp runs and the conditional torsion-free scope, as well as the convergence obstruction on the infinite cyclic group.

## Finite model ranks

The independent review at 10:45 UTC covered the complete [finite-model criterion](../../claims/Yin_C_A0144_v1.md#statement) and its [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0144_v1.md#argument).
The reviewer checked Theorems 1.3, 2.2 and 8.1 of Jaikin-Zapirain in the published paper, at their respective interfaces of fixed-parameter sofic approximation and uniform rank loss over finite permutation sets.
The auxiliary identity contributes the same rank in every normalized model; shifts of the parameter preserve the single fixed-pencil application of the uniform bound.
The exceptional set is finite at each fixed tolerance, so the two limits also hold in probability for a common random parameter with the specified escape property.
The reviewer checked both Schur complements, recovery of the orthogonal kernel from an affiliated inner inverse, and the necessity and sharp size of the finite rank loss in the cyclic example.
The external theorems were accepted at the inspected interfaces.
