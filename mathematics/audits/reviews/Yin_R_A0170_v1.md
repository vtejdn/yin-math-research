# Reviews of rational series, graph kernels and free copies of projections

Each reviewer worked from the assigned fixed statement and proof, independently of its development.
The reviewers received no substantive development dialogue, author explanations or previous findings on their respective claims before review.
The following findings record the reviews on November 25, 2026.

## Graphs of groups

At 11:08 UTC the reviewer cleared the [graph theorem](../../claims/Yin_C_A0145_v1.md#statement) and its [proof](../../claims/Yin_C_A0145_v1.md#argument), including countable unions of finite subgraphs.
The orbit restrictions and finite stabilizers supply the required rational edge projection at each induction step.
The reviewer checked the two-row kernel calculation, the diagonal edge in the HNN model and the corner that recovers the original group-ring kernel.
The review covered the cited interfaces in Ueda's arXiv v4 Proposition 2.1, the operator-valued atom theorem of Belinschi, Bercovici and Liu and the local coset and free-amalgam theorems.
The comparison with the finite-edge results of Sánchez-Peralta preserves their strong Atiyah and bounded-torsion hypotheses.

## Laurent evaluation

At 11:09 UTC a separate reviewer cleared the [Laurent evaluation criterion](../../claims/Yin_C_A0146_v1.md#statement) and its [proof](../../claims/Yin_C_A0146_v1.md#argument).
The review included the affiliated matrix resolvent, companion linearization, stabilization of its power kernels, and the independent lamp coordinates with ranks \(r^m\).
It also covered the finite truncation isomorphisms and the difference between inner inverses modulo every power and a rational inner inverse.
The reviewer checked the formal evaluation and scalar matrix inversion inputs at their used scopes and Lück and Linnell's Theorem 2.3(3) for complex coefficients and torsion-free groups.

## Idempotent lifting

At 11:12 UTC the independent reviewer cleared the [lifting criterion](../../claims/Yin_C_A0147_v1.md#statement) and its [proof](../../claims/Yin_C_A0147_v1.md#argument).
For a nonselfadjoint lift the left factor in the compression is \(qV^*\), and the right factor is \(Vq\).
This gives \(q(q+K)^{-1}q=q\) with the factors in the required order.
The review covered scalar Gram pivots, identification of the Jacobson radical, sufficiency of scalar kernel projections, and exact stabilization by a complementary or a constant block.
It checked the cited constant-series, finite-truncation and unrestricted-equation results at their interfaces, and Nicholson's published Theorem 2.9, p. 1114, for the characterization of semiregularity.

## Cyclic expected supports

At 11:15 UTC the independent reviewer cleared the [cyclic intersection and support theorem](../../claims/Yin_C_A0148_v1.md#statement) and its [proof](../../claims/Yin_C_A0148_v1.md#argument).
The review checked the minimum-expression and homogeneous-coset assertions in the arXiv v4 text of Jaikin-Zapirain and López-Álvarez, including Proposition 5.1 and the proof of Corollary 6.10.
The bounded transform and left denominator give the affiliated subgroup intersection, and the coefficient comparison works for a nonnormal subgroup of finite index.
The review checked the cyclic fraction intersection, the shift-fixed algebra of the amalgam of copies, all endpoint cases in the trace bound, and the fiber rank computation.
The two-vertex application uses the kernel of the stacked vertex rows in the additive linearization.
The reviewers checked the applicability of imported results at the stated places without recursively re-auditing the original papers.

## Finite determination

At 11:23 UTC the independent reviewer cleared the [finite-determination theorem](../../claims/Yin_C_A0149_v1.md#statement) and its [proof](../../claims/Yin_C_A0149_v1.md#argument).
The triangular and cyclic truncations differ on at most \(nd\) output coordinates even when the truncation is shorter than the polynomial degree.
The reviewer checked the exact finite cokernel, the injection of the surviving polynomial columns and the rank bounds for the bordered matrix, including degree zero.
Noetherian descent gives a uniform threshold even though the scalar parameters and individual pole orders vary.
The dilation and idempotent-defect realizations fit the same finite support at twice the matrix size.
The review checked the cited rank, Laurent and descent results at their interfaces, together with the exact lifting and unrestricted-equation characterizations.

## Free copies

At 11:21 UTC the independent reviewer cleared the [free-copy formula](../../claims/Yin_C_A0150_v1.md#statement) and its [proof](../../claims/Yin_C_A0150_v1.md#argument).
The common kernel at the proposed maximizing projection has full expected support on its corner, so the two-projection formula applies at every preceding meet.
The reviewer checked the zero-support and one-copy cases, both rationality conclusions, matrix amplification and the spectral sufficient condition.
In the example, an affiliated-unit column difference gives the full range after two copies even though the expectation has spectrum accumulating at zero on its support.
The review used the existing two-projection theorem and its source inspection, together with the stated regularity and matrix inverse inputs.
