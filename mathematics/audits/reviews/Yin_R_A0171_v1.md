# Reviews of degree bounds, spectral data and vertex conjugations

The five independent reviews below took place on November 25, 2026.
Each reviewer worked from the assigned fixed statement and proof and the relevant mathematical inputs, without development dialogue, author explanations or previous findings on that result.
The cited results were checked at their places of use without a recursive re-audit of the original proofs.

## Sublinear degree

The review covered the complete [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0151_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0151_v1.md#argument).
At 11:38 UTC the reviewer cleared all three implications, the degree-zero endpoint and every fixed-support and sequence quantifier.
The rank gap is obtained in the parameter space of ordinary matrices before specialization in the central variable, so it is independent of the degree and the pole order.
The review checked simultaneous specialization, the two doubled realizations, the affine Gram realization for every residue, and the weaker idempotent condition at a kernel projection.

## Vertex conjugations

The review covered the complete [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0152_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0152_v1.md#argument).
At 11:41 UTC the reviewer cleared the full affiliated graph identity, the two nullity bounds and the adjoint argument for row rank.
Bounded row normalization preserves the null modules and the ranks on the expected edge support. Stacking the row and its conjugate gives the stated factor of two.
The review checked the Fourier centralizer argument, the rational intersection assumption, the upper and lower triangular vertices in the congruence-group double, and arbitrary affiliated edge perturbations.

## Varying support

The review covered the complete [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0153_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0153_v1.md#argument).
At 11:46 UTC the reviewer cleared the rank comparison over a skew Laurent series field, the equality construction and all size-one and degree-zero cases.
The truncated corank estimate uses right dimensions over the division ring, and the finite-cover construction uses the Hodge projection on a connected presentation cover.
The review checked the Chinese remainder surjection, the two distinct coset translations, the first nonzero coefficient and the size and support of the triangular realization.
For the external inputs the reviewer inspected Young's journal Section 2.3, equation (1), p. 975, and Shalom's Corollary 1, p. 148, at rank three.

## Commuting spectral data

The review covered the complete [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0154_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0154_v1.md#argument).
At 11:43 UTC the reviewer cleared the joint-law projection classification, the kernel-separation criterion and the sharp degree bound.
The transition between two different component values forces a denominator to vanish on their intersection, and sums of squares construct the converse.
A measurable choice of maximal minors excludes identically singular fiber pivots on positive measure. The cofactor degree gives the bound even with unrestricted coefficient denominators.
In the cyclic example the kernel has mass one half and the two joint branches meet on a null set. The leading coefficients give equality for both types of jets.

## Rank at ordinary parameters

The review covered the complete [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0155_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0155_v1.md#argument).
At 11:50 UTC the reviewer cleared the estimates at all admissible parameters, including points below the generic bordered rank.
The loss from changing evaluation coordinates is at most a factor of order \(|s|^{1-N}\), and lower semicontinuity of rank in measure applies to the resulting rectangular affiliated matrices.
The review checked truncated Vandermonde evaluation, the two unit border pivots, both small-size cases, the kernel formula modulo a power of the parameter and the ordered blocks in the final recovery matrix.
