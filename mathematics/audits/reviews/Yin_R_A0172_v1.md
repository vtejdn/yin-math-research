# Reviews of rank-one separation, spectral moments and coefficient descent

Five independent reviewers examined the assigned complete statements and proofs on November 25, 2026.
They checked the directly invoked mathematical inputs without author explanations or development dialogue.

## Amenable limits

The review covered the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0156_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0156_v1.md#argument).
The reviewer completed the whole proof at 12:50 UTC and reread the final statement and proof at 12:59 UTC, including the finite abelianization bound.
The finite-window bounds hold uniformly in all complex coefficients for marked groups and finite permutation models.
The review checked the simultaneous realization of limiting ranks through a prime ideal, both directions of the Schur criterion, the coordinate pivots over a division ring, and the determinant and adjugate support count in a finite quotient.

## Rank-one separation

The review covered the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0157_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0157_v1.md#argument).
The reviewer checked the original statement and proof at 12:52 UTC and cleared the extended statement and proof at 12:55 UTC.
The review checked the sign and adjoints of the constructed finite-rank tuple, the column bounds, the tensor flip and the conversion between right supports and closed left-translation spans.
Flatness gives the separator in the regular closure, complementary supports force uniqueness, and rank-one exclusion gives exactly the division-ring condition.

## Subgroup supports

The review covered the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0158_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0158_v1.md#argument).
At 12:56 UTC the reviewer cleared the complete statement and argument, including arbitrary subgroups of amenable groups.
The review checked the orientation of cosets, the realization of regular-closure matrix equations, the finite-index transfer of their solutions, and the increasing cuts for operators affiliated to the subgroup.
The central rank estimate survives passage to the rank-metric closure and excludes an irrational scalar kernel projection inside a subgroup with semisimple Artinian division closure.

## Countable coefficient test

The review covered the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0159_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0159_v1.md#argument).
At 12:56 UTC the reviewer cleared the argument from the algebraic rank loci through the integer-input formulation.
The ring isomorphism preserves ranks under specialization even though it need not respect adjoints.
The review checked both directions of restriction of scalars, the legality of inverse gates after conjugation and the finite-stage quantifiers.
The final wording specifies enumeration of syntactic candidates, which does not decide the legality of inverses.

## Spectral moments

The review covered the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0160_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0160_v1.md#argument).
At 13:11 UTC the reviewer cleared all four mathematical assertions and their application to the unrestricted equation.
The review checked normalized traces, singular moment matrices, the strict inequality for a single finite certificate, and simultaneous maximum rank on a component containing infinitely many of the given coefficient tuples.
In the cyclic example the Jacobian is a positive Gram matrix after row scaling, and an invertible input reproduces the finite observations made at the fixed singular input.
The proof of finite determination on a fixed support uses both lists of input coefficients in the same polynomial ring.
The reviewer requested precise sources for the real implicit function theorem and the Hilbert basis theorem.
At 13:19 UTC the reviewer checked de Oliveira's [Theorem 5, p. 214](../literature/dO14/Yin_U001.md#do14-u001) against the polynomial moment map and the Stacks Project's [Lemma 10.31.3](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00FO) against the finite real coefficient ring.
These checks resolved both source questions.
