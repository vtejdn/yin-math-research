# Reviews of finite-rank, angular and periodic rationality criteria

Separate reviewers received the fixed statements and arguments without proof-development dialogue, author explanations or earlier assessments of their assigned results.
The reviews cover the cited inputs at their points of application.
The two-run review also checked the named passages of the three external papers against their original texts.

## Witness range tensors

The review covers the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0171_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0171_v1.md#argument).
On November 25 at 14:07 UTC the reviewer checked the inverse difference identity, the order of its two tensor factors and the simultaneous positive denominator.
Following the shortest relator in both orientations gives the claimed ranges after disjoint right translations, including cycles of length one and two.
The free word example forces a tensor factor outside its witness space by homogeneous injectivity.
The statement specifies bounded finite-rank operators, as used by the tensor identification.

## Mixed trace moments

The review covers the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0172_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0172_v1.md#argument).
On November 25 at 14:09 UTC the reviewer derived the nested compact minima and the strict rational-coefficient tests, checked stabilization of the algebraic sublevels and attainment on one component, and reduced the two block matrices by Schur elimination.
The semicircle multiplier gives a false positive with the reversed limits already on the infinite cyclic group.
The recorded proof identifies the zero atom using the unnormalized trace.

## Two-run kernel

The review covers the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0173_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0173_v1.md#argument).
Two independent inspections covered the positive integral example.
The November 25 reviewer checked transfer of finite vector witnesses even for nonergodic full-support measures, the signed currents and the parity of the cofactor signs.
On November 26 at 04:29 UTC a second reviewer reconstructed the preservation of every inverse from kernel inclusions, the border determinant and the complementary supports of the idempotent from an inner inverse.
The reviewer computed the distinguished diagonal entry as \(3/(2n^3+n)\) when the two lengths equal \(n\), and zero when they differ.
The review checked the precise group and closure in Linnell's Problem 4.5 and compared the conclusions for the regular closure and the partial-shift subalgebra in the two cited papers.
The torsion-free construction was checked only for its stated intrinsic subalgebra conclusion.

## Angular contractivity

The review covers the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0174_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0174_v1.md#argument).
On November 25 at 14:13 UTC the reviewer checked the leading coefficient inequality in measure, the compression used for an unbounded coefficient, measurability of the contraction event and the angular cluster argument.
The review reconstructed the graph support from the finite equations, the constant coefficient of the inverse, accretivity inside the half-plane and the unitary Cayley transform at admissible imaginary boundary points.
Power substitution and recurrence on a finite torus give precisely the claimed alternatives for prescribed rays.

The review checked the hypotheses of Ore localization and the row and column reduction in the product modulo the direct sum, also for rectangular matrices of rank zero.

## Decorated periods

The review covers the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0176_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0176_v1.md#argument).
On November 25 at 14:19 UTC the reviewer checked the return-interval equations, cancellation of the common Bloch factor and the trace contributed at every site by a successful cell.
The endpoint collision probabilities give both quotient formulas, including period one and the trivial cyclic reduction.
The subgroup embeddings preserve the rational lamp pivots, so the same group-ring Schur matrix realizes the three nullity computations.
The transfer assertion for torsion-free lamps retains its nontrivial-projection hypothesis.

## Spectral atoms

The review covers the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0177_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0177_v1.md#argument).
On November 26 at 04:28 UTC the reviewer verified the pinned gradient inverse, the cell projection and the three eigenvalue multiplicities, including cells with a run of length one.
The return probabilities and their geometric sums account for the full trace, and the dense positive atoms exclude a meromorphic continuation through any point of the interval.
The periodic projection at a matching slope has diagonal value \(m/[n(n+m)]\) at height zero.
Off that slope the numerator vanishes, contradicting the bounded array rank for a rational expression on the selected diagonal subarrays.
The review checked the full-support and group-trace hypotheses separately.

## Spectral functions

Two reviewers independently inspected the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0178_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0178_v1.md#argument) on November 26 at 04:31 UTC.
They checked that compression to the cylinder for one fixed pair of run lengths gives a bounded scalar identity even for an unbounded spectral function.
The two kernel inclusions then give solvability and a unique periodic output by adjoint orthogonality over the Bloch field.
Both reviewers derived the recurrence for the stable domain, image, kernel and multivalued part of a finite-dimensional linear relation.
They checked the generic minor argument, the doubling of rational degree in each exponential variable, and nonvanishing of the denominator at arbitrarily large multiples of all but finitely many slopes.
Complex interpolation, isolation of the exceptional spectral atoms and the separate endpoint values give the claimed criteria for both closures.
Both reviewers checked the four local inputs at their points of use.
