# Reviews of witnesses and obstructions at the half-probability threshold

Each independent reviewer received the fixed claim and its argument without prior proof-development dialogue, author explanations or earlier assessments of that claim.
The reviews took place on November 26, 2026.

## Low witness ranks

The review covers the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0189_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0189_v1.md#argument).
At 04:52 UTC the reviewer verified rank preservation when clearing both outer denominators, the two fraction identities and the geometry of the endpoint spaces of three simple witnesses.
The eigenvector change of basis removes repeated labels, the four distinct sites give a nonzero scalar, and the least occupied height has an injective coefficient whenever the pair admits a nonzero integer character.
The review checked the tensor identity, the endpoint dimension bound and the extreme-coefficient input.

## Abelian subgroup functions

The review covers the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0190_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0190_v1.md#argument).
At 04:54 UTC the reviewer checked the independent samples, the lower degree bound from the divided-difference minors, and its sharpness after clearing denominators, including negative weights and unbounded measurable inputs.
The review reconstructed interpolation on a positive-measure set of slices, the finite-index coset argument for nonnormal subgroups, and the constant graph lift.

## Lifts of binary lamps

The review covers the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0191_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0191_v1.md#argument).
At 04:51 UTC the reviewer checked the finite-index and central-fiber descents for the projection, including the common conull set at all inversions.
The shift preserves the square-class kernel, and the separate character and square maps give the codimension bound even with nontrivial action on the kernel.
The review checked the application of Linnell's theorem, the binary two-run input and the joint sign projections in the Klein bottle fibers.

## Least coset and finite heights

The review covers the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0192_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0192_v1.md#argument).
At 04:52 UTC the reviewer checked the quotient order and multiplication sides in Linnell's Theorem 4 on printed pages 50–51, its finite-group matrix summand and the final support of the compressed operator.
The reviewer verified the monotone height increments and their summable deficits, and both implications of the finite-attainment assertion on square units.
The statement uses range closure and the example identifies the dimension of the translated right-group span.

## Ranks outside a subgroup

The review covers the [statement](../../claims/Yin_C_A0193_v1.md#statement) and [argument](../../claims/Yin_C_A0193_v1.md#argument).
At 04:59 UTC the reviewer checked that contracting the opposite tensor factors gives respectively a right and a left span, and verified both orientations of the fraction and their single exceptional boundary.
The rank inequality over the division ring forces intersecting endpoint planes on at least one side, including for repeated labels and zero divisors among the outer factors.
The review checked the direct sum of rotated eigenspaces and the step from zero kernel to a unit, as well as the denominator, cyclic field and constant lift inputs.
