## Odd twisted norms and two-sided clearing

At 2026-11-26T09:23 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
An independent reviewer inspected the revised fixed proof outside the correction process.
Eliminating each invariant coordinate pair uses at most one scalar inverse in the next layer.
For size thirteen over the first layer this gives a nonzero fractional-rank scalar by layer six or an actual scalar norm by layer seven.
In the latter case two-sided clearing would turn a half-dimensional idempotent into a nonzero group-ring scalar of fractional rank on the index-two extension.
The revised identification of the commutator subgroup follows from the exclusion of larger abelian quotients.
