# Original-group Gram tests and single translations of half kernels

## Positive Gram compressions and the center

At 2026-11-26T11:24 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
An independent reviewer inspected all five sections and the stated free-projection input.
The projection obtained from the column pivot is bounded even when its scalar inverse is unbounded.
A triangular scaling and center-valued conjugation average give the monomial increment and the exponential estimate in a factor.
Equalizing the traces of the diagonal entries makes the centered matrix projections free after adjoining the indicated free direction.
The reviewer checked all trace normalizations, the exact half rank in that extension and the two positive scalar inverses used to recover the original projections.

## One substitution for the two companion projections

At 2026-11-26T11:36 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
An independent reviewer checked the complete argument and both three-column Gram matrices.
The finite lists of words for the two traces are combined before a common substitution is chosen.
The free evaluations are injective, and only preservation of the identity coefficients of the finite moments is needed after substitution.
A positive gap in the sum of the two actual nullities gives a simultaneous exact pair and both original projections in the second scalar inverse layer.
The reviewer checked separately the weaker finite-conjugacy condition and the rate it yields for columns with growing support.

## Single translations of a half-trace kernel

At 2026-11-26T11:39 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
A reviewer outside development checked the full proof and inspected Hull and Osin's original statements on pages 26 and 30 of the arXiv edition.
The normalized free half-projection moment is one half the central binomial coefficient divided by four to the indicated power.
There are twice the power index many replacements on each of the original and free sides, giving the displayed total error without a selfadjointness hypothesis on the approximation.
The reviewer checked the third-coordinate kernel identification and the precise positive-gap hypothesis giving the algebraic matrix units.
Independent lamp coordinates give the quarter-trace intersection at every nonzero shift, for an arbitrary bounded projection in that example.
