# Independent reviews of the cyclic order-twenty exclusion and the order-sixteen spectral gap

## Five fibers and a bounded transfer

At 2026-11-26T16:09 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
The independent reviewer ran the maintained enumeration and independently formed the nine-monomial Hermite interpolation matrices over the same finite field, including the derivative rows at double roots. Both methods gave 60 and 32 surviving masks with four and five antipodal choices out of 100000. The reviewer also checked the original-group entry on the two axes, the count on each translate, and the argument at the nonzero off-circle roots.

## Excluding a cyclic quotient of order twenty

At 2026-11-26T16:12 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
The independent reviewer checked the normalization and the exact abelianization in the sign argument, the adjacent nonamenable subgroup, both possible orders of the boundary poles, and the five legitimate character evaluations. Counting equal-modulus entries in both orientations and treating the two possible affine tails then excludes the original half kernel. The proof uses the five-point calculation and does not require the six-root cross-ratio identity.

## Closed range at the order-sixteen marking

At 2026-11-26T16:14 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
The independent reviewer checked the degree-four character, the sign of each translated degree, and equality of the two decay rates and complex ratios from the forward and reverse coefficient counts. The trace and singleton alternatives, the formula for a single range inverse, and the propagation estimate for the orthogonal kernel projection were verified on the original group and the indicated quotients. A further reader who had developed part of the earlier pole reduction checked all five sections.
