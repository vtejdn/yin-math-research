# Independent reviews of cubic and quartic transfers and their window bounds

## Level sets of the bounded middle product

At 2026-11-26T16:33 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
The independent reviewer verified the exact determinant tails for nonsingular coefficient matrices, including repeated roots in a Jordan block. For a surviving rank-one product the same superlevel set has quadratic size and linear boundary for both left translations. The reviewer checked the orbitwise nonamenability contradiction, exceptional coefficients, and the compression that identifies the two nonzero poles and leaves a monomial determinant.

## The order-sixteen quotient obstruction

At 2026-11-26T16:33 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
The independent reviewer checked the passage from four cosets to the nonamenable adjacent group, the equality in the absolutely summable convolution algebra, separation of torsion in the subgroup abelianization by characters, and the incompatible two degrees modulo sixteen. An independent topological winding observation was not used in the determinant proof.

## Finite support at a general four-coset marking

At 2026-11-26T16:39 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
The independent reviewer verified the polar part and sign action, the even part of the two cyclic resolvents, and nonvanishing after two-sided clearing. The reviewer checked the simple-pole argument using a quadratic number of labels and two linear-size boundaries, followed by the infinite-axis contradiction. The common finite root can lie on or off the unit circle.

## Reversing the square of a mark

At 2026-11-26T16:45 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
The independent reviewer checked the sign character for a general normalized cyclic power, amenability for the square relation, the exchanged adjoint rectangle, and the two-coset equality in its square. The periodic-image argument uses its actual finite cyclic order, including the trivial reduction. The local bound in the square case was checked both when the square is in the adjacent subgroup and when it is outside.

## Matching the four finite coefficient labels

At 2026-11-26T16:43 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
Independent readers checked the full proof. Without a four-coset premise the quartic polynomial and the two-coset bound give the needed fractional fixed space. The original proof inputs for pole reduction and the periodic-image count were rechecked at this scope. In the nonconstant determinant case the readers checked all positive words of length four. For constant determinant they computed the two surviving axis coefficients and exhausted all images of the first label, including the reversed-square relation. They also checked the strict uniform gap and both normal-generation conclusions. One additional reader had developed several analytic antecedents and independently checked the new finite matching.

## The cubic shift and the three-coset endpoint

At 2026-11-26T16:46 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
The independent reviewer checked the reduction to three positive fractional eigenspaces outside the cyclic subgroup, the fold into a cyclic group, and the common-power alternative. In every periodic image the single complete Fourier column gives equality of left stabilizers; the orders one and two are covered. Comparing determinant orders at zero gives the three cyclic shifts, each realized explicitly. The local three-coset gap follows at the stated scope.
