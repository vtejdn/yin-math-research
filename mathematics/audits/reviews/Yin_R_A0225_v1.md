# Independent reviews of a bounded scalar pivot and kernel projections at nonclosed range

## A bounded scalar pivot in the regular representation

At 2026-11-26T16:40 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
Independent reviewers verified the unique possible scalar fiber, the measure-zero exceptional set of constant column lines and the approximate joint eigenvector criterion for unitary representations. In the regular representation the latter vectors would imply amenability. The bounded Schur congruence, the equivalence of closed range and the scalar projection in the reduced algebra were also checked.

## The two kernel projections at nonclosed range

At 2026-11-26T16:50 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
The independent reviewer checked the uniformly bounded graph inverse on its image and the spectral cut recovering the scalar projection from the bottom coordinate even when the original operator has nonclosed range. The norm distances follow from spectral perturbation and the trace along the projection path. For each Sobolev exponent above the rapid decay exponent the reviewer checked the two-sided convolution bound, interpolation, the spectral-radius estimate and closure under inversion, as well as the critical-exponent divergence. The reviewer had inspected the previous version and did not develop the new argument.
