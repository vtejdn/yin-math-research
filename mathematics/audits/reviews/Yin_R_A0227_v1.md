# Independent reviews of one-inverse Schur witnesses and index-four descent

## Two original kernels after one scalar inverse

At 2026-11-26T17:07 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
Two independent reviewers checked the rank and both syzygy statements for the cleared quadratic matrix, the exact second coordinate of each original Schur kernel, the common algebraic idempotents and the elementary completion of a kernel column. They verified the order of the single denominator, the conditional augmentation test, the scalar norm over the sign subgroup and impossibility of any two-sided clearing of its solution.

## Four signed kernels on their common subgroup

At 2026-11-26T17:12 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
Independent reviewers checked both bounded group-ring involutions, all four half-dimensional joint spaces and the left and right syzygies on the eight-site subgroup matrix. They checked the reduction of the two kernel projections to each other using bounded coordinates, closed ranges of the individual auxiliary maps and the subgroup expectation. The elimination alternative gives the stated two norm equations and their original commutator.

## The explicit second-layer basis

At 2026-11-26T17:15 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
The independent reviewers checked restriction of a scalar inverse to the two-coset matrix and its second-layer inverse even when the first layer has nonunits. Even and odd combinations of the two kernel columns are in the subgroup layer and have the stated constant left inverse. The reviewers verified exact inner rank two, both actions, the explicit group-ring matrix for the second mark, and the commutator in the compatibility identity.

## Bounded rank-one outputs for both Schur kernels

At 2026-11-26T17:24 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
The independent reviewer checked both exact orthogonal range projections, the sign coinvariant identity and the split of the mixed word with coefficient minus the spectral parameter. The initial source omitted the explicit linear-pencil hypothesis, and its denominator product covered the final factors but did not explicitly include the two pivots used in intermediate expressions. The reviewer checked the clarified statement and the corrected product, the single further bounded positive inverse, both support projections and the exact ordinary input ranks.
