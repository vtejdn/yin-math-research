# Independent reviews of the remaining abelian markings and the order-sixteen module

## Corner ranks and dihedral images at order sixteen

At 2026-11-26T17:03 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
Independent reviewers checked the two possible extreme kernel lines and the individual column-degree loss, the projective endpoint comparison and the group-ring companion when the endpoint is full. The 48 actual phases at rotation order three, both choices of an invertible corner, all larger rotations and the sign module were checked.

## The fourth-root component and the size bound

At 2026-11-26T17:05 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
The reviewer checked the signed pigeonhole approximation on the whole integral module and its positive-dimensional character orbit. The native enumeration and a separate conservation-mask computation both recovered the seven fourth-root extrema, including the prime five where different ends require different corners. The module filtration, orders and signs of the residual factors were verified.

## The finite eighth-root sieve

At 2026-11-26T17:12 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
Independent reviewers reproduced the standard-library verifier and recomputed the field factors and individual matchings separately. They found 1614 split primes, 43 quadratic primes with 86 factors, and 178 remaining marked cases, each with two possible missing corners. The maximal ratio was 11/48. The sixteenth-root action, the upper bound on its module and the free action on nonzero elements were also checked.

## The remaining marked abelianizations

At 2026-11-26T17:22 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
The independent reviewer enumerated all 564 sublattices of index at most 26 by Hermite normal form and compared the actual character groups after the marking symmetries. The 103 cyclic costs, the seven noncyclic table entries and the count of 35 surviving marking classes were reproduced independently. No realization of a half kernel is inferred from the list.
