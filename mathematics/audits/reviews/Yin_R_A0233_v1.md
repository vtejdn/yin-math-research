# Independent review of constant forms, annular moments and central returns

## Constant forms for the prototype

At 2026-11-26T18:06 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
The lead reviewer checked the five Gram coefficient comparisons, the Pauli normal forms in both signatures and uniqueness up to a real scalar.
Independent traces of the explicit example and the two pole residues confirm the numerical realization and the invertibility of the recovered linear assembly.

## Optimal norm below two and bounded positive returns

At 2026-11-26T18:12 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
The lead reviewer verified the reciprocal-angle product, the anticommuting Hermitian and skew-Hermitian parts, and the minimization of the denominator modulus on the circle.
The thresholds and the use of the annular moment bound involve both the forward and inverse operator norms.

## Annular trace inequalities and finite group returns

At 2026-11-26T18:04 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
The independent reviewer checked the spectral-radius argument in the normalized trace, the complementary corner, the real boundary polynomial, and the two trace normalizations.
The computed reciprocal upper bound is approximately 0.488761.
The reversed negative word and both geometric tails give the claimed finite search.
The wording about trace contributions was clarified to allow complex coefficients.

## Central trace disks and spectral radius one

At 2026-11-26T18:06 UTC the fixed proof received the following inspection.
An independent reviewer checked the central rank-one cut on every projection, the similarity to approximate contractions and the passage to a tracial disk in the cyclic center.
The complete description of the central words, the winding and maximum-principle cases and the bounded Cesaro contradiction were verified, including when the operators are nonnormal.
