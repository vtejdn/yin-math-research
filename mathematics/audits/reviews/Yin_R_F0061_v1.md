## One-normal-generator finding

The complete mathematical scope bound above is valid under the exact [C_F0001 hypotheses and scalar-closure definition](../../claims/Yin_C_F0001_v1.md#statement-and-scope).
No unresolved mathematical gap was found.
The conclusion retains every bounded dual D with [D,X]=P and every bounded f, without an added density, freeness or vacuum-normalization assumption.

1. Normality and M=W*(X) give the abelian spectral representation with X=M_z, Omega=1, compact S equal to the operator spectrum and probability law mu.
   Faithfulness gives full support.
   At an atom, E[D,X]E=0 but EPE(E Omega)=mu({lambda})E Omega is nonzero.
   Thus mu has no atoms, the diagonal is product-null and every finite polynomial zero set is null.
2. For T=[D,f], expansion of fX=Xf gives TX+fP=Pf+XT.
   T, rather than D, has a finite L2 kernel sum a_j(z)b_j(w), with conjugates absorbed in the bilinear factors.
   Injectivity of the Hilbert-Schmidt/kernel identification gives (w-z)K=f(w)-f(z), hence the divided difference off the null diagonal.
   Rank zero gives [P,f]=0 and f=tau(f)1.
3. Fubini supplies a full-measure set of valid column parameters containing r+1 distinct choices.
   Their columns lie in a space of dimension at most r, so a nontrivial dependence yields Bf=A with both degrees at most r.
   Polynomial evaluation at a selected point with nonzero coefficient proves B is nonzero; no almost-everywhere identity is evaluated at that point.
   Removing the finite zero set and cancelling factors gives a reduced quotient of degrees at most r.
4. A root lambda of its reduced denominator on S would be a genuine pole.
   The modulus exceeds any essential bound on a small punctured neighborhood, whose measure is positive by support and nonatomicity.
   This contradicts boundedness, so compactness makes q(X)^(-1) bounded in M.
   Conversely the product/inverse commutator formulas preserve finite rank at every allowed scalar rational operation, as in [C_F0002's rational proof](../../claims/Yin_C_F0002_v1.md#proof-maximal-bounded-domain-and-rational-rules).
5. For exact rank, equality of reduced quotients mu-almost everywhere forces their cross-product polynomial to vanish identically.
   The existing degree bound therefore gives n=max(deg p,deg q)<=r.
   The polynomial (p(z)q(w)-p(w)q(z))/(z-w) has degree at most n-1 in each variable.
   Expanding in 1,z,...,z^(n-1) and dividing by zero-free q(z)q(w) gives at most n separated L2 factors for the actual commutator kernel, so r<=n.
   Constants and zero have rank zero; a polynomial part contributes its correct pole at infinity.
6. H_j=L2(W*(X_j)) reduces every coordinate-algebra multiplier and contains Omega.
   Thus its projection commutes with X_j and f, the compressed bounded dual satisfies the rank-one relation, and compression cannot increase commutator rank.
   The restricted trace is faithful normal and normalized, so the one-generator theorem applies.
   The denominator is a unit in the coordinate algebra and M, and all D_i commutators of its rational expression have finite rank.
   The corollary asserts only an upper degree bound relative to uncompressed rank, not equality with that rank.

The covariance uses fX=Xf.
The coordinate conclusion covers the union of the individual W*(X_j), without proving a converse for arbitrary jointly generated elements.
The proof neither adjoins X* to C[X], assumes an L2-dense holomorphic polynomial core, assigns a kernel to D, nor identifies an affiliated intersection with bounded scalar closure.

The conclusion is membership in F_X=Div_U(C<X>), with no bounded scalar rational representation asserted.

1. Right multiplication rho is a bounded linear anti-homomorphism commuting with lambda(M).
   The trace gives rho(a)Omega=lambda(a)Omega and P rho(a)=P lambda(a), hence [rho(a),P]=[lambda(a),P] with the needed sign.
   Jacobi yields [K,lambda(X_j)]=[lambda(X_j),P] for K=sum_i[rho(X_i),D_i], so K+P commutes with each generator.
2. The normal-commutation proof is complete: exp(zN*)T exp(-zN*) is entire in z when T commutes with N.
   Inserting the commuting N exponentials gives the expression with zN*-bar(z)N, whose skewadjoint exponent bounds the norm by ||T||.
   The first expression supplies holomorphy; the second supplies the bound.
   Liouville for scalar matrix coefficients and differentiation give [N*,T]=0.
   With T=K+P, commutation extends to the adjoints and lambda(M), without a finite-rank Fuglede theorem or a holomorphic-core density assertion.
3. Thus [lambda(f),P]=sum_i[rho(X_i),[D_i,lambda(f)]] for every bounded f.
   A zero common derivative gives [f,P]=0; at Omega, faithfulness gives f=tau(f)1.
   Two bounded primitives with the same actual derivatives differ by a scalar, but this supplies no rational representative.
4. The [C_F0002 tensor proof](../../claims/Yin_C_F0002_v1.md#proof-tensor-identification-and-module-actions) applies with the second-slot-linear inner product.
   Theta(g tensor h)zeta=g< Jh,zeta> is bilinear, injective and maps algebraic tensors onto finite-rank operators.
   The identity rho(x)*Jh=J(xh) gives [rho(x),Theta(g tensor h)]=Theta(gx tensor h-g tensor xh).
   Both products remain L2 for bounded x, even if g,h are unbounded affiliated vectors.
5. Finite-rank commutators therefore give f tensor 1-1 tensor f=sum(gX_i tensor h-g tensor X_i h).
   Finiteness supplies algebraic sums; merely Hilbert-Schmidt membership would not justify the next quotient.
   The canonical complex-linear L2-to-U injection preserves bounded products and remains injective after algebraic tensoring over C.

6. MSY23 supplies a unital division subring F_X of the nonzero ring U.

   If f were outside F_X, then 1,f would be right-F_X-linearly independent: a+fb=0 with b nonzero gives f=-ab^(-1) in F_X.
   Extend them to a right basis and take phi:U to F_X with phi(1)=0 and phi(f)=1.
   Applying phi tensor id to f tensor_F_X 1=1 tensor_F_X f gives 1=0 in U.
   Zero divisors in U and lack of continuity of phi do not affect this algebraic contradiction.
