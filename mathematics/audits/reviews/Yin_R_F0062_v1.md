## Coordinate transfers

Sandwiching with R_h sends the output h^{-1} to 1 and the input functional to tau(h^{-1} xi h)=tau(xi).
Thus E=Delta^{-1}R_h D R_h is a bounded dual, and invertible right multipliers preserve each individual rank.

The inverse dual map Delta R_{h^{-1}} E R_{h^{-1}} works for every eligible dual, preserving the universal dual-system quantifier.
