## Details of the Critical Transfers

For any positive scale s and fixed words u,v, put \(m=|u|+|v|\).
The exact identity and bound are
\[
\sum_w s^{2|w|}|\alpha_{uwv}|^2
=s^{-2m}\sum_w s^{2|uwv|}|\alpha_{uwv}|^2
\le s^{-2m}\sum_z s^{2|z|}|\alpha_z|^2.
\]
This works even when \(s<1\), which occurs for the symmetric subclass with negative q.
The factor can then exceed one, but it is finite and fixed.
For a matrix-entry expansion \(\beta_w=\sum_{u,v}d^{uv}\alpha_{uwv}\), the weighted Hilbert norm is bounded by
\[
\left(\sum_w s^{2|w|}|\beta_w|^2\right)^{1/2}
\le\sum_{u,v}|d^{uv}|s^{-(|u|+|v|)}
\left(\sum_zs^{2|z|}|\alpha_z|^2\right)^{1/2}.
\]
All sums over u,v here are finite.
Therefore the constants in the padding transfer do not depend on w or its length.

For the symmetric subclass, let \(E_n=\sum_{|w|=n}|\alpha_w|^2\).
Then
\[
e_n=\kappa_n(q)(1-q)^{-n}E_n.
\]
Weighted ordinary square summability follows from actual q-square summability by the positive lower bound for \(\kappa_n(q)\).
Its finite-state exponential decay then returns an exponential upper bound for \(e_n\) using the upper bound for \(\kappa_n(q)\).
This two-sided scalar comparison is available on the symmetric subspace; it is not asserted for arbitrary q-word coefficients.

For the elimination step, write \(R=\operatorname{Div}_{\mathcal M}\mathbb C\langle c\rangle\).
After choosing N with \(\max_i\sum_j\|(T^N)_{ij}\|<1\), every leading principal block of \(I-T^N\) has an inverse.
Assume the preceding block inverse has entries in R.
Its scalar Schur complement belongs to R and is invertible in \(\mathcal M\), since both block matrices are invertible.
The definition of R therefore admits the inverse of this scalar Schur complement.
The block inverse formula completes the induction.
Finally,
\[
(I-T)^{-1}=(I+T+\cdots+T^{N-1})(I-T^N)^{-1}
\]
has entries in R.
Every expression uses the unstarred c-algebra; no adjoints are inserted by this construction.
