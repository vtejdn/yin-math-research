## Fock Model, Parameter Range and Homogeneous Norm

For \(a_i>0\), the source cumulants \(\kappa_n(X_i)=a_i^{n-2}\), \(n\ge2\), and \(\kappa_1(X_i)=0\) make \(a_i^{-1}X_i+a_i^{-2}\) a jump-one free-Poisson variable of rate \(a_i^{-2}\).
Thus every positive real rate is represented by a finite positive parameter.
The zero parameter gives a semicircular coordinate, and nonzero affine transformations preserve the three original coordinate algebras.
The source's free-product/direct-sum statements allow distinct parameters and mixtures.
A vacuum atom for rates below one does not spoil cyclicity, separation or the orthonormal Wick basis.

For each ordinary cut at fixed length, left and right words have fixed respective lengths and give orthogonal families of creation ranges.
The cut operator is the corresponding finite coefficient matrix acting between these decompositions, with an unchanged Fock tail.
Its operator norm is at most the scalar coefficient matrix's Hilbert-Schmidt norm.
The preservation cuts have left words \(pi\), right words \(q^{\rm rev}i\), and coefficients \(a_i\alpha_{piq}\) in distinct matrix positions.
Their norm has the additional factor at most \(a_*\).
There are precisely \(n+1\) ordinary cuts and \(n\) preservation cuts.
Hence the bound \(n+1+n a_*\) in (5) is valid, including degree zero, with the actual orthonormal coefficient norm.
Its subexponential growth requires no smallness assumption on \(a_*\).
