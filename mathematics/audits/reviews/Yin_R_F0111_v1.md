## Reconstruction of the proof

1. **Deletion.**
   With the ordinary last-letter projection Q_i, the positive-operator inequality P_(n-1) tensor Q_i <= P_(n-1) tensor I bounds literal deletion in the deformed norm.
   Orthogonal degrees extend it to the full direct sum.
   Reversal conjugates each permutation by the longest permutation, preserving inversion number; it therefore transfers the bound to first-letter deletion.
   Iterating a fixed finite number of deletions gives exactly the padded coefficients, including the empty word and the zero output on shorter words.
   No q-annihilation formula is substituted for deletion.

2. **Minimal states.**
   Reachability and observability provide invertible row and column matrices U,V with padded coefficient matrix U A_w V for every middle word w.
   Thus each complete state-entry series is a fixed finite linear combination of boundedly stripped scalar series.
   All its actual homogeneous q-energies are summable.
   Minimality is needed for this implication; the proof does not transfer summability to arbitrary unused states.

3. **Concatenation.**
   Splitting a word after degree n gives the exact identity Xi_(n+m)^(ij) = J_(n,m) sum_k Xi_n^(ik) tensor Xi_m^(kj).
   The tensor triangle inequality followed by scalar Cauchy-Schwarz yields the product of the row and column squared energies for each i,j.
   Summing i,j separates into a_n squared times a_m squared; there is no omitted factor equal to the state dimension.
   The bound a_(n+m) <= sqrt(C_q) a_n a_m follows in the correct direction.

4. **Decay and certification.**
   After rescaling by sqrt(C_q), the state energy norm is submultiplicative and tends to zero.
   One finite block below one gives geometric decay by residue classes.
   A zero block makes every later block zero.
   The n=0 energy is the state dimension and is explicitly accommodated.
   The four equivalences in Section 4 follow without using summability in the finite-block-to-decay direction.
   The search is a positive certificate with exact or rigorously enclosed input values, not an algorithm deciding every negative instance.

5. **Bounded scalar reconstruction.**
   Positive q-circular words applied to the vacuum are the specified raw tensor words, so the homogeneous norm estimate applies to every scalar state entry.
   The elementary operator-matrix norm bound controls powers of the actual matrix T.
   Their norm sum is finite, furnishing a bounded two-sided inverse of I-T.
   To obtain scalar division closure, choose k with norm(T^k)<1: all leading principal blocks of I-T^k are boundedly invertible, so successive Schur pivots are bounded scalar inverses.
   This is the complete [Yin_C_F0017](../../claims/Yin_C_F0017_v1.md#yin_c_f0017) scalar-elimination interface.
   It neither assumes that arbitrary bounded rational outputs have bounded pencil inverses nor silently replaces scalar division closure by matrix rational closure.
   The identity with the finite geometric polynomial recovers (I-T)^(-1) using only the original unstarred generators.

6. **Strict example.**
   For the alternating two-state realization, the four permutations preserving 1212 have inversion numbers 0,3,3,4.
   The two nonzero state entries give a_4 squared = 137781/524288.
   The first four product factors and the tail estimate give C_(1/2) <= 16384/4725 < 7/2, and the displayed finite certificate is strictly below one.
   Reachability, observability, noncommutation and failure of permutation invariance all hold.
   The old weighted coefficient energy is the divergent geometric series with ratio 81/64.
