\[
 \eta(X)=\sum_j a_jXa_j^*,\qquad \rho(X)=\sum_j a_jXa_j.

\[
 (I+\eta(R))Y_RR^{-1}+\rho(Y_R^*)=a

\[
 \log\Delta_\varphi(S)=\frac1{2m}
 \inf_{R>0}\left[
 \log\det(I+\eta(R))-\log\det R-m+

\]
Thus neither covariance loss nor hidden feasibility conditions reduce the coefficient scope.
