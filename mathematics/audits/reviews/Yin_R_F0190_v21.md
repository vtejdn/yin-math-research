\((1+B_0^2)^{3/2}q_1q_2q_3\) bounds every required graph area.

The Frobenius calculation is valid without fourth moments.

The weak law gives the asserted off-diagonal limit.
The random diagonal's squared Frobenius contribution divided by \(N\) has expectation \(O(N^{-1})\).

\[
 \|L_N\|_F^2/N\longrightarrow
 \|A_0\|_F^2+\sum_j\|A_j\|_F^2=Q_A

in probability.

No almost-sure Frobenius or bounded original-input operator-norm conclusion is used.
