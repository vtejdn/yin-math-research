Thus \(2F_l(Y)\le F_l(Z)+l\log^+\|D\|\).
In the reconstruction the two occurrences of \(G\), the factor \(L\), and the term \(Y\) cost at most \(2F_l(G)+F_l(L)+F_l(Y)+O(l)\).

Weak convergence of the squared singular laws and control of the positive tails identify every truncated logarithmic limit.

For any \(h>p\ge1\), Jensen bounds the \(h\)-th power of the normalized total positive input logarithm by the normalized sum of their \(h\)-th powers.
These have uniformly bounded expectations since \((\log^+t)^h\le C_h(1+t^2)\) and the expected normalized input square trace is bounded.
The absolute normalized determinant logarithm is controlled by these input averages on both sides by the direct affine and inverse estimates.
Uniform integrability of its \(p\)-th power now gives the asserted \(L^p\) convergence even for atomic or heavy-tailed input laws allowed by the Task.
