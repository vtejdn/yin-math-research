### Signed inverse compression and the deterministic direction estimate

The low-subspace determinant hypothesis implies
\[
 \|(aU+bV)h\|^2\ge g(a^2+b^2)\|h\|^2
\]

In particular the smallest singular values of \(U,V\) are at least \(\sqrt g\).
Multiplication by \(U\) on both sides gives \(\|UAU^*\|_{\rm F}\ge g\|A\|_{\rm F}\) for arbitrary signed selfadjoint \(A\); positivity of the inverse weights is not needed.

The invariant-subspace identity \(XU=UT-V\), with \(\|T\|\le\rho\), yields
\[
 \|(X-cI)Uh\|\ge(\sqrt{g(1+c^2)}-\rho)\|h\|
 \ge(\sqrt g-\rho)\|h\|.
\]
The statement's choice \(\rho\le\sqrt{g_0}/2\) supplies the required strict positive margin.
No estimate on the full input norm occurs here.

For the normalized symmetric \(F\), its associated \(B,C\), and the raw tensor \(\mathscr K\), projection onto \(F^\perp\otimes F^\perp\) gives \(\|B-cF\|_{\rm F}^2\le k\).

\[
 \|(X-cI)F\|_{\rm F}^2
