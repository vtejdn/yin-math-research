The tail Frobenius expression is an average of integrable squared raw tail variables, with diagonal expectation of order \(N^{-1}\) after division by \(N\).

The mask degree bound follows from the fixed Bernoulli tail probability and exponential Markov, then a union over vertices.
The conditional quadratic-form replacement bounds sum to at most \(8L^2/N\), and the \(1/4\)-net has at most \(9^N\) points.

For any projection and real unit vector fixed under the conditioning, the raw edge identity gives projected squared energy at least \(\operatorname{rank}P-2\).

\[
 \sum_{\rm bad}\|z_je_i+z_ie_j\|^2

The conditional variance bound consequently retains precisely the displayed denominator \(2N\), including for \(n=N-1\).

### Uniform separation of rank-one residuals

The Frobenius operator estimate for \(Q\mathcal F_\lambda\) follows from the bounds on \(QC,QD\), \(\|T\|=\sqrt2\), \(\|K\|=\sqrt2\), and \(|\lambda|\le K_0\).

In particular, every approximation error is multiplied by the original inputs only after left compression.

Near a signed coordinate vector, replacing \(b\) by \(\sigma e_k\) costs at most \(A\bar h\).

\[
 \|P_0Wz\|
 \le(1+2A)(r+A\bar h)

contradicting the discarded-space gap.

Away from every signed coordinate, choose \(k\) with maximal \(b_k^2\), so \(b_k^2\ge1/3\).

\[
 1-b_k^2\ge\bar h^2-\bar h^4/4\ge3\bar h^2/4.
