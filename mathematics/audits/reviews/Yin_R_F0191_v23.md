### Obligations and finite-codimension separation

The required conclusions are lower bounds for the Frobenius norm of the arithmetic-average symmetric coefficient tensor of the complete signed determinant.
Consequently the proof must control the complete selected bidegree, not only one formal term, uniformly in residual dimension and without imposing an upper bound on the residual Frobenius norm relative to a head value.
The all-scale conclusion additionally requires an exhaustive, noncircular division of every ordered triple of positive leading singular values.

For the orthogonal decomposition into the head-image span and its complement, the three nonzero blocks of \(B-PBP\) give
\[
 \|B-PBP\|_F^2
 =\|QBQ\|_F^2+2\|QBP\|_F^2
 \le2m\|B\|^2.
\]
This estimate uses the rank \(m\le3k\) of the removed output space, not the residual rank.
The whole-space Gram inequality gives \(U(a)^*U(a)\succeq g|a|^2I\) on the residual support, and the stack bound gives \(\|U(a)\|^2\le\Lambda|a|^2\).
The polar isometry preserves Frobenius norm, while multiplication by the inverse square root on each side proves \(\|U(a)AU(a)^*\|_F\ge g|a|^2F\) for signed selfadjoint \(A\).
Together with the operator bound this gives exactly the loss \(\sqrt{2m}\Lambda|a|^2r\) in v23.
No Gram lower bound for the projected maps is inferred or needed.
