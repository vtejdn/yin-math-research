### Exact complete numerator and original-row comparison

Conditioning on the two full factors, the first principal block and the first exposed diagonal fixes \(D,\mu,f,g\), while the first off-diagonal border retains its independent original real law.
In particular no full-\(M\) norm guard is included in this conditioning.
For \(m=N-1\ge2\) with the stated separation \(0\le t_1<t_2\), the singular-value expansion gives
\[
 \operatorname{adj}D
 =\omega\left(\prod_{j=2}^m t_j\right)
 \left(v_1u_1^*+\sum_{j=2}^m(t_1/t_j)v_ju_j^*\right).
\]
The orthonormal singular vectors make its squared Frobenius norm the product factor squared times \(1+\rho^2\).
This proves both normalized identities, including the phase \(\omega=\det U\,\overline{\det V}\).
At \(t_1=0\), the same adjugate formula holds and has rank one; phase choices in the zero singular spaces cancel in the product.
The determinant term becomes exactly \(\omega a\mu t_1\).

The remaining singular-vector sum has operator norm \(r=t_1/t_2\), so the bilinear border error is at most \(ar\|x+f\|\|x+g\|\).
Complex transposes do not invalidate that norm bound.
The Frobenius residual contains the additional \(\sqrt{m-1}\) factor, but the displayed border estimate correctly avoids it.
Since \(\rho^2\le(m-1)r^2\), the claimed \(a\ge1/\sqrt2\) holds whenever \(r\le N^{-1/2}\).
No approximation is used at exact corank one.

The conditional row second moment is \(m/N\le1\), giving the stated \(N^{-2q}\) tail by Markov.

\[
