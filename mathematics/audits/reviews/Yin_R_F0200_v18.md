### Cutoff, arbitrary range and complex covariance

Finite second moments and unit centered variance allow one fixed cutoff R for all finitely many laws, with the sum of tail probabilities at most 1/64 and each centered conditional variance at least 1/2.

The bad physical-index indicators are independent, so their exponential moment gives the displayed exponentially small probability of more than p/8 bad indices.

Conditional on a specified good-index set, the retained coordinates factor into their individual modulus-conditioned laws.
Conditioning on which other physical blocks failed their cutoff does not correlate the retained family.
Their conditional means may be nonzero and are explicitly removed.
The coordinate projection of H loses at most m times the number of deleted physical indices in codimension.
Adding the entire range of the deterministic shift plus conditional mean costs at most m further dimensions.
Thus the displayed codimension at least mp/4 holds for p at least 8.

Coordinate contraction followed by enlargement gives the correct lower bound for the original distance.
The enlarged space contains the shift-plus-mean image for every auxiliary direction at once.
Its orthogonal projection consequently leaves only the centered random matrix.
This is why no bound on the deterministic shift is needed for the subsequent net.
The projection is fixed under the permitted conditioning and does not depend on retained centered coordinate values.

For a complex unit column direction v, testing the conditional covariance against an arbitrary complex unit u gives
\[
 u^*T_{v,i}u
 =\sum_j \operatorname{Var}(\omega_{j,i}\mid |\omega_{j,i}|\le R)

\]
