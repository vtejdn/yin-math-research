## Independent whole-proof finding

No substantive gap was found in the stated low inverse-energy assertions or their complete proof.
The argument establishes the relative bad-minor limit, both complete-numerator inequalities, both graph-normalized conditional tails and the stated slow-threshold marginal and averaged consequences.
It preserves the original entry laws and the full Schur numerator.
The conclusion is conditional on the invertible low-energy branch and does not establish its prevalence, a simultaneous good event for every minor, or the full T13 assertion.

### Quantifiers and original conditional law

The coefficient size, number of inputs, coefficient matrices, input laws and deletion bound are fixed as N grows.
The deterministic shift is the same real scalar sequence for each parent and its actual minors.
Deleting physical indices leaves the normalization at \(N^{-1/2}\); the proof consistently divides physical partial traces by N, not by the retained size n.
The three levels are distinguished: exposed parent, retained zero-parameter minor, and internal single deletions used only at nonreal parameter.

The sigma-field generated by the original retained minors and exposed diagonals fixes M, Z, the inverse energy, g_0, B_0 and D_i.
It leaves the d original border vectors independent, with their prescribed centered variance-one raw coordinate laws.
In particular the good event is measurable under that sigma-field.
The fresh-row deviation event is estimated after conditioning and is never inserted into the conditioning.
The graph uses the actual original row, without a copied sign law, a conditional truncation model or a parent norm guard.
Neither fourth moments nor bounded support are assumed.

### Uniform quadratic concentration

For independent centered variance-one coordinates, the off-diagonal absolute second moment uses only the two pairings of the same distinct coordinate pair.
This gives \(2\|H\|_F^2/N^2\), including complex H, without a coordinate fourth moment.
For the diagonal terms, centering the bounded part of \(v_\alpha^2\) gives an L2 estimate of order \(\|H\|_F/N\) at fixed truncation level.
Centering the remainder costs at most
\[
 \frac{2\omega(R)}{N}\sum_\alpha|H_{\alpha\alpha}|
 \le 2\sqrt{dC}\,\omega(R)
\]
when \(\|H\|_F^2\le CN\).
The finite number of fixed laws makes \(\omega(R)\to0\).
Taking N to infinity before R proves the claimed uniform L1 estimate.

For the exposed border, \(\|U_a\|\le\sqrt\Lambda\) and
\(\|U_aZU_b^T\|_F\le\Lambda\|Z\|_F\).
The fixed auxiliary size permits summation of entrywise estimates into the displayed operator-norm error \(\delta_N(K)\to0\).
The covariance calculation gives exactly \(\mathcal S(g_0)\), with the actual factor \(1/N\).
The estimate is therefore uniform over all background values in the stated energy event, rather than merely averaged over a conveniently chosen row law.

### Fixed-eta relation and return to zero

At fixed positive eta, M and its internal minors are real symmetric, so all nonreal resolvents have norm at most \(\eta^{-1}\).
The diagonal resolvent block has positive definite imaginary part and is invertible.
The exact block Schur identity and its multiplication on the right by \((G_\eta)_{kk}\) are valid in the stated noncommuting order.

Decoupling one physical block changes the matrix by rank at most \(2m\).
The difference of resolvents has that rank bound and norm at most \(2/\eta\), so its nuclear norm is at most \(4m/\eta\).
For an arbitrary block matrix X, testing the physical partial trace against unit vectors gives
\[
 \left\|\sum_kX_{kk}\right\|\le\|X\|_1.
\]
Removing the decoupled block adds at most \(m/\eta\).
This verifies the stated \(5m/(N\eta)\) normalized comparison.

The quadratic estimate applies to each actual internal minor with squared Frobenius norm at most \(mN/\eta^2\).
It gives unconditional \(\sup_k\mathbb E\|E_k\|\to0\), because the internal fresh row is independent of that minor and the original diagonal expectation is \(O(N^{-1/2})\).
No independence between E_k and \((G_\eta)_{kk}\) is needed: the latter has norm at most \(\eta^{-1}\).
Consequently the averaged identity has the exact right-hand side \((n/N)I_m\), with the final replacement by \(I_m\) costing at most \((r+1)/N\).

On the invertible energy event, spectral calculus gives
\[
 \|g_0\|,\|g_\eta\|\le\sqrt{mK},
 \qquad \|g_\eta-g_0\|\le\eta K.
\]
The first bound follows from the nuclear norm and the second from
\(\left|(\lambda-i\eta)^{-1}-\lambda^{-1}\right|\le\eta/\lambda^2\).
Thus the comparison is valid even when \(\|Z\|\) grows with N.
Expanding the two ordered products yields the stated defect bound
\[
 \|B_0g_0-B_\eta g_\eta\|
 \le\eta(aK+h_K+2\Lambda Kh_K).
\]
For each fixed K and tolerance, eta is first chosen small, and the unconditional fixed-eta error then tends to zero with N.
This proves the relative probability estimate without conditioning the internal row on a zero-parameter energy event.

Physical permutation invariance and the finite set of deletion cardinalities justify the marginal supremum over the deterministic deletion pairs.
The proof makes no union bound over their number and supplies no simultaneous-minor event.

### Complete Schur block and normalized distances

On \(\|B_0g_0-I_m\|\le1/2\), square-matrix invertibility gives
\(B_0^{-1}=g_0(B_0g_0)^{-1}\) and \(s_{\min}(B_0)\ge(2h_K)^{-1}\).
This does not require commutation of B_0 and g_0.
The exposed diagonal bound and one fresh-row deviation of size \((8h_K)^{-1}\) then leave the gap
\(c_K=(4h_K)^{-1}\).
The conditional exception is at most \(\min(1,8h_K\delta_N(K))\).
The determinant event is contained in the same exception because all m singular values are then at least c_K.
The nonzero comparison comes from the actual-pencil relation, and the full \(A_N+D_i\) term remains in S.

Direct multiplication verifies the arbitrary-m graph formula:
the columns of \(\binom{I_m}{-ZR_i}\Gamma^{-1/2}\) are orthonormal and orthogonal to the retained parent columns.
Invertibility of M ensures that the orthogonal complement of the retained parent columns has dimension exactly m.
It follows that \(F=\Gamma^{-1/2}S\) and
\(|\det F|=|\det S|/\sqrt{\det\Gamma}\).
This rechecks the precise elementary part reused from C194v16.

The conditional second-moment calculation gives
\[
 \mathbb E[\operatorname{Tr}\Gamma\mid\mathcal F_{T,i}]
 =m+\frac1N\operatorname{Tr}
       \left[\left(\sum_jA_j^2\otimes I_n\right)Z^2\right]
 \le m+\Lambda K.
\]
Here \(Z^2\) is positive semidefinite and \(\sum_jA_j^2\le\Lambda I_m\).
On the Schur gap event,
\[
 s_{\min}(F)\ge c_K(\operatorname{Tr}\Gamma)^{-1/2},
 \qquad
 |\det F|\ge c_K^m
             \left(\frac m{\operatorname{Tr}\Gamma}\right)^{m/2}.
\]
Markov's inequality gives exactly the two constants in the statement, including the factor \(1/m\) in the determinant tail.
A uniform fresh-row bound on graph area is not used.

### Slow thresholds, boundaries and unresolved scope

For each fixed integer K, the relative minor error plus the conditional row error tends to zero uniformly in the stated marginal sense.
The deterministic cutoffs can therefore be chosen so that this sum is at most \(1/K\) for all later N.
Choosing \(K_N\to\infty\) with \(K_N\le\log(N+1)\) and \(N\ge N_{K_N}\) preserves that limit.
No quantitative finite-second-moment convergence rate or monotonicity in K is needed.

The remaining distance term is
\(16mK_N(m+\Lambda K_N)N^{-2\beta}\), and the determinant term is
\(O((K_N+K_N^2)N^{-2\beta/m})\).
Both vanish for every fixed positive beta with the same K_N.
A deterministic probability-weighted average is bounded by the marginal supremum.
These conclusions do not require independence between sizes or between deletion pairs.

The boundary \(m=1\), zero coefficient directions and even \(\Lambda=0\) introduce no division by a frame constant.
The probability assertions retain their explicit invertibility and energy qualifiers, so singular or high-energy minors remain outside the asserted branch bounds.
No positive coefficient frame is required for this deduction.
