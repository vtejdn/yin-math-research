## Application boundary and verdict

An unrestricted root net retains leading entropy proportional to the final precision, so it cannot supply the required precision-independent family bound.
The two-color convolution concentration is not generally bounded by the product of the separate concentrations, and the single-color full auxiliary cross map indeed has a nontrivial kernel.
The reduced one-color root reconstruction avoids that kernel while leaving the high-concentration covering problem open.
The dimension restrictions \(2|I|\le|J|\) and \(|I|\le|J|\) are correct for the two respective identification maps.
The displayed exponent \((C/3-1/2+o(1))N\) is a bookkeeping obstruction to the stated proposed count, not a probability lower bound or an ensemble counterexample.
The unused outside compatibility condition involves the same cross blocks as its target, so no additional independence is available for free.

These examples prevent automatic summability of the guard complement without refuting Brown convergence.
