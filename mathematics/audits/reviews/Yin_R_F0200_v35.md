## Conditional row product

All other terms, including the shift by \(z\), are known under the specified outside conditioning.
Symmetry reuses the same \(U\) in both orientations, and the rectangular coordinates themselves are independent since they correspond to distinct unordered pairs.

A maximum-determinant row restriction of a column basis gives each interpolation coefficient modulus at most one by row replacement.
Subtracting this linear combination of the pivot equations removes the whole transpose term at a cost at most \((1+\sqrt{r_B})\sqrt N\,\varepsilon\) in each nonpivot residual.

## The two sign estimates

The real open-interval estimate agrees with Theorem 1 and its antichain argument on printed page 898 of [Erdős, *On a lemma of Littlewood and Offord*](https://www.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1945-04.pdf).
