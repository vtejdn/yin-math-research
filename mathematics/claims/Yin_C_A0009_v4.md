# Short signed cycles restrict the trinomial collision defect

Date: 2026-11-12 (Asia/Hong_Kong).
This argument advances [route four](../strategies/Yin_S_A0004.md) of [A0001](../tasks/Yin_T_A0001_v1.md#contract).

## Statement

For every torsion-free group G and every \(u,v\in G\), a nonzero kernel of the actual regular-group operator \(T=1+u-v\) forces
\[
 m_2=15,\qquad m_3=87,\qquad
 \Gamma=m_4-543\in8\mathbb Z,\qquad \Gamma\le8.
\tag{1}
\]
Here \(m_j=\tau((T^*T)^j)\), and \(\Gamma\) is exactly the weighted defect of [C_A0009_v3](../claims/Yin_C_A0009_v3.md#statement).
The resulting quadratic certificate gives the unconditional scalar bound
\[
 \boxed{\dim_G\ker(1+u-v)<\frac8{29}.}
\tag{2}
\]
This does not prove zero kernel, integer matrix rank, or a new positive class for the Atiyah conjecture.
If the kernel dimension is at least \(1/4\), exactly one of \((vu^j)^2=u^3\), \(j=0,1,2\), holds, none of the negative classes (6) is an identity, and the first four moments are \((3,15,87,551)\).
The complete finite enumeration, actual-group exclusions, and scope of the conclusion follow below.

## Reused inputs and the conditional reduction

Write \(a=u\), \(b=v\), \(H=\langle a,b\rangle\), \(X=T^*T\), and \(\delta=\tau(P_{\ker T})\).
Right-coset decomposition identifies the kernel dimension over \(H\) with that over the ambient group.
The coincident-label cases have zero kernel by the elementary reductions in [L_A0021](../ledger/Yin_L_A0021_v1.md#actual-setting-and-fixed-inputs).
Thus only distinct \(e,a,b\) need be considered.
If \(H\) is cyclic, the nonzero Laurent polynomial \(T\) has zero kernel by the [cyclic regular-representation certificate](../ledger/Yin_L_A0002_v1.md#cyclic-rational-functions).

The argument assumes \(\delta>0\) while deriving (1).
It then excludes certain relations by proving that the actual supporting subgroup has a nontrivial homomorphism onto \(\mathbb Z\).
Such a quotient makes \(T\) injective by the [ordered-quotient certificate](../ledger/Yin_L_A0021_v1.md#a-nontrivial-right-orderable-quotient-suffices), contradicting the assumption.
That certificate is the already checked scalar application of Lin92 Theorem 4, with independently injective coefficients of support at most two.
No quotient of a group known to satisfy Atiyah is assumed to inherit that property.
The [pairwise-Haar obstruction](../ledger/Yin_L_A0025_v1.md#the-explicit-failure-of-the-regular-joint-word-condition) and [diagonal tensor limit](../ledger/Yin_L_A0027_v1.md#outcome-of-this-tensor-test) are respected: the new input here is the exact finite multiplication law of the original labels.
Neither freeness, a big-powers theorem, unique products nor orderability is inferred from torsion-freeness.

## A cyclic-normalizer exclusion valid in the actual quotient

Suppose a nontrivial torsion-free group \(J=\langle x,t\rangle\) satisfies
\[
 txt^{-1}=x^k,\qquad k\in\{-2,-1,1,2\}.
\tag{3}
\]
Then \(J\) has a nontrivial homomorphism onto \(\mathbb Z\).
If \(x=e\), the group is cyclic, and if \(k=1\), it is finitely generated torsion-free abelian; both cases give that conclusion directly.
Assume henceforth \(x\ne e\) and \(k\ne1\).

Put \(x_n=t^{-n}xt^n\) for \(n\ge0\).
The relation \(x_{n+1}^k=x_n\) makes
\[
 A=\bigcup_{n\ge0}\langle x_n\rangle
\]
an abelian subgroup normal under both \(t\) and \(t^{-1}\).
The quotient \(J/A\) is generated by the image of \(t\).
For \(k=2\) or \(k=-2\), a relation \(t^m\in A\) with \(m>0\) would imply that \(t^m\) commutes with \(x\), while (3) gives
\[
 t^mxt^{-m}=x^{k^m}.
\]
Thus \(x^{k^m-1}=e\), contrary to torsion-freeness and \(x\ne e\).
Consequently \(J/A\cong\mathbb Z\).
For \(k=-1\), one has \(A=\langle x\rangle\).
If \(t^m=x^r\) with \(m>0\), conjugation by \(t\) gives \(x^r=x^{-r}\), hence \(r=0\) and then \(t=e\) by torsion-freeness.
Equation (3) would then give \(x=e\), again a contradiction.
Thus this case also has \(J/A\cong\mathbb Z\).
This proof takes place inside the actual group \(J\), and covers every torsion-free quotient in which the stated generators remain nontrivial.

The same exclusion applies if \(J=\langle x,y\rangle\) satisfies \(x^2=y^2\).
Indeed \(w=xy^{-1}\) satisfies \(ywy^{-1}=w^{-1}\), as in [L_A0021](../ledger/Yin_L_A0021_v1.md#normality-of-the-trinomial-itself-has-a-second-exact-collapse), and \(J=\langle w,y\rangle\).
If \(w=e\), the group is cyclic; otherwise (3) applies.
Below, a common-square relation after a displayed change of generators is therefore enough to exclude \(\delta>0\).

## Signed paths and a complete finite counting convention

Use the bipartite signed graph of
\[
 B=\begin{pmatrix}0&T^*\\T&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Every vertex has one edge of each color \(0,1,2\), with labels \(e,a,b\) and signs \(+,+,-\), respectively.
Distinctness of the labels makes this a simple graph of degree three.
For an even path starting in the first part with chronological color string \(s_0\cdots s_{n-1}\), record the inverse of its chronological transport and its sign as
\[
 W(s)=g_{s_0}^{-1}g_{s_1}g_{s_2}^{-1}\cdots g_{s_{n-1}},
 \qquad \sigma(s)=(-1)^{\#\{i:s_i=2\}},
 \qquad (g_0,g_1,g_2)=(e,a,b).
\tag{4}
\]
The path is closed exactly when \(W(s)=e\).
It has no immediate reversal, including at its marked starting vertex, exactly when adjacent colors are distinct cyclically.
The trace \(m_j\) is the signed count of all length-\(2j\) closed paths from that vertex.

For the tables, uppercase letters mean inverse generators: \(A=a^{-1}\), \(B=b^{-1}\).
Free reduction and removal of an inverse first/last pair leave a cyclically reduced word.
The representative of its class is the lexicographically least cyclic rotation of that word or its inverse, with \(A<B<a<b\).
All words in such a class are simultaneously identities or nonidentities in every actual group.
Their signs agree, since the parity of the number of \(b\)-letters is unchanged by free reduction, conjugation and inversion.
The signed multiplicity in the tables therefore multiplies one exact identity indicator, without any probabilistic independence assumption.

Here is a small complete enumeration of the color patterns themselves, before conversion by (4).
Patterns are taken up to cyclic rotation, reversal and permutation of the three colors.
The orbit sizes include all distinct marked color strings.

| Length | Representatives and orbit sizes |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | \(0101:6\), \(0102:12\) |
| 6 | \(010101:6\), \(010102:36\), \(010212:18\), \(012012:6\) |
| 8 | \(01010101:6\), \(01010102:48\), \(01010202:24\), \(01010212:48\), \(01012012:48\), \(01012102:48\), \(01020102:12\), \(01021012:24\) |

These orbits are disjoint by their cyclic equality patterns.
Their sizes can be checked by applying the listed operations and identifying repeated strings.
Their totals are \(18,66,258\), respectively.
These equal \(2^n+2\), the number of proper colorings of an even cycle of length \(n\) with three colors.
For a direct count, fix the initial color and let \(r_n\) count length-\(n\) color strings with adjacent colors distinct and final color equal to the first.
Then \(r_1=1\), and \(r_{n+1}=2^{n-1}-r_n\), so the number with final color different from the first is \(3(2^{n-1}-r_n)=2^n+2\) for even \(n\).
The disjoint orbit totals therefore prove exhaustion.
Applying (4) to those explicitly given orbits gives the following word tables.
A complete short implementation reproducing their entries is included below.

The power conclusions use torsion-freeness.
The cyclic, commuting, common-square and cyclic-normalizer alternatives all make \(T\) injective by the preceding arguments.
Consequently \(\delta>0\) implies that the underlying graph has no simple cycle of length four or six, and hence has girth at least eight.
There are no odd cycles because it is bipartite, and no loops or double edges because the three labels are distinct.

Every closed path of length at most six then reduces to the empty path by cancellation of immediate reversals.
The same is true of every length-eight closed path except a cyclically nonbacktracking length-eight path.
For the latter assertion, any surviving shorter reduced closed portion or nonempty tail would contain a cycle of length at most six.
The paths reducing to the empty path have sign plus one and exactly the counts in the degree-three tree: cancellation removes an edge and its reverse, and the color sequence lifts uniquely to the tree with the same local three-edge choices.
The counts \(3,15,87,543\) were derived completely in [C_A0009_v3](../claims/Yin_C_A0009_v3.md#the-exact-free-word-moments-and-a-sufficient-collision-hypothesis).
It follows that
\[
 m_1=3,\quad m_2=15,\quad m_3=87,\quad
 m_4=543+C_8,
\tag{5}
\]
where \(C_8\) is the signed count of cyclically nonbacktracking closed paths of length eight from the marked vertex.
In particular the original weighted correction is exactly \(\Gamma=C_8\).

Assume still that \(\delta>0\), so every excluded positive row is absent.
The three retained rows are exactly
\[
 R_j:\qquad (ba^j)^2=a^3,\qquad j=0,1,2.
\tag{7}
\]
Each \(R_j\) makes \(a^3\) central in \(H\), since \(H=\langle a,ba^j\rangle\) and \(a^3=(ba^j)^2\).
No two distinct \(R_i,R_j\) can hold.
Indeed they give \(ba^i b=a^{3-i}\) and \(ba^j b=a^{3-j}\), whose quotient yields
\[
 ba^{j-i}b^{-1}=a^{-(j-i)}.
\]
Conjugating \(a^{3(j-i)}\) by \(b\) now both fixes it, by centrality of \(a^3\), and inverts it, by the displayed equation.
Thus \(a^{6(j-i)}=e\), contrary to torsion-freeness and \(a\ne e\).

Let \(r\in\{0,1\}\) count the true relations in (7), and let \(n_-\) count the true classes from (6).
Both are counts of distinct free cyclic word classes evaluated in the actual group, not counts modulo extra actual-group conjugacies.
Such extra coincidences do not affect the sum of their original color-string multiplicities.
Equations (5)--(7) therefore give the exact restriction
\[
 \boxed{\Gamma=8(r-n_-),\qquad r\in\{0,1\},\qquad n_-\ge0.}
\tag{8}
\]

\[
 (m_1,m_2,m_3,m_4)=(3,15,87,551).
\tag{9}
\]
This is only a necessary condition for such a kernel; no actual positive-kernel group is constructed.

## The optimized polynomial certificate

For \(\delta>0\), (5) and (8) imply \(m_4\le551\).
Set
\[
 q(s)=1-\frac{s}{2}+\frac{3s^2}{58}.
\tag{10}
\]
The same bounded functional-calculus argument as in C_A0009_v3 gives \(q(X)p=p\) and
\[
 \delta\le\tau(q(X)^2)
 =1-m_1+\frac{41}{116}m_2-\frac3{58}m_3+\frac9{3364}m_4
 \le\frac8{29}.
\tag{11}
\]
The constants follow by substituting \((3,15,87,551)\); the coefficient of \(m_4\) is positive.
Equivalently, the value in the middle is \(8/29+9(\Gamma-8)/3364\).

The first inequality in (11) is strict.
Equality would imply \(q(X)^2=p\) by positivity and faithfulness of the trace, and hence the finite-support group-ring identity \(q(X)^4=q(X)^2\).
Ordinary group-ring augmentation gives \(\varepsilon(X)=1\) and \(q(1)=16/29\), whose square is neither zero nor one, contradicting that identity.
No continuous augmentation on the von Neumann algebra or rationality assumption on \(p\) is used.
This proves (2) under the assumption \(\delta>0\); the already separated \(\delta=0\) and degenerate cases satisfy (2) automatically.

For the exact moment vector (9), (10) is the optimal quadratic certificate with constant term one.
Writing a real candidate as \(1+cs+ds^2\), its minimizing normal equations are
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}15&87\\87&551\end{pmatrix}
 \binom c d=-\binom3{15}.
\]
The determinant is 696 and the unique solution is \((c,d)=(-1/2,3/58)\).
The minimum is \(1+3c+15d=8/29\).
Complex coefficients cannot improve it, since their imaginary parts contribute a nonnegative squared norm with zero constant term.
This is the same finite-dimensional Christoffel minimization already attributed and checked in [C_A0009_v3](../claims/Yin_C_A0009_v3.md#source-attribution-known-coverage-and-remaining-joint-requirement), not a new variational principle.
Optimality is asserted only for this quadratic class and the specified moments.

## Reproducible finite expansion

The following native JavaScript was executed to check every signed multiplicity in the tables.
It uses no group solver and asserts no group-theoretic consequence.
The complete color orbit table, resulting word tables, and explicit group calculations above supply the retained mathematical certificate.
The enumeration loops over at most \(3^8=6561\) strings.
It can be run directly with Node.js; no external package or input file is required.

```javascript
const flip = { a: "A", A: "a", b: "B", B: "b" };
function inverse(w) {
  return [...w].reverse().map(x => flip[x]).join("");
}
function reduce(w) {
  let out = "";
  for (const x of w) {
    out = out && out.at(-1) === flip[x] ? out.slice(0, -1) : out + x;
  }
  return out;
}
function representative(w) {
  w = reduce(w);
  while (w.length > 1 && w[0] === flip[w.at(-1)]) w = w.slice(1, -1);
  if (!w) return "";
  return [w, inverse(w)].flatMap(v =>
    [...v].map((_, i) => v.slice(i) + v.slice(0, i))
  ).sort()[0];
}
for (const n of [4, 6, 8]) {
  const table = {};
  for (let code = 0; code < 3 ** n; code++) {
    let value = code;
    const colors = [];
    for (let i = 0; i < n; i++) {
      colors.push(value % 3);
      value = Math.floor(value / 3);
    }
    if (colors.some((s, i) => s === colors[(i + 1) % n])) continue;
    let word = "";
    for (let i = 0; i < n; i++) {
      const letter = ["", "a", "b"][colors[i]];
      word += i % 2 ? letter : inverse(letter);
    }
    const key = representative(word);
    const sign = (-1) ** colors.filter(s => s === 2).length;
    table[key] = (table[key] || 0) + sign;
  }
  console.log(n, JSON.stringify(table));
}
```

## An actual cyclic obstruction to an unconditional defect bound

The qualification \(\delta>0\) in (1) is essential to this argument.
An unconditional assertion \(\Gamma\le21\), or even \(\Gamma\le8\), is false for torsion-free groups.
In \(G=\mathbb Z=\langle z\rangle\), take \(a=z^2\), \(b=z\), so \(T=1+z^2-z\).
Writing \(y=z+z^{-1}\) gives
\[
 X=3+z^2+z^{-2}-2z-2z^{-1}=(y-1)^2.
\]
The constant coefficient of \(y^{2k}\) is \(\binom{2k}{k}\), while odd powers have zero constant coefficient.
Expanding \((y-1)^{2j}\) therefore yields
\[
 (m_1,m_2,m_3,m_4)=(3,19,141,1107),\qquad \Gamma=44.
\tag{12}
\]
For example the fourth moment is \(1+56+420+560+70=1107\).
The same ordinary Fourier polynomial certificate gives \(\delta=0\), so this is a counterexample only to an unconditional defect ceiling, not to injectivity or Atiyah integrality.
Its known cyclic coverage is explicit.

## Remaining scope

The advance is the exact finite restriction (8) on a hypothetical nonzero kernel, the necessary common-power configuration (9) for a kernel of trace at least \(1/4\), and the optimized bound (2).
It uses the existing scalar ordered-quotient theorem only after deriving an actual quotient for each excluded relation.
The retained common-power alternatives are not declared injective, orderable or covered by any known whole-group theorem.
Further relations in their actual torsion-free quotients are allowed throughout.
The analysis supplies neither an example outside all known Atiyah classes nor a proof that any residual quotient escapes those classes.
The next substantive question is whether the central element \(a^3=(ba^j)^2\) in the remaining positive-defect configuration supplies an independent stronger kernel constraint, or whether longer signed cycles improve the moment certificate without assuming the desired conclusion.
No such further implication is established here.
