# Three double fibers exclude full-support cancellation on a four-coset pair with no unique products

## Statement

Take Nielsen and Soelberg's torsion-free group \(G_3\) and the eight row and eight column elements in their [author manuscript, Section 4, presentation (4.2)](../literature/pdf/NS24--author.pdf#page=8).
Write \(x=a_2,\ y=b_5,\ H=\langle yxy\rangle\).
The rows occupy exactly four right \(H\)-cosets and the columns exactly four left \(H\)-cosets.
Their difference tables have repeated double cosets, the six conjugate-subgroup intersections on each side are not all equal, and the mixed double coset through the cell of maximal widths has four cells.
There are no unique products, and the subgroup generated by all shared row and column differences is \(G_3\), whose abelianization is \(C_4\oplus C_4\).

For any nonzero coefficients \(r_1,\ldots,r_8,c_1,\ldots,c_8\) over a commutative domain,
\[
                     \left(\sum_i r_i a_i\right)
                     \left(\sum_j c_j b_j\right)\ne0 .
\]
The authors already report this nonvanishing for their sets. The four-coset description and the following certificate locate precisely which coefficient equations suffice in the residual case of [A0001](../tasks/Yin_T_A0001_v1.md#contract).

## Argument

The [coset calculation](../ledger/Yin_L_A0042_v1.md#the-two-covers) derives the words from the specified presentation, separates the four cosets by the homomorphism \(G_3\to\mathbb Z/4,\ x\mapsto-2,\ y\mapsto1\), and gives Laurent widths \((0,2,1,1)\) and \((2,0,1,1)\) in its indicated order.
The [double-coset calculation](../ledger/Yin_L_A0042_v1.md#double-cosets-in-the-product-and-in-the-rank-test) verifies the repetitions, intersection distinctions and the stated mixed multiplicity.
The differences include \(x=a_1^{-1}a_2=b_1b_2^{-1}\) and \(y=a_4^{-1}a_8=b_5b_1^{-1}\).
The two-generator presentation gives the indicated abelianization.

Write \(ij\) for the cell \(a_i b_j\).
The [finite-image certificate](../ledger/Yin_L_A0041_v1.md#certification-of-the-three-double-fibers) separates the image fibers \(\{15,34\},\{62,71\},\{65,74\}\) from every other cell.
Each image pair is one actual fiber since a singleton is excluded by the source's theorem for the original table.
The equalities \(12=23=31=x^{-1}\) and the source's maximum fiber size of three give the complete fourth fiber.
The [verifier](../computations/programs/Yin_J_A0002/verify.py) checks all fourteen source relations of the finite image and enumerates its sixty-four cells.

In the fraction field of the coefficient domain, the double equations of a zero product would give
\[
 \frac{r_1c_2}{r_3c_1}
   =\frac{r_1c_5}{r_3c_4}\,
     \frac{r_6c_2}{r_7c_1}\,
     \frac{r_7c_4}{r_6c_5}
   =(-1)^3=-1 .
\]
The triple equation \(r_1c_2+r_2c_3+r_3c_1=0\) would then require \(r_2c_3=0\).
This argument works in characteristic two as well.
It verifies the particular overlapping table, using the published torsion-freeness and multiplicity results. A different four-coset table need not have these three paired ratios or this triple.
