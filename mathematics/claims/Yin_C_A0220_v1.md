# Quartic characters of the order-25 commutator quotient

## Statement

Let \(H=\langle s,t\rangle\) have abelianization \(C_{25}\) with marked images \(1,7\), and suppose \(s\) has infinite order.
Write \(N=[H,H]\), \(M=N_{\rm ab}\), \(S=\operatorname{Ad}_s\) on \(M\), and \(M_{25}=M/\Phi_{25}(S)M\).
Suppose \(M_{25}\) has a simple quotient of characteristic \(p\equiv7,18\bmod25\) with \(p\le18^5\).
For \(p\ne7\), any complex two-by-two coefficients with \(B-yD\) invertible over \(\mathbb C(y)\) satisfy
\[
              \dim_H\ker\bigl(B+At-s(D+Ct)\bigr)\le 2/5
\]
with the unnormalized coordinate trace.
At \(p=7\) the [coupled-orbit estimate](Yin_C_A0217_v1.md#factor-coverage-and-numerical-bounds) gives \(1918/4375<1/2\) when all four corners and the assembled coefficient block are invertible.

Consequently a torsion-free group satisfying regular scalar injectivity with a rank-\(3/2\) rectangle at this marking can have only primes \(p\equiv1,24\bmod25\) dividing \(|M|\).
Indeed the [earlier prime-divisor restriction](Yin_C_A0216_v1.md#the-remaining-prime-divisors) gives \(M=M_{25}\) finite and leaves the four classes \(1,7,18,24\).
For a prime divisor in class 7 or 18, the nonzero module \(M/pM\) has a simple quotient of order \(p^4\).
The [Euclidean size bound](Yin_C_A0218_v1.md#statement) gives \(p<18^5\); the [corner test](Yin_C_A0201_v1.md#statement) and [four-column reduction](Yin_C_A0184_v1.md#the-four-group-elements-and-the-trace-bound) supply all required coefficient hypotheses.
The resulting nullity bound contradicts the assumed nullity \(1/2\).

## The five periods of the trace

By the [marked representation construction](Yin_C_A0216_v1.md#orbit-coordinates-on-the-five-slices), the simple quotient is additively \(E=\mathbb F_{p^4}\), and for a suitable primitive twenty-fifth root \(\alpha\in E\) its character exponents are
\[
                         e_i=\lambda(\alpha^i)\quad(i\bmod25)
\]
for any \(\mathbb F_p\)-linear functional \(\lambda:E\to\mathbb F_p\).
This formula incorporates the negative shift of indices from \(t=(ts^{-7})s^7\).
It gives actual representations \(s\mapsto P,\ t\mapsto P^7\operatorname{diag}(w^{e_i})\) for every \(w^p=1\), where \(P\) shifts the 25 coordinates forward.
If each of the five residue classes of indices modulo five has strictly positive representatives summing to \(p\), then the [positive-character interpolation bound](Yin_C_A0212_v1.md#regular-twists-with-positive-powers) gives \(2/5\).
In particular, the constant coefficient of the twisted polynomial is \(B-sPD\), which has full rank by the stated pencil condition and the infinite order of \(s\).

For \(\lambda(x)=c\operatorname{Tr}_{E/\mathbb F_p}(x)\), the Frobenius action on the indices is generated by \(p\bmod25\) and has multiplier group \(\Gamma=\{1,7,18,24\}\).
At index zero the trace of \(1\) is \(4\).
At the four nonzero multiples of five each trace is the sum of the primitive fifth roots and equals \(-1\).
On each of the four nonzero residue classes modulo five the traces take the same five values, counted with multiplicity.
Indeed the twenty unit indices split into five \(\Gamma\)-orbits, each meeting every nonzero residue class once.
For representatives \(k=1,2,3,6,9\) those values are the periods \(\eta_k=\sum_{h\in\Gamma}\alpha^{kh}\).

Their polynomial over \(\mathbb F_p\) is the reduction of
\[
                    F(Y)=Y^5-10Y^3+5Y^2+10Y+1 .
\]
For the integer identity, multiply \(\prod_k(Y-\sum_{h\in\Gamma}X^{kh})\) with the exponents taken modulo 25 and then impose \(\Phi_{25}(X)\).
To reduce a coefficient vector \(v_0,\ldots,v_{24}\), replace its entries at \(i=0,\ldots,19\) by \(v_i-v_{20+(i\bmod5)}\).
This uses \(X^{20+j}=-(X^j+X^{5+j}+X^{10+j}+X^{15+j})\) for \(0\le j<5\).
The resulting coefficients in ascending powers of \(Y\) are \(1,10,5,-10,0,1\), all constant in \(X\), as reproduced by the [integer verifier](../computations/programs/Yin_J_A0008/verify_periods.py).
Consequently the unordered five period values apply to every primitive marking.

Write \([x]_p\in\{0,\ldots,p-1\}\) for the representative modulo \(p\).
If \(3p/4<c<p\), the five positive representatives on the zero residue class sum to \(p\), since \([4c]_p+4[-c]_p=(4c-3p)+4(p-c)\).
Thus it suffices to have \(\sum_k[c\eta_k]_p=p\).
The periods are nonzero because \(F(0)=1\).

## Bounded prime check and two other functionals

The [period calculation](../computations/programs/Yin_J_A0008/readme.md#quartic-periods-through-the-size-cutoff) sieves every prime in the two residue classes up to \(18^5=1889568\).
There are 14140 such primes including seven.
At every other prime the program reconstructs five distinct roots of \(F\bmod p\) and verifies each by substitution, accounting for the complete degree.
For 14137 primes it finds \(3p/4<c<p\) with period sum \(p\).
The two exceptions are \(43,157\), for which it exhausts all \(p/2<c<p\) without that sum.
This exception concerns the chosen scalar trace functionals.

At these two primes choose one quartic factor \(Q\) of \(\Phi_{25}\bmod p\), a root \(\alpha_0\), and the values of a functional at \(1,\alpha_0,\alpha_0^2,\alpha_0^3\) as follows.

| \(p\) | \(Q(X)\) | Initial values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 43 | \(X^4+3X^3+21X^2+3X+1\) | \(1,3,2,11\) |
| 157 | \(X^4+6X^3+45X^2+6X+1\) | \(1,7,19,2\) |

The verifier checks polynomial divisibility.
The factors are irreducible since \(\operatorname{ord}_{25}(p)=4\).
For \(Q(X)=X^4+\sum_{j<4}q_j X^j\), extend the four initial values by \(e_{i+4}=[-\sum_{j<4}q_j e_{i+j}]_p\).
For both rows the resulting 25 entries are positive and the five residue-class sums all equal \(p\).
All primitive twenty-fifth roots in \(E\) are of the form \(\alpha_0^d\) for a unit \(d\bmod25\).
Evaluating this same functional on their powers permutes the indices by \(i\mapsto di\), carrying the five residue classes to the five residue classes.
Hence each certificate covers every possible quartic marking, and positive-character interpolation proves the claimed bound.
