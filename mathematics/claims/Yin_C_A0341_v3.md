# A normal alternating subgroup on six letters in an arbitrary quotient

## Statement

Under the [cyclic order-sixteen marked hypotheses](Yin_C_A0341_v1.md#statement) of degrees \(1,6\), suppose a quotient \(J\) of \(H\) contains a normal subgroup isomorphic to \(A_6\).
Every complex two-square rectangle of noninteger regular rank then has regular nullity at most \(19/40\).
This includes quotients inducing automorphisms outside the natural six-letter permutation action, with no restriction on the centralizer of \(A_6\) in \(J\).

In particular at a half kernel no quotient can have a normal subgroup \(A_6^k\) for \(k=1,3,5\).
Together with the [other two normal-subgroup tests](Yin_C_A0341_v2.md#statement), this gives the same restriction for any of \(A_5,A_6,{\rm GL}_3(\mathbb F_2)\) as the common simple factor.

## All three outer actions of cyclic order two

Let \(T=A_6\).
Its action on the ten unordered partitions of six points into two triples is intrinsic: each partition gives the order-nine subgroup independently cycling its two triples.
These are all Sylow three-subgroups, since they are Sylow by their order and are conjugate under \(A_6\), and conjugation by \(A_6\) preserves the ten listed subgroups.
The two triple orbits of each subgroup recover its partition.
Let \(T_{10}\) be this action, of order 360 by the [integer verifier](../computations/programs/Yin_J_A0011/verify_alternating_normalizer_sixteen.mjs).
Indeed the three-cycle \((0\,1\,2)\) and the five-cycle \((1\,2\,3\,4\,5)\) generate 360 permutations in both the six- and ten-point models.
Since \(T\) is normal in \(J\), conjugation gives an actual ten-point image \(K\) of \(J\) containing \(T_{10}\), inside its normalizer \(N\) in \(S_{10}\).

Here \(N\) has order 1440, and \(N/T_{10}=C_2^2\).
To verify this directly let \(a,b\) be the two generators just given in the ten-point model.
For each \(a',b'\in T_{10}\) of the respective cycle types and each of ten proposed images \(j\) of site zero, propagate a map \(h\) from that site using \(h(ai)=a'h(i),\ h(bi)=b'h(i)\).
Transitivity determines the whole map; inconsistent or nonbijective assignments are discarded.
The resulting 1440 distinct permutations are exactly the normalizing ones, since conjugating the two generators into the group suffices by equality of finite cardinalities.
The verifier checks every square lies in \(T_{10}\), hence the quotient of order four is elementary abelian.

As in [the five- and six-site test](Yin_C_A0341_v1.md#the-endpoint-test-with-one-possible-singular-corner), suppose \(\delta>7/16\); otherwise the assertion already holds.
There cannot be a binary character negative on \(t\), since it and the given order-sixteen character would supply a quotient \(C_{16}\times C_2\) with \(\delta\le7/16\).
Thus the image of \(t\) in \(K/T_{10}\) is trivial, since its characters are binary and separate elements.
The quotient is generated by the image of \(s\), so \(K=T_{10}\) or it is obtained by adjoining a single nontrivial coset of \(T_{10}\) in \(N\).
The first case is covered by the [six-letter permutation bound](Yin_C_A0341_v1.md#statement), since \(T_{10}\cong A_6\).
The three cosets in the second case give three concrete subgroups of order 720.

## Ordinary and induced actions for every marking

Write \(x,y\) for the images of \(s,t\) in \(K\).
For each of the three nontrivial cosets the verifier takes \(x\) in that coset and \(y\in T_{10}\), retaining pairs generating all 720 elements and choosing one representative per simultaneous conjugacy class of this ten-site action.
The three counts are 53, 78 and 78.
For 53, 78 and 76 of these classes, respectively, the ten-site action already satisfies the [matching and recurrent-cycle test](Yin_C_A0341_v1.md#the-endpoint-test-with-one-possible-singular-corner).
For each chosen lift with spread \(D\), the selected end requires rank-one changes at at most \(q\) sites, and \(2D+16q\le76\).
The candidate masks are chosen deterministically.
The proof uses their exact cost comparison against every feasible matching and checks all recurrent cycles, independently of how the candidate was found.
As all sixteen phases come from tensoring the actual action with the marked character, this gives \(\delta\le76/160=19/40\).

For the remaining two pairs induce the six-letter \(T_{10}\)-action \(\rho\) to \(K\) on two blocks of six sites.
Represent the two cosets by \(1,x\).
The element \(x\) sends \((0,i)\) to \((1,i)\) and \((1,i)\) to \((0,\rho(x^2)i)\).
The element \(y\) preserves the blocks, acting by \(\rho(y)\) on the first and \(\rho(x^{-1}yx)\) on the second.
These permutations are the ordinary induced action on \(K\times_{T_{10}}\{0,\ldots,5\}\), with the three six-letter lifts computed via the faithful ten-site action.
The resulting lifts and all their matching costs are checked against the full \(4^{12}\) possible path assignments in each case.

| 1 | 2048 | 3242 | 14196086 | 1120 | 4 | 44 | 0 |
| 3 | 48 | 0 | 3900 | 4224 | 1 | 44 | 0 |

The two masks specify the initial sites at which to subtract sixteen from the costs \(c,\ 6c\bmod16\), respectively; the joint anchor lists first edges before second edges.
The verifier checks the tight recurrent union for the fourfold minimum, including nonzero original-group word detection by its cyclic degree or permutation action.
The estimate in either exceptional action is \(2\cdot44/(16\cdot12)=11/24\).
The largest bound over all 209 cases is \(19/40\).
This proves the normal \(A_6\) assertion.
For the odd powers \(k\le5\), conjugation on the \(k\) simple factors at a half kernel fixes a factor by the [small permutation argument](Yin_C_A0341_v2.md#the-marked-normalizer-and-the-fourteen-klein-subgroups), reducing to a normal copy of \(A_6\) in \(J\).
