## 1. Exact local kernel

For a unital complex subalgebra A of M_n(C), let D_A consist of complex linear maps d:M_n->M_n satisfying d L_a=L_a d for every a in A, d(1)=0, and tau composed with d=0.
Then

    intersection_(d in D_A) ker d = A''.                          (1)

To prove the first inclusion, regard a matrix as n columns, hence M_n as n copies of the natural left A-module C^n.
Every A-module map is a block matrix of maps c_(rs) in A', acting from input column s to output column r.
Equivalently it has a finite expression d(X)=sum_k c_k X b_k with c_k in A' and b_k in M_n.
Thus d commutes with L_q for every q in A''.
Since d(1)=0, d(q)=q d(1)=0.
This proves A'' is contained in the left side of (1), without using the trace constraint.

Conversely, for every b in A' the map d_b(X)=Xb-bX belongs to D_A.
It kills the unit, has trace zero, and

    d_b(aX)=aXb-baX=a(Xb-bX)=a d_b(X)

because ba=ab.
Their joint kernel is precisely (A')'=A''.
This proves (1), including the trace-constrained assertion.

For A=UT_2, commutation with both diagonal matrix units forces an element of A' to be diagonal, and commutation with e12 forces its two diagonal entries to coincide.
Therefore A'=C1 and A''=M2.
Every A-module endomorphism of M2 is consequently right multiplication R_b; d(1)=0 forces b=0.
All permitted d vanish.
Thus this entire family of local maps cannot detect the missing e21 direction for UT_2.
This is a limitation of the method, not a counterexample to T_F0007.

## 2. Finite free-product consequence under A_i=A_i''

Let there be finitely many normalized full matrix factors M_i=M_(n_i)(C), with unital subalgebras A_i satisfying A_i=A_i''.
Let (M,tau) be their reduced tracial free product and let A be the algebra generated by the embedded A_i.
The conclusion is

    Div_L0(M)(A) intersect M = Div_M(A).                         (2)

It follows by the complete argument below from the fixed [C_F0116 (recorded assessment 2)](Yin_C_F0116_v1.md#decision-candidate-0002) mechanism and [C_F0106 (recorded assessment 2)](Yin_C_F0106_v1.md#decision-candidate-0002).
The A_i need not be star closed.
If they star-generate the respective factors, this is a sufficient input class in the original T_F0007 contract.
No generation premise is needed for the equality (2) in the stated ambient M.

Choose a finite vector-space basis b_(i,r) of A_i'.
For each pair i,r put d_(i,r)(c)=c b_(i,r)-b_(i,r)c.
These maps are bounded, kill 1 and A_i, preserve the centered subspace M_i^circ, commute with L_a for a in A_i, and have joint kernel A_i^circ on M_i^circ by (1).

Extend d_(i,r) to T_(i,r) on L2(M) by acting only on the terminal centered block if that block belongs to factor i, and by zero on other terminal factors and the vacuum.
The reduced-word orthogonal decomposition makes this extension bounded.
It creates no scalar component.
For centered a in A_j, it commutes with L_a: different first and last blocks commute; deleting the first block leaves the same last block; on a sole block from factor i the identity d_(i,r)(ac)=a d_(i,r)(c), including its scalar part, supplies the boundary case.
On the vacuum d_(i,r)(a)=0.
This checks every boundary without any square-zero premise.

C_F0116 Section 4 proves for an arbitrary bounded T, not just its square-zero example, that the class of actual closed affiliated a satisfying T Dom(a) subset Dom(a) and aT=Ta on Dom(a) is a unital division-closed algebra.
Its proof uses the original sum/product domains, their graph closures, and Dom(a^-1)=Ran(a).
Consequently every affiliated rational element of A satisfies this full domain commutation with every T_(i,r).

For bounded f in that affiliated closure, it follows that f commutes with all T_(i,r).
Choose an orthonormal basis of M_i^circ whose first vectors span A_i^circ and whose remaining vectors span its Hilbert orthogonal complement.
The joint kernel of the local maps on each terminal block is exactly A_i^circ.
Thus if every T_(i,r) kills a vector, that vector has no coefficient with terminal i-letter outside A_i^circ.
For a reduced word w=v t s with t one of the complementary i-letters, all T_(i,r) kill s* Omega because s is empty or begins outside i.
They therefore kill f s* Omega.
Its coefficient at v t is zero, and

    tau((v t)* f s*)=tau(s* (v t)* f)=tau((v t s)* f)

shows that the coefficient of f at w is zero.
This handles any forbidden letter at any position.
Hence f Omega is supported on reduced words made only from A_i^circ.

Apply C_F0106's full finite-factor minimal residual realization to f: its state pencil V=sum_t mu_t tensor c_t in full centered selfadjoint factor bases has ||V^k||->0, f=lambda(I-V)^-1 gamma, and all same-factor state products zero.
A factorwise unitary basis change to the just chosen bases leaves V itself unchanged.
Every coefficient containing a complementary letter is zero.
Minimal reachability and observability, using the same-factor zero products also for nonreduced words, therefore force every complementary transition matrix to vanish.
This is exactly C_F0116 Section 5's explicitly proved change-of-basis and elimination argument, with a possibly larger permitted subspace in each factor.
Thus V has entries in the prescribed A.
The already audited adjoint-free DR97-U004 small-power scalar pivots give each entry of (I-V)^-1 in Div_M(A), proving (2).
The reverse inclusion is immediate from actual bounded inverse evaluation.

Each input equality is automatic here: in a finite-dimensional matrix factor every injective affiliated operator is bounded and invertible, and Cayley-Hamilton puts the inverse of any invertible element of a unital finite-dimensional subalgebra back in that subalgebra.
Thus (2) is a genuine permanence family, not an assumed output equality.

## 3. Boundary for continuation

The joint-kernel identity (1) is proved only for a full matrix ambient algebra.
It must not be copied to arbitrary finite direct sums: for example the algebraic relative double commutant of C1 in C2 is all C2, although general C1-module maps killing 1 can distinguish its complement.
The terminal method can be formulated more generally through its actual family of local maps, but no assertion about those direct sums is made here.

For full matrices, the condition A_i=A_i'' is the precise local detection condition for this specified class of endomorphisms.
UT2 is the first explicit input outside it.
The theorem (2) does not resolve UT2 free products, arbitrary proper non-star input algebras, or diffuse factors.
The exact Task contract remains open.
