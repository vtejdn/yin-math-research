# Normal free congruences have equivalent actual-vacuum and full-domain conditions

## Current assessment
[Selected decision](#decision-reviewed)

## Statement

Let \(N=W^*(Z)\) be an abelian finite probability von Neumann algebra generated by a bounded normal \(Z\), with faithful trace and spectral law \(\mu\).
Assume the complete scalar actual-vacuum condition: on \(\mathbb C[Z,Z^*]\), the outer derivation normalized by \(\delta_Z Z=1\otimes1\) has \(1\otimes1\in\operatorname{Dom}\delta_Z^*\).
Write its actual vacuum as \(\xi_Z\).

Let \(S\) be a standard variance-one semicircular element free from \(N\), and put \(M=W^*(N,S)\), \(H=L^2(M)\), \(\Omega=1\), \(P=P_\Omega\).
Let \(f\) be real-valued and \(C^1\) on an open neighborhood of a compact rectangle containing \(\sigma(Z)\) in its interior.
Suppose
\[
 h=f(Z)\ge0,\qquad \ker h=0,
\]
and set \(b=h^{-1}\), \(T=hSh\), \(X=(Z,T)\).
The inverse is an affiliated operator and need not be bounded.
Then \(X\) is a normal generating pair for \(M\).

For a generating tuple \(Y\), let \(\mathsf A(Y)\) mean the represented full-star-algebra derivations with values \(\delta_iY_j=\delta_{ij}1\otimes1\) and actual vacuum adjoints; let \(\mathsf F(Y)\) mean arbitrary complex-linear \(D_i:M\Omega\to H\) with \([Y_j,D_i]=\delta_{ij}P\); and let \(\mathsf S(Y)\) impose in addition actual \(M\Omega\subseteq\operatorname{Dom}D_i\cap\operatorname{Dom}D_i^*\) for densely defined Hilbert operators.
No continuity or closability premise is part of \(\mathsf F\).
All conditions are existential, and all tensor actions are outer unless explicitly called inner.

Then
\[
 \boxed{\mathsf A(X)\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \mathsf F(X)\
 \Longleftrightarrow\ \mathsf S(X)\
 \Longleftrightarrow\ b\in L^2(N).}
 \tag{N1}
\]
When these conditions hold, both canonical contraction closures and their actual adjoints have domains containing \(M\Omega\).

For explicit vacua, put
\[
 \phi(z,w)=\frac{f(z)-f(w)}{z-w},\quad
 \psi_1(z,w)=b(z)\phi(z,w),\quad
 \psi_2(z,w)=b(w)\phi(z,w),
\]
off the product-null diagonal.
These are Hilbert tensors when \(b\in L^2\), since \(\phi\) is bounded.
Let
\[
 \Lambda(a\otimes c)=a(S^2-1)c,\qquad a,c\in N,
\]
extended as the free degree-two isometry from \(L^2(N)\otimes_2L^2(N)\) to \(H\).
The actual vacuum vectors are
\[
 \xi_1=\xi_Z-\Lambda(\overline{\psi_1+\psi_2}),\qquad
 \xi_2=bSb.
\]
The bar is pointwise complex conjugation in the marginal tensor algebra, without flipping the factors.
Inner products are linear in the second variable.

The proof also supplies a concrete example with spectral support the whole disk:
\[
 d\mu(z)=\frac3\pi |z|^4\,1_{\{|z|\le1\}}\,dA(z),\quad
 h=|Z|^2,\quad \xi_Z=2|Z|^4Z,\quad
 \|h^{-1}\|_2^2=3.
\]
Thus this comparison class includes a genuinely planar normal coordinate and a singular congruence.
It does not settle the equivalences for arbitrary correlated normal tuples.

## Argument

The scalar/free reference operators are the existing [C_F0055](Yin_C_F0055_v1.md#statement) and [C_F0045](Yin_C_F0045_v1.md#statement) interfaces.
The free Hilbert model and right-module decomposition are the same as [C_F0012 Section 1](Yin_C_F0012_v1.md#1-an-explicit-tracial-fock-model), now for the abelian algebra generated by \(Z\).
That earlier Claim has two selfadjoint coordinates and a particular law; its theorem is not transferred as a theorem for the present normal pair.
The new free correction below is proved for every \(L^2\) marginal tensor.
It is a semicircular specialization of the end-module idea in [C_F0058 Sections 3-4](Yin_C_F0058_v1.md#3-a-right-end-annihilation-estimate), with a direct creation/projection decomposition and actual adjoint bounds.

### Reference operator and the two scalar kernels

C_F0055 and C_F0045 give the normalized reference operator E_Z on M with
\[
 [E_Z,Z]=P,\quad [E_Z,S]=0,\quad E_Z1=0,\quad E_Z^*1=\xi_Z,
\]
and both actual M domains and operator-norm-to-H bounds C_Z,C_Z*.
The scalar vacuum is unchanged on free adjoining S.

Put
\[
 \phi(z,w)=\frac{f(z)-f(w)}{z-w}.
\]
Its diagonal value is irrelevant since mu is diffuse.
The C1 assumption bounds phi uniformly.
C1 star-polynomial approximants to f converge uniformly together with the divided differences, so the bounded M-to-H reference operator and Hilbert-Schmidt convergence give the actual identity
\[
 [E_Z,h]=\Theta(\phi)\quad\text{on }M.
 \tag{N2}
\]
Here Theta(a tensor b)=aPb.
In particular no use of h as an unspecified polynomial-core vector is needed.

Assume b in L2.
The marginal tensors
\[
 \psi_1=(b\otimes1)\phi,\qquad \psi_2=(1\otimes b)\phi
 \tag{N3}
\]
belong to L2(mu squared).
Since phi is symmetric in z,w, their norms are equal.
Denote their common norm by L.

### A free correction estimate for every L2 marginal tensor

Use the standard N--semicircular Fock realization
\[
 H=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}L^2(N)^{\otimes(n+1)}.
\]
Right creation r appends 1, right annihilation r* removes the last tensor factor by its trace, and rho(S)=r+r*.
They commute with left N, [r*,S]=P, and r*r=I.
Put Q=rr* and W=S squared-1.
Then
\[
 \rho(W)=r^2+Q+r^{*2}.
 \tag{N4}
\]

For a finite marginal tensor psi=sum a_j tensor b_j with bounded N factors, define on M
\[
 V_\psi=\sum_j b_j W a_j,\quad
 C_\psi(p)=\sum_j\rho(a_j)r^2\rho(b_j)p,\quad
 H_\psi(p)=\sum_j\rho(a_j)Q\rho(b_j)p.
\]
The following exact bounds hold:
\[
 \|V_\psi\|_2=\|\psi\|_2,\qquad
 \|C_\psi(p)\|_2\le\|p\|\|\psi\|_2,\qquad
 \|H_\psi(p)\|_2\le\|p\|\|\psi\|_2.
 \tag{N5}
\]
The first identity is the free degree-two isometry bWa -> b tensor 1 tensor a.

For the second, decompose H as the right N-module direct sum of u L2(N), where u=1 or a tensor of positive semicircular degree with final N factor 1.
Use orthonormal bounded prefixes.
Write p=sum_u u p_u.
Its conditional column identity is
\[
 \sum_u|p_u(s)|^2=E_N(p^*p)(s)\le\|p\|^2
\]
almost everywhere.
The creation operation sends its u component to the same prefix followed by p_u(s)b_j(s) tensor 1 tensor a_j(t); different prefixes remain orthogonal.
Thus
\[
 \|C_\psi(p)\|_2^2
 =\int\!\!\int\sum_u|p_u(s)|^2|\psi(t,s)|^2\,d\mu(s)d\mu(t)
 \le\|p\|^2\|\psi\|_2^2.
\]
Initially this is proved on finite reduced vectors, then on M by its right-module coefficients and Hilbert limits.

For the third, Q retains the scalar final N coefficient in all nonvacuum right-module sectors and kills the vacuum sector.
The map c -> Q(pc) from L2(N) to QH has norm at most ||p||.
Multiplication QH tensor_2 L2(N) -> H is an isometry.
Applying this bounded map to one tensor leg of psi and then using that isometry proves the bound.

Consequently all three maps extend continuously in the L2 tensor norm.
Define
\[
 B_{1,\psi}(p)=pV_\psi-C_\psi(p)-H_\psi(p),\qquad
 B_{2,\psi}(p)=pV_\psi-C_\psi(p).
 \tag{N6}
\]
For finite psi, (N4) gives the exact expressions
\[
 B_{1,\psi}=\sum_j\rho(a_j)r^{*2}\rho(b_j),\quad
 B_{2,\psi}=\sum_j\rho(a_j)(r^{*2}+Q)\rho(b_j).
 \tag{N7}
\]
Hence
\[
 \|B_{1,\psi}:M\to H\|\le3\|\psi\|_2,\qquad
 \|B_{2,\psi}:M\to H\|\le2\|\psi\|_2.
 \tag{N8}
\]
For psi dagger=sum b_j* tensor a_j*, the actual adjoint pairings on M give
\[
 B_{1,\psi}^*p=C_{\psi^\dagger}(p),\qquad
 B_{2,\psi}^*p=C_{\psi^\dagger}(p)+H_{\psi^\dagger}(p),
 \tag{N9}
\]
with bounds ||psi||_2 and 2||psi||_2 respectively.
To justify actual adjoints for general psi, approximate it in L2 by finite bounded separated tensors.
Both the operators on M and the displayed adjoint candidates converge in B(M,H); pairing against arbitrary bounded inputs passes to the limit.
This puts all M in the actual adjoint domain of each densely defined B operator.
No assertion about a complete product domain or boundedness on all H is made.

Both B operators commute with every left element of N.
Using (N7), the elementary identity [r* squared,S]=P r*, and the fact rho(S) commutes with left S, one obtains
\[
 [B_{1,\psi},S]=\Theta(\psi\cdot S),\qquad
 [B_{2,\psi},S]=\Theta(S\cdot\psi).
 \tag{N10}
\]
Here psi dot S=sum a_j tensor b_j S and S dot psi=sum S a_j tensor b_j.
These tensors have Hilbert norms ||psi||_2 by freeness.
Thus the commutator identities pass to general psi by Hilbert-Schmidt and B(M,H) convergence.
Also B_{1,psi}1=B_{2,psi}1=0.

### A strong dual for T

For bounded \(c\in N\), the Fock operator \(\rho(c)r\) appends the vector \(c\), so \(\|\rho(c)r\eta\|_2=\|c\|_2\|\eta\|_2\).
Bounded spectral cuts \(b_n=b1_{\{b\le n\}}\) therefore make \(\rho(b_n)r\) converge in \(B(H)\) to an operator \(C_b\) of norm \(\|b\|_2\).
The spectral-domain criterion for the closed right multiplier \(\rho(b)\) puts \(rH\) in its domain and gives \(C_b=\rho(b)r\) there.
This repeats the weighted creation mechanism of [C_F0134 Section 2](Yin_C_F0134_v1.md#2-right-creation-and-a-free-weighted-estimate) for the present abelian algebra \(N\); it does not require \(N=W^*(h)\).
On M define
\[
 Kp=\rho(b)r^*\rho(b)p.
 \tag{N11}
\]
Both unbounded products are actual.
The first is p b in L2.
Since r* =rho(S)-r,
\[
 r^*(pb)=pbS-r(pb).
\]
After multiplying on the right by b, its first term is p(bSb), and the second is C_b(pb).
Freeness gives bSb in L2 with norm ||b||_2 squared, by bounded truncation and the degree-one tensor b tensor b.
Hence
\[
 Kp=p(bSb)-C_b(pb),\qquad
 \|Kp\|_2\le2\|b\|_2^2\|p\|.
 \tag{N12}
\]
The closed right multiplier rho(b) identifies this product from its bounded spectral cuts, since both terms converge in H.

Pairing on M and using the actual product rho(b)r gives
\[
 K^*p=C_b(pb),\qquad
 \|K^*p\|_2\le\|b\|_2^2\|p\|.
 \tag{N13}
\]
This is an inclusion of M in the actual Hilbert adjoint domain of K with domain M.
It suffices for closability.
In particular K1=0 and K*1=bSb.
Direct commutator calculation on these actual bounded-vector domains yields
\[
 [K,a]=0\ (a\in N),\quad [K,S]=\Theta(b\otimes b),\quad
 [K,T]=h\Theta(b\otimes b)h=P.
 \tag{N14}
\]

### The corrected Z operator and its actual vacuum

Set
\[
 E_1=E_Z-B_{1,\psi_1}-B_{2,\psi_2},\qquad E_2=K
 \quad\text{on }M.
 \tag{N15}
\]
The preceding estimates and actual adjoint pairings put M in both operator and adjoint domains, and give bounds C_Z+5L and C_Z*+3L for E_1 and its adjoint.
Their commutators with Z are P and zero respectively.

By (N10) and (N3),
\[
 h[B_{1,\psi_1}+B_{2,\psi_2},S]h
 =\Theta(\phi)Sh+hS\Theta(\phi)
 =[E_Z,T].
\]
Thus [E_1,T]=0 and [E_2,T]=P.
Negating E_1,E_2 gives S(X); both operators are closable because their actual adjoints contain M.
Generation holds even before b is L2: bounded spectral cuts c_n=b 1_{b<=n} satisfy c_n T c_n -> S strongly, since c_n h ->1 strongly.

If Lambda(a tensor c)=a(S squared-1)c denotes the free degree-two isometry, the normalized actual vacuum vectors are
\[
 \xi_1=\xi_Z-\Lambda(\overline{\psi_1+\psi_2}),\qquad
 \xi_2=bSb .
 \tag{N16}
\]
Here the bar is componentwise conjugation in the abelian tensor algebra, without swapping factors.
Formula (N9) at the vacuum proves (N16) directly.
Since S(X) holds for a normal tuple, C_F0070 Part C identifies these vectors as actual vacua for the unique represented starred derivations.
C_F0075 supplies canonical full-domain compatibility.

### Necessity from a bare full dual or from A(X)

Assume F(X).
Automatic boundedness and the homogeneous normal commutator interface of C_F0070 imply that the normalized dual E_T for T commutes with Z*, and hence with left C*(Z).
It commutes with h.
Since S belongs to M and all operators in the following expression have bounded-vector inputs,
\[
 P=[E_T,hSh]=h[E_T,S]h.
\]
Applying to Omega gives h v=Omega for \(v=[E_T,S](h\Omega)\) in H.
Regularity therefore implies v=b Omega and b in L2.
This proves F(X) implies the last condition in (N1).

Now assume A(X).
The derivation delta_T has value zero on Z and Z*, and 1 tensor 1 on the selfadjoint T.
The zero starred value follows directly from normality: differentiating \(ZZ^*=Z^*Z\) gives \((\lambda(Z)\otimes I-I\otimes\rho(Z))\delta_T(Z^*)=0\).
The reference scalar actual-vacuum hypothesis makes \(\mu\) diffuse, and the product spectral diagonal is null, so this operator is injective.
Hence \(\delta_T(Z^*)=0\).
Thus this single derivation is real, algebraic-tensor-valued and coassociative on the entire original star core.
Its actual vacuum is part of A(X).
The zero-defect real energy argument in C_F0071's mixed reverse proof applies coordinatewise: its adjoint formula and coassociativity use only these displayed values, not selfadjointness of Z.
It gives the original right contraction and adjoint bounds 4||xi_T||_2 and 5||xi_T||_2.
The same bounded-approximant weak-closed-graph argument gives this coordinate's full M domain and generator commutators.
Therefore a bare E_T as in the previous paragraph exists, and b is L2.
The ordinary implications S(X) implies F(X), and S(X) implies A(X), complete (N1).

### A concrete planar instance

Take
\[
 d\mu(z)=\frac3\pi |z|^4\,1_{\{|z|\le1\}}\,dA(z),
 \qquad Z(z)=z,\qquad h=|Z|^2.
\]
This is a probability law because \(6\int_0^1r^5\,dr=1\), with spectrum the entire closed disk.
Its density is bounded by \(3/\pi\).
Since the disk lies in the radius-two disk about each point of its support,
\[
 \sup_z\int\frac{d\mu(w)}{|z-w|}
 \le\frac3\pi\int_{|v|\le2}\frac{dA(v)}{|v|}=12.
\]
The identical column bound gives the elementary Schur estimate for its Cauchy integral operator.
In particular all double integrals below are absolutely integrable for bounded polynomial inputs.

Use the convention
\[
 C_\mu(z)=\int\frac{1}{z-w}\,d\mu(w).
\]
For \(z\ne0\), angular integration gives \(2\pi/z\) when the radius of \(w\) is below \(|z|\), and zero when it is above \(|z|\).
Therefore
\[
 C_\mu(z)=\frac6z\int_0^{|z|}r^5\,dr
          =\frac{|z|^6}{z}=|z|^4\bar z.
\]
At zero the value is zero by rotation symmetry.
For any represented star polynomial \(p\), its divided difference is a bounded coarse kernel, and antisymmetry of \(1/(z-w)\), followed by Fubini, gives
\[
 (\tau\otimes\tau)\delta_Zp
 =\iint\frac{p(z)-p(w)}{z-w}\,d\mu(z)d\mu(w)
 =2\int p(z)C_\mu(z)\,d\mu(z)
 =\langle 2\overline{C_\mu},p\rangle .
\]
Thus its actual scalar conjugate variable, with the stipulated second-variable-linear inner product, is exactly
\[
 \xi_Z=2\overline{C_\mu}=2|z|^4z.
\]
The represented derivation is well defined: the law has full disk support, so a star polynomial which vanishes almost everywhere vanishes as a polynomial function there, and its divided difference is zero.
The divided-difference Leibniz identity gives the outer derivation law.
This verifies the complete scalar actual-vacuum premise directly, including its conjugation.

The spectral inverse \(b=|Z|^{-2}\) is unbounded, but
\[
 \|b\|_2^2=\frac3\pi\int_{|z|\le1}1\,dA(z)=3.
\]
The function \(f(z)=|z|^2\) meets the stated \(C^1\) hypothesis.
Consequently
\[
 X=(Z,|Z|^2S|Z|^2)
\]
satisfies all conditions in (N1), with a genuinely two-dimensional normal coordinate and an unbounded inverse in the congruence.
It is a positive comparison class, not a separating example.


## Scope and self-review

The reference scalar vacuum is an assumption except in the displayed disk instance, where it is verified directly.
Freeness is used to construct and estimate the reference and correction operators; it is not claimed for the target pair.
The only singular inverse is the positive affiliated \(b\), whose every Hilbert product is justified either by a closed right-multiplier spectral cutoff or a free tensor isometry.
The corrected operators are defined on \(M\Omega\); their complete domains after closure are not identified beyond the asserted inclusions.

The necessity from \(\mathsf A(X)\) uses only the selfadjoint target coordinate \(T\).
Its real, algebraic-tensor-valued, zero-defect derivation admits the established energy proof even though the annihilated coordinate \(Z\) is normal rather than selfadjoint.
This coordinate check is explicitly included above.
No general normal \(\mathsf A\Rightarrow\mathsf F\) implication is imported.

The author self-checked the free right-module column bound, both correction operators and actual adjoints, all multiplier orders, the two unbounded products in \(K\), generation by regular cutoffs, necessity from a single coordinate, and the disk Cauchy-transform conjugation.
The initial decision retains the complete proof for separate independent review.
No novelty or historical-priority assertion is made.
