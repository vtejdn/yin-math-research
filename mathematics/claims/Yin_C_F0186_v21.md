# Finite-variance three-channel cubic determinants

This theorem assembles the original real three-channel probability floor with the adopted middle-range theorem.
It extends the determinant assembly from the [two-channel model](Yin_C_F0186_v20.md#statement) to the three-channel path and its ordered cubic at zero, while preserving the earlier theorem at its own scope.
The argument was developed for Yin through ordinary collaboration and authoring self-checks.
The [middle input](Yin_C_F0187_v25.md#decision-three-channel-middle-reviewed) and the present assembly have independent whole-proof review at their exact scopes.
The [current assessment](#decision-finite-variance-three-channel-reviewed) adopts the fixed-model theorem without completing either general Task.

<a id="statement"></a>
## Exact original model and conclusions

Let \(C_N,D_N\) be independent real symmetric Wigner matrices.
For input \(\ell=1,2\), its strict upper entries are independent copies of \(\xi_\ell/\sqrt N\), where \(\xi_\ell\) is fixed, real, centered and has variance one.
Its diagonal entries are independent copies of \(\zeta_\ell/\sqrt N\), where \(\zeta_\ell\) is fixed, real, centered and has finite second moment.
All upper-triangular coordinates and the two inputs are independent within each size.
All sizes may be coupled arbitrarily on one probability space.
No density, bounded support, higher moment or original-input operator-norm bound is assumed.

Put
\[
 L_N:=\begin{pmatrix}
 C_N&I_N&0\\
 I_N&C_N+D_N&I_N\\
 0&I_N&C_N+\sqrt2D_N
 \end{pmatrix},
 \qquad P_N:=P(C_N,D_N),
\]
\[
 P(x,y):=(x+\sqrt2y)(x+y)x-2x-\sqrt2y.
\]
Let \(c,d\) be free centered variance-one semicircular elements in a finite von Neumann algebra \((\mathcal M,\tau)\) with faithful normal tracial state, and put
\[
 \ell:=\begin{pmatrix}c&1&0\\1&c+d&1\\0&1&c+\sqrt2d\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad p:=P(c,d),\qquad \tau_3:=\operatorname{tr}_3\otimes\tau.
\]
Use the ordinary FK determinant including zero mass, and set \(\log0=-\infty\).

The conclusions are that
\[
 v:=\log\Delta_{\tau_3}(\ell)\in\mathbb R,\qquad
 u:=\log\Delta_\tau(p)=3v\in\mathbb R,
 \tag{Three-finite-free-targets}
\]
and, along all dimensions,
\[
 \frac1{3N}\log|\det L_N|\longrightarrow v,\qquad
 \frac1N\log|\det P_N|\longrightarrow u
 \quad\text{in probability}.
 \tag{Three-probability-logarithms}
\]
Under every prescribed cross-size coupling,
\[
 \limsup_{N\to\infty}\frac1{3N}\log|\det L_N|=v,\qquad
 \limsup_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\log|\det P_N|=u
 \quad\text{almost surely}.
 \tag{Three-every-coupling-limsup}
\]
The probability-one event may depend on the fixed laws and coupling.
These statements concern this fixed coefficient tuple and this ordered scalar cubic at the fixed parameter zero.
No full-sequence almost-sure logarithmic convergence, expectation convergence of raw logarithms, nonzero scalar parameter extension, complex-input extension or Brown-measure conclusion is asserted.
They do not complete the arbitrary-coefficient [F0013 contract](../tasks/Yin_T_F0013_v1.md#contract) or arbitrary-polynomial [F0014 contract](../tasks/Yin_T_F0014_v1.md#contract).

## Complete assembly

The proof below uses the exact adopted input (Three-middle-input).
It uses one fixed \(D_*\), for example \(D_*=2\), throughout.
This is sufficient for probability convergence, and no inverse-power estimate for the common error is needed.

<a id="middle-and-free-log"></a>
### Small singular mass and finiteness of the free target

Write \(a_{1,N}\le\cdots\le a_{3N,N}\) for the singular values of \(L_N\) in increasing order and put \(r_N:=6m_N+2\).
The index conversion in [C187v25](Yin_C_F0187_v25.md#middle-logarithms), using \(j=r-1\), gives a fixed \(c_1>0\) such that on its middle good event
\[
 a_{r,N}\ge c_1(r/(3N))^{7/2}\qquad(r_N\le r\le3N).
 \tag{Three-increasing-profile}
\]
Consequently, for sufficiently small \(t>0\), a value \(a_{r,N}\le t\) with \(r\ge r_N\) must have \(r\le3N(t/c_1)^{2/7}\).
Thus, for a fixed \(C\),
\[
 \frac1{3N}\#\{r:a_{r,N}\le t\}
 \le\frac{r_N}{3N}+Ct^{2/7}.
 \tag{Three-small-mass}
\]

Apply C186v4's accepted weak convergence to the fixed selfadjoint degree-one matrix polynomial defining \(L_N\), followed by the continuous absolute-value map.
Its empirical singular probability law \(\nu_N\) converges almost surely under each allowed coupling to \(\nu:=\nu_{|\ell|}\).
At each positive continuity point \(t\), the left side of (Three-small-mass) converges in probability to \(\nu([0,t])\).
The middle good event has probability tending to one and \(r_N/N\to0\), so \(\nu([0,t])\le Ct^{2/7}\).
Decreasing continuity points give the same bound for every sufficiently small \(t>0\).
In particular,
\[
 \nu(\{0\})=0,\qquad
 \int_{(0,t)}(-\log x)\,d\nu(x)
 \le Ct^{2/7}(1+|\log t|).
 \tag{Three-free-negative-tail}
\]
For the second bound, integrate the small-mass bound against \(dx/x\), retaining its endpoint term.
The support of \(\nu\) is bounded because \(c,d\) are bounded semicircular elements.
Therefore \(v=\int\log x\,d\nu(x)\) is finite.
This step needs the middle input but not the polynomial floor.

<a id="negative-logarithms"></a>
### Negative logarithmic tails on the original parent

The fixed [C187v25 logarithmic calculation](Yin_C_F0187_v25.md#middle-logarithms) gives, on the same middle good event and simultaneously for sufficiently small \(t>0\),
\[
 \frac1{3N}\sum_{\substack{r\ge r_N\\a_{r,N}<t}}(-\log a_{r,N})
 \le Ct^{2/7}(1+|\log t|).
 \tag{Three-middle-negative-tail}
\]
It sums the upper bound \(-\log c_1+(7/2)\log(3N/r)\) only to the counting cutoff from (Three-increasing-profile).
The adopted floor supplies \(a_{1,N}\ge aN^{-17/2}\) outside a probability tending to zero.
On that event the omitted bottom values satisfy
\[
 \frac1{3N}\sum_{r<r_N}(-\log a_{r,N})_+
 \le\frac{r_N}{3N}\left(\frac{17}{2}\log N+|\log a|\right)
 =O\!\left(N^{-1/6}(\log N)^{7/6}\right)\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{Three-bottom-negative-tail}
\]
This is precisely the floor/middle compatibility already stated in C187v25, now used for the original \(L_N\).
Combining the two events by a union bound proves
\[
 \forall\eta>0:\quad
 \lim_{t\downarrow0}\limsup_{N\to\infty}
 \mathbb P\left\{\frac1{3N}\sum_{a_{r,N}<t}(-\log a_{r,N})>\eta\right\}=0.
 \tag{Three-negative-log-UI}
\]
Zero singular values have infinite negative logarithm in this expression and are excluded only on the floor good event.
No independence between the floor and middle events, summable common exception or almost-sure middle bound is used.

<a id="pencil-probability-log"></a>
### Positive logarithmic tails and the pencil probability limit

The accepted C194v16 parent calculation gives
\[
 \frac{\|L_N\|_F^2}{N}\longrightarrow10
 \quad\text{in probability}.
\]
The limit includes the diagonal-channel contributions \(1+2+3\) and the four identity blocks.
For fixed \(B>10\), an event of probability tending to one therefore satisfies
\[
 \frac1{3N}\sum_{r=1}^{3N}a_{r,N}^2\le B/3.
\]
For \(T>1\), on this event,
\[
 \frac1{3N}\sum_{a_{r,N}>T}\log a_{r,N}
 \le\frac B3\sup_{x>T}\frac{\log x}{x^2}\longrightarrow0
 \quad(T\to\infty).
 \tag{Three-positive-log-UI}
\]
Only an average squared-singular-value bound has been used; neither original input needs a fixed operator-norm bound.
The accepted [positive-tail theorem](Yin_C_F0186_v4.md#positive-tail) also gives this positive-logarithmic conclusion for the same fixed degree-one matrix polynomial under the original finite-second-moment laws.

For fixed \(0<t<1<T\), the function \(x\mapsto\log(\min(T,\max(t,x)))\) is bounded and continuous on \([0,\infty)\).
Weak singular-law convergence gives convergence of its integrals.
The estimates (Three-negative-log-UI), (Three-positive-log-UI) and (Three-free-negative-tail) permit \(t\downarrow0\) and \(T\uparrow\infty\) in probability.
Thus
\[
 X_N:=\frac1{3N}\log|\det L_N|\longrightarrow v
 \quad\text{in probability}.
\]
This is the clipping mechanism of [C187v11](Yin_C_F0187_v11.md#finite-variance-middle-log) and [C186v20](Yin_C_F0186_v20.md#finite-variance-logarithmic-conclusions), with the present profile, bottom count and normalization verified above.

<a id="finite-and-free-determinants"></a>
### Exact finite and bounded free determinant identities

For equally sized blocks \(A,B,E\), the fixed [C194v16 elimination](Yin_C_F0194_v16.md#cubic-inverse-block) swaps the first two block columns, eliminates the first block column, then swaps the last two block columns and eliminates the remaining lower block.
It uses only constant identity pivots and yields
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}A&I&0\\I&B&I\\0&I&E\end{pmatrix}
 \longmapsto
 \begin{pmatrix}I&0&A\\0&I&I-BA\\0&0&EBA-E-A\end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{Three-two-pivots}
\]
No block is inverted.
At finite size the two column-swap signs cancel, while the row factors have determinant one.
Taking \(A=C_N\), \(B=C_N+D_N\) and \(E=C_N+\sqrt2D_N\) gives
\[
 \det L_N=\det P_N.
 \tag{Three-finite-identity}
\]

Apply the same algebra with \(A=c\), \(B=c+d\) and \(E=c+\sqrt2d\) in \(M_3(\mathcal M)\).
The row factors are bounded invertible unitriangular elements and the column swaps are unitaries, all of FK determinant one under the accepted bounded interface.
The final matrix factors exactly as
\[
 \operatorname{diag}(1,1,p)
 \begin{pmatrix}1&0&c\\0&1&1-(c+d)c\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Its second factor is bounded unitriangular, and the normalized auxiliary trace gives
\[
 \Delta_{\tau_3}(\ell)^3=\Delta_\tau(p),\qquad
 \log\Delta_\tau(p)=3\log\Delta_{\tau_3}(\ell).
 \tag{Three-free-identity}
\]
Bounded FK multiplicativity includes zero determinants, so this calculation needs no inverse or prior negative-logarithmic integrability of \(p\).
The already finite \(v\) now gives \(u=3v\in\mathbb R\).
Together with the finite identity,
\[
 Y_N:=\frac1N\log|\det P_N|=3X_N\longrightarrow u
 \quad\text{in probability}.
\]
The ordered cubic, the parameter zero and the factor three are unchanged throughout.
There is no passage from a shifted pencil logarithm to the original cubic.
This is the [exact finite/free linearization normalization](../ledger/Yin_L_F0061_v2.md#linearization-transfer) specialized through the displayed two-pivot calculation.

<a id="every-coupling-limsup"></a>
### Almost-sure upper bounds and every-coupling lower limits

Fix any allowed coupling.
For this exact degree-three scalar polynomial at parameter zero, C186v4 gives, for every fixed \(\delta>0\),
\[
 Y_{N,\delta}:=\frac1{2N}\log\det(P_N^*P_N+\delta^2I_N)
 \longrightarrow
 u_\delta:=\frac12\tau\log(p^*p+\delta^2)
 \quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
Its finite-second-moment positive-tail argument covers the possibly unbounded random-input norms.
Since \(Y_N\le Y_{N,\delta}\), intersection over a countable decreasing sequence \(\delta\downarrow0\), followed by \(u_\delta\downarrow u\), gives
\[
 \limsup_NY_N\le u\quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
This is exactly the [fixed-regularizer upper-bound interface](../ledger/Yin_L_F0061_v2.md#upper-limit).

Probability convergence supplies deterministic increasing dimensions \(N_k\) such that
\[
 \mathbb P\{Y_{N_k}<u-1/k\}\le2^{-k}.
\]
The full within-size entry law is preserved, and the summable marginal probabilities satisfy first Borel--Cantelli under every cross-size coupling.
The sufficiency direction of the accepted [C190v1 criterion](Yin_C_F0190_v1.md#coupling-criterion) therefore gives \(\limsup_NY_N\ge u\) almost surely.
The upper bound proves equality, and the pointwise identity \(Y_N=3X_N\) transfers it to the pencil.
No full-dimensional failure probability has been asserted summable.
All conclusions are for each prescribed coupling, with a permitted coupling-dependent event.
