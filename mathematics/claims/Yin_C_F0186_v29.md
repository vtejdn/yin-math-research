# A summable planar defect bound for structured singular directions

<a id="statement"></a>
## Original model and planar conclusion

Use the real Wigner model of [C196v3](Yin_C_F0196_v3.md#statement), including finite fourth moments off the diagonal and finite second moments on the diagonal.
Write \(M=AB+2BA\) and use the [clipped potential and excess loss](Yin_C_F0186_v27.md#clipping-definition) \(V_N,D_N\) with \(L_N=\exp\sqrt{\log(N+1)}\).
Thus \(D_N\) is the normalized sum of the excess negative logarithms below singular value \(e^{-L_N}\), set to zero on the finite eigenvalue set.
Fix \(a,b>0\), \(0<\gamma\le1\), \(0\le\alpha<1\).
Let \(\mathcal V_N\) consist of the unit complex vectors with at least \(\lceil\gamma N\rceil\) coordinates whose restriction is within Euclidean distance \(a/\sqrt N\) of a vector taking at most \(\lfloor N^\alpha\rfloor\) distinct values.
Define
\[
 F_N(z)=\left\{\min_{h\in\mathcal V_N}\|(M-zI)h\|\le b/\sqrt N\right\},
 \qquad
 J_N^{\rm str}(R)=\int_{|z|\le R}D_N(z)\mathbf1_{F_N(z)}\,dA(z),
 \qquad R>0 .
\]
The class is compact, as follows by taking finite unions over coordinate subsets and label partitions of closed distance sublevel sets for linear equality subspaces, then intersecting with the unit sphere.
It is nonempty, for example it contains the constant unit vector.
The minimum is measurable jointly in the matrix entries and parameter by continuity and compactness.
For every fixed \(R>0\) and threshold \(v>0\) there exist \(c,C>0\) such that, for all sufficiently large \(N\),
\[
 \mathbb P\{J_N^{\rm str}(R)>v\}
 \le C N^{-3}+C e^{-cN^{1/8}} .
 \tag{Structured-planar-deviation}
\]
The integrand counts the entire logarithmic loss at a parameter admitting even one near-mode from the indicated class, regardless of the other singular vectors or the multiplicity.
Consequently the integral converges to zero almost surely under every coupling across sizes.

Put \(J_N^{\rm rem}(R)=\int_{|z|\le R}D_N(z)\mathbf1_{F_N(z)^c}\,dA(z)\).
Every-coupling full-sequence weak Brown convergence for this original model is equivalent to summability of \(\mathbb P\{J_N^{\rm rem}(R)>v\}\) for every integer \(R\ge1\) and every positive rational \(v\).
This latter estimate remains open.

## Simultaneous exclusion off a real strip

Fix \(0<\delta<R\) for the moment.
The [unguarded structured-root proof](Yin_C_F0196_v3.md#argument) gives the final partial-alphabet bound with \(a,b\) replaced by any fixed constants, uniformly for \(|z|\le R+1,\ |\operatorname{Im}z|\ge\delta/2\).
Here uniformity refers initially to the probability bound at each individual parameter.
To verify its constants, the truncation and the exterior-map representation use only the fixed entry laws, and the compensator for a unit near-mode has coefficient bound at most \(C_K(1+3K^2+R+1)\).
In the two- and three-rectangle tests for the real phase, division by \(y\) in the choice of \(a=-\operatorname{Re}p/y\) costs at most \(2/\delta\).
The upper bound on \(\|M_0-xI\|\) is \(3K^2+R+1\), and every multiplication by \(y\) costs at most \(R+1\).
The highest word isolated by each alternating difference has coefficient one for every \(x,y\).
The small fixed input and mesh constants in the two tests can thus be chosen in the same order and uniformly over these parameters.
The resulting common real-phase norm lower bound fixes the fourth rectangle test's coefficient radius and its sparse entropy bound uniformly as well.
The phase spread lower bound, deletion fraction, covariance windows and truncation-law small-ball constants can then be fixed independent of \(z\) in this set.
The finite-dictionary transfer uses only \(3K^2+R+1\) and the common coefficient radius, and the positive-fraction alphabet count does not involve \(z\).

Cover \(\{|z|\le R,\ |\operatorname{Im}z|\ge\delta\}\) by a deterministic net of radius \(N^{-1/2}\) with \(O_R(N)\) points lying in the indicated enlarged set, for example the nearby square mesh of spacing \(N^{-1/2}\).
On replacing \(z\) by a net point \(z'\), the residual for the same unit \(h\) changes in norm by at most \(|z-z'|\).
The estimate with constant \(b+1\) at the net points and a union bound therefore give
\[
 \mathbb P\{\exists\, |z|\le R,\ |\operatorname{Im}z|\ge\delta:\ F_N(z)\}
 \le C_{R,\delta}\exp(-c_{R,\delta}N^{1/8}).
\]
The polynomial net size is absorbed by decreasing the exponential constant.

## A uniform planar envelope on the real strip

Write \(U_N=u_{N,1}\) for the regularized potential at parameter one.
The [regularized-potential theorem](Yin_C_F0186_v4.md#statement) gives almost-sure locally uniform convergence to the deterministic \(u_1\) under every cross-size coupling, since it needs only the second moments.
Consequently one can choose a deterministic \(C_R\) with
\[
 \sum_N\mathbb P\{\sup_{|z|\le R} U_N(z)>C_R\}<\infty.
\]
Indeed choose \(C_R>\sup_{|z|\le R}u_1(z)+1\) and apply the convergence on the independent product coupling of the within-size laws.
Independence and second Borel--Cantelli give the claimed series.
For the explicit rate in (Structured-planar-deviation), the following direct estimate gives \(O_R(N^{-3})\) after increasing \(C_R\).

The crude bound \(\max(\|A\|,\|B\|)\le N^2\) fails with probability \(O(N^{-3})\), by \(\mathbb E\|A\|_{\rm HS}^2+\mathbb E\|B\|_{\rm HS}^2=O(N)\).
At each fixed mesh point of the disk, replace \(\frac12\log(1+s^2)\), as a function of the singular value \(s\) of \(M-zI\), by its upper cap at \(6\log N\).
On the crude norm bound \(s\le3N^4+R\), this cap is eventually inactive throughout the disk.
Changing an independent upper row block of either color alters \(M-zI\) by rank at most four.
The difference of its singular counting functions is at most four by min-max on the kernel of the perturbation, so the normalized capped statistic changes by at most \(24\log N/N\), using monotonicity and its total variation.
Bounded differences for the \(2N\) independent blocks gives a deviation tail \(2\exp(-cN/(\log N)^2)\) at a fixed positive margin from the mean, as in the [row concentration proof](Yin_C_F0186_v4.md#row-concentration).
The means at fixed mesh points are bounded uniformly in \(N\) by the [fixed-parameter regularized moment bound](Yin_C_F0186_v4.md#statement), since the statistic is nonnegative and the upper cap only decreases it.
Use a fixed finite mesh of radius one for the whole disk.
Singular values are \(1\)-Lipschitz under scalar shift and \(s\mapsto\frac12\log(1+s^2)\) has derivative at most \(1/2\) on \([0,\infty)\), so a single constant increase covers all \(z\).
This proves the indicated quantitative upper tail for \(\sup U_N\).

For any matrix and almost every \(z\), the [clipping sandwich](Yin_C_F0186_v27.md#stretched-clipped-potential) gives
\[
 0\le D_N(z)\le U_N(z)+\frac1N\sum_{j=1}^N\log^-|z-\lambda_j(M)|.
\]
Here \(\log^-r=\max(0,-\log r)\), and the second term bounds the negative of the finite eigenvalue potential, including algebraic multiplicities.
For any measurable planar set \(S\) of area \(m\le\pi\) and any \(\lambda\in\mathbb C\), layer integration yields
\[
 \int_S\log^-|z-\lambda|\,dA(z)
 \le\int_0^\infty\min(m,\pi e^{-2t})\,dt
 =\frac m2\bigl(1+\log(\pi/m)\bigr)
\]
with the value zero when \(m=0\).
Thus except on the upper-tail event just estimated, every \(S\subset\{|z|\le R\}\) of such area has
\[
 \int_S D_N(z)\,dA(z)\le C_R m+\frac m2(1+\log(\pi/m)).
\]
This bound holds even when \(S\) depends on the matrices.
The strip \(|\operatorname{Im}z|<\delta\) in the disk has area at most \(4R\delta\).
Choose \(\delta>0\) sufficiently small for its bound to be less than \(v\).
Outside this strip the structured indicator vanishes except on the simultaneous exclusion event above.
Combining the two exceptional probabilities proves (Structured-planar-deviation).

Finally \(J_N^{\rm str}+J_N^{\rm rem}=\int_{|z|\le R}D_N\,dA\), with both terms nonnegative.
The new estimate and a union bound at half the threshold give summability for the full integral if and only if the remaining part satisfies the indicated series.
Apply the [every-coupling equivalence](Yin_C_F0186_v27.md#extreme-planar-defect) to obtain the last assertion.
