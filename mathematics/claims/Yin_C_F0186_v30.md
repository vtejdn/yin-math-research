# Planar loss for structured projections of an actual Wigner graph image

<a id="statement"></a>
## Model and conclusions

Use the original real symmetric Wigner model of [C196v3](Yin_C_F0196_v3.md#statement), including finite fourth moments off the diagonal, finite second moments on the diagonal and arbitrary coupling across sizes.
For \(M=AB+2BA\), write \(h=x+iy\), \(g=Bh\), with \(x,y\in\mathbb R^N\), and fix constants \(\gamma\in(0,1]\) and \(a,b>0\).
The following two events \(G_N^{(j)}(z)\) refer to existence of a complex unit \(h\) with \(\|(M-zI)h\|\le b/\sqrt N\), a coordinate set \(S\) of size at least \(\gamma N\), and one of the indicated graph projections.

1. For \(j=1\), fix \(0\le\alpha_1<1/2\).
   Some real \((a_1,a_2,c_1,c_2)\in\mathbb S^3\) makes
   \[
   \big(a_1x+a_2 y+c_1 Bx+c_2 By\big)_S
   \]
   lie at Euclidean distance at most \(a/\sqrt N\) from an array with at most \(\lfloor N^{\alpha_1}\rfloor\) distinct real coordinate values.
2. For \(j=2\), fix \(0\le\alpha_2<1\).
   Some \((v,w)\in\mathbb C^2\) with \(|v|^2+|w|^2=1\) makes \((v h+w g)_S\) lie at Euclidean distance at most \(a/\sqrt N\) from an array with at most \(\lfloor N^{\alpha_2}\rfloor\) distinct complex coordinate values.

Put \(G_N(z)=G_N^{(1)}(z)\cup G_N^{(2)}(z)\).
All witnesses, including the common projection and the successful sites, can depend on both input matrices.
For \(0<\delta<R\), there are \(c,C>0\) such that for all sufficiently large \(N\)
\[
 \mathbb P\{\exists\, |z|\le R,\ |\operatorname{Im}z|\ge\delta:\ G_N(z)\}
 \le C N^{-3}+C e^{-cN^{1/8}} .
 \tag{Off-axis-graph-projections}
\]
Constants may depend on the fixed parameters and the two entry laws.

Use \(D_N\) and the deeply clipped scale from [C186v27](Yin_C_F0186_v27.md#clipping-definition).
For every \(R,t>0\), one has
\[
 \mathbb P\left\{\int_{|z|\le R} D_N(z)\mathbf1_{G_N(z)}\,dA(z)>t\right\}
 \le C N^{-3}+C e^{-cN^{1/8}} .
 \tag{Graph-projection-planar-deviation}
\]
The entire loss at a parameter is counted whenever any admissible witness exists.
This implies the same estimate with indicator \(\mathbf1_{G_N(z)\cap F_N(z)^c}\), where \(F_N\) is the structured-root event of [C186v29](Yin_C_F0186_v29.md#statement).
In particular, with the old parameters chosen as \(\alpha_2,\gamma,a,b\), one has \(F_N(z)\subset G_N^{(2)}(z)\) by taking \(v=1,w=0\).
The integral on \(G_N\) tends to zero almost surely under every cross-size coupling.
The full every-coupling Brown assertion is equivalent to summable deviations of the remaining nonnegative integral on \(G_N^c\), on all integer disks and at all positive rational thresholds.
The required series on that complement remains open.

## Singular dimensions of the maps to selected sites

Treat the unit sphere of \(\mathbb C^N\) as a real sphere in dimension \(2N\).
For case \(j\) the output has \(d_j=j\) real components per coordinate.
On all sites let \(T_p\) be the real linear map specified above for the normalized parameter \(p\), using realification also in the complex-output case.
For \(j=1\), in a real orthonormal eigenbasis for \(B\), the eigenvalues of \(T_p T_p^*\) are
\[
   (a_1+c_1\lambda)^2+(a_2+c_2\lambda)^2
      =|d+e\lambda|^2,\qquad d=a_1+ia_2,\quad e=c_1+ic_2.
\]
For \(j=2\), they are \(|d+e\lambda|^2\) with \(d=v,e=w\), each repeated twice.
Indeed a complex multiplier \(d+e\lambda\) acts on its real plane by a scaled orthogonal map when nonzero, with the same identity for its Gram matrix when zero.
In both cases \(|d|^2+|e|^2=1\).

Choose an arbitrarily small constant \(\mu>0\).
There is a single event measurable in \(B\), with failure \(Ce^{-cN}\), on which the spectrum of \(B\) has at most \(\mu N/2\) eigenvalues outside \([-3,3]\), and every interval of sufficiently short fixed length \(2u>0\) meeting \([-3,3]\) contains at most \(\mu N/2\) eigenvalues.
To prove this, cover the shorter intervals by a fixed finite grid of intervals of length \(4u\) on a slightly larger compact interval, choosing \(u\) small enough that each grid interval has semicircle mass less than \(\mu/8\).
The two tails outside \([-3,3]\) have limiting mass zero.
Their expected empirical masses and those of the grid intervals converge by the [weak semicircle input](Yin_C_F0186_v4.md#finite-variance-weak).
Changing one independent upper-row block of \(B\) costs at most two in either cumulative count, or four in an interval count.
Applying [bounded differences](Yin_C_F0186_v4.md#row-concentration) to the fixed finite list, with a sufficiently small positive margin for each tail, proves the assertion.

If \(|e|\le1/12\), the quantity \(|d+e\lambda|\) exceeds \(1/2\) for \(|\lambda|\le3\).
For \(|e|>1/12\), all such real \(\lambda\) with \(|d+e\lambda|<\tau\) lie inside the interval of radius \(12\tau\) about \(\operatorname{Re}(-d/e)\).
Fix \(\tau>0\) with \(12\tau<u\) and \(\tau<1/2\).
On the single spectral event, \(T_pT_p^*\) has at most \(d_j\mu N\) eigenvalues below \(\tau^2\), simultaneously in the full parameter spheres.
Compressing this positive matrix to the output sites \(S\), leaving all input coordinates free, gives the left Gram matrix of \(T_{p,S}\).
By min-max, the number of its eigenvalues below \(\tau^2\), including any zero eigenvalues, is again at most \(d_j\mu N\).
Thus for \(|S|=s\) the input subspace of right singular directions of \(T_{p,S}\) at singular values at least \(\tau\) has real dimension at least \(d_j(s-\mu N)\).
This is a count for the full input space and imposes no independence assumption on the chosen sites.

## A dictionary chosen before sampling the other color

Add the event \(\|B\|\le N^2\), whose failure is \(O(N^{-3})\) by \(\mathbb E\|B\|_{\rm HS}^2=O(N)\).
Fix \(j\), and reduce each witness \(S\) to exactly \(s=\lceil\gamma N\rceil\) sites.
Take a deterministic \(N^{-3}\)-net of the unit parameter sphere of cardinality \(N^{O(1)}\).
In operator norm the maps on that parameter sphere are Lipschitz with constant \(C(1+\|B\|)\), by the displayed linear formulas.
Consequently replacing \(p\) by a net point \(p'\) increases the image approximation error by \(O(N^{-1})\) on the event just imposed.

Put \(k=\lfloor N^{\alpha_j}\rfloor\).
The used alphabet values have modulus at most \(C(1+N^2)+a\) by the unit input norm and the assumed error.
Round these values on a mesh with spacing \(N^{-1}\) in each of their \(d_j\) real components.
This changes the total Euclidean error by \(O(\sqrt{s}/N)=O(N^{-1/2})\).
Enumerate the rounded lists of length \(k\), padding as necessary, and all their labelings on \(S\).
This costs at most \(N^{O(k)}k^s\) choices, since the box has polynomial side length.
In particular there is a rounded target \(u\) for which \(\|T_{p',S}h-u\|\le C_a/\sqrt N\).

For the fixed \(B,p',S\), let \(H\) be the right singular subspace at singular values at least \(\tau\), and let \(Q\) be the inverse of the map on this subspace followed in input notation by its embedding into \(\mathbb R^{2N}\), taking the inverse only after projecting the data onto \(T_{p',S}H\).
Equivalently \(Q T_{p',S}\) is the orthogonal projection onto \(H\), and \(\|Q\|\le1/\tau\).
For the rounded \(u\), the projected root has distance at most \(C_a/(\tau\sqrt N)\) from \(Qu\).
Independently cover the unit ball of \(H^\perp\), of dimension at most \(2N-d_j(s-\mu N)\), at radius \(N^{-1/2}\) with at most \((1+2\sqrt N)^{2N-d_j(s-\mu N)}\) points.
Add each such point to \(Qu\) and normalize every nonzero resulting vector.
Some candidate before normalization is within \((1+C_a/\tau)/\sqrt N\) of the true unit root, hence is nonzero eventually, and normalizing it increases this distance bound by at most a factor two.
The truncated inverse and projections are Borel matrix functions, even at multiplicities, using the fixed spectral cutoff.
A measurable basis of \(H^\perp\) can be obtained by ordered Gram--Schmidt on the projections of the standard basis, with zero pivots omitted, so its covering points can be selected measurably using fixed nets in each dimension.

There are at most \(2^N\) choices of \(S\).
Combining them with the parameter grid, the labels, the alphabet lists and the remaining unit-ball nets gives a \(B\)-measurable dictionary of unit roots with logarithm to base \(N\) at most
\[
 \left[1-\gamma(d_j/2-\alpha_j)+d_j\mu/2+o(1)\right]N .
\]
Here \(k=o(N)\) and all constants are fixed.
Choose \(\mu\) so small that the displayed coefficient is eventually at most \(1-\chi\) for some fixed \(\chi>0\).
The construction is made on the favorable \(B\) event, and an arbitrary singleton dictionary is used off it.
This bounds the dictionary size in every background.
The actual witness \(h\), the actual \(g=Bh\), and the projection selecting the condition can depend on both matrices, while the enumerated dictionary depends on \(B\) alone.

Apply the [original-law dictionary theorem](Yin_C_F0196_v3.md#statement) with this entropy saving and the fixed enlarged root-approximation constant.
As verified in [C186v29](Yin_C_F0186_v29.md#argument), the constants of its dictionary proof are uniform for \(|z|\le R+1\) and \(|\operatorname{Im}z|\ge\delta/2\).
Use a deterministic \(N^{-1/2}\)-net of at most \(O_R(N)\) such points on the target compact set, enlarging the residual constant from \(b\) to \(b+1\) by the unit root norm.
Add the common spectral and norm exceptions once, and sum the dictionary bound over this grid and the two output types to get (Off-axis-graph-projections).

## Planar consequence and scope

The indicators are jointly measurable in matrices and \(z\).
For each fixed \(S\) and partition into at most \(k\) label classes, the test is a distance to the closed linear subspace of arrays constant within each class.
The graph map is continuous in the parameters, matrices and unit root.
The parameter and root spheres are compact and the choice of sites and partitions is finite, making the existence event closed under limits for each fixed \(N\).
The same reasoning gives measurability of the compact off-axis existence event.

The [planar envelope in C186v29](Yin_C_F0186_v29.md#argument) supplies a deterministic constant \(C_R\) except on an event of probability \(O_R(N^{-3})\).
On its good event, every measurable set in the disk of area \(m\le\pi\) has total \(D_N\) integral at most \(C_Rm+\frac m2(1+\log(\pi/m))\), continuously interpreted at zero.
Given the fixed threshold, choose \(\delta>0\) so small that the real strip of area at most \(4R\delta\) contributes less than that threshold.
At every other point the structured indicator vanishes on the good event in (Off-axis-graph-projections).
This proves (Graph-projection-planar-deviation).
The integral is nonnegative, so first Borel--Cantelli gives the stated almost-sure assertion under arbitrary coupling.
Summability for the complement is equivalent to summability for the whole integral by positivity and splitting the threshold in half.
The [full defect criterion](Yin_C_F0186_v27.md#extreme-planar-defect) gives the equivalence to the Brown assertion.

The singular-dimension bound here concerns maps on the full input space with \(\gamma N\) imposed site equations.
It does not count the small singular directions of the joint map restricted to an exterior near-kernel, or test an unrestricted actual inside target against its own original row entries.
