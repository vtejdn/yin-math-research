# Lower comparison with the exterior logarithmic potential for a regular component

<a id="statement"></a>
## One mesoscopic exterior compression

Use the [finite fourth-moment real two-color Wigner model](Yin_C_F0186_v26.md#statement) and write \(M=AB+2BA\).
Assume the raw strict upper law of \(A\) has a fixed component of positive mass \(p\) whose probability law \(\nu\) satisfies the [second logarithmic concentration hypothesis](Yin_C_F0186_v32.md#statement).
Outside that component the law can have atoms.
The off-diagonal law of \(B\) and the diagonal laws may have atoms.
Choose any deterministic coordinate sets \(I\) of even size \(2\le k\le\delta N\) with \(k\log N=o(N)\), where \(\delta\) is the [cross-block constant](Yin_C_F0196_v4.md#statement).
If \(p<1\) require in addition \(k/\log N\to\infty\), for instance an even integer asymptotic to \((\log N)^2\).
Write \(J=I^c\), \(V=B_{JI}\), \(U=A_{II}\) and \(m=N-k\).
On the event \(\mathcal R=\{\operatorname{rank}V=k\}\) put \(H=\ker V^*\subset\mathbb C^m\) and, writing \(\Pi_H\) for orthogonal projection, set
\[
 M_J=A_{JJ}B_{JJ}+2B_{JJ}A_{JJ},\qquad C=\Pi_H M_J|_H .
\]
The entries in \(V\) are independent of both minor matrices.
Embed \(H\) in \(\mathbb C^N\) by extending vectors by zero on \(I\).
For a fixed bounded nonnegative Borel \(g\) with compact support define
\[
 Z_N(g)=\frac1N\int g(z)\log|\det(M-zI)|\,dA(z),\qquad
 Z_C(g)=\frac1N\int g(z)\log|\det(C-zI_H)|\,dA(z).
\]
On rank failure assign \(Z_C=0\).
The normalization of the compression is by the original \(N\), and no expectation of the inverse cross-block Gram matrix is assumed.

On the full rank event the disk average of the negative part of the logarithm of the D42 factor, divided by \(N\), converges completely to zero for each fixed disk, including on arbitrary jointly measurable parameter restrictions.
For every such \(g\) and \(v>0\), the probabilities
\[
 \Pr\{Z_N(g)<Z_C(g)-v\}
 \tag{Potential-transfer}
\]
are summable.
For \(g=\mathbf1_{D_R}\), \(D_R=\{|z|<R\}\), set \(u_*=\tau\log|s_1s_2+2s_2s_1-z|\) as a function of \(z\).
A sufficient condition for almost-sure weak Brown convergence along the full sequence under every cross-size coupling is
\[
 \sum_N\Pr\left\{Z_C(\mathbf1_{D_R})<\int_{D_R}u_*(z)\,dA(z)-v\right\}<\infty
 \qquad (R\in\mathbb N,\ v\in\mathbb Q_{>0}).
 \tag{Exterior-potential-criterion}
\]
In particular full Brown convergence for this class is still conditional on the displayed exterior estimates.
The lower comparison uses nonnegative test functions and controls the loss from the realized interior at the normalization of the original determinant.

## A negative logarithm estimate for a matching

Split \(I\) into equal halves of size \(l=k/2\).
If \(p=1\) fix a pairing between the halves.
Otherwise reveal the independent mixture labels of the edges of the cross rectangle and select the first perfect matching of regular edges in a deterministic ordering.
By the [matching estimate](Yin_C_F0194_v36.md#argument) the failure probability is at most \(Ce^{-ck}\), summable at the specified sizes.
Conditional on these labels and the existence of the matching, its raw values are independent with common law \(\nu\).
Write \(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_l\) for the raw variables at its distinct unordered edges.
Reveal every other entry and the exterior target \(W(z)\) of [D42](../drafts/Yin_D_F0042.md#repeated-interior).
For fixed \(z\) outside its finite pole set, \(P=\det(\operatorname{diag}(U,U)-W(z))\) has individual degree at most four in each matched raw variable and coefficient \(N^{-k}\) at \(\prod_i \xi_i^4\), by the [coefficient computation](Yin_C_F0194_v36.md#argument).
Let \(P_0=P\), \(P_i=[\xi_i^4]P_{i-1}\), so \(P_l=N^{-k}\).
For fixed remaining real inputs \(P_{i-1}\) has exact degree four with leading coefficient \(P_i\) whenever \(P_i\ne0\).
Starting from \(P_l\ne0\), each \(P_i\ne0\) almost surely at the given parameter by atomlessness of \(\nu\).
On these sections \(P_{i-1}/P_i\) is monic and has four complex roots counted with multiplicity.
Consequently, for independent fresh \(\xi_i\) its logarithm has the following uniform one-sided bound,
\[
 \Pr\{Y_i(z)>t\mid\text{unmatched entries and }\xi_{i+1},\dots,\xi_l\}
 \le 4Q_\nu(e^{-t/4}),\qquad
 Y_i(z)=\log^-\left|P_{i-1}/P_i\right|.
\]
Indeed if the product of four distances is below \(e^{-t}\), some real draw lies within \(e^{-t/4}\) of the real part of a root.
Values on null denominator sections can be set to zero.
All ratios are measurable directly from polynomial coefficients.
After reversing the order of the \(l\) draws, their prior sigma fields contain every variable in the displayed conditioning.
This argument applies to almost every parameter under the conditional laws by Fubini.

For a fixed disk \(D_R\) and each \(i\) put \(Y_i=|D_R|^{-1}\int_{D_R}Y_i(z)\,dA(z)\).
The conditional second moment of \(Y_i\) is bounded by a fixed constant by Jensen and the displayed tail under the second logarithmic concentration assumption.
For \(b\ge1\), the conditional stop-loss bound \(\mathbb E[(Y_i-b)_+\mid\text{past}]\le \int_b^\infty 4Q_\nu(e^{-t/4})\,dt\) also follows by Jensen under the same disk measure.
Thus \(\sum_i\mathbb E[Y_i\mid\text{past}]=O(l)=o(N)\) deterministically given the unmatched entries.
For the centered increments \(H_i=Y_i-\mathbb E[Y_i\mid\text{past}]\) the second moment is uniformly bounded and, for all sufficiently large \(b\), the conditional probability that \(|H_i|>b\) is at most \(C b^{-1}\int_{b/2}^\infty Q_\nu(e^{-t/4})\,dt\), by nonnegativity and the same stop-loss inequality.
Apply the [truncated martingale argument](Yin_C_F0186_v32.md#argument) with \(m=l\le N\), constant conditional variance bound in place of its \(O(\log^2 N)\) bound, and displacement \(xN\) for any fixed \(x>0\).
The large increments have summable union probabilities since \(\int_1^\infty t Q_\nu(e^{-t/4})\,dt<\infty\).
The bounded increments have summable probabilities by the explicit exponential moment computation there.
These bounds are uniform in the unmatched entries and the exterior.
Since pointwise \(\log^-|P|\le k\log N+\sum_i Y_i(z)\), it follows on \(\mathcal R\) that
\[
 N^{-1}\int_{D_R}\log^-|P(W(z))|\,dA(z)
\]
converges completely to zero for \(k\log N=o(N)\).
Here complete convergence to zero means summability of the fixed positive threshold exceedance probabilities.

## Lower comparison for the compression and finite determinants

Identify the two versions of the exterior compression as follows.
Writing \(h_I=0\) shows that the other defining condition of the kernel of the [exterior observation map](Yin_C_F0196_v4.md#statement) is \(V^*h_J=0\).
The \(JJ\) block of \(M\) is \(M_J+A_{JI}V^*+2V A_{IJ}\), so its compression to \(H\) is exactly \(C\).
The [conditional determinant factorization](../drafts/Yin_D_F0042.md#repeated-interior) therefore gives
\[
  \log|\det(M-z)|-\log|\det(C-z)|
    =\log(2^k\det(V^*V))+\log|P(W(z))|.
 \tag{Residual-log}
\]
On the [original norm and cross-floor event](Yin_C_F0196_v4.md#argument), with summable exception, the first term is \(O(k\log N)\) in absolute value.
The negative part of the difference on the left thus has disk integral \(o(N)\) in complete convergence.
For each bounded nonnegative test \(g\) supported in that disk this proves (Potential-transfer), charging the cross-block and matching exceptions once.
There is no assertion about the positive part of (Residual-log).

For the disk indicator, the [clipped planar comparison](Yin_C_F0186_v28.md#clipped-planar-defect) and the [global clipped mean convergence](Yin_C_F0186_v27.md#statement) give summable upper deviations of \(\int_{D_R} V_N\) above \(\int_{D_R}u_*\).
Subtract \(Z_N(\mathbf1_{D_R})\), whose lower deviations are summable under the exterior hypothesis by (Potential-transfer).
Splitting a positive threshold into three parts gives summable deviations of \(\int_{D_R}D_N\).
The [exact planar criterion](Yin_C_F0186_v27.md#statement) now gives the Brown conclusion under the displayed sufficient condition.
The proof leaves this lower tail of the exterior compression unresolved for the full class.
