# A fixed-center joint residual gap for finite-variance Wigner inputs

This version extends the [joint approximate-input gap in C187v5](Yin_C_F0187_v5.md#joint-gap-proof) to the original real finite-second-moment laws.
A projection adapted to a fixed-cutoff tail replaces the full operator-norm guard in the small-spectral-subspace argument.
The projection both removes the tail's large spectral directions and annihilates its action on the selected first-input subspace.
The complete fixed proof has [independent review](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_F0191_v12.md#finite-variance-joint-gap-reviewed), selected in the proved assessment below.
No novelty claim is made.

<a id="statement"></a>
## Exact model and conclusion

Let \(C_N,D_N\) be independent real symmetric Wigner matrices.
For input \(a=1,2\), the upper off-diagonal coordinates are independent copies of \(\xi_a/\sqrt N\), where \(\xi_a\) is fixed, real, centered and has variance one.
The diagonal coordinates are independent copies of \(\zeta_a/\sqrt N\), where \(\zeta_a\) is fixed, real, centered and has finite second moment.
All upper-triangular coordinates and both inputs are independent within each size.
There is no moment assumption above two, and dependence across sizes is arbitrary.
For \(S\subset[N]\), \(|S|\le1\), put \(n:=N-|S|\) and let \(C_{N,S},D_{N,S}\) be the corresponding principal minors, retaining the original \(N^{-1/2}\) normalization.
For a fixed \(R_0>0\), define
\[
 \Gamma_{R_0}(C,D):=
 \inf_{\substack{z\in\mathbb R^n,\ \|z\|=1\\
                  a,b\in[-R_0,R_0]}}
 \max\{\|(C-aI_n)z\|,\|(D-bI_n)z\|\}.
\]
There exists a fixed \(\epsilon_{R_0}>0\), depending only on \(R_0\) and the two fixed laws, such that
\[
 \mathbb P\left\{
 \min_{\substack{S\subset[N]\\|S|\le1}}
       \Gamma_{R_0}(C_{N,S},D_{N,S})<\epsilon_{R_0}
 \right\}\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{Finite-variance-joint-gap}
\]
More precisely, the proof supplies two common events with probabilities of failure
\(p_{C,N}\to0\) and \(p_{T,N}\to0\), and fixed \(c,C>0\), for which the displayed probability is eventually at most
\[
 p_{C,N}+p_{T,N}+Ce^{-cN}.
 \tag{Joint-gap-exceptions}
\]
Each common exception is counted once, rather than once per principal deletion.
No summability of these two sequences is asserted.

This statement concerns a fixed bounded range of the two scalar centers.
It does not give the auxiliary Gram conclusion of C187v5, a coefficient-energy lower bound, or a least singular-value floor for the two-channel pencil.
In particular, the [flat two-mode slope calculation](../drafts/Yin_D_F0033.md#flat-two-mode-slope-test) concerns a different boundary involving growing input directions and does not become excluded merely by the fixed-center conclusion here.
The original [F0013](../tasks/Yin_T_F0013_v1.md#contract) and [F0014](../tasks/Yin_T_F0014_v1.md#contract) obligations are not completed.

## Inputs and retained source boundaries

The probabilistic inputs are the accepted [bounded-product convex concentration inequality (9)](Yin_C_F0187_v2.md#argument) and the ordinary independent-block bounded-differences inequality recorded in [C186v4](Yin_C_F0186_v4.md#argument).
The latter input retains its inspected War16 Theorem 1 scope; no typical-good-set variant is invoked.
The former retains its recorded Götze--Naumov--Tikhomirov source-inspection boundary.
Neither external concentration proof is audited here.
The [finite-variance scalar weak spectral convergence](Yin_C_F0186_v4.md#row-concentration) supplies the first-input semicircle law.
Its limiting density is bounded and continuous at every interval endpoint used below.
The elementary quadratic-net argument in [C187v4](Yin_C_F0187_v4.md#spectral-residual-spread) motivates the conditional bounded-matrix estimate, whose proof is given explicitly for independent but possibly nonidentically distributed coordinates and missing edges.
The old bounded-law gap proof supplies the argument's spectral-subspace organization, not an unbounded-law theorem being invoked without verification.

## Complete proof

<a id="conditional-tail-split"></a>
### A tail measurable before the remaining bounded coordinates

Fix \(\delta:=1/32\).
For a cutoff \(L>0\) with \(\mathbb P\{|\xi_2|\le L\}>0\), write
\[
 p_L:=\mathbb P\{|\xi_2|\le L\},\qquad
 m_L:=\mathbb E[\xi_2\mid|\xi_2|\le L],\qquad
 \eta_L:=\xi_2\mathbf1_{\{|\xi_2|>L\}}
                 +m_L\mathbf1_{\{|\xi_2|\le L\}}.
\]
As \(L\to\infty\), one has \(p_L\to1\), \(m_L\to0\), and
\[
 \mathbb E\eta_L=0,\qquad
 e_L:=\mathbb E\eta_L^2
 =\mathbb E[\xi_2^2\mathbf1_{\{|\xi_2|>L\}}]+p_Lm_L^2
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
Also \(\operatorname{Var}(\xi_2\mid|\xi_2|\le L)\to1\).
Choose one fixed \(L\) sufficiently large that
\[
 e_L<\delta/2,\qquad
 \mathbb P\{|\xi_2|>L\}<\delta/(2e),\qquad
 \operatorname{Var}(\xi_2\mid|\xi_2|\le L)\ge1/2.
 \tag{Fixed-cutoff-choice}
\]

Decompose the full second input as \(D_N=T_N+W_N\).
On an off-diagonal edge with raw coordinate \(\xi_{2,ij}\), the raw coordinate of \(T_N\) is \(\eta_{L,ij}\).
The raw coordinate of \(W_N\) is \(\xi_{2,ij}-m_L\) on the good event \(|\xi_{2,ij}|\le L\), and zero on its complement.
Put every original second-input diagonal in \(T_N\) and set the diagonal of \(W_N\) to zero.
Every normalized entry still has the original factor \(N^{-1/2}\).

Let \(\mathcal A_N\) be the sigma-field generated by all cutoff indicators, all raw values on bad edges, and all second-input diagonals.
Then \(T_N\) is \(\mathcal A_N\)-measurable.
Conditional on \(\mathcal A_N\), the remaining good coordinates of \(W_N\) are independent, centered conditional truncated laws, with variance at least \(1/2\) and support diameter at most \(2L\).
Bad coordinates and all diagonal coordinates of \(W_N\) are deterministic zero.
The conditional coordinates need not be identically distributed across the whole upper triangle.

Define the common tail event and its error by
\[
 \Omega_{T,N}:=\{\|T_N\|_{\rm F}^2\le\delta N\},
 \qquad p_{T,N}:=\mathbb P(\Omega_{T,N}^c).
\]
The finite-mean weak law applied to the independent copies of \(\eta_L^2\) gives
\[
 \frac{\|T_N\|_{\rm F}^2}{N}
 =\frac2{N^2}\sum_{i<j}\eta_{L,ij}^2
       +\frac1{N^2}\sum_i\zeta_{2,i}^2
 \longrightarrow e_L
 \quad\hbox{in probability}.
\]
The diagonal term tends to zero by Markov.
The off-diagonal weak law requires only integrability and follows by a fixed truncation, the bounded-variable variance estimate, and Markov for the discarded part.
Thus \(p_{T,N}\to0\).
Every principal minor \(T_{N,S}\) has no larger Frobenius norm.

Let \(\deg_{\rm bad}(i)\) count bad off-diagonal edges incident to \(i\), and put
\[
 \Omega_{{\rm mask},N}:=
 \{\max_i\deg_{\rm bad}(i)\le\delta N\}.
\]
For each \(i\), exponential Markov at parameter one and (Fixed-cutoff-choice) give
\[
 \mathbb P\{\deg_{\rm bad}(i)>\delta N\}
 \le\exp[-\delta N+(e-1)N\mathbb P\{|\xi_2|>L\}]
 \le e^{-\delta N/2}.
\]
A union over the \(N\) vertices therefore yields
\[
 \mathbb P(\Omega_{{\rm mask},N}^c)\le Ce^{-cN}.
 \tag{Common-mask-error}
\]
The degree bound is inherited by every principal deletion.

<a id="conditional-bounded-norm"></a>
### A conditional norm estimate with missing and nonidentical coordinates

For any deterministic real unit vector \(v\in\mathbb R^N\), conditional centering gives
\(\mathbb E[v^TW_Nv\mid\mathcal A_N]=0\).
Changing one good raw upper off-diagonal coordinate changes the quadratic form by at most
\[
 \frac{4L}{\sqrt N}|v_i v_j|.
\]
Thus the sum of squared replacement bounds is at most
\[
 \frac{16L^2}{N}\sum_{i<j}v_i^2v_j^2\le\frac{8L^2}{N}.
\]
Missing edges contribute zero.
The accepted ordinary bounded-differences inequality, applied conditionally, gives
\[
 \mathbb P\{|v^TW_Nv|>t\mid\mathcal A_N\}
 \le2\exp[-Nt^2/(4L^2)].
\]
A deterministic \(1/4\)-net of the real unit sphere has at most \(9^N\) points.
For a real symmetric matrix,
\(\|W_N\|\le2\max_v|v^TW_Nv|\) over that net.
Choose a fixed \(B_L\ge1\) sufficiently large.
The union bound then gives, uniformly in the realized tail data,
\[
 \mathbb P\{\|W_N\|>B_L\mid\mathcal A_N\}
 \le Ce^{-cN}.
 \tag{Conditional-bounded-norm}
\]
This is a conditional calculation for the actual missing-edge matrix, not an application of an identically distributed variance-one Wigner norm theorem.
The same full event bounds every principal minor of \(W_N\).

<a id="tail-adapted-projection"></a>
### An adapted projection and its uniform variance

Fix \(S\subset[N]\), \(|S|\le1\), and abbreviate
\(T:=T_{N,S}\), \(W:=W_{N,S}\), \(D:=D_{N,S}\), \(n:=N-|S|\).
Fix a real subspace \(V\subset\mathbb R^n\) of dimension at most \(\kappa n\), where \(0<\kappa\le1/32\) will be chosen later.
The subspace will be measurable with respect to the first input.
Condition on the full first input and \(\mathcal A_N\), so that \(T\) and \(V\) are fixed and the remaining second-input coordinates retain the conditional independent laws above.

On \(\Omega_{T,N}\), define
\[
 P_0:=\mathbf1_{[-1,1]}(T).
\]
There are at most \(\delta N\) eigenvalues of \(T\) outside this interval, since their squared absolute values exceed one.
Let \(P\) be the orthogonal projection onto
\[
 \operatorname{Ran}P_0\cap(TV+V)^\perp.
\]
This gives the deterministic relations
\[
 \operatorname{codim}P\le\delta N+2\dim V,\qquad
 P\preceq P_0,\qquad \|PT\|\le1,\qquad PV=PTV=0.
 \tag{Adapted-projection-properties}
\]
Indeed \(P=PP_0\), and \(\|P_0T\|\le1\) by spectral calculus.
For every \(z\in V\) and every real \(b\),
\[
 P(D-bI_n)z=PWz.
 \tag{Tail-and-center-annihilation}
\]

Here is a variance estimate that remains valid although \(P\) depends on the mask and bad values.
For any real unit \(z\in\mathbb R^n\), put
\[
 g_{ij}:=z_je_i+z_ie_j,\qquad 1\le i<j\le n.
\]
Direct entrywise calculation gives
\[
 \sum_{i<j}g_{ij}g_{ij}^T
 =I_n+zz^T-2\operatorname{diag}(z_1^2,\ldots,z_n^2).
\]
Consequently
\[
 \sum_{i<j}\|Pg_{ij}\|^2
 \ge\operatorname{rank}P-2.
\]
On \(\Omega_{{\rm mask},N}\), the deleted bad edges contribute at most
\[
 \sum_{\rm bad}\|Pg_{ij}\|^2
 \le\sum_{\rm bad}\|g_{ij}\|^2
 =\sum_i\deg_{\rm bad}(i)z_i^2\le\delta N.
\]
Conditional independence and zero means eliminate cross terms, and each good raw variance is at least \(1/2\).
It follows that, on the two common guards,
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathbb E[\|PWz\|^2\mid C_N,\mathcal A_N]
 &\ge\frac{\operatorname{rank}P-2-\delta N}{2N}\\
 &\ge\frac{n-2\kappa n-2\delta N-2}{2N}
 \ge\frac14
 \end{split}
 \tag{Adapted-projected-variance}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(N\).
This estimate is deterministic in the conditioned projection and is uniform over all unit \(z\); no independence of \(P\) from the cutoff pattern is asserted or needed.

As a function of the remaining raw good coordinates, \(F_z:=\|PWz\|\) is convex and \(\sqrt{2/N}\)-Lipschitz.
The Lipschitz estimate follows from
\(\|(W-W')z\|\le\|W-W'\|_{\rm F}\) and the two symmetric copies of each changed edge.
The accepted bounded-product inequality therefore gives
\[
 \mathbb P\{|F_z-\operatorname{med}F_z|>t
                         \mid C_N,\mathcal A_N\}
 \le C\exp(-cNt^2/L^2),\qquad
 \mathbb E|F_z-\operatorname{med}F_z|^2\le C_L/N.
\]
Together with (Adapted-projected-variance), the \(L^2\) triangle inequality implies
\(\operatorname{med}F_z\ge1/2-C_L^{1/2}/\sqrt N\).
With \(\gamma:=1/4\), it follows eventually that
\[
 \mathbb P\{\|PWz\|<\gamma\mid C_N,\mathcal A_N\}
 \le C e^{-c_LN}.
 \tag{Adapted-fixed-vector-gap}
\]

<a id="small-subspace-gap"></a>
### A fixed net and a bounded compressed transfer

Choose
\[
 \theta:=\min(1/2,\gamma/(8B_L)),
\]
and then fix \(0<\kappa\le1/32\) so small that
\(\kappa\log(3/\theta)<c_L/4\).
For any such \(V\), a \(\theta\)-net of its unit sphere has at most
\((3/\theta)^{\kappa n}\) points.
The net can be chosen after conditioning on the first input.
Using (Adapted-fixed-vector-gap) at these points and (Conditional-bounded-norm), then extending by
\(\|PW(z-z')\|\le B_L\|z-z'\|\), gives
\[
 \inf_{\substack{z\in V\\\|z\|=1}}\|PWz\|\ge\gamma/2
 \tag{Adapted-small-subspace-gap}
\]
outside conditional probability \(Ce^{-cN}\), on the two common guards.
If \(V=\{0\}\), this infimum is \(+\infty\) and the assertion is automatic.
By (Tail-and-center-annihilation), the same lower bound holds for
\(\|P(D-bI_n)z\|\) simultaneously for every real \(b\).
No net for the second scalar center is required.

On the same conditional norm event, the operator on the entire ambient space satisfies
\[
 \|P(D-bI_n)\|
 \le\|PT\|+\|PW\|+|b|
 \le1+B_L+|b|.
 \tag{Compressed-transfer-bound}
\]
It is this bound, rather than a bound on \(\|D\|\), that controls approximation by vectors in \(V\).
The projection is allowed to depend on the selected \(V\), so both its annihilation and transfer properties are retained for that subspace.

<a id="spectral-windows-and-joint-gap"></a>
### First-input windows, all single deletions and the joint gap

Choose a fixed \(h>0\) sufficiently small, and a finite \(h\)-mesh grid
\(\{a_j\}_{j=1}^J\) covering \([-R_0,R_0]\), such that every interval
\([a_j-2h,a_j+2h]\) has semicircle mass at most \(\kappa/4\).
This is possible because the semicircle density is bounded.
Let \(\Omega_{C,N}\) be the event that the full first input has at most \(\kappa N/2\) eigenvalues in each of these finitely many intervals, and put
\[
 p_{C,N}:=\mathbb P(\Omega_{C,N}^c).
\]
The accepted finite-variance scalar weak convergence gives \(p_{C,N}\to0\).
Every endpoint is a continuity point of the semicircle law.
Principal interlacing changes an interval count by at most two after one coordinate deletion.
Hence, on \(\Omega_{C,N}\), eventually all the subspaces
\[
 V_{S,j}:=\operatorname{Ran}
   \mathbf1_{[a_j-2h,a_j+2h]}(C_{N,S}),
 \qquad |S|\le1,
\]
have dimension at most \(\kappa(N-|S|)\).

Condition on the full first input and \(\mathcal A_N\).
On \(\Omega_{C,N}\cap\Omega_{T,N}\cap\Omega_{{\rm mask},N}\), apply the preceding adapted construction to each \(V_{S,j}\).
A union over the \(J(N+1)\) choices still has conditional error at most \(Ce^{-cN}\), after decreasing \(c\).
The full bounded-\(W_N\) norm event can be included once; alternatively its repeated exponentially small upper bound gives the same estimate.
Integrating the conditional estimates and adding the two common errors once, along with (Common-mask-error), gives total exception at most
\[
 p_{C,N}+p_{T,N}+Ce^{-cN}.
\]

On the resulting event, suppose that for some \(S\), some real unit \(z\), and some \(a,b\in[-R_0,R_0]\), both residuals are at most \(\epsilon\).
Choose \(a_j\) with \(|a-a_j|\le h\), and let \(Q\) be the orthogonal projection onto \(V_{S,j}\).
Spectral calculus gives
\[
 \|(I_n-Q)z\|\le\epsilon/h.
\]
If \(\epsilon<h/4\), the unit vector \(z':=Qz/\|Qz\|\) exists and satisfies
\(\|z-z'\|\le2\epsilon/h\).
For the adapted projection \(P\) belonging to this \(S,j\), contraction and (Compressed-transfer-bound) imply
\[
 \begin{split}
 \|P(D_{N,S}-bI_n)z'\|
 &\le\|(D_{N,S}-bI_n)z\|
       +\|P(D_{N,S}-bI_n)\|\,\|z-z'\|\\
 &\le\epsilon+2(1+B_L+R_0)\epsilon/h.
 \end{split}
\]
Choose a fixed \(\epsilon_{R_0}>0\) smaller than \(h/4\) so that the last expression, with \(\epsilon=\epsilon_{R_0}\), is strictly below \(\gamma/2\).
This contradicts (Adapted-small-subspace-gap) and (Tail-and-center-annihilation).
It proves (Finite-variance-joint-gap) with (Joint-gap-exceptions).

The parameter order is fixed: first \(\delta\), then the cutoff \(L\), the bounded-coordinate constants \(B_L,\gamma,c_L\), the net parameters \(\theta,\kappa\), the first-input window \(h\) and its finite grid, and finally \(\epsilon_{R_0}\).
Every parameter is chosen before \(N\) tends to infinity.
The two common \(o(1)\) events are not multiplied by the number of minors.
The entire argument concerns fixed scalar centers and supplies no norm bound for the original two inputs.
