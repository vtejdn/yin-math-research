# Approximate-mode Gram separation and high-rank fourth-layer energy at finite variance

This version fixes the approximate-mode stability calculation and the low-spectral-subspace replacement for the raw six-Gram norm comparison.
It was developed collaboratively as ordinary proof work.
The final whole-file postimage has been read, and the complete fixed argument has received the [independent review](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_F0191_v14.md#finite-variance-high-rank-reviewed) selected below.
The two source developments remain at [D10](../drafts/Yin_D_F0010.md#approximate-mode-gram-continuation) and [D33](../drafts/Yin_D_F0033.md#finite-variance-low-subspace-six-gram) as provenance, not logical proof dependencies.
No novelty claim, low-effective-rank fourth-layer estimate or least singular-value floor is asserted.

<a id="statement"></a>
## Exact model and conclusions

Let \(C_N,D_N\) be independent real symmetric Wigner matrices.
For input \(a=1,2\), its independent upper off-diagonal coordinates have the fixed real centered variance-one law \(\xi_a/\sqrt N\), and its independent diagonal coordinates have the fixed real centered finite-second-moment law \(\zeta_a/\sqrt N\).
All upper-triangular coordinates and both inputs are independent within each size; cross-size coupling is arbitrary.
For \(S\subset[N]\), \(|S|\le1\), put \(n:=N-|S|\), let \(C,D\) be the corresponding principal minors with original normalization, and define
\[
 \alpha:=\sqrt2,\qquad X:=C+D,\qquad Y:=C+\alpha D,\qquad
 M:=\begin{pmatrix}X&I_n\\I_n&Y\end{pmatrix}.
\]
For every fixed \(K_0\ge0\), there are fixed \(\rho_0,g_0>0\) such that, with probability tending to one, simultaneously for all these minors and all real \(\lambda,w=(u,v)\) satisfying
\[
 |\lambda|\le K_0,\qquad \|w\|=1,\qquad
 \|(M-\lambda I_{2n})w\|\le\rho_0,
\]
one has
\[
 \det\operatorname{Gram}(u,v)\ge g_0,\qquad
 \operatorname{Gram}(u,v)\succeq g_0I_2.
 \tag{Approximate-Gram-statement}
\]
The Gram matrix has entries \(\|u\|^2,\langle u,v\rangle,\|v\|^2\).
In particular, choose a fixed
\[
 0<\rho\le\min(\rho_0/2,\sqrt{g_0}/2).
\]
Every real unit vector in \(\operatorname{Ran}\mathbf1_{[-\rho,\rho]}(M)\) satisfies the same determinant bound, and \(\rho<\sqrt{g_0}\).
This is a statement about the whole subspace, not only a chosen eigenbasis.

For a real symmetric block matrix \(Z=\begin{pmatrix}G&H\\H^T&J\end{pmatrix}\), define
\[
 H_s:=(H+H^T)/2,\qquad
 \mathscr Q(Z):=(G\otimes J+J\otimes G)/2-H_s\otimes H_s.
\]
The tensor lies in the Hilbert tensor product of two real symmetric matrix spaces, each with Frobenius inner product.
It is not the fully symmetrized four-coordinate tensor and not the fourth Walsh coefficient layer.
There exists a fixed \(c>0\) such that
\[
 \mathbb P\left\{\exists\,|S|\le1:
 M\text{ invertible},\quad
 \|\mathscr Q(M^{-1})\|_{\rm F}<c\|M^{-1}\|_{\rm F}^2
 \right\}\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{Whole-raw-six-Gram-statement}
\]
This is an unconditional bad-event estimate intersected with invertibility.
It does not assert that these minors are invertible with probability tending to one.
The proof also gives the signed low-subspace inverse comparison and the explicit deterministic tensor error below.
The original finite-variance task, the low-effective-rank branches, and the finite-variance least-value floor remain unresolved.
There are also fixed constants \(r_*,c_*>0\) giving the following high-effective-rank conclusion.
On an invertible branch put \(Z:=M^{-1}\), \(A_Z:=\|Z\|_{\rm F}\), \(r_Z:=A_Z^2/\|Z\|^2\), and
\[
 h_Z(x):=(x^TGx)(x^TJx)-(x^TH_sx)^2.
\]
After substituting \(x_i=\varepsilon_i/\sqrt N\) and reducing every \(\varepsilon_i^2\) to one, write its fourth multilinear layer as
\[
 h_4:=\sum_{|I|=4}c_I\varepsilon_I,\qquad
 \sigma_4^2:=\sum_{|I|=4}c_I^2,\qquad
 L_i^{(4)}:=\sum_{\substack{|I|=4\\i\in I}}c_I^2,
 \qquad \tau_4:=\frac{\max_i L_i^{(4)}}{\sigma_4^2}
 \quad(\sigma_4>0).
\]
The coefficient normalization includes the original factor \(N^{-2}\).
Then
\[
 \mathbb P\{\exists\,|S|\le1:\ M\text{ invertible},\ r_Z\ge r_*,\
                  \sigma_4^2<c_*A_Z^4/N^4\}\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{Finite-variance-high-rank-energy}
\]
For every fixed \(\varepsilon>0\), the same threshold satisfies
\[
 \mathbb P\{\exists\,|S|\le1:\ M\text{ invertible},\ r_Z\ge r_*,\
 [\sigma_4=0\text{ or }(\sigma_4>0\text{ and }\tau_4>\varepsilon)]\}
 \longrightarrow0.
 \tag{Finite-variance-high-rank-load}
\]
These are unconditional bad-event estimates intersected with the high-rank invertible branch, not a claim that the branch is typical.
For arbitrary original laws the signs define a coefficient functional.
For a single deletion it is the formal fourth layer of the actual fresh-row Schur determinant, since the retained second border and exposed diagonals affect only terms of degree below four in the first border.
Only a Rademacher first law makes it the actual fourth Walsh layer of that fresh random row.
For the full matrix it remains a formal sign probe, not a border of the original parent matrix.

## Complete proof

<a id="approximate-mode-stability"></a>
### Stability on the unchanged low-mode event

Use \(X=C+D\), \(Y=C+\alpha D\), \(\alpha:=\sqrt2\), and
\(M:=\begin{pmatrix}X&I_n\\I_n&Y\end{pmatrix}\), with the model and all single-deletion minors of C187v13.
Fix \(K_0\ge0\).
Write \(A:=A_0\), \(b:=b_*\), and \(\gamma:=1/4\) for the constants in its [component argument](Yin_C_F0187_v13.md#component-lower-bound).
In particular
\[
 A\ge1+K_0+\max(\|QX\|,\|QY\|),\qquad
 b\le\min\left(\frac14,\frac1{16A},
                       \frac{\gamma}{8(2+4A^2)}\right).
\]
Let \(\epsilon>0\) be its [compressed joint-gap constant](Yin_C_F0187_v13.md#compressed-joint-gap) for the original fixed center box used in the exact-mode Gram conversion.
The projections \(Q\), the small-subspace lower bounds and this center-box event are unchanged below.

Let \(w=(u,v)\) be real and unit, let \(|\lambda|\le K_0\), and suppose
\[
 \|(M-\lambda I_{2n})w\|\le\rho.
\]
Define
\[
 r_1:=Xu+v-\lambda u,\qquad
 r_2:=u+Yv-\lambda v.
\]
Then \(\|r_1\|,\|r_2\|\le\rho\).

### The original component bound survives

Suppose \(\|u\|\le b\).
Then \(\|v\|\ge1/2\) and
\[
 \|Qv\|\le Ab+\rho.
\]
Thus \(z:=v/\|v\|\) is within \(2Ab+2\rho\) of \(V_0:=\ker Q\).
For \(\rho\le1/16\), the inequality \(Ab\le1/16\) ensures that the normalized projection \(z'\) of \(z\) to \(V_0\) exists and
\[
 \|z-z'\|\le4(Ab+\rho).
\]
If \(V_0=\{0\}\), the preceding distance bound is already impossible.
Use the same projection onto
\(\operatorname{Ran}Q\cap(V_0+CV_0+TV_0)^\perp\) as in C187v13.
The second approximate block equation gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 \|P(Y-\lambda I_n)z'\|
 &\le2(b+\rho)+4A(Ab+\rho)\\
 &=(2+4A^2)b+(2+4A)\rho.
 \end{split}
\]
The first term is at most \(\gamma/8\).
If
\[
 \rho\le\rho_{\rm comp}:=
 \min\left(\frac1{16},\frac{\gamma}{8(2+4A)}\right),
\]
the second term is at most \(\gamma/8\).
Their sum is below the small-subspace lower bound
\(\alpha\|PWz'\|\ge\alpha\gamma/2\).
Interchanging the components uses the other equation and the coefficient one of \(W\) in \(X\), giving the same contradiction.
Consequently
\[
 \|u\|,\|v\|\ge b
 \tag{Approximate-component-bound}
\]
on the original event.
There is no need to reduce \(b\) to \(b/2\), so the original center box remains valid.

### Explicit errors in the Gram conversion

Put
\[
 \Delta:=\|u\|^2\|v\|^2-\langle u,v\rangle^2,\qquad
 t:=\frac{\langle u,v\rangle}{\|v\|^2},\qquad e:=u-tv.
\]
If \(\Delta\le b^4/4\), then
\[
 b/2\le|t|\le1/b,\qquad
 \|e\|\le\sqrt\Delta/b.
\]
Define \(x_0:=\lambda-t^{-1}\) and \(y_0:=\lambda-t\).
The exact residual identities are
\[
 (X-x_0I_n)v=t^{-1}\bigl((\lambda I_n-X)e+r_1\bigr),
 \qquad
 (Y-y_0I_n)v=r_2-e.
\]
After multiplying by \(Q\), the respective upper bounds are
\[
 (2/b)(A\|e\|+\rho),\qquad \|e\|+\rho.
\]
Let \(d:=\alpha-1\) and define the original-input centers by
\[
 a_0:=\frac{\alpha x_0-y_0}{d},\qquad
 b_0:=\frac{y_0-x_0}{d}.
\]
The bounds on \(\lambda,t\) are identical to those in the exact-mode argument, so \(a_0,b_0\) belong to the same previously fixed center box.
Using \(C=(\alpha X-Y)/d\), \(D=(Y-X)/d\), and \(\|v\|\ge b\), one obtains
\[
 \max\left\{
 \left\|Q(C-a_0I_n)\frac v{\|v\|}\right\|,
 \left\|Q(D-b_0I_n)\frac v{\|v\|}\right\|
 \right\}
 \le L_\Delta\sqrt\Delta+L_\rho\rho,
\]
where
\[
 L_\Delta:=\frac{2\alpha A/b+1}{d b^2},\qquad
 L_\rho:=\frac{2\alpha/b+1}{d b}.
 \tag{Approximate-Gram-errors}
\]
All constants have already been fixed independently of \(\rho\).

Choose
\[
 \rho_0:=\min\left(\rho_{\rm comp},\frac{\epsilon}{4L_\rho}\right),
 \qquad
 g_0:=\min\left(\frac{b^4}{4},
                         \left(\frac{\epsilon}{4L_\Delta}\right)^2\right).
\]
If \(\rho\le\rho_0\) and \(\Delta<g_0\), the two compressed residuals are at most \(\epsilon/2\), contradicting the original compressed joint gap.
Thus every such approximate unit mode has \(\Delta\ge g_0\).
Its auxiliary Gram matrix has trace one, so its least eigenvalue is at least \(\Delta\).
This proves the uniform approximate-mode conclusion
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}\|u\|^2&\langle u,v\rangle\\
                 \langle u,v\rangle&\|v\|^2\end{pmatrix}
 \succeq g_0 I_2
 \quad\text{when }\|(M-\lambda I_{2n})w\|\le\rho_0,\
                  |\lambda|\le K_0,\ \|w\|=1.
 \tag{Approximate-low-mode-Gram}
\]
The common event and its failure probability have not been reselected as \(\rho\) changes.

### Consequences on a whole low-spectral subspace

Fix \(0<\eta\le\rho_0\), and let
\[
 \mathcal E:=\operatorname{Ran}\mathbf1_{[-\eta,\eta]}(M),
 \qquad M_{\mathcal E}:=M|_{\mathcal E}.
\]
Every unit \(w\in\mathcal E\) satisfies \(\|Mw\|\le\eta\).
Applying (Approximate-low-mode-Gram) with \(\lambda=0\) gives the same auxiliary Gram lower bound for every such vector, including combinations of different eigenmodes.

Define the coordinate maps \(U,V:\mathcal E\to\mathbb R^n\) by
\(U(u,v):=u\) and \(V(u,v):=v\).
Then
\[
 U^TU\succeq g_0 I_{\mathcal E},\qquad
 V^TV\succeq g_0 I_{\mathcal E}.
 \tag{Low-window-coordinate-injectivity}
\]
For \(\mathcal E\ne\{0\}\), their smallest singular values are at least \(\sqrt{g_0}\).
For every self-adjoint linear map \(A_{\mathcal E}\) on \(\mathcal E\), including one with positive and negative eigenvalues,
\[
 \|UA_{\mathcal E}U^T\|_{\rm F}
 \ge g_0\|A_{\mathcal E}\|_{\rm F}.
 \tag{Signed-low-window-compression}
\]
To see this, write a singular-value decomposition of \(U\).
In orthonormal bases, left and right multiplication of the matrix of \(A_{\mathcal E}\) multiply its \((i,j)\) entry by singular values whose product is at least \(g_0\).
Squaring and summing proves the inequality without a positivity assumption on \(A_{\mathcal E}\).
For the zero subspace the assertion is vacuous.

Finally, invariance of \(\mathcal E\) under \(M\) and the first block equation give the exact map identity
\[
 XU=-V+UM_{\mathcal E},\qquad
 \|XU\|\le1+\eta.
 \tag{Low-window-input-action}
\]
The last inequality uses \(\|U\|,\|V\|\le1\) and \(\|M_{\mathcal E}\|\le\eta\).

<a id="low-subspace-six-gram"></a>
## Deterministic six-Gram estimate on a low subspace

The next calculation uses \(g:=g_0\) and the fixed spectral radius \(\rho\) chosen in the statement when applied to the random model.
Its hypotheses and proof are first stated for arbitrary real symmetric blocks, so the signed inverse and normalization are explicit.

### Exact conditional hypotheses and signed inverse comparison

Let \(X,Y\) be real symmetric \(n\)-by-\(n\) matrices and let
\[
 M:=\begin{pmatrix}X&I\\I&Y\end{pmatrix}
\]
be invertible.
Fix \(\rho,g>0\) with \(\rho<\sqrt g\), and put
\[
 L:=\operatorname{ran}1_{[-\rho,\rho]}(M),\qquad
 W:=\binom UV:L\hookrightarrow\mathbb R^{2n},\qquad
 T:=M|_L,\qquad A:=T^{-1}.
\]
Here \(W\) is the isometric inclusion, so \(U^*U+V^*V=I_L\), and \(\|T\|\le\rho\).
The required hypothesis is
\[
 \det\operatorname{Gram}(Uh,Vh)\ge g
 \quad\text{for every }h\in L,\quad \|h\|=1.
 \tag{Low-subspace-Gram-hypothesis}
\]
The Gram matrix has trace one, and its smaller eigenvalue is at least its determinant.
Consequently, for all \(a,b\in\mathbb R\) and \(h\in L\),
\[
 \|aUh+bVh\|^2\ge g(a^2+b^2)\|h\|^2.
 \tag{Low-subspace-auxiliary-injection}
\]
In particular \(U^*U,V^*V\ge gI_L\), \(\|U\|,\|V\|\le1\), and \(r:=\dim L\le n\).

Write
\[
 Z_\rho:=M^{-1}1_{[-\rho,\rho]}(M)
 =WAW^*
 =\begin{pmatrix}G&H\\H^*&J\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad G:=UAU^*,\quad H:=UAV^*,\quad J:=VAV^*.
\]
The weight \(A\) is selfadjoint and can have both signs.
For every matrix \(B\) with columns in \(L\), \(\|UB\|_F\ge\sqrt g\|B\|_F\).
Applying this inequality on both sides, using transpose for the right multiplication, gives
\[
 s:=\|G\|_F\ge g\|A\|_F=g\|Z_\rho\|_F.
 \tag{Low-subspace-signed-G-comparison}
\]
No positivity or independence of \(A\) is used.
The last equality follows from the isometry \(W\).
If \(L=\{0\}\), the energy conclusion below is vacuous; otherwise \(s>0\).

### Replacing the second-direction argument without multiplying by \(Y\)

The top block of \(MW=WT\) is the exact identity
\[
 XU=UT-V.
 \tag{Low-subspace-graph-identity}
\]
For every real \(c\), the auxiliary injection therefore gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 \|(X-cI)Uh\|
 &=\|UTh-(V+cU)h\|\\
 &\ge\bigl(\sqrt{g(1+c^2)}-\rho\bigr)\|h\|\\
 &\ge(\sqrt g-\rho)\|h\|.
 \end{split}
 \tag{Low-subspace-direction-gap}
\]
Thus the strict relation between the two fixed constants is essential.
It is not supplied merely by saying that both constants are positive.

Set
\[
 F:=G/s,\qquad B:=(FX+XF)/2,\qquad C:=XFX,
\]
\[
 \mathscr K:=(F\otimes C+C\otimes F)/2-B\otimes B,
 \qquad k:=\|\mathscr K\|_F,\qquad c:=\langle F,B\rangle_F.
\]
These tensor products are in the Hilbert space of real symmetric matrices with the Frobenius inner product.
They are not fully symmetrized four-coordinate tensors.
Let \(P_F\) project that Hilbert space onto \(F^\perp\).
Since \(\|F\|_F=1\),
\[
 (P_F\otimes P_F)\mathscr K
 =-(B-cF)\otimes(B-cF),\qquad
 |\langle F,C\rangle_F-c^2|\le k.
\]
It follows that \(\|B-cF\|_F^2\le k\).
The exact finite-dimensional trace calculation is
\[
 2\|B\|_F^2
 =\operatorname{Tr}(F^2X^2)+\operatorname{Tr}(FXFX),
 \qquad c=\operatorname{Tr}(F^2X),
\]
and hence
\[
 \begin{split}
 \|(X-cI)F\|_F^2
 &=2\|B\|_F^2-\langle F,C\rangle_F-c^2\\
 &=2\|B-cF\|_F^2-(\langle F,C\rangle_F-c^2)
 \le3k.
 \end{split}
 \tag{Low-subspace-six-Gram-direction-control}
\]
This is a pure algebraic estimate with no bound on \(\|X\|\).
Apply (Low-subspace-direction-gap) to every column of \(AU^*/s\).
Since \(\|AU^*\|_F\ge\|UAU^*\|_F=s\), this yields
\[
 3k\ge\|(X-cI)F\|_F^2
 \ge(\sqrt g-\rho)^2\frac{\|AU^*\|_F^2}{s^2}
 \ge(\sqrt g-\rho)^2.
 \tag{Low-subspace-six-Gram-positive-constant}
\]
This conditional argument does not multiply the direction estimate by \(Y\).


### Actual truncated blocks and the tensor error

The truncated matrix satisfies \(MZ_\rho=Z_\rho M=WW^*\), not the identity.
Because \(TA=AT=I_L\), its blocks obey
\[
 H=UU^*-GX,\qquad
 J=XGX-UTU^*+UV^*+VU^*.
 \tag{Low-subspace-projected-inverse-identities}
\]
For example, expanding
\((UT-V)A(TU^*-V^*)\) gives
\(XGX=UTU^*-UV^*-VU^*+J\), which verifies the second formula including its signs.
Moreover,
\[
 \|XG\|_F\le(1+\rho)\|A\|_F,\qquad
 \|B\|_F\le(1+\rho)/g.
\]
Let \(T_H:=(H+H^*)/2\), \(\delta:=\sqrt n/s\), and
\[
 \mathscr Q_\rho:=(G\otimes J+J\otimes G)/2-T_H\otimes T_H.
\]
The identities give
\[
 T_H/s=-B+UU^*/s,\qquad
 J/s=C+(-UTU^*+UV^*+VU^*)/s.
\]
Each of \(\|UU^*\|_F,\|UV^*\|_F,\|VU^*\|_F\) is at most \(\sqrt r\le\sqrt n\), and \(\|UTU^*\|_F\le\rho\sqrt n\).
Using the product formula for Frobenius tensor norms therefore gives
\[
 \left\|\frac{\mathscr Q_\rho}{s^2}-\mathscr K\right\|_F
 \le
 \left(\rho+2+\frac{2(1+\rho)}g\right)\delta+\delta^2.
 \tag{Low-subspace-six-Gram-projection-error}
\]
In a sequence satisfying the fixed conditional hypotheses and
\(\|Z_\rho\|_F^2/n\to\infty\), the signed comparison implies \(\delta\to0\).
Writing \(k_0:=(\sqrt g-\rho)^2/3\), the preceding estimates give eventually
\[
 \|\mathscr Q_\rho\|_F
 \ge(k_0/2)s^2
 \ge(k_0g^2/2)\|Z_\rho\|_F^2.
 \tag{Conditional-low-subspace-six-Gram-coercivity}
\]
All constants are fixed before the dimension and the inverse.

### Whole-inverse high-tail transfer and remaining interfaces

For \(Z:=M^{-1}\), spectral calculus gives
\[
 R_\rho:=Z-Z_\rho,\qquad
 \|R_\rho\|\le\rho^{-1},\qquad
 \|R_\rho\|_F\le\sqrt{2n}/\rho.
 \tag{Low-subspace-high-tail}
\]
For fixed \(\rho>0\), the full-inverse divergence in [C187v10](Yin_C_F0187_v10.md#finite-variance-residual-splitting) implies
\[
 \|R_\rho\|_F=o(\|Z\|_F),\qquad
 \|Z_\rho\|_F/\|Z\|_F\longrightarrow1.
\]
For the six-Gram tensor \(\mathscr Q(Z)\) formed from its actual blocks, bilinearity and the fact that block extraction and symmetrization contract Frobenius norm give an absolute constant \(C\) with
\[
 \|\mathscr Q(Z)-\mathscr Q(Z_\rho)\|_F
 \le C(\|Z\|_F+\|Z_\rho\|_F)\|R_\rho\|_F
 =o(\|Z\|_F^2).
 \tag{Low-subspace-whole-six-Gram-transfer}
\]
Thus (Conditional-low-subspace-six-Gram-coercivity) transfers to the whole inverse, with a smaller positive constant depending only on \(\rho,g\).

<a id="probabilistic-six-gram-assembly"></a>
### Uniform application to the original finite-variance minors

On the common event of the approximate-mode conclusion, every vector in each fixed low spectral subspace satisfies the determinant hypothesis with \(g=g_0\).
The choice \(\rho\le\sqrt{g_0}/2\) supplies the strict inequality required in (Low-subspace-direction-gap).
On C187v10's uniform invertible-branch divergence event, the high-tail estimate implies both
\(\|Z_\rho\|_{\rm F}^2/n\to\infty\) and
\(\|Z-Z_\rho\|_{\rm F}/\|Z\|_{\rm F}\to0\), uniformly over the retained invertible minors.
Consequently their low subspaces are nonzero eventually on those branches, and the deterministic projection error tends to zero uniformly.
The low-subspace coercivity and whole-inverse transfer prove (Whole-raw-six-Gram-statement), after decreasing a fixed positive constant.
The approximate-mode event has probability tending to one; the divergence event is almost sure, so this assembly has precisely the asserted in-probability strength.
No probability is divided by the probability of invertibility, and empty invertible collections cause no bad event.

<a id="finite-variance-high-rank-fourth"></a>
### The distinct fourth layer on a fixed high-effective-rank branch

Let \(d_*>0\) be a fixed lower-bound constant in (Whole-raw-six-Gram-statement).
For the rank-two physical coordinate projections \(P_i\), put
\[
 \mathcal D(Z):=\sum_i\|P_iZP_i\|_{\rm F}^2,\qquad
 d_i(Z):=\|P_iZ\|_{\rm F}^2.
\]
The finite-variance physical controls in C187v10 give
\[
 \mathcal D(Z)/A_Z^2\longrightarrow0,\qquad
 \max_i d_i(Z)/A_Z^2\longrightarrow0
\]
uniformly over all the stated invertible minors, with the unconditional intersected-branch interpretation.
Define the symmetric off-diagonal blocks
\[
 A_1:=\operatorname{off}G,\qquad B_1:=\operatorname{off}J,\qquad
 C_1:=\operatorname{off}H_s,
\]
and let
\[
 \mathscr Q_{\rm off}:=(A_1\otimes B_1+B_1\otimes A_1)/2-C_1\otimes C_1,
 \qquad \mathcal G:=2\|\mathscr Q_{\rm off}\|_{\rm F}^2.
\]
Each removed matrix diagonal has Frobenius norm at most \(\sqrt{\mathcal D(Z)}\).
All full and off-diagonal block Frobenius norms are at most \(A_Z\).
Telescoping the three tensor products consequently gives
\[
 \|\mathscr Q(Z)-\mathscr Q_{\rm off}\|_{\rm F}
 \le4A_Z\sqrt{\mathcal D(Z)}.
\]
Thus, on the common good event and eventually uniformly,
\[
 \mathcal D(Z)/A_Z^2\le(d_*/8)^2
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \mathcal G\ge d_*^2A_Z^4/2.
\]
The accepted deterministic coefficient identity and four-cycle estimate in C187v6 give
\[
 \left|\sigma_4^2-\frac4{N^4}\mathcal G\right|
 \le\frac{C\kappa_1^2B_\Sigma^2}{N^4},
\]
where
\[
 \kappa_1:=\max(\|A_1\|,\|B_1\|,\|C_1\|)\le2\|Z\|,
 \qquad
 B_\Sigma^2:=\|A_1\|_{\rm F}^2+\|B_1\|_{\rm F}^2+
                         \|C_1\|_{\rm F}^2\le A_Z^2.
\]
That estimate is pure algebra for real symmetric blocks in the original coordinate basis; it requires no bound on the input matrices.
Hence
\[
 \sigma_4^2\ge N^{-4}
             (2d_*^2A_Z^4-C\|Z\|^2A_Z^2).
\]
Choose the fixed \(r_*\) large enough that \(C/r_*\le d_*^2\).
On \(r_Z\ge r_*\), this proves
\(\sigma_4^2\ge d_*^2A_Z^4/N^4\).
The same fixed algebraic input gives the exact complete-layer coordinate-load bound
\[
 L_i^{(4)}\le C A_Z^2d_i(Z)/N^4.
\]
Dividing by the energy lower bound yields
\[
 \tau_4\le C_{d_*}\max_i d_i(Z)/A_Z^2\longrightarrow0.
\]
The common raw-coercivity and physical-control events have the required uniform probability scope, proving both high-rank assertions without any additional minor union.
The effective-rank threshold is fixed and no inverse-norm upper bound is used.
The raw six-Gram bound by itself does not exclude full symmetrization cancellation; it is the displayed rank-sensitive error that makes this passage valid.
The cross-energy and comparable-mode branches at low effective rank, their graph-area interfaces and the complete finite-variance floor remain outside this result.
