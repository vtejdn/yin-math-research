# Shifted low-effective-rank fourth-layer control at finite variance

This version supplies the separated and comparable inverse-mode branches for the original real finite-second-moment two-channel pencil.
The whole-low-subspace input comes from the proved approximate-mode assertion of C187v14.
The new signed-weight geometry, compactness argument and low-rank assembly have received [independent whole-proof review](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_F0191_v15.md#shifted-finite-variance-low-rank-reviewed), adopted in the [proved decision](#decision-shifted-finite-variance-low-rank-reviewed).
The graph scale and the matrix reduction below retain the high-rank and singular-minor exceptions.
No all-regime least-value floor, determinant limit or completion of the original Tasks is asserted.

## Exact model, low-rank assertions and retained matrix exception


Let \(C_N,D_N\) be independent real symmetric Wigner matrices.
For input \(a=1,2\), its independent upper off-diagonal entries have law \(\xi_a/\sqrt N\), where \(\xi_a\) is fixed, real, centered and has variance one.
Its independent diagonal entries have law \(\zeta_a/\sqrt N\), where \(\zeta_a\) is fixed, real, centered and has finite second moment.
All upper-triangular coordinates and both inputs are independent within each size; cross-size coupling is arbitrary.
Fix any deterministic real sequence \(\theta_N\to0\), and retain the original \(N^{-1/2}\) normalization in every \(|S|\le1\) principal minor.
Write \(n=N-|S|\), \(\alpha=\sqrt2\), and
\[
 M^\theta_{N,S}
 =\begin{pmatrix}C_{N,S}+D_{N,S}&I_n\\
 I_n&C_{N,S}+\alpha D_{N,S}\end{pmatrix}+\theta_NI_{2n}.
\]
For an invertible such minor set
\[
 Z=(M^\theta_{N,S})^{-1}
   =\begin{pmatrix}G&H\\H^T&J\end{pmatrix},\quad
 H_s=(H+H^T)/2,\quad Q=\|Z\|,\quad A_Z=\|Z\|_F,
\]
\[
 m=s_2^\downarrow(Z),\qquad E_F=(A_Z^2-Q^2)^{1/2},
 \qquad r=A_Z^2/Q^2.
\]
Define
\[
 h_Z(x)=(x^TGx)(x^TJx)-(x^TH_sx)^2,\quad
 p_4=\Pi_4h_Z(\varepsilon/\sqrt N)
      =\sum_{|I|=4}c_I\varepsilon_I,
\]
\[
 \sigma_4^2=\sum_Ic_I^2,\qquad
 L_j=\sum_{I\ni j}c_I^2,\qquad
 \tau_4=\max_jL_j/\sigma_4^2\quad(\sigma_4>0).
\]
The notation \(\Pi_4\) means reduction of all sign squares followed by retaining exactly four distinct coordinates.
For arbitrary original laws this is a formal sign coefficient functional.
For a single deletion it is the fourth homogeneous-coordinate contribution of the actual Schur determinant, regardless of the exposed diagonals and the conditioned second border.

There is a fixed \(\gamma\in(0,1/2]\) such that, for every fixed \(K\ge1\), one can choose \(c_K>0\) with the following assertion.
On the low-rank branch \(r\le K\), put
\[
 a_{\mathrm{low}}(M)=
 \begin{cases}
 QE_F,&m\le\gamma Q,\\
 Q^2,&m>\gamma Q.
 \end{cases}
 \tag{Finite-variance-low-rank-scale}
\]
Then
\[
 \mathbb P\{\exists\,|S|\le1:
 M^\theta_{N,S}\text{ invertible},\ r\le K,\
 \sigma_4^2<c_Ka_{\mathrm{low}}(M^\theta_{N,S})^2/N^4\}
 \longrightarrow0.
 \tag{Shifted-low-rank-energy}
\]
For every fixed \(\epsilon>0\),
\[
 \mathbb P\{\exists\,|S|\le1:
 M^\theta_{N,S}\text{ invertible},\ r\le K,\
 [\sigma_4=0\text{ or }(\sigma_4>0,\tau_4>\epsilon)]\}
 \longrightarrow0.
 \tag{Shifted-low-rank-load}
\]
These are unconditional bad-event assertions intersected with the stated invertible branch, not probabilities conditioned on its occurrence.
They do not assert that low effective rank is typical.
All quantifiers include the full matrix as a formal sign probe and all single-deletion minors.


For every fixed boundary norm \(B_0<\infty\), there is a fixed \(C_{B_0}\) such that on the low-rank invertible branch with \(Q,E_F\ge1\),
\[
 \sup_{\substack{B\in\mathbb R^{2n\times2}\\\|B\|\le B_0}}
 \sqrt{\det(I_2+B^TZ^2B)}
 \le C_{B_0}a_{\mathrm{low}}(M).
 \tag{Low-rank-graph-statement}
\]
The scalar scale and branch tests are functions of the retained minor alone.
They require no measurable eigenvector choice.

Let \(M_i=M^\theta_{N,\{i\}}\), and let \(r_i\) denote its effective inverse rank on its invertible branch.
There are fixed \(c,C>0\), depending on the laws and \(K\), such that
\[
 \mathbb P\{s_{\min}(M^\theta_{N,\varnothing})\le cN^{-6}\}
 \le o(1)+\frac C N\sum_{i=1}^N
 \mathbb P\{M_i\text{ singular or }(M_i\text{ invertible},r_i>K)\}.
 \tag{Low-rank-matrix-statement}
\]
The error tends to zero for the fixed laws, fixed \(K\) and fixed deterministic vanishing shift.
It is not asserted summable.
This is a reduction, not an unconditional least-value floor, since neither retained minor exception is estimated here.
The original first off-diagonal law need not be Rademacher.
The full model remains the real two-input pencil with identity coupling and \(\alpha=\sqrt2\); arbitrary matrix coefficients and complex input laws are outside the statement.

### The approximate-mode input transfers to the same parent shift

Let \(\rho_0,g>0\) be the fixed constants in (Approximate-Gram-statement) at \(K_0=1\).
Choose once and for all
\[
 0<\rho\le\min(\rho_0/2,\sqrt g/2).
\]
On its common event of probability tending to one, consider any unit vector in
\(\operatorname{ran}1_{[-\rho,\rho]}(M^\theta_{N,S})\).
For the unshifted \(M^0_{N,S}\), it satisfies
\[
 \|(M^0_{N,S}-(-\theta_N)I)w\|
 =\|M^\theta_{N,S}w\|\le\rho\le\rho_0.
\]
Eventually \(|\theta_N|\le1\).
The fixed approximate-mode assertion therefore gives the whole-subspace Gram lower bound with this same \(g\), simultaneously for all required minors.
This argument needs the uniformity in real \(\lambda\), not a separate assertion about shifted entry laws.
It does not infer any shifted conclusion from the fixed theorem's unshifted raw six-Gram statement.

C187v10 supplies, uniformly on the shifted invertible branches,
\[
 Q\to\infty,\quad E_F^2/n\to\infty,\quad A_Z^2/n\to\infty,
\]
\[
 \eta_*:=\max_j\|P_jw\|^2\to0,\quad
 d_*(E)/E_F^2\to0,\quad \mathcal D(E)/E_F^2\to0,
 \quad d_*(Z)/A_Z^2\to0,\quad \mathcal D(Z)/A_Z^2\to0,
 \tag{Shifted-low-rank-physical-inputs}
\]
where \(w\) is any closest mode, \(E=Z-qww^T\), \(|q|=Q\), and
\(d_*(B)=\max_j\|P_jB\|_F^2\), \(\mathcal D(B)=\sum_j\|P_jBP_j\|_F^2\).
These physical conclusions hold almost surely at their fixed-coupling scope.
Intersecting them with the common approximate-Gram event yields the required in-probability inputs without a union bound over the minors.

## Comparable modes from whole-subspace geometry

### Conditional quantitative statement

Fix \(g>0\), \(K<\infty\), and \(0<d\le1\).
Let \(E\) and \(\mathcal H\) be finite-dimensional real Hilbert spaces, and let
\[
 W=\binom UV:E\longrightarrow\mathcal H\oplus\mathcal H,
 \qquad W^*W=I_E.
\]
Assume
\[
 \det\operatorname{Gram}(Uh,Vh)\ge g
 \quad\text{for every unit }h\in E.
 \tag{Comparable-whole-subspace-Gram}
\]
For every selfadjoint \(A:E\to E\) satisfying
\[
 \|A\|=1,\qquad \|A\|_F^2\le K,\qquad
 s_2^\downarrow(A)\ge d,
 \tag{Comparable-weight-hypotheses}
\]
put
\[
 G=UAU^*,\quad H=UAV^*,\quad J=VAV^*,\quad
 S=(H+H^*)/2,
\]
\[
 p_A(x)=\langle x,Gx\rangle\langle x,Jx\rangle
                  -\langle x,Sx\rangle^2.
\]
The conditional claim is that there exists \(b=b(g,K,d)>0\), independent of both dimensions, such that
\[
 \sup_{\|x\|=1}|p_A(x)|\ge b.
 \tag{Comparable-subspace-raw-separation}
\]
The constants are existence constants.
The argument allows both signs of \(A\), and uses neither \(X,Y\) nor a bound on their operator norms.
The rank-one case is excluded by the second singular-value hypothesis.

### Exact zero classification on a possibly infinite Hilbert space

We first prove impossibility of \(p_A\equiv0\) when \(A\) is injective on \(E\), has rank at least two, and \(E,\mathcal H\) may be infinite-dimensional.
Assume \(U,V,A\) are bounded and that the same isometry and whole-subspace Gram hypotheses hold.
The two-by-two Gram matrix has trace one and determinant at least \(g\) for unit \(h\), so its smaller eigenvalue is at least \(g\).
Thus they imply
\[
 \|aUh+bVh\|^2\ge g(a^2+b^2)\|h\|^2.
 \tag{Comparable-auxiliary-injection}
\]
In particular \(U\) has closed range, is bounded below by \(\sqrt g\), and \(U^*\) maps onto \(E\).
Thus \(G=UAU^*\) has rank at least two.

The algebraic input is precisely [C187v8's finite quadratic-product lemma](Yin_C_F0187_v8.md#finite-quadratic-product), together with its finite-restriction passage in [the next subsection](Yin_C_F0187_v8.md#infinite-space-zero-transfer).
That lemma states that real quadratic forms \(a,b,c\), with \(\operatorname{rank}a\ge2\) and \(ac=b^2\), satisfy \(b=t a,\ c=t^2a\) for a real scalar \(t\).
It covers rank-two indefinite forms as well as definite forms.
Its fixed proof complexifies \(a\) to a sum of at least two squares, evaluates \(b\) at \(e_i\pm i e_j\), and then adds arbitrary radical vectors to remove radical coefficients.
Consequently \(b\) is a scalar multiple of \(a\), and cancellation in the polynomial identity gives \(c=t^2a\).
No uninspected external factorization theorem is introduced here.

For completeness in the infinite-space use, choose one finite-dimensional subspace on which \(G\)'s quadratic form has rank at least two.
On every larger finite-dimensional subspace, apply that fixed algebraic lemma to \(p_A=0\).
The scalar is unique on the first subspace, so is the same real number \(c\) on all enlargements.
Polarization and continuity then yield
\[
 S=cG,\qquad J=c^2G.
 \tag{Comparable-zero-product-identities}
\]
For \(x\perp\operatorname{ran}U\), one has \(U^*x=0\), hence \(H^*x=Gx=0\).
The first identity gives \(Hx=0\), that is, \(UA V^*x=0\).
Injectivity of \(U\) and \(A\) gives \(V^*x=0\).
Thus \(\operatorname{ran}V\subset\operatorname{ran}U\), where closedness of the latter range is used.
There is a bounded operator
\[
 B=(U^*U)^{-1}U^*V:E\to E,\qquad V=UB.
\]
Canceling the injective \(U\) and surjective \(U^*\) in (Comparable-zero-product-identities) gives
\[
 AB^*+BA=2cA,\qquad BAB^*=c^2A.
\]
For \(D=B-cI_E\), these imply
\[
 DA+AD^*=0,\qquad DAD^*=0.
\]
The auxiliary injection and \(\|U\|\le1\) give
\[
 \|Dh\|\ge\|UDh\|
 =\|(V-cU)h\|
 \ge\sqrt{g(1+c^2)}\|h\|.
\]
Since \(D\) is injective, \(DAD^*=0\) implies \(AD^*=0\).
Injectivity of \(A\) then gives \(D^*=0\), contradicting this lower bound on the nonzero space \(E\).
This proves the exact zero classification without any lower bound on the nonzero eigenvalues of \(A\).

### Quantitative compactness with an operator-norm tail

Suppose (Comparable-subspace-raw-separation) fails along a sequence indexed by \(m\).
First restrict each weight to its nonzero spectral support.
The isometry and Gram hypotheses survive restriction, as do the three weight bounds and its polynomial.
Choose orthonormal eigenvectors \(e_{j,m}\) with eigenvalues \(a_{j,m}\) in nonincreasing absolute value.
After taking a subsequence, either the dimensions are one fixed finite integer \(r\), or they tend to infinity.
In the latter case every fixed index is present eventually.
The finite case is treated by the same argument with only \(r\) labels and no tail.
In either case pass to subsequences so that every eigenvalue label converges and every pairwise inner product among
\[
 u_{j,m}=U_me_{j,m},\qquad v_{j,m}=V_me_{j,m}
\]
converges.
There are only countably many bounded scalar entries.
Their limits define a positive semidefinite Gram form.
Quotienting its zero-norm vectors and completing produces a real Hilbert space \(\mathcal H_\infty\) with vectors \(u_j,v_j\).

On \(E_\infty=\ell^2\), or \(\mathbb R^r\) in the finite case, define \(Ue_j=u_j\), \(Ve_j=v_j\).
The identities
\[
 \langle u_{j,m},u_{k,m}\rangle+
 \langle v_{j,m},v_{k,m}\rangle=\delta_{jk}
\]
pass to the limit.
Thus \(W=(U,V)\) extends to an isometry, with \(\|U\|,\|V\|\le1\).
For every finitely supported \(h\), its two-by-two Gram determinant passes to the limit, so (Comparable-whole-subspace-Gram) holds on that dense subspace.
Continuity gives it for every \(h\in E_\infty\).

Set \(Ae_j=a_je_j\), where \(a_j=\lim_m a_{j,m}\).
Then
\[
 |a_1|=1,\qquad |a_2|\ge d,\qquad
 \sum_j a_j^2\le K.
\]
The last inequality follows by passing each finite partial sum to the limit.
In particular \(A\) is bounded and compact in the infinite case.
For the spectral truncation to its first \(J\) labels, both before and after taking the limit,
\[
 \|A-A^{(J)}\|\le\sqrt{\frac K{J+1}},
 \qquad
 \|A_m-A_m^{(J)}\|\le\sqrt{\frac K{J+1}}.
 \tag{Comparable-uniform-weight-tail}
\]
This follows because the first \(J+1\) absolute eigenvalues are all at least the \((J+1)\)-st.
It does not assert a uniformly small Frobenius tail.

Every block extracted from \(WAW^*\) has norm at most one, and replacing \(A\) by \(A^{(J)}\) changes each block by at most \(t_J:=\sqrt{K/(J+1)}\).
Therefore
\[
 |p_A(x)-p_{A^{(J)}}(x)|\le4t_J\|x\|^4.
 \tag{Comparable-quartic-tail-transfer}
\]
The same inequality holds for the \(m\)-th sequence member.
For a fixed finite linear combination \(x\) of the labels \(u_j,v_j\), use the identical coefficients to form \(x_m\).
Its norm and every finite-truncation quadratic form converge by the Gram construction.
The assumed vanishing unit-sphere supremum gives \(p_{A_m}(x_m)\to0\).
Applying (Comparable-quartic-tail-transfer) on both sides of the Gram limit and then sending \(J\to\infty\) yields \(p_A(x)=0\).
The label span is dense in \(\mathcal H_\infty\), so boundedness extends this identity to every \(x\).

Finally restrict \(E_\infty\) to the closed span of labels with \(a_j\ne0\).
The restriction does not change \(G,H,J\), preserves the isometry and whole-subspace Gram inequality, and makes \(A\) injective.
It retains at least two nonzero eigenvalues by \(|a_2|\ge d\).
The exact zero classification now gives a contradiction.
This proves the conditional quantitative assertion.

### Actual-pencil scope and remaining boundary

For a low spectral subspace of an actual pencil, the representation \(Z_\rho=W T^{-1}W^*\) supplies this geometry once the whole-subspace Gram hypothesis is established.
Normalize the weight by \(Q=\|Z\|\).
When \(Q\to\infty\), the largest inverse mode belongs to every fixed window \([-\rho,\rho]\) eventually.
If \(s_2^\downarrow(Z)\ge dQ\), the second mode also belongs to that window eventually.
Thus \(\|Z_\rho/Q\|=1\) and its second singular value is at least \(d\).
If \(\|Z\|_F^2\le KQ^2\), its normalized weight has squared Frobenius norm at most \(K\).
The conditional assertion then gives raw separation at scale \(bQ^2\) for \(Z_\rho\).
Since \(\|Z-Z_\rho\|\le\rho^{-1}\), the corresponding unit-ball polynomial error for the whole inverse is \(O(Q/\rho+\rho^{-2})=o(Q^2)\).
This transfers the conditional raw lower bound to the actual whole inverse.

## Cross energy from whole-subspace geometry

### The residual weight and the perpendicular upper block

Let \(E,\mathcal H,U,V,W\) satisfy (Comparable-whole-subspace-Gram) and \(W=(U,V)\) be an isometry.
Let \(e\in E\) be unit, put \(w=We=(u,v)\), and let \(A:E\to E\) be selfadjoint with \(Ae=0\).
Write
\[
 \mathsf E=WAW^*
   =\begin{pmatrix}F&H\\H^*&J\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad T_H=(H+H^*)/2,\qquad
 P=I-\frac{uu^*}{\|u\|^2}.
\]
The Gram hypothesis implies \(u\ne0\), \(U^*U\ge gI_E\), and
\[
 \|Pv\|^2
 =\frac{\det\operatorname{Gram}(u,v)}{\|u\|^2}\ge g.
 \tag{Low-subspace-cross-projected-v}
\]
Let \(E_0=e^\perp\), \(U_0=U|_{E_0}\), and \(A_0=A|_{E_0}\).
Selfadjointness and \(Ae=0\) make \(E_0\) invariant and give \(A=0\oplus A_0\).
For \(h\in E_0\),
\[
 \begin{split}
 \|PU_0h\|
 &=\inf_{t\in\mathbb R}\|U(h-te)\|\\
 &\ge\sqrt g\inf_{t\in\mathbb R}\|h-te\|
 =\sqrt g\|h\|.
 \end{split}
 \tag{Low-subspace-cross-perpendicular-injection}
\]
Consequently, including for signed \(A_0\),
\[
 \|PFP\|_F
 =\|(PU_0)A_0(PU_0)^*\|_F
 \ge g\|A_0\|_F
 =g\|A\|_F
 =g\|\mathsf E\|_F.
 \tag{Low-subspace-cross-upper-block}
\]
To verify the signed comparison, apply the lower singular-value inequality separately to left multiplication by \(PU_0\) and, by transpose, to right multiplication by its adjoint.
It requires no lower bound on the nonzero eigenvalues of \(A_0\).

### Exact symmetric-tensor lower bound

Use the arithmetic-average symmetrization convention and define
\[
 \mathscr S=
 \operatorname{Sym}_4
 \bigl(u\otimes u\otimes J+v\otimes v\otimes F
                   -2u\otimes v\otimes T_H\bigr).
\]
The pure projection calculation in [C187v5](Yin_C_F0187_v5.md#coercive-tensor-projection) gives
\[
 P^{\otimes4}\mathscr S
  =\operatorname{Sym}_4((Pv)\otimes(Pv)\otimes PFP).
\]
Its dimension-free product estimate can be checked directly.
For a unit \(f\) and symmetric \(B\), write
\(B=a ff^*+fb^*+bf^*+C\), with \(b\perp f\) and \(Cf=0\).
The groups with four, three and two tensor indices in direction \(f\) are orthogonal, and their permutation counts give
\[
 \|\operatorname{Sym}_4(f\otimes f\otimes B)\|_F^2
 =a^2+\|b\|^2+\tfrac16\|C\|_F^2
 \ge\tfrac16\|B\|_F^2.
\]
Since projection contracts norm, (Low-subspace-cross-projected-v) and (Low-subspace-cross-upper-block) imply
\[
 \|\mathscr S\|_F
 \ge\frac{\|Pv\|^2}{\sqrt6}\|PFP\|_F
 \ge\frac{g^2}{\sqrt6}\|\mathsf E\|_F.
 \tag{Conditional-low-subspace-cross-coercivity}
\]
This uses no \(Xv\), no bounded operator norm for the pencil inputs, and no tangent-inverse estimate.

### Fixed-window transfer for an actual closest-mode residual

For an invertible actual pencil \(M\), let \(Z=M^{-1}\), choose a closest mode \(w\), and write
\[
 Z=qww^*+E_w,\qquad \|Z\|=|q|.
\]
Under C187v10's divergence, the eigenvalue \(q^{-1}\) belongs to every fixed window \([-\rho,\rho]\) eventually.
On \(L=\operatorname{ran}1_{[-\rho,\rho]}(M)\), represent \(w=We\) and use the signed residual weight
\[
 A=T^{-1}-qee^*,\qquad T=M|_L.
\]
Then \(Ae=0\), including when the closest eigenvalue has multiplicity, and
\[
 E_{\mathrm{low}}=WAW^*=Z_\rho-qww^*.
\]
The full and low residuals differ by the spectral high tail:
\[
 E_w-E_{\mathrm{low}}=Z-Z_\rho,\qquad
 \|E_w-E_{\mathrm{low}}\|_F\le\sqrt{2n}/\rho.
\]
C187v10 gives \(\|E_w\|_F^2/n\to\infty\), so this error is \(o(\|E_w\|_F)\), uniformly at its stated minor/mode scope.
For fixed unit \(w\), the cross tensor is linear in the residual, with operator norm between the corresponding Frobenius spaces bounded by an absolute constant.
Therefore
\[
 \|\mathscr S(w,E_w)-\mathscr S(w,E_{\mathrm{low}})\|_F
 \le C\sqrt n/\rho=o(\|E_w\|_F).
\]
If the whole-low-subspace Gram hypothesis holds with a fixed \(g\), the conditional bound above yields eventually
\[
 \|\mathscr S(w,E_w)\|_F\ge c_g\|E_w\|_F
\]
for a fixed \(c_g>0\).

The [fixed distinct-index calculation](Yin_C_F0187_v5.md#distinct-index-transfer) is pure algebra and applies to this actual residual.
Writing \(P_i\) for the rank-two physical coordinate projection, it gives
\[
 \|\mathscr S_{\mathrm{rep}}\|_F^2
 \le C\left[
  \max_i\|P_iw\|^2\,\|E_w\|_F^2
  +\sum_i\|P_iE_wP_i\|_F^2\right].
\]
C187v10's closest-mode leverage and physical diagonal-block estimates make this \(o(\|E_w\|_F^2)\).
With the original \(N^{-1/2}\) sign normalization, the exact fourth-Walsh conversion is
\[
 \sigma_{\mathrm{cross}}^2
 =\frac{24}{N^4}\|\mathscr S_{\mathrm{dist}}\|_F^2
 \ge c'_g\frac{\|E_w\|_F^2}{N^4}
\]
eventually on the same conditional intersection.
The factor \(24\) counts the ordered permutations of each four-element physical set.

## Complete fourth-layer energy and loads

### The separated branch uses the complete fourth layer

The [conditional cross proof](#cross-subspace-lemma) gives a fixed \(c_x>0\) such that
\[
 \sigma(s_4)^2\ge c_xE_F^2/N^4
\]
outside a uniform error tending to zero.
Here \(s_4\) is the cross layer in the exact identity \(p_4=q s_4+e_4\).
The residual layer \(e_4\) is the fourth layer of
\((x^TFx)(x^TJ_0x)-(x^TT_0x)^2\), for the blocks of \(E\).
The [C187v7 mixed-inner-product algebra](Yin_C_F0187_v7.md#mixed-fourth-inner-product), applied to those arbitrary real blocks, gives an absolute \(C_1\) with
\[
 |\langle s_4,e_4\rangle|\le C_1mE_F^2/N^4.
\]
Its proof only uses the unit vector norms and the actual residual operator/Frobenius norms; it has no bound on \(X,Y\).
Consequently
\[
 \sigma_4^2
 \ge(c_xQ^2-2C_1Qm)E_F^2/N^4.
\]
Fix \(\gamma=\min(1/2,c_x/(4C_1))\).
On \(m\le\gamma Q\), this yields \(\sigma_4^2\ge(c_x/2)Q^2E_F^2/N^4\).
It controls cancellation in the complete fourth layer, rather than merely the cross polynomial.

The [pure pole-load calculation](Yin_C_F0194_v13.md#dominant-coefficients), before its dominant-mode specialization, gives
\[
 L_j(p_4)\le\frac C{N^4}
 \bigl(q^2\eta_j\mathcal R^2+(q^2+\mathcal R^2)\rho_j\bigr),
\]
where \(\mathcal R^2\le E_F^2\), \(\rho_j\le d_j(E)\), and \(\eta_j=\|P_jw\|^2\).
Dividing by the complete-layer energy lower bound gives
\[
 \tau_4\le C_{c_x}
   \left[\eta_*+
     \left(1+\frac{E_F^2}{Q^2}\right)\frac{d_*(E)}{E_F^2}\right]
 \le C_{c_x}\left[\eta_*+K\,\frac{d_*(E)}{E_F^2}\right]\to0.
 \tag{Finite-variance-separated-load}
\]
No visible-residual lower comparison is used: only the indicated upper bounds by the full residual occur.
The value of \(m\), and hence this branch test, is independent of the closest-mode choice.

### The comparable branch and distinct physical indices

On \(r\le K\) and \(m>\gamma Q\), the [conditional comparable lemma](#comparable-subspace-lemma), with parameters \(g,K,\gamma\), gives
\[
 \sup_{\|x\|=1}|h_Z(x)|\ge b_KQ^2
\]
outside a uniform error tending to zero.
Its fixed-window and high-tail transfer apply to the shifted actual minor exactly as stated there.
Let
\(\mathscr T_Z=\operatorname{Sym}_4(G\otimes J-H_s\otimes H_s)\).
The raw lower bound implies \(\|\mathscr T_Z\|_F\ge b_KQ^2\).
The [C187v8 collision calculation](Yin_C_F0187_v8.md#comparable-mode-walsh-transfer) is purely algebraic and yields
\[
 \|\mathscr T_Z^{\mathrm{rep}}\|_F^2
 \le C A_Z^2\bigl(\mathcal D(Z)+d_*(Z)\bigr)=o(Q^4).
\]
Indeed division by \(Q^4\) costs at most \(K^2\) times the two physical ratios in (Shifted-low-rank-physical-inputs).
The exact coefficient identity is
\[
 \sigma_4^2=\frac{24}{N^4}
      \|\mathscr T_Z^{\mathrm{dist}}\|_F^2
 \ge c'_KQ^4/N^4.
\]
The [C187v6 coordinate-load calculation](Yin_C_F0187_v6.md#rank-refined-four-cycle-error), independent of its old norm-sensitive coercivity, gives
\[
 L_j\le C A_Z^2d_j(Z)/N^4,\qquad
 \tau_4\le C_{c'_K}K^2d_*(Z)/A_Z^2\to0.
 \tag{Finite-variance-comparable-load}
\]
The two branch tests \(m\le\gamma Q\) and \(m>\gamma Q\) exhaust \(r\le K\).
Tied largest absolute inverse eigenvalues have \(m=Q\) and therefore lie in the comparable branch.
Taking a minimum of the two positive energy constants and a union of their uniform bad events proves both low-rank assertions.


<a id="low-rank-graph-area"></a>
### Graph area with a finite second-border guard

For any fixed \(B_0<\infty\), the graph scale satisfies, whenever \(Q,E_F\ge1\),
\[
 \sup_{\|B\|\le B_0}
   \sqrt{\det(I_2+B^TZ^2B)}
 \le C_{B_0}a_{\mathrm{low}}(M).
 \tag{Finite-variance-low-rank-graph-scale}
\]
On the comparable branch use \(1+B_0^2Q^2\le(1+B_0^2)Q^2\).
On the separated branch write \(Z=qww^T+E\).
The [exact largest-mode area identity](Yin_C_F0194_v13.md#pole-area) gives
\[
 \sqrt{\det(I_2+B^TZ^2B)}
 \le C_{B_0}(1+Q)(1+m)
 \le4C_{B_0}QE_F,
\]
using \(m\le E_F\) and \(Q,E_F\ge1\).
C187v10 makes the latter size conditions fail with uniform probability tending to zero.
This replaces the old use of a fixed input norm to deduce \(E_F\ge1\).

For a single deletion, condition on the whole second input, the retained first minor and the exposed first diagonal.
If its second border is \(y_i\), the actual boundary columns are
\((x+y_i,0)^T\) and \((0,x+\alpha y_i)^T\).
When \(\|x\|,\|y_i\|\le2\), their boundary operator norm is at most
\[
 B_0=2(1+\alpha).
\]
For the original finite-second-moment second law,
\[
 \frac1N\sum_i\mathbb P\{\|y_i\|>2\}\to0
\]
by the fixed-row weak law, as in [C194v14](Yin_C_F0194_v14.md#finite-variance-second-input-corollary).
This is an averaged exception, not a simultaneous bound over all rows.
The scalar shift changes only exposed and retained diagonals, and leaves these borders and their independence unchanged.


<a id="low-rank-matrix-reduction"></a>
### Matrix reduction retaining the high-rank exception

There is a deterministic \(t_N\to0\), with \(t_N\ge N^{-1/2}\), such that the uniform low-rank load exception at level \(t_N\) tends to zero.
This follows by choosing successive fixed load levels and increasing their deterministic dimension thresholds.
For each minor let \(\mathcal G_i\) require invertibility, \(r_i\le K\), \(Q_i,E_{F,i}\ge1\), the energy bound, \(\tau_{4,i}\le t_N\), and \(\|y_i\|\le2\).
All these are measurable in the bridge's conditioning sigma-field.
Set \(a_i=C_{B_0}a_{\mathrm{low}}(M_i)\) there and any positive measurable value elsewhere.
The graph bound and energy lower bound give
\(\sigma_{4,i}\ge\kappa a_i/N^2\) with a fixed \(\kappa>0\).
The formal fourth coefficients depend only on the retained inverse; lower-degree terms may depend arbitrarily on the exposed diagonal and second border.
Apply the bridge with first input \(C_N+\theta_NI_N\) and second input \(D_N\).
The first off-diagonal law is unchanged, while the independently shifted first diagonals satisfy the bridge's allowance for arbitrary diagonal laws.
Thus the [finite-variance bridge](Yin_C_F0194_v14.md#statement) applies to the original first law.
Its only remaining minor exception is bounded by
\[
 \frac1N\sum_i\mathbb P(\mathcal G_i^c)
 \le o(1)+\frac1N\sum_i
 \mathbb P\{M_i\text{ singular or }(M_i\text{ invertible},r_i>K)\}.
\]

For completeness one may use a fixed \(B>0\) and parent guard \(b_N=B\sqrt N\).
The [Frobenius weak law in C187v11](Yin_C_F0187_v11.md#argument) gives
\[
 \frac{\|C_N+D_N\|_F^2+\|C_N+\alpha D_N\|_F^2}{N}
 \longrightarrow5
\]
in probability.
The unshifted block pencil therefore has squared Frobenius norm divided by \(N\) tending to seven.
Adding \(\theta_NI_{2N}\) changes its Frobenius norm divided by \(\sqrt N\) by at most \(\sqrt2|\theta_N|\).
Consequently \(\mathbb P\{\|M^\theta_{N,\varnothing}\|>B\sqrt N\}\to0\) for any fixed \(B>\sqrt7\).
The bridge's \(b_N^{-2}N^{-5}\) scale is then \(cN^{-6}\), with \(B^{-2}\) absorbed in \(c\).
Its pair-row weak-law, matching and anti-concentration errors all tend to zero.
Hence
\[
 \mathbb P\{s_{\min}(M^\theta_{N,\varnothing})\le cN^{-6}\}
 \le o(1)+\frac C N\sum_i
 \mathbb P\{M_i\text{ singular or }(M_i\text{ invertible},r_i>K)\}.
 \tag{Low-rank-finite-variance-matrix-reduction}
\]
This reduction includes singular parent matrices and does not assert that the retained high-rank or singular-minor average vanishes.
It uses no inverse-norm truncation, conditional division by an invertibility probability or fixed parent operator norm.
The broader all-regime and determinant assembly remains outside this low-rank result.
