# Physical spread and complete inverse tensors for the finite-variance three-channel path

This theorem supplies the physical-coordinate and complete-inverse inputs for the actual three-channel path model.
It combines the adopted whole-low-subspace Gram theorem with a new free zero-mass calculation, a fixed-deletion proof extension, and the finite-variance fresh-row mechanism.
The complete argument has received [independent whole-proof review](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_F0191_v20.md#three-channel-physical-whole-proof), adopted in the [proved decision](#decision-three-channel-physical-reviewed).
The statement does not include an unshifted least-singular-value floor or a determinant Task conclusion.

<a id="statement"></a>
## Exact original model and conclusions

Let \(C_N,D_N\) be independent real symmetric Wigner matrices.
For input \(j=1,2\), its strict upper entries are independent copies of \(\xi_j/\sqrt N\), where \(\xi_j\) is a fixed real centered law of variance one.
Its diagonal entries are independent copies of \(\zeta_j/\sqrt N\), where \(\zeta_j\) is fixed, real, centered and has finite second moment.
All upper-triangular coordinates and the two inputs are independent within each size, and the coupling across sizes is arbitrary.
There is no density, bounded-support, higher-moment or original-input operator-norm assumption.

Put
\[
 T=\operatorname{diag}(0,1,\sqrt2),\qquad
 K=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\1&0&1\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Fix an integer \(r\ge0\) and a deterministic real sequence \(\theta_N\to0\), allowing \(\theta_N=0\).
For \(S\subset[N]\), put \(n=N-|S|\), let \(C_{N,S},D_{N,S}\) be the principal minors with original normalization \(N^{-1/2}\), and write
\[
 \mathcal L_{N,S}=I_3\otimes C_{N,S}+T\otimes D_{N,S}+K\otimes I_n,
 \qquad M_{N,S}=\mathcal L_{N,S}+\theta_NI_{3n}.
\]
The physical projections are \(P_i=I_3\otimes e_ie_i^T\) for retained physical positions.
All statements below concern fixed deletion bounds, not a number of deletions growing with \(N\).

### Fixed-deletion geometry and weighted weak convergence

For every fixed integer \(R\ge0\) and fixed \(K_0\ge0\), the complete approximate-mode and whole-low-subspace conclusions of [C187v19](Yin_C_F0187_v19.md#statement) extend to all \(|S|\le R\), with fixed constants and failure \(p_N+Ce^{-cN}\), where \(p_N\to0\).
More explicitly, for every real \(V=[u_1\ u_2\ u_3]\) and real \(\lambda\), outside that common event,
\[
 \|V\|_F=1,\quad |\lambda|\le K_0,\quad
 \|C_{N,S}V+D_{N,S}VT+VK-\lambda V\|_F\le\rho
 \ \Longrightarrow\
 s_2(V)\ge d,\quad\min_j\|u_j\|\ge m,\quad
 V^TV+TV^TVT\succeq gI_3.
 \tag{Fixed-deletion-combined-geometry}
\]
The constants \(\rho,d,m,g>0\) depend only on the fixed laws, \(K_0,R\).
In particular the same combined Gram bound holds for every real unit vector in every spectral subspace of \(\mathcal L_{N,S}\) centered in \([-K_0,K_0]\) with radius at most \(\rho\), including arbitrary repeated eigenspaces.

Let \(s_1,s_2\) be free variance-one semicircular elements and
\[
 \ell=K\otimes1+I_3\otimes s_1+T\otimes s_2.
\]
Let \(\mu\) be its scalar spectral law under \(\operatorname{tr}_3\otimes\tau\), and let \(\mu_a\), \(a=1,2,3\), be its diagonal auxiliary spectral probability measures.
Then
\[
 d\mu/dx\le1/\pi,\qquad d\mu_a/dx\le3/\pi,\qquad
 \mu([-h,h])\ge c_0h\quad(0<h\le h_0)
 \tag{Free-three-channel-mass}
\]
for fixed \(c_0,h_0>0\).
With \(\Pi_a=e_ae_a^T\otimes I_n\), almost surely, for every bounded continuous \(f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb C\),
\[
 \max_{\substack{|S|\le R\\1\le a\le3}}
 \left|\frac1n\operatorname{Tr}\Pi_af(M_{N,S})
              -\int f\,d\mu_a\right|\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{Three-weighted-weak-AS}
\]
The event can be chosen to work for every such \(f\).
Consequently for each fixed sufficiently small \(h>0\), almost surely eventually,
\[
 \min_{|S|\le R}
 \frac{\operatorname{rank}\mathbf1_{[-h,h]}(M_{N,S})}{n}
 \ge c_1h,
 \tag{Three-low-count-AS}
\]
where \(c_1>0\) is independent of \(h\).

### Physical blocks and inverse ratios

There are fixed \(h_*,C_0>0\) such that, for each fixed \(0<h\le h_*\), with probability tending to one,
\[
 \max_{\substack{|S|\le r\\i\notin S}}
 \|P_i(M_{N,S}-ih)^{-1}P_i\|\le C_0,\qquad
 \max_{\substack{|S|\le r\\i\notin S}}
 \|P_i\mathbf1_{[-h,h]}(M_{N,S})P_i\|\le2C_0h.
 \tag{Three-physical-projector-statement}
\]
The constant \(C_0\) does not depend on \(h\).

For an invertible \(M_{N,S}\), define
\[
 Z=M_{N,S}^{-1},\quad F=\|Z\|_F,\quad Q=\|Z\|,\qquad
 d_i(Z)=\|P_iZ\|_F^2,\quad d_*(Z)=\max_i d_i(Z),\quad
 \mathcal D(Z)=\sum_i\|P_iZP_i\|_F^2.
\]
Uniformly over \(|S|\le r\) restricted to their actual invertible branches,
\[
 F^2/n\to\infty,\qquad Q\to\infty
 \quad\text{almost surely},
 \tag{Three-inverse-divergence-statement}
\]
\[
 d_*(Z)/F^2\to0,\qquad \mathcal D(Z)/F^2\to0
 \quad\text{in probability}.
 \tag{Three-inverse-spread-statement}
\]
Uniformity is unconditional: the probability of any permitted invertible minor violating a prescribed tolerance tends to zero; no conditioning on invertibility is made.
For the almost-sure divergence, for every fixed threshold eventually no permitted invertible minor is below that threshold.
Empty invertible collections impose no condition.

Choose the geometry constants for \(R=r+1\) and a fixed center range containing \(-\theta_N\) eventually.
For any fixed \(0<\rho_1\le\rho\), define
\[
 Z_{\rho_1}=Z\mathbf1_{[-\rho_1,\rho_1]}(M_{N,S}),\qquad
 R_{\rho_1}=Z-Z_{\rho_1},\qquad F_{\rho_1}=\|Z_{\rho_1}\|_F.
\]
Then
\[
 \|R_{\rho_1}\|\le\rho_1^{-1},\quad
 \|R_{\rho_1}\|_F^2\le3n/\rho_1^2,\quad
 F^2=F_{\rho_1}^2+\|R_{\rho_1}\|_F^2,
\]
\[
 F_{\rho_1}/F\to1,\quad
 \|R_{\rho_1}\|_F/F\to0,\quad
 F_{\rho_1}^2/n\to\infty,\quad
 \|R_{\rho_1}\|/Q\to0
 \quad\text{almost surely},
 \tag{Three-inverse-cutoff-statement}
\]
uniformly on those invertible branches, and \(\|Z_{\rho_1}\|=Q\) eventually.
The two spread ratios also hold for \(Z_{\rho_1}\) relative to its own squared Frobenius norm, in probability.
The divergence and two spread conclusions remain valid for the residual obtained by removing any fixed number of orthonormal eigenmodes from \(Z\), relative to the residual's own Frobenius and operator norms, uniformly over those choices, including within repeated eigenspaces.

### Actual high-effective-rank coefficient consequence

For \(z=(x,y)\in\mathbb R^n\oplus\mathbb R^n\), put \(V_a z=x+t_ay\), where \((t_1,t_2,t_3)=(0,1,\sqrt2)\).
For the physical blocks \(Z_{ab}\), define
\[
 K_{ab}(Z)=\tfrac12(V_a^TZ_{ab}V_b+V_b^TZ_{ba}V_a),\qquad
 h_Z(z)=\det[z^TK_{ab}(Z)z]_{a,b=1}^3.
\]
There is a fixed \(\eta_*>0\) such that, uniformly over the actual invertible minors, with probability tending to one,
\[
 \inf_{\|a\|=1}\left\|\sum_{b,c}a_ba_cK_{bc}(Z)\right\|_F
 \ge\eta_*F.
 \tag{Three-full-quadratic-statement}
\]
For the arithmetic-average symmetric degree-six tensor representing \(h_Z\), one also has
\[
 \|\mathscr T(Z)-\mathscr T(Z_{\rho_1})\|_F=o(F^3)
\]
uniformly almost surely on these branches.

Substitute formal independent signs \(x_i=\epsilon_{i,1}/\sqrt N\), \(y_i=\epsilon_{i,2}/\sqrt N\), and let \(c_J\) be the complete degree-six Walsh coefficients.
Let \(\sigma_{6,\mathrm{phys}}^2\) sum \(c_J^2\) over sets whose six physical positions are different, let \(\sigma_{6,\mathrm{complete}}^2=\sum_{|J|=6}c_J^2\), and let
\[
 L_i^{(6)}=\sum_{\substack{|J|=6\\
 J\cap(\{i\}\times\{1,2\})\ne\varnothing}}c_J^2.
\]
There are fixed \(R_*,c>0\) such that, uniformly over the actual invertible minors on the branch \(F^2\ge R_*Q^2\), with unconditional exceptional probability tending to zero,
\[
 \sigma_{6,\mathrm{phys}}^2\ge cF^6/N^6,\qquad
 L_i^{(6)}\le C_t F^4d_i(Z)/N^6,\qquad
 \max_i L_i^{(6)}/\sigma_{6,\mathrm{complete}}^2\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{Three-actual-high-rank-statement}
\]
The displayed load upper bound is deterministic for every such \(Z\); its normalization uses the energy lower bound on the high-rank good event.
The two coordinates at a physical position remain separate variables.
This is coefficient energy and load control, not anti-concentration for the original entry laws.

Finally, there exists a deterministic \(0<\theta_N<N^{-10}\) for which all minors with \(|S|\le r+1\) are simultaneously invertible almost surely.
This gives actual complete inverses for the shifted sequence, but does not by itself supply an unshifted floor.
No bounded-effective-rank coefficient conclusion, graph-normalized distance, singular-minor probability, least-singular-value floor, middle logarithmic estimate, determinant convergence or Brown-measure theorem is asserted.

### Extending the combined-Gram event to a fixed number of deletions

The fixed statements of C187v17 and C187v19 cover \(|S|\le1\).
Here their proofs give the following additional, ordinary extension: for any fixed integer \(R\), their approximate-mode and whole-low-subspace conclusions hold simultaneously over \(|S|\le R\), with failure \(p_N+Ce^{-cN}\), where \(p_N\to0\).
The constants may depend on \(R\), although only the sufficiently large dimension threshold needs to change in the construction.
This is a proof extension, not an assertion already present in those statements.

To check it, use the full-matrix tail, bad-mask and bounded-remainder events in [C187v17](Yin_C_F0187_v17.md#original-tail-conditioning) and [C187v19](Yin_C_F0187_v19.md#separate-original-law-events).
Every restricted tail has no larger Frobenius norm, every restricted bad degree is no larger, and every restricted bounded remainder has no larger norm.
These are single full-matrix events, so their \(o(1)\) probabilities are not multiplied by the number of minors.
The full conditioned-input weak law gives all the finitely many high-energy and spectral-window counts used in either proof on one common event.
Deleting at most \(R\) physical coordinates changes a scalar half-line eigenvalue count by at most \(R\), and hence an interval count by at most \(2R\), by principal interlacing.
Choose the full-matrix window counts strictly below their final budgets, as in the fixed proofs, and then take \(N\) large enough that these additive \(2R\) losses fit the slack.
In particular the bounds \(q\le\kappa n/4\), small-space dimension at most \(\kappa n\), and auxiliary projection codimension at most \(13\kappa n/4\) remain valid, since \(n\ge N-R\).
The full-input and tail high-energy counts can each be bounded by \(\kappa N/16\) before these harmless losses, still leaving the final \(\kappa n/4\) compression budget.
The constants \(K_C,K_D,H,B_L\), finite grids, residual tolerances and entropy budgets retain their order of choice.

The linear projected variance in C187v17 is still
\[
 \mathbb E\|PWz\|^2\ge
 \frac{\operatorname{rank}P-2-\delta_{\rm mask}N}{2N}\|z\|^2,
\]
which retains its fixed positive lower bound for the same fractional codimension and \(n=N-O_R(1)\).
In the quadratic endpoint proof of C187v19, the rectangles have rank at least \(n/3-1-q\), and consequently
\[
 \frac{\operatorname{rank}\Pi_J-N/32}{N}\ge\frac14,
 \qquad
 \frac{\operatorname{rank}\Pi_K-N/32}{N}\ge\frac14
\]
eventually, because \(q\le n/256\) and \(n\ge N-R\).
The two convex concentration estimates, the four-copy inequality and the fixed-parameter exponential rate therefore apply without changing the coordinate normalization or conditioning.
Every net has the same dimension budget and mesh as in the fixed proofs.
Their inequalities were chosen with strict exponential slack, so a union over at most \(C_RN^R\) minors and the same finite grids leaves \(C_Re^{-c_RN}\) after decreasing the exponent.
The full first-input and tail failures remain one common \(p_N=o(1)\), before that union.
Finally the rank-one exclusion and the opposite-conditioning column event are proved separately for the original law and intersected by a union bound exactly as in C187v19.
No conditioning on the other event is introduced.
The deterministic residual-to-singular-value, three-column-to-combined-Gram and whole-subspace arguments do not depend on the number of deletions.
This proves the stated extension and in particular supplies the extra deletion needed below.

<a id="free-zero-mass"></a>
### Free scalar mass at zero without a matrix-fiber hypothesis

Let \(s_1,s_2\) be free variance-one semicircular elements, and set
\[
 \ell=K\otimes1+I_3\otimes s_1+T\otimes s_2,\qquad
 G(z)=(\operatorname{id}_3\otimes\tau)((z-\ell)^{-1}).
\]
The reused [semicircular Cauchy identity and uniqueness proof](Yin_C_F0119_v1.md#resolvent-existence-and-pointwise-uniqueness), equations (6)--(7), apply to the fixed selfadjoint coefficients \(I_3,T,K\).
As in the [identity-direction density calculation](../ledger/Yin_L_F0066_v5.md#identity-direction-free-density), put \(V=-\operatorname{Im}G(z)>0\), obtaining
\[
 G^{-1}=zI_3-K-G-TGT,\qquad
 V=G^*(\operatorname{Im}z\,I_3+V+TVT)G\succeq G^*VG.
\]
Thus \(V^{1/2}GV^{-1/2}\) is a contraction, every eigenvalue of \(G\) has modulus at most one, and
\[
 0<-\operatorname{Im}\operatorname{tr}_3G(z)\le1.
 \tag{Three-free-Poisson-upper}
\]
Integrating this Poisson bound over an interval and applying Fatou gives the scalar spectral law \(\mu\) of \(\ell\) a density bounded by \(1/\pi\).
In particular it is atomless and its negative logarithm at zero is integrable.
Each positive diagonal auxiliary spectral measure \(\mu_a\) has mass one and obeys \(\mu_a\le3\mu\), so it too is atomless, with density at most \(3/\pi\).
No claim that these three densities are individually positive at zero is needed.

For the lower scalar mass, take \(z=i\eta\), \(0<\eta\le1\), and let \(J=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,1)\).
Transposition preserves the Dyson equation and its strict imaginary sign, so uniqueness gives \(G^T=G\).
Since \(JKJ=-K\) and \(JT=TJ\), uniqueness also gives \(G=-J\overline GJ\).
Write \(G=A-iV\), where \(A,V\) are real symmetric.
Then \(A\) has only the off-diagonal blocks between the two-dimensional \(+1\) eigenspace of \(J\) and its one-dimensional \(-1\) eigenspace.
Thus \(A\) is singular.
Also \(V\succeq0\) and \(\operatorname{Tr}V\le3\) by (Three-free-Poisson-upper).

Put \(H=\eta I_3+V+TVT\) and \(B=K+A+TAT\).
The inverse identity reads \(G^{-1}=-B+iH\), and the real part of \(\operatorname{Tr}(G^{-1}G)=3\) gives
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(A^2)+\operatorname{Tr}(ATAT)
       +\operatorname{Tr}(KA)=\operatorname{Tr}(HV)-3.
\]
All diagonal entries of \(T\) are nonnegative, so
\(\operatorname{Tr}(ATAT)=\sum_{i,j}t_it_j A_{ij}^2\ge0\).
Since \(\|K\|_F=2\), \(\|T\|^2=2\) and \(\operatorname{Tr}V\le3\),
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}(HV)\le3+9+18=30,
 \qquad \|A\|_F^2-2\|A\|_F\le27.
\]
Consequently
\[
 \|G\|\le4+2\sqrt7,\qquad
 \|G^{-1}\|\le C_*:=13+\sqrt2+6\sqrt7.
\]
Choose a real unit vector \(v\in\ker A\).
Then \(Gv=-iVv\), whence
\[
 1\le\|G^{-1}\|\,\|Vv\|
 \le C_*\operatorname{Tr}V,
 \qquad
 -\operatorname{Im}\operatorname{tr}_3G(i\eta)
 \ge a_*:=\frac1{3C_*}>0.
 \tag{Three-free-Poisson-lower}
\]
This argument uses the odd path's two-to-one parity split and the nonnegative slopes; the identity-noise density upper bound alone would not provide this lower bound for arbitrary coefficients.

Choose a fixed \(b>1\) with \(2/(\pi b)\le a_*/2\).
The density upper bound makes the contribution from \(|x|>b\eta\) to the scalar Poisson transform at most \(2/(\pi b)\).
Since its kernel on the remaining interval is at most \(1/\eta\), (Three-free-Poisson-lower) gives
\[
 \mu([-b\eta,b\eta])\ge(a_*/2)\eta.
\]
After putting \(h=b\eta\), there are fixed \(c_0,h_0>0\) such that
\[
 \mu([-h,h])\ge c_0h\qquad(0<h\le h_0).
 \tag{Three-free-linear-mass}
\]
This completes the free input, rather than leaving an unproved free-atom or matrix-fiber regularity premise.

### Weighted weak limits for the actual finite-variance minors

Let \(\Pi_a=e_ae_a^T\otimes I_n\) and
\[
 \nu_{N,S,a}(E)=n^{-1}\operatorname{Tr}\Pi_a\mathbf1_E(M_{N,S}).
\]
Write \(F_{N,S,a}(z)=\int(t-z)^{-1}\,d\nu_{N,S,a}(t)\).
For every fixed deletion bound \(R\), almost surely these probability measures converge weakly to \(\mu_a\), uniformly over \(|S|\le R\) and \(a=1,2,3\).
Here is the precise application of the C187v10 mechanism.
Normalize centered bounded off-diagonal cutoffs to variance one and use zero diagonal entries, exactly as in [its expected-resolvent calculation](Yin_C_F0187_v10.md#finite-variance-weighted-resolvents).
If \(e_L\to0\) is the sum of the two coordinate \(L^2\) cutoff errors, the new fixed coefficient matrices give
\[
 \mathbb E\|M_{N,\varnothing}-M^{L}_{N,\varnothing}\|_F^2
 \le C N(e_L+\theta_N^2)+C\sum_{j=1}^2\mathbb E\zeta_j^2.
\]
Restriction cannot increase this difference.
The bounded colored-moment proof in [C186v4](Yin_C_F0186_v4.md#bounded-moments), with the auxiliary trace replaced by its \(a\)-th diagonal entry as explicitly checked in [C187v4](Yin_C_F0187_v4.md#weighted-minor-mass), gives the cutoff weighted expected weak limit \(\mu_a\).
The physical index graph and its paired-tree power count are unchanged by the three-dimensional coefficient matrices, and \(n/N\to1\).
For fixed \(z\) with imaginary part \(y>0\), the Hilbert--Schmidt resolvent bound remains
\[
 \frac1n\left|\operatorname{Tr}\Pi_a[(A-z)^{-1}-(B-z)^{-1}]\right|
 \le\frac{\|A-B\|_F}{\sqrt n\,y^2}.
\]
Taking first \(N\to\infty\), then the cutoff to infinity, proves convergence of the expected weighted resolvents.
There are only \(R+1\) deletion sizes and the law within each size is exchangeable, so that convergence is uniform over all the stated minors.

Replacement of one independent first-upper-index group changes one physical block row and column, now of rank at most six.
The resolvent difference has rank at most six and norm at most \(2/y\), giving replacement bound \(12/(ny)\) for each weighted normalized trace.
The same accepted bounded-differences inequality as in [C187v10](Yin_C_F0187_v10.md#weighted-rank-concentration) therefore gives
\[
 \mathbb P\left\{\max_{|S|\le R,a}
 |F_{N,S,a}(z)-\mathbb EF_{N,S,a}(z)|>t\right\}
 \le C_RN^R e^{-cNy^2t^2}.
\]
It is summable for fixed \(z,t\), without an input-entry norm bound.
First Borel--Cantelli on a countable dense set of \(z\), the integer imaginary points \(iT\), and positive rational tolerances gives simultaneous uniform convergence of these transforms.
The tightness inequality and Poisson-transform uniqueness argument in that same fixed input apply unchanged to probability measures and give the asserted uniform weighted weak limit for every bounded continuous test on one probability-one event.
This use does not assume weighted eigenvalue interlacing.

Taking the average of the three auxiliary measures and using (Three-free-linear-mass), a continuous cutoff equal to one on \([-h/2,h/2]\) and supported in \((-h,h)\) gives
\[
 \operatorname{rank}\mathbf1_{[-h,h]}(M_{N,S})\ge c_1hn
 \tag{Three-all-minor-low-mass}
\]
almost surely eventually for every fixed sufficiently small \(h>0\), uniformly over \(|S|\le R\).
The positive constant \(c_1\) is independent of the window.

<a id="fresh-row-covariance"></a>
### The combined Gram is the missing fresh-row covariance

Fix \(|S|\le r\), \(i\notin S\), \(n'=N-|S|-1\), and condition only on the actual further-deleted minor \(M_{N,S\cup\{i\}}\).
Let \(P=\mathbf1_{[-h,h]}(M_{N,S\cup\{i\}})\).
Choose \(h\le\rho\), where \(\rho\) is the extended C187v19 low-window constant for a fixed center range containing all \(-\theta_N\) eventually.
On its local Gram and mass conditions, take a real orthonormal basis \(v^\ell=(u_1^\ell,u_2^\ell,u_3^\ell)\) of \(\operatorname{ran}P\).
Write \(V_\ell=[u_1^\ell\ u_2^\ell\ u_3^\ell]\).
For a unit \(a\in\mathbb C^3\), the raw border has the form
\[
 \widehat B_i a=\sqrt N B_i a
 =\sum_{j=1}^{n'}\bigl(g_{1j}(a)\xi_{1j}+g_{2j}(a)\xi_{2j}\bigr),
\]
\[
 g_{1j}(a)=\sum_{k=1}^3a_ke_{kj},\qquad
 g_{2j}(a)=\sum_{k=1}^3t_ka_ke_{kj},\qquad
 w_{bj}=\|Pg_{bj}(a)\|^2\le2.
\]
The two fresh original rows are independent of the conditioned minor.
The path matrix contributes only within each physical diagonal block and does not enter this border.
Summing over physical coordinates and the real low basis gives exactly
\[
 H_a:=\sum_{b,j}w_{bj}
 =\sum_\ell a^*\bigl[(V_\ell)^TV_\ell+
                T(V_\ell)^TV_\ell T\bigr]a
 \ge g\operatorname{rank}(P)\|a\|^2
 \ge gc_1hn'.
 \tag{Three-combined-projected-energy}
\]
The real Gram lower bound extends to complex \(a\) by applying it separately to real and imaginary parts.
This is the substantive replacement for the positive \(2\times2\) slope covariance in C187v10.
For three channels that slope covariance has rank at most two and would not give the displayed bound on its own.

Choose a single raw cutoff \(L_0\), independent of \(h\), at which both original off-diagonal laws have cutoff probability at least \(1/2\) and conditional variance at least \(1/2\).
Expose the cutoff indicators and the values outside the cutoff, after conditioning on the minor.
The weighted-indicator exponential estimate in [C187v10](Yin_C_F0187_v10.md#finite-variance-projected-row) applies to \(0\le w_{bj}\le2\), and makes the retained weight at least \(H_a/4\), except with probability \(e^{-cH_a}\le e^{-chN}\).
The remaining coordinates are independent, have support diameter at most \(2L_0\), and produce an arbitrary fixed offset through the revealed values and their conditional means.
Their norm \(F_a=\|P\widehat B_i a\|\) is convex and \(\sqrt3\)-Lipschitz, since at every physical coordinate the raw map has squared Frobenius norm
\(\sum_k(1+t_k^2)|a_k|^2\le3\).
Its conditional second moment is at least \(H_a/8\ge chN\).
The accepted bounded-product convex concentration and its integrated median estimate, at the exact [C187v2 inequality (9) interface](Yin_C_F0187_v2.md#argument), therefore give
\[
 \mathbb P\{\|P\widehat B_i a\|<a_0\sqrt{hN}
       \mid M_{N,S\cup\{i\}}\}\le Ce^{-chN}
 \tag{Three-fresh-fixed-direction}
\]
whenever that conditioned minor has the stated local mass and Gram properties.
The constants are independent of the fixed sufficiently small \(h\), though the eventual dimension threshold may depend on \(h\).
No global combined-Gram event is inserted into the conditional law.

<a id="physical-projectors"></a>
### From conditional rows to physical projectors

Use the same polynomial raw-row guard as C187v10,
\[
 \Omega_N=\left\{\sum_{b=1}^2\sum_{j<k}|\xi_{b,jk}|^2\le N^4\right\},
 \qquad \mathbb P(\Omega_N^c)\le C/N^2.
\]
On this event every raw border has norm at most \(C N^2\).
A complex-unit-sphere net in \(\mathbb C^3\) of mesh \(a_0\sqrt{hN}/(2CN^2)\) has at most \(C h^{-3}N^9\) points.
Integrate (Three-fresh-fixed-direction) only against its permitted local minor condition, take the union over at most \(C_rN^{r+1}\) pairs \((S,i)\), and use the guard only for deterministic extension from the net.
It follows that
\[
 \mathbb P\left\{\begin{array}{c}
 \text{every required local mass and Gram condition holds, but}\\
 \exists(S,i): B_i^TPB_i\not\succeq c_2hI_3
 \end{array}\right\}
 \le C N^{-2}+C_r h^{-3}N^{r+10}e^{-chN}.
\]
The extended C187v19 event and (Three-all-minor-low-mass), applied with deletion bound \(r+1\), make the local conditions hold jointly with probability tending to one.
They are not used as extra conditioning on fresh coordinates.
Thus \(B_i^TPB_i\succeq c_2hI_3\) simultaneously with probability tending to one for each fixed window.

For \(R_i=(M_{N,S\cup\{i\}}-ih)^{-1}\), spectral calculus gives \(\operatorname{Im}R_i\succeq P/(2h)\).
The physical Schur block of \(M_{N,S}-ih\) is
\[
 A_i-ihI_3-B_i^TR_iB_i,
\]
where \(A_i\) is real symmetric even for arbitrary realized original diagonal entries.
Its negative imaginary part is at least \(c_2I_3/2\), so the norm of its inverse is at most \(2/c_2\).
The usual spectral projector inequality now yields, for some fixed \(C_0\),
\[
 \max_{|S|\le r,i}\|P_i(M_{N,S}-ih)^{-1}P_i\|\le C_0,
 \qquad
 \max_{|S|\le r,i}\|P_i\mathbf1_{[-h,h]}(M_{N,S})P_i\|
 \le2C_0h
 \tag{Three-physical-window-bound}
\]
with probability tending to one for every fixed sufficiently small \(h\).
All norms here are physical \(3\times3\) block norms, with zero extension to the ambient space if desired.
The constant does not depend on \(h\).
The conclusion is not upgraded to almost sure convergence from the summable conditional-row errors, because the local combined-Gram event itself only has an \(o(1)\) error.

<a id="inverse-and-truncation"></a>
### Full inverse and low-cutoff physical energy

On the probability-one weighted-limit event, (Three-all-minor-low-mass) gives for every invertible minor
\[
 F^2\ge c_1hn/h^2=c_1n/h.
\]
Taking \(N\to\infty\) first and then decreasing \(h\) along a countable sequence proves \(F^2/n\to\infty\) uniformly on the invertible branches, almost surely.
Also \(Q^2\ge F^2/(3n)\to\infty\).
For a fixed such \(h\), write \(Z=Z_<+Z_>\) at the spectral projector \(P_<\) for \([-h,h]\).
The pieces commute with the minor, are spectrally orthogonal, and satisfy \(\|Z_>\|\le h^{-1}\).
Using (Three-physical-window-bound),
\[
 \|P_iZ_<\|_F^2\le2C_0hF^2,
 \qquad \|P_iZ_>\|_F^2\le3h^{-2}.
\]
Their row energies add, since \(Z_<Z_>=0\), giving
\[
 d_*(Z)\le2C_0hF^2+3h^{-2}.
 \tag{Three-row-energy-split}
\]
For the physical diagonal blocks,
\[
 \|P_iZ_<P_i\|_F^2
 \le\|P_iP_<\|^2\|Z_<P_i\|_F^2
 \le2C_0h\|Z_<P_i\|_F^2.
\]
The high block has squared Frobenius norm at most \(3h^{-2}\).
The two-term square bound and summation therefore give
\[
 \mathcal D(Z)\le4C_0hF^2+6nh^{-2}.
 \tag{Three-diagonal-energy-split}
\]
At fixed \(h\), the error ratios vanish by the already established energy divergence.
Then choose \(h\) small for a prescribed tolerance and use the probability convergence of (Three-physical-window-bound).
This proves both ratios in (Three-inverse-spread-statement), uniformly in probability.
The maximum row ratio and the sum of physical diagonal energies have been estimated separately.

Fix any \(0<\rho_1\le\rho\), and put
\[
 Z_{\rho_1}=Z\mathbf1_{[-\rho_1,\rho_1]}(M_{N,S}),\qquad
 R_{\rho_1}=Z-Z_{\rho_1},\qquad F_{\rho_1}=\|Z_{\rho_1}\|_F.
\]
The fixed-cutoff comparison is exactly
\[
 \|R_{\rho_1}\|\le\rho_1^{-1},\qquad
 \|R_{\rho_1}\|_F^2\le3n/\rho_1^2,
 \qquad F^2=F_{\rho_1}^2+\|R_{\rho_1}\|_F^2.
\]
Hence uniformly on invertible branches, almost surely,
\[
 F_{\rho_1}/F\to1,\qquad
 \|R_{\rho_1}\|_F/F\to0,\qquad
 F_{\rho_1}^2/n\to\infty,
 \qquad\|R_{\rho_1}\|/Q\to0.
 \tag{Three-low-full-comparison}
\]
There are low eigenvalues eventually by (Three-all-minor-low-mass), so \(\|Z_{\rho_1}\|=Q\) eventually.
Repeating (Three-row-energy-split) and (Three-diagonal-energy-split) with \(h<\rho_1\) and with the high band now restricted to \(h<|\lambda|\le\rho_1\) gives
\[
 d_*(Z_{\rho_1})/F_{\rho_1}^2\to0,
 \qquad \mathcal D(Z_{\rho_1})/F_{\rho_1}^2\to0
\]
in probability.
This direct diagonal argument avoids inferring the diagonal ratio from the row ratio.

The same calculation permits deletion of any fixed number \(k\) of orthonormal eigenmodes, including arbitrary choices inside repeated eigenspaces.
The resulting residual still commutes with the minor, its low mass loses at most \(k\), and its energy is at least \((c_1hn-k)/h^2\).
Its high spectral part has norm at most \(h^{-1}\), so all the displayed row and diagonal estimates apply with its own total Frobenius energy.
This observation has uniformity over the removed modes and requires no simplicity assumption.

<a id="actual-high-rank-coefficients"></a>
### Supplying the actual high-effective-rank coefficient input

Let \(U=(U_1,U_2,U_3)\) be the isometric inclusion of the whole low spectral subspace of \(M_{N,S}\), and let \(A=(M_{N,S}|_{\operatorname{ran}U})^{-1}\).
Then \(Z_{\rho_1}=UAU^T\) and \(\|A\|_F=F_{\rho_1}\).
Define \(\widetilde U_jh=(U_jh,t_jU_jh)\) and \(L_a=\sum_j a_j\widetilde U_j\) for \(a\in\mathbb R^3\).
On the combined-Gram event,
\[
 L_a^*L_a\succeq g\|a\|^2I.
\]
For any selfadjoint signed \(A\), multiplication on each side gives
\[
 \|L_aAL_a^*\|_F\ge g\|a\|^2\|A\|_F.
\]
Indeed each of \(\|L_aB\|_F\ge\sqrt g\|a\|\|B\|_F\) and its transpose version holds columnwise; no positivity of \(A\) is used.
With \(V_j(x,y)=x+t_jy\) and the physically local matrices \(K_{ij}(Z)\) defined in C187v18, this is exactly
\[
 \left\|\sum_{i,j}a_ia_jK_{ij}(Z_{\rho_1})\right\|_F
 \ge gF_{\rho_1}\|a\|^2.
\]
The combined maps need not have an isometric stack for this argument, because it checks C187v18's quadratic-separation input directly.
Their original stack \(U\) is isometric, and their local border map \(B_a(x,y)=(a_j(x+t_jy))_{j=1}^3\) has norm at most \(\sqrt3\|a\|\).
Consequently
\[
 \left\|\sum_{i,j}a_ia_j
       [K_{ij}(Z)-K_{ij}(Z_{\rho_1})]\right\|_F
 \le3\|a\|^2\|R_{\rho_1}\|_F.
\]
The low-to-full comparison proves, uniformly with probability tending to one on all invertible branches,
\[
 \inf_{\|a\|=1}\left\|\sum_{i,j}a_ia_jK_{ij}(Z)\right\|_F
 \ge(g/2)F.
 \tag{Three-full-inverse-quadratic-separation}
\]
If desired, the fully symmetrized raw determinant tensor itself obeys the telescoping bound
\[
 \|\mathscr T(Z)-\mathscr T(Z_{\rho_1})\|_F
 \le C_t(F+F_{\rho_1})^2\|R_{\rho_1}\|_F=o(F^3),
\]
because each of its six signed terms is a triple tensor product of local blocks and arithmetic-average symmetrization contracts Frobenius norm.
This is a raw tensor comparison, not a coefficient-probability conclusion.

Apply the physical part of the adopted C187v18 with \(p=3\), \(t=(0,1,\sqrt2)\), and separation parameter \(g/2\).
There is a fixed effective-rank threshold \(R_*\) such that, uniformly over the actual invertible minors on the branch \(F^2\ge R_*Q^2\), with unconditional exceptional probability tending to zero,
\[
 \sigma_{6,\mathrm{phys}}^2\ge cF^6/N^6,
 \qquad L_i^{(6)}\le C_tF^4d_i(Z)/N^6,
 \qquad
 \frac{\max_iL_i^{(6)}}{\sigma_{6,\mathrm{complete}}^2}\to0.
 \tag{Three-actual-high-rank-coefficients}
\]
Here the signs are the formal coefficient variables \(x_i=\epsilon_{i,1}/\sqrt N\), \(y_i=\epsilon_{i,2}/\sqrt N\), as in the fixed theorem.
The two variables at one physical row remain distinct independent variables, although the lower energy uses only monomials with all six physical rows distinct.
The complete sixth-layer energy is at least the displayed physical subenergy.
The collision hypothesis is now supplied by the separately proved \((\mathcal D+d_*)/F^2\to0\), rather than imposed on an unspecified inverse.
This proves an actual high-rank coefficient interface for the three-channel model, while retaining the original \(N^{-1/2}\) row scale.

<a id="atom-avoidance-and-scope"></a>
### Invertibility and the remaining original-contract boundary

All conclusions above hold with \(\theta_N=0\) on actual invertible branches; they supply no estimate for how often such branches occur.
Alternatively, the countable eigenvalue-atom avoidance argument in [C187v10](Yin_C_F0187_v10.md#finite-variance-atom-corollary) applies verbatim to the finitely many ordered eigenvalue laws of this three-channel pencil for \(|S|\le r+1\).
For every \(N\), choose \(0<\theta_N<N^{-10}\) outside the countable union of negatives of their atoms.
All these shifted minors are then simultaneously invertible almost surely, including across sizes after a countable intersection.
The shifted sequence therefore supplies literal complete inverses for the preceding interface.
This countable avoidance is not an unshifted least-singular-value floor and does not transfer a shifted determinant conclusion back by itself.

No new free no-atom or matrix-fiber hypothesis remains in the physical-spread argument for this fixed pencil.
The unresolved chain begins later: the bounded-effective-rank regimes not covered by a comparable third inverse singular value still need quantitative determinant coefficient control, and formal-sign coefficient energy must be converted under the original finite-variance fresh-row law with the required graph normalization and singular-minor bookkeeping.
Those steps, a usable floor, the middle negative-logarithmic estimate and the original determinant conclusion are not proved here.
The special path symmetry and the coefficient-specific combined-Gram event also prevent treating this calculation as a result for the arbitrary matrix coefficients of F13 or the arbitrary polynomials of F14.
