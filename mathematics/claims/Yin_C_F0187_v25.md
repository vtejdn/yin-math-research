# Large-block joint gaps and the three-channel middle singular-value range

This theorem proves a weighted joint gap on large deterministic principal blocks and uses it to control the true retained-row kernel covariance in every auxiliary direction.
The resulting middle profile has exponent \(7/2\), and its omitted bottom range is compatible with the separately proved polynomial floor.
The argument has ordinary collaborative development and an [independent whole-proof review](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_F0191_v25.md#three-channel-middle-whole-proof), adopted at the [current decision](#decision-three-channel-middle-reviewed).
The main research agent supplied the central-channel reduction through the large principal-block joint gap and the proposed middle scaling; the new complete joint-gap supply and all-direction bridge are proved here.
The [deterministic Frobenius-only obstruction](../ledger/Yin_L_F0066_v8.md#three-channel-middle-frobenius-obstruction) remains valid because the present proof uses additional random joint geometry.
No working draft is a logical dependency.

<a id="statement"></a>
## Exact model and conclusions

Let \(C_N,D_N\) be independent real symmetric Wigner matrices.
For input \(\ell=1,2\), the strict upper entries are independent copies of \(\xi_\ell/\sqrt N\), where \(\xi_\ell\) is fixed, real, centered, and has variance one.
Its diagonal entries are independent copies of \(\zeta_\ell/\sqrt N\), where \(\zeta_\ell\) is fixed, real, centered, and has finite second moment.
All upper-triangular coordinates and the two inputs are independent within each size, and coupling across dimensions is arbitrary.
No density, bounded-support, higher-moment, or original-input operator-norm assumption is made.
Put
\[
 T:=\operatorname{diag}(0,1,\sqrt2),\qquad
 K:=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\1&0&1\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 L_N:=I_3\otimes C_N+T\otimes D_N+K\otimes I_N.
\]
Singular values are listed in decreasing order.
The middle assertion is that fixed \(c>0\) and \(p_N\to0\) exist such that, for every fixed \(D_*>0\), suitable fixed \(A_{D_*},C_{D_*}>0\) give
\[
 m_N:=\left\lceil A_{D_*}N^{5/6}(\log N)^{1/6}\right\rceil,
 \qquad
 \mathbb P\left\{\exists\,6m_N\le j\le3N-1:
       s_{3N-j}(L_N)<c(j/N)^{7/2}\right\}
 \le p_N+C_{D_*}N^{-D_*}
 \tag{Candidate-three-middle}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(N\).
The common error \(p_N\) is paid once and is not claimed summable.

The proof also establishes the [weighted joint gap](#large-block-joint-gap) simultaneously over any specified deterministic family of at most \(N^2\) principal blocks of size at least \(N/3\), uniformly over all real scalar centers with lower bound \(\gamma/(1+|\beta|)\).
Its deterministic [retained-kernel bridge](#retained-kernel-trace), under that joint gap on the retained principal block and a Frobenius guard on the surrounding minor, gives \(H_a\ge cN(m/N)^6\) for every real unit auxiliary direction.
The [middle logarithmic estimate](#middle-logarithms) has modulus \(Ct^{2/7}(1+|\log t|)\).
Together with the fixed C194v16 floor, it yields negative-logarithmic uniform integrability in probability for this pencil: for increasing singular values \(a_1,\ldots,a_{3N}\) and every \(\varepsilon>0\),
\[
 \lim_{t\downarrow0}\limsup_{N\to\infty}
 \mathbb P\left\{\frac1{3N}\sum_{a_r<t}(-\log a_r)>\varepsilon\right\}=0.
\]
No almost-sure middle estimate, logarithmic determinant limit, arbitrary matrix-coefficient or complex-input extension, or Task completion is asserted.

## Complete proof

<a id="large-block-joint-gap"></a>
### A uniform weighted joint gap on large deterministic principal blocks

Let \(\mathscr U_N\) be any deterministic family of at most \(N^2\) subsets \(U\subset[N]\), each of size \(n_U\ge N/3\).
Write \(C_U:=C_N[U,U]\) and \(D_U:=D_N[U,U]\), keeping normalization \(N^{-1/2}\).
The required new random assertion is that a fixed \(\gamma>0\) satisfies, simultaneously for every \(U\in\mathscr U_N\), every real \(\alpha,\beta\), and every \(h\in\mathbb R^U\),
\[
 \max\{\|(C_U-\alpha I)h\|,\|(D_U-\beta I)h\|\}
 \ge\frac{\gamma}{1+|\beta|}\|h\|.
 \tag{Candidate-large-block-joint-gap}
\]
Its exceptional probability is at most \(p_{T,N}+Ce^{-cN}\), with one common \(p_{T,N}\to0\).
In particular the zero-center gap has a fixed positive constant, while centers of size \(B\ge1\) have a gap at least a fixed multiple of \(B^{-1}\).

Here is the complete extension argument.
Fix \(\delta:=1/64\).
Split the second input as \(D_N=\Theta_N+W_N\) by the exact fixed-cutoff construction of C187v12, choosing one sufficiently large raw cutoff so that the conditional good variance is at least \(1/2\), the bad-edge probability is less than \(\delta/(2e)\), and the normalized tail second moment is less than \(\delta/2\).
The sigma-field of all cutoff indicators, bad values, and original second-input diagonals makes \(\Theta_N\) fixed and leaves the good raw coordinates of \(W_N\) independent and centered, with fixed bounded support.
Its full-matrix events satisfy
\[
 \mathbb P\{\|\Theta_N\|_F^2>\delta N\}=p_{T,N}\longrightarrow0,
 \qquad
 \mathbb P\{\max_i\deg_{\rm bad}(i)>\delta N\}\le Ce^{-cN},
\]
\[
 \mathbb P\{\|W_N\|>B_0\mid\text{tail data}\}\le Ce^{-cN}
 \tag{Candidate-common-tail-guards}
\]
for a fixed \(B_0\).
These are full-matrix events and bound all their principal restrictions without multiplying \(p_{T,N}\) by the number of sets.

Condition also on the entire first input.
For any specified conditioned subspace \(V\subset\mathbb R^U\) of dimension at most \(\kappa n_U\), with \(0<\kappa\le1/64\), let
\[
 P_0:=\mathbf1_{[-1,1]}(\Theta_U),\qquad
 P_V:=P_{\operatorname{ran}P_0\cap(V+\Theta_UV)^\perp}.
\]
Then
\[
 \operatorname{codim}P_V\le\delta N+2\kappa n_U,\qquad
 \|P_V\Theta_U\|\le1,\qquad
 P_V(D_U-\beta I)v=P_VW_Uv\quad(v\in V,\ \beta\in\mathbb R).
\]
On the full tail and mask guards, the C187v12 covariance calculation gives, for every fixed unit \(z\in\mathbb R^U\),
\[
 \mathbb E[\|P_VW_Uz\|^2\mid C_N,\text{tail data}]
 \ge\frac{n_U-2\kappa n_U-2\delta N-2}{2N}
 \ge\frac18
\]
eventually, since \(n_U\ge N/3\).
The norm is convex and \(\sqrt{2/N}\)-Lipschitz in the remaining raw coordinates.
The accepted bounded-product concentration and its integrated median estimate therefore give a fixed-vector lower tail \(Ce^{-c_0N}\) below a fixed positive constant.
Choose a fixed sphere-net mesh using \(B_0\), and then choose \(\kappa>0\) sufficiently small that the entropy \(\kappa N\log(3/\text{mesh})\) is less than \(c_0N/4\).
The C187v12 net extension, using the full \(W_N\) norm event only after establishing the conditional estimates, yields a fixed \(\eta>0\) such that
\[
 \inf_{\substack{v\in V\\\|v\|=1}}\|P_VW_Uv\|\ge\eta,
 \qquad
 \|P_V(D_U-\beta I)\|\le1+B_0+|\beta|,
 \tag{Candidate-all-center-small-space}
\]
outside conditional probability \(Ce^{-cN}\).
The first bound holds simultaneously for every second center \(\beta\) after replacing \(W_Uv\) by \((D_U-\beta I)v\).
The empty sphere imposes no condition.

It remains to supply finitely many such spaces for each \(U\), uniformly over every first center.
The law of \(C_U\) is that of \(\sqrt{n_U/N}\) times the normalized Wigner matrix of size \(n_U\) with the fixed first-input laws.
The expected empirical distribution functions of those normalized matrices converge uniformly on the real line to the continuous semicircle distribution function.
Indeed weak convergence gives convergence at each real point, bounded convergence gives the same for expectations, and a finite monotonicity grid for a continuous distribution function upgrades this to uniform convergence.
Taking the supremum over all sizes \(n_U\ge N/3\) preserves convergence to zero.
Consequently the expected spectral counts of \(C_U\) are uniformly approximated by semicircle laws scaled by \(\sqrt{n_U/N}\in[1/\sqrt3,1]\).
These scaled laws have density at most \(\sqrt3/\pi\) and support in \([-2,2]\).

Choose \(A:=3\), a sufficiently small fixed \(h_0>0\), and a finite \(h_0\)-mesh grid \(\{\alpha_\ell\}\) covering \([-A-h_0,A+h_0]\).
Choose \(h_0\) small enough that every interval \([\alpha_\ell-2h_0,\alpha_\ell+2h_0]\) has mass below \(\kappa/8\) under every scaled limiting law.
For every \(U\), all these intervals and the set \(\{|x|>A\}\) have expected empirical mass below \(\kappa/4\) eventually.
Changing one independent upper-triangular row block of \(C_U\) changes its rank by at most two.
For each of these indicator functions, whose total variation is at most two, the normalized count therefore changes by at most \(4/n_U\).
The accepted ordinary bounded-differences inequality gives probability at most \(2e^{-c\kappa^2n_U}\) for a fixed deviation of size \(\kappa/2\).
A union over the fixed grid, the one tail count, and at most \(N^2\) sets yields, with exception \(Ce^{-cN}\), the simultaneous bounds
\[
 \dim\operatorname{ran}\mathbf1_{[\alpha_\ell-2h_0,\alpha_\ell+2h_0]}(C_U)
 \le\kappa n_U,\qquad
 \dim\operatorname{ran}\mathbf1_{\{|C_U|>A\}}\le\kappa n_U.
 \tag{Candidate-large-block-window-counts}
\]
This is a concentration estimate around expectations, followed by an eventual deterministic expectation bound.
It does not multiply an unquantified weak-convergence probability by \(N^2\).
Nor does it use interlacing across a number of deleted coordinates proportional to \(N\).

Apply (Candidate-all-center-small-space) to these finitely many conditioned spaces for all \(U\), and take their union before integrating the conditional law.
There are only a fixed multiple of \(N^2\) spaces.
The only nonexponential error remains the one common tail Frobenius error.
For a unit \(z\), put
\[
 r_C:=\|(C_U-\alpha I)z\|,\qquad
 r_D:=\|(D_U-\beta I)z\|.
\]
If \(|\alpha|\le A+h_0\), choose a grid center within \(h_0\), and use its spectral space \(V\).
If \(|\alpha|>A+h_0\), use the single space \(V=\operatorname{ran}\mathbf1_{\{|C_U|>A\}}\).
In either case spectral calculus gives \(\operatorname{dist}(z,V)\le r_C/h_0\).
If \(r_C<h_0/4\), the normalized projection \(z'\in V\) exists and satisfies \(\|z-z'\|\le2r_C/h_0\).
Thus
\[
 \eta
 \le\|P_V(D_U-\beta I)z'\|
 \le r_D+\frac{2(1+B_0+|\beta|)}{h_0}r_C.
\]
If instead \(r_C\ge h_0/4\), a joint lower bound already holds.
In both cases a sufficiently small fixed \(\gamma>0\) gives (Candidate-large-block-joint-gap), after homogeneity.
This proves the claimed random supply at its actual growing-deletion and all-center scope.

<a id="rank-one-residual"></a>
### A deterministic rank-one residual bound from the joint gap

The next deterministic calculation is applied to a block \(U\) satisfying (Candidate-large-block-joint-gap).
Let \(b\in\mathbb R^3\) be unit, let \(h\in\mathbb R^U\), and assume
\[
 \|D_Uh\|\le B\|h\|,\qquad B\ge1.
\]
Define the three-channel residual
\[
 \mathcal R_bh:=b\otimes C_Uh+(Tb)\otimes D_Uh+(Kb)\otimes h.
\]
There is a fixed \(c_1>0\), depending only on \(\gamma,T,K\), such that
\[
 \|\mathcal R_bh\|\ge c_1B^{-2}\|h\|
 \quad\text{for every unit }b.
 \tag{Candidate-rank-one-residual}
\]
Here and below constants are independent of \(U,N,m,B\).

The case \(h=0\) is immediate.
For \(h\ne0\), normalize \(\|h\|=1\), and write
\[
 t:=\langle b,Tb\rangle,\quad k:=\langle b,Kb\rangle,\quad
 v:=Tb-tb,\quad w:=Kb-kb,\quad \sigma:=\|v\|.
\]
There is no common real eigenvector of \(T\) and \(K\): the eigenspaces of \(T\) are coordinate lines, and \(K\) preserves none of them.
Compactness of the unit sphere therefore gives
\[
 d_*:=\min_{\|b\|=1}(\|v\|+\|w\|)>0.
\]
If \(\sigma\le d_*/(4B)\), then \(\|w\|\ge3d_*/4\), and projection of \(\mathcal R_bh\) to \(b^\perp\) gives
\[
 \|\mathcal R_bh\|\ge\|w\|-\sigma\|D_Uh\|
 \ge d_*/2.
\]
Otherwise \(\sigma>d_*/(4B)\).
Set \(q:=v/\sigma\), \(\beta:=-\langle q,w\rangle/\sigma\), and \(\alpha:=-(t\beta+k)\).
If \(r:=\|\mathcal R_bh\|\), projections onto \(q\) and \(b\) give
\[
 \|(D_U-\beta I)h\|\le r/\sigma,\qquad
 \|(C_U-\alpha I)h\|\le r+|t|r/\sigma.
\]
Also \(|\beta|+|\alpha|\le C B\), because \(\sigma^{-1}\le4B/d_*\).
The joint gap is therefore at least \(c/B\), whereas both residual upper bounds are at most \(CBr\).
It follows that \(r\ge c_1B^{-2}\), proving (Candidate-rank-one-residual).
No determinant lower bound for the auxiliary vectors \(b,Tb,Kb\) is assumed.

<a id="coordinate-observation-graphs"></a>
### Compression and the three coordinate observation graphs

Now fix a real symmetric minor pair \(C,D\) on a physical set \(V\) of size \(n\), with
\[
 \|C\|_F^2+\|D\|_F^2\le C_0N,\qquad C_0\ge1.
\]
Assume \(n\le N\), let \(J\subset V\) have size \(1\le m\le n\), put \(U:=V\setminus J\), and assume that \(C[U,U],D[U,U]\) satisfy (Candidate-large-block-joint-gap).
Define
\[
 M:=I_3\otimes C+T\otimes D+K\otimes I_n,\quad
 \mathcal K:=\ker(R_UM),\quad P:=P_{\mathcal K},\quad
 B:=\max\{1,\sqrt{12C_0N/m}\}.
\]
Here \(R_U\) restricts each of the three channels to \(U\), so the \(3(n-m)\) retained rows give \(\dim\mathcal K\ge3m\).
Let
\[
 H_0:=\operatorname{ran}\mathbf1_{[-B,B]}(C)
          \cap\operatorname{ran}\mathbf1_{[-B,B]}(D).
\]
Then
\[
 \operatorname{codim}H_0\le C_0N/B^2\le m/12,\qquad
 \|Cx\|,\|Dx\|\le B\|x\|\quad(x\in H_0).
\]
Imposing \(u_1,u_2,u_3\in H_0\) on a vector \(z=(u_1,u_2,u_3)\in\mathcal K\) removes at most \(3\operatorname{codim}H_0\le m/4\) dimensions.
This is an intersection of spectral domains, not an assertion that \(C,D\) commute or preserve \(H_0\).
Every linear combination of the three components still belongs to \(H_0\), so the displayed input norm bounds apply to all component errors used below.

For the first coordinate, further impose \(u_{2,J}=u_{3,J}=0\).
The resulting subspace \(F_1\subset\mathcal K\) has dimension at least \(3m-m/4-2m\ge3m/4\).
The retained path equations give, with zero extension from \(U\),
\[
 u_2=-\operatorname{ext}_U C[U,V]u_1,\qquad
 u_3=-\operatorname{ext}_U\bigl(u_{1,U}+(C+D)[U,V]u_2\bigr).
\]
Consequently \(\|u_2\|\le B\|u_1\|\) and \(\|u_3\|\le(1+2B^2)\|u_1\|\).
The reversed path gives the same type of bound on a subspace \(F_3\) of dimension at least \(3m/4\), with constants allowing the fixed coefficient \(\sqrt2\).
Thus
\[
 \|z\|\le C B^2\|u_j\|\quad(z\in F_j,\ j=1,3).
\]

For the second coordinate, impose instead \(u_{1,J}=u_{3,J}=0\), in addition to the same three-component compression.
The resulting \(F_2\) again has dimension at least \(3m/4\).
Put \(e:=u_1+u_3\), so \(e_J=0\), \(e\in H_0\), and the second retained equation gives
\[
 \|e\|\le2B\|u_2\|.
\]
The first and third retained equations, with \(E:=C+\sqrt2D\), yield
\[
 \|C[U,U]u_{1,U}\|\le\|u_2\|,\qquad
 E[U,U]u_{1,U}=E[U,V]e+u_{2,U}.
\]
Hence \(\|E[U,U]u_{1,U}\|\le C B^2\|u_2\|\), and the same bound holds for \(D[U,U]u_{1,U}\).
The zero-center case of the joint gap gives \(\|u_1\|\le C B^2\|u_2\|\); the bound for \(e\) then controls \(u_3\).
We have proved the uniform coordinate estimates
\[
 \dim F_j\ge3m/4,\qquad
 \|u_j\|\ge c_2B^{-2}\|z\|\quad(z\in F_j,\ j=1,2,3).
 \tag{Candidate-coordinate-graphs}
\]
The center case is where the new random joint gap replaces the false Frobenius-only premise.

<a id="retained-kernel-trace"></a>
### All auxiliary directions and the actual kernel trace

Write \((t_1,t_2,t_3):=(0,1,\sqrt2)\).
For a real unit \(a=(a_1,a_2,a_3)\), define
\[
 \mathcal O_a z:=\left(\sum_j a_ju_j,\ \sum_j t_ja_ju_j\right),
 \qquad
 H_a:=\operatorname{Tr}P\mathcal O_a^*\mathcal O_a,
 \qquad
 \delta(a):=\sqrt{1-\max_j a_j^2}.
\]
The target covariance is exactly
\(\mathcal O_a^*\mathcal O_a=(aa^T+(Ta)(Ta)^T)\otimes I_n\).
Choose a fixed sufficiently small \(\varepsilon_0>0\).
If \(\delta(a)\le\varepsilon_0B^{-2}\), choose \(j\) with maximal \(|a_j|\).
For \(z\in F_j\), the first component of \(\mathcal O_a z\) and Cauchy--Schwarz give
\[
 \|\mathcal O_a z\|
 \ge |a_j|\|u_j\|-\delta(a)\|z\|
 \ge c_3B^{-2}\|z\|,
\]
by (Candidate-coordinate-graphs) and a sufficiently small choice of \(\varepsilon_0\).
Signs of \(a_j\) have no effect on this inequality.

Suppose now that \(\delta(a)>\varepsilon_0B^{-2}\).
The two vectors \(a,Ta\) are independent.
Their \(2\)-by-\(2\) Gram determinant is
\[
 \|Ta\|^2-\langle a,Ta\rangle^2
 =\sum_{i<j}a_i^2a_j^2(t_i-t_j)^2
 \ge\frac{(\sqrt2-1)^2}{3}\delta(a)^2.
\]
Its largest eigenvalue is at most \(3\), so the smaller singular value of the \(3\)-by-\(2\) matrix \([a,Ta]\) is at least \(c_T\delta(a)\ge c_T\varepsilon_0B^{-2}\).
Let \(b\) be a real unit normal to \(\operatorname{span}(a,Ta)\), and decompose
\[
 z=b\otimes h+e,\qquad
 h:=\sum_jb_ju_j,\qquad
 e\in\operatorname{span}(a,Ta)\otimes\mathbb R^V.
\]
Impose the three-component \(H_0\) condition and \(h_J=0\) on \(\mathcal K\).
The resulting subspace \(F_a\) has dimension at least \(3m-m/4-m\ge7m/4\).
Its components \(h,e_1,e_2,e_3\) all lie in \(H_0\), and
\[
 \|e\|\le C B^2\|\mathcal O_a z\|,\qquad
 \|R_UMe\|\le C B\|e\|.
\]
Because \(h_J=0\), the retained kernel equation becomes exactly
\[
 b\otimes C[U,U]h_U+(Tb)\otimes D[U,U]h_U
                +(Kb)\otimes h_U=-R_UMe.
\]
Also \(\|D[U,U]h_U\|\le B\|h\|\).
Apply (Candidate-rank-one-residual) to obtain
\[
 \|h\|\le C B^3\|e\|,\qquad
 \|z\|\le C B^5\|\mathcal O_a z\|\quad(z\in F_a).
\]
Together with the near-coordinate case, every unit \(a\) therefore has a subspace of \(\mathcal K\) of dimension at least \(3m/4\) on which
\[
 \|\mathcal O_a z\|\ge c_4B^{-5}\|z\|.
\]
Taking an orthonormal basis of that subspace and using its projection being at most \(P\) proves the deterministic bridge
\[
 H_a\ge c mB^{-10}
 \ge c\frac{m^6}{N^5}
 =cN(m/N)^6
 \qquad(\|a\|=1).
 \tag{Candidate-retained-kernel-trace}
\]
The subspaces may depend on \(a\); this is a trace assertion, not a pointwise observation lower bound on every vector of \(\mathcal K\).
Only the joint gap of \(C[U,U],D[U,U]\) and the Frobenius guard on \(C,D\) entered this bridge.

<a id="fresh-row-middle-indices"></a>
### Fresh rows, common events, and the middle singular-value indices

For \(1\le m\le\lfloor N/2\rfloor\), let \(J_m:=\{N-m+1,\ldots,N\}\).
For \(i\notin J_m\), put \(V_i:=[N]\setminus\{i\}\) and \(U_{i,m}:=V_i\setminus J_m\).
For all sufficiently large \(N\), \(|U_{i,m}|\ge N/3\), and these sets form a deterministic family of at most \(N^2\) members.
Apply (Candidate-large-block-joint-gap) to that family.
Also take one common event
\[
 \Omega_{F,N}:=\{\|C_N\|_F^2+\|D_N\|_F^2\le C_0N\},
 \qquad C_0>2,
\]
whose failure tends to zero by the finite-mean weak law on squared off-diagonal entries and Markov on the normalized diagonals.
Every principal minor satisfies its Frobenius guard on this event.
All qualitative errors are included once in \(p_N:=p_{T,N}+\mathbb P(\Omega_{F,N}^c)\).

Retain the first \(N-m\) physical row blocks of \(\sqrt N L_N\), obtaining a \(3(N-m)\)-by-\(3N\) matrix \(\mathsf B_m\).
For a retained \(i\), remove its three physical columns from its current block and from all other rows.
Condition only on both actual principal minors on \(V_i\).
The span of the other projected rows has orthogonal complement
\[
 \mathcal K_{i,m}
 =\ker\!\left(R_{U_{i,m}}
    [I_3\otimes C_{V_i}+T\otimes D_{V_i}+K\otimes I_{N-1}]\right).
\]
Define the local guard \(\mathcal G_{i,m}\) by
\[
 \|C_{V_i}\|_F^2+\|D_{V_i}\|_F^2\le C_0N
\]
together with (Candidate-large-block-joint-gap) for \(U=U_{i,m}\), using the same fixed \(\gamma\) and all real centers and vectors.
This guard and the kernel projection are measurable with respect to the two conditioned minors.
The preceding common joint-gap event intersected with \(\Omega_{F,N}\) implies every \(\mathcal G_{i,m}\).
Both original fresh off-diagonal rows remain independent with their fixed laws; every mirrored fresh entry, deterministic path entry, and exposed diagonal disappears from this projected current block.
For that raw block \(F_i\) and a fixed real unit \(a\),
\[
 F_i^Ta=\sum_{j\in V_i}
 \left[(a\otimes e_j)\xi_{1j}+((Ta)\otimes e_j)\xi_{2j}\right].
\]
The squared projected coefficient weights lie in \([0,2]\), their sum is \(H_a\), and the raw linear map has operator norm at most \(\sqrt3\).
On \(\mathcal G_{i,m}\), (Candidate-retained-kernel-trace) gives \(H_a\ge c m^6/N^5\).

Use one fixed raw cutoff for both fresh laws, with cutoff probability and conditional variance at least \(1/2\).
The C187v11 weighted-indicator calculation makes retained weight a fixed fraction of \(H_a\), except with probability \(Ce^{-cH_a}\).
After exposing the indicators and discarded values, the remaining norm is convex and \(\sqrt3\)-Lipschitz with bounded independent coordinates, with arbitrary fixed offset and conditional second moment at least a fixed multiple of \(H_a\).
The accepted convex concentration and integrated median estimate give, once \(m^6/N^5\) exceeds a fixed constant,
\[
 \mathbb P\left\{\|P_{\mathcal K_{i,m}}F_i^Ta\|
                  <c\,m^3/N^{5/2}
       \,\middle|\, C_{V_i},D_{V_i}\right\}
 \le C\exp(-c m^6/N^5)
 \tag{Candidate-three-fresh-direction}
\]
on those local guards.
No full-matrix good event is inserted into this conditional fresh-row law.

On \(\Omega_{F,N}\), the raw row bound is \(\|F_i\|\le C N\).
A deterministic \(N^{-2}\)-net of the real unit sphere in \(\mathbb R^3\) has at most \(CN^6\) points and extension error at most \(C/N\).
For \(m\ge m_N\), this is smaller than half the preceding lower threshold eventually.
Integrate (Candidate-three-fresh-direction) on its local minor-measurable guards, then use the common events for the net extension and take a union over the net and at most \(N^2\) pairs \((i,m)\).
The resulting exception is
\[
 p_N+Ce^{-cN}
       +CN^8\exp(-c m_N^6/N^5)
 \le p_N+C_{D_*}N^{-D_*}
\]
after choosing \(A_{D_*}\) sufficiently large.

Let \(G_{i,m}\) be the full row-distance Gram of the current three-row block of \(\mathsf B_m\) against all its other rows.
Coordinate contraction under removal of the three columns gives
\[
 G_{i,m}\succeq c\,m^6N^{-5}I_3.
\]
Therefore every \(\mathsf B_m\) in the stated range has full row rank.
The block Schur identity for \((\mathsf B_m\mathsf B_m^T)^{-1}\) gives
\[
 \sum_{r=1}^{3(N-m)}s_r(\mathsf B_m)^{-2}
 =\sum_{i=1}^{N-m}\operatorname{Tr}G_{i,m}^{-1}
 \le C N^6/m^6.
\]
For \(6m_N\le j\le3N-1\), take \(m:=\lfloor j/6\rfloor\).
Then \(m_N\le m\le\lfloor N/2\rfloor\), \(m\ge j/12\) eventually, and \(j-3m+1\ge j/2\).
The index \(3N-j\) belongs to the singular list of \(\mathsf B_m\), and at least \(j-3m+1\) entries of that list are at most \(s_{3N-j}(\mathsf B_m)\).
Hence row-deletion interlacing and the factor \(\sqrt N\) give
\[
 s_{3N-j}(L_N)
 \ge N^{-1/2}s_{3N-j}(\mathsf B_m)
 \ge c\frac{\sqrt j\,m^3}{N^{7/2}}
 \ge c'(j/N)^{7/2}.
\]
This proves the middle assertion (Candidate-three-middle).

<a id="middle-logarithms"></a>
### Logarithmic range supplied by the middle estimate

Let \(a_1\le\cdots\le a_{3N}\) be the singular values of \(L_N\), and set \(r_N:=6m_N+2\).
On the same middle good event, the index conversion gives
\[
 a_r\ge c(r/(3N))^{7/2}\qquad(r\ge r_N).
\]
Therefore, for all sufficiently small fixed \(t>0\), the elementary logarithmic integral comparison gives
\[
 \frac1{3N}\sum_{\substack{r\ge r_N\\a_r<t}}(-\log a_r)
 \le C t^{2/7}(1+|\log t|).
 \tag{Candidate-three-middle-log}
\]
Indeed only \(r\le C N t^{2/7}\) can contribute, and summing
\(-\log c+\tfrac72\log(3N/r)\) over that interval gives the displayed bound.
The empty interval contributes zero.

The separately [proved original-model floor](Yin_C_F0194_v16.md#finite-variance-parent-floor) gives \(a_1\ge c_*N^{-17/2}\) outside a probability tending to zero.
On its intersection with the middle event, the omitted bottom indices contribute at most
\[
 \frac{r_N}{3N}\left(\frac{17}{2}\log N+|\log c_*|\right)
 =O\!\left(N^{-1/6}(\log N)^{7/6}\right)\longrightarrow0.
\]
Thus the middle estimate and that fixed floor provide negative-logarithmic uniform integrability in probability for this fixed pencil.
This last deduction neither re-proves nor changes the original-row floor.
The independent whole-proof finding covers this deduction and the growing-deletion joint-gap extension at the exact fixed-input boundaries.
It asserts no almost-sure middle estimate, arbitrary coefficient or complex-law extension, or completion of F0013 or F0014.
