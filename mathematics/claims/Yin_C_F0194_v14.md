# A finite-variance conditional graph-energy bridge for two-channel pencils

This version extends the [fair-sign graph bridge](Yin_C_F0194_v13.md#statement) to an arbitrary fixed real centered variance-one first off-diagonal law with finite second moment.
The second input remains any independent real symmetric random matrix.
A matching chosen from the formal fourth layer survives independent two-copy amplitude tests, while a pair-row norm guard supplies a graph-area bound uniformly over the remaining signs.
The theorem retains the average failure of its actual-minor coefficient hypotheses; it does not establish those hypotheses for unbounded off-diagonal laws.
A separate selective-sign energy/load statement is proved below for reuse, but the shorter matrix proof does not depend on that statement.
The [independent whole-proof finding](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_F0200_v14.md#finite-variance-graph-bridge) supports the [proved adoption](#decision-finite-variance-graph-reviewed) of this exact conditional result.

<a id="statement"></a>
## Exact conditional reduction

Fix real \(\alpha,K\).
Let \(W_N\) be real symmetric, with independent upper off-diagonal entries \(\xi/\sqrt N\), where \(\xi\) is a fixed real law satisfying
\[
 \mathbb E\xi=0,\qquad \mathbb E\xi^2=1.
\]
Its real diagonal entries may have arbitrary laws, independent of one another and of the off-diagonal family.
Let \(B_N\) be any independent real symmetric random matrix, without any moment or coordinate-independence assumption.
Put
\[
 L_N:=\begin{pmatrix}W_N+B_N&K I_N\\K I_N&W_N+\alpha B_N\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Cross-size dependence is unrestricted.

For each physical index \(i\), let \(\mathcal F_i\) be the sigma-field generated by the whole \(B_N\), the retained first-input principal minor and the exposed first diagonal.
Set \(n:=N-1\).
After putting the two exposed coordinates first, write the actual pencil as
\[
 L_N\simeq
 \begin{pmatrix}A_i&\mathcal B_i(x)^T\\\mathcal B_i(x)&M_i\end{pmatrix},
\]
where \(x\in\mathbb R^n\) is the scaled original first-input row and \(\mathcal B_i(x)\) retains the conditioned second-input border.
In auxiliary-first order, if that second border is \(y_i\), its two columns are \((x+y_i,0)^T\) and \((0,x+\alpha y_i)^T\).
The blocks \(A_i,M_i\) and the affine map \(\mathcal B_i\) are \(\mathcal F_i\)-measurable.
On the invertible-minor branch put
\[
 Z_i:=M_i^{-1},\quad
 p_i(x):=\det(A_i-\mathcal B_i(x)^TZ_i\mathcal B_i(x)),\quad
 \Gamma_i(x):=I_2+\mathcal B_i(x)^TZ_i^2\mathcal B_i(x).
\]
The polynomial \(p_i\) has degree at most four.
Define its formal fourth sign layer and its energy and load ratio by
\[
 \Pi_4p_i(\varepsilon/\sqrt N):=\sum_{|I|=4}c_{i,I}\varepsilon_I,
 \qquad \sigma_{4,i}^2:=\sum_{|I|=4}c_{i,I}^2,
\]
\[
 \tau_{4,i}:=
 \frac{\max_j\sum_{I\ni j}c_{i,I}^2}{\sigma_{4,i}^2}
 \quad\text{when }\sigma_{4,i}>0.
\]
The formal signs in this definition are a coefficient functional, not an assumption on the original law.
Every \(c_{i,I}\) includes the original \(N^{-2}\) normalization.

Fix \(\kappa>0\), and let \(N^{-1/2}\le t_N\to0\) be deterministic.
Suppose there are \(\mathcal F_i\)-measurable events \(\mathcal G_i\) and positive scales \(a_i\) such that on \(\mathcal G_i\),
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 M_i\text{ invertible},\qquad
 \sup_{x\in\mathbb R^n:\|x\|\le2}
                 \sqrt{\det\Gamma_i(x)}\le a_i,\\
 \sigma_{4,i}\ge\kappa a_i/N^2,\qquad \tau_{4,i}\le t_N.
 \end{gathered}
 \tag{Finite-variance-graph-hypotheses}
\]
The graph supremum is over the first scaled row only, with the actual conditioned second border fixed.
Thus no second-input support bound is hidden in this hypothesis.

For independent copies \(\xi_j',\xi_j''\), define
\[
 e_N:=\mathbb P\left\{
 \frac1N\sum_{j=1}^{N-1}((\xi_j')^2+(\xi_j'')^2)>4
 \right\},\qquad r_N:=\lfloor(16t_N)^{-1}\rfloor.
\]
One has \(e_N\to0\), and \(r_N\ge2\) eventually.
There are fixed \(c,C>0\), depending only on \(\xi,\alpha,K,\kappa\), such that every deterministic \(b_N\ge1\) satisfies, for all sufficiently large \(N\),
\[
 \begin{split}
 &\mathbb P\{\|L_N\|\le b_N,\
                s_{\min}(L_N)\le c b_N^{-2}N^{-5}\}\\
 &\qquad\le Ce^{-cN}
       +\frac C N\sum_{i=1}^N\mathbb P(\mathcal G_i^c)
       +C e_N+C e^{-c r_N}
       +C\sqrt{\log r_N}\,r_N^{-1/17}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{Finite-variance-conditional-matrix-reduction}
\]
This includes singular full matrices of every corank.
No polynomial bound on a minor inverse, invertibility probability estimate, or summability of \(e_N\) is asserted.
The separate selective-sign conclusion in [the energy/load section](#selective-sign-energy-load) applies to every conditioned real polynomial of degree at most four under the stated finite-second-moment row law.

<a id="argument"></a>
## Complete proof

<a id="formal-fourth-matching"></a>
### A normalized matching from the formal fourth layer

Fix \(\mathcal F_i\) on \(\mathcal G_i\).
Write \(S:=\sigma_{4,i}^2\), \(\ell_j:=\sum_{I\ni j}c_{i,I}^2\), and \(\tau:=\max_j\ell_j/S\).
There are at most \(n^4\) fourth monomials.
Retain the coefficients with modulus at least
\[
 \lambda_i:=\frac{\sigma_{4,i}}{\sqrt2\,n^2}
           \ge\frac{\kappa a_i}{\sqrt2\,N^4}.
 \tag{Formal-fourth-threshold}
\]
At least \(S/2\) of the squared energy remains.
After selecting a retained four-set, deleting all intersecting sets removes squared energy at most \(4\tau S\).
Greedy selection therefore gives at least \((8\tau)^{-1}\) disjoint retained sets, with integer rounding absorbed by the factor sixteen in \(r_N\).
Take the first \(r_N\) sets in a fixed lexicographic greedy construction.
This matching is \(\mathcal F_i\)-measurable and is chosen before any two-copy variables are introduced.

<a id="finite-variance-two-copy-thinning"></a>
### Two-copy thinning with no upper amplitude cutoff

For this one physical index, use an enlarged probability space carrying independent copies \(\xi_j',\xi_j''\) and independent fair signs \(\varepsilon_j\), independent across \(j\) and of \(\mathcal F_i\).
Set
\[
 a_j:=(\xi_j'+\xi_j'')/2,\qquad
 b_j:=(\xi_j'-\xi_j'')/2,\qquad
 x_j:=(a_j+b_j\varepsilon_j)/\sqrt N.
\]
For each coordinate this selects one of two independent copies with equal probability, so the resulting row has exactly the original independent-coordinate law.
This is the exact representation used in the [fixed quadratic matching proof](Yin_C_F0194_v3.md#two-copy-matching); the fourth-degree application is supplied here.

Conditional on all pairs, the coefficient of four distinct signs indexed by \(I\) in \(p_i(x)\) is exactly
\[
 c_{i,I}\prod_{j\in I}b_j.
 \tag{Actual-fourth-pair-coefficient}
\]
Translation by \(a\) does not change the four-distinct contribution of a degree-four monomial.
Every raw repeated-index monomial and every lower raw degree involves at most three distinct coordinates, and cannot produce four distinct signs.
All lower Walsh degrees are unrestricted.

Since \(\mathbb E b_j^2=1/2\), some fixed \(b_0>0\) has
\(p_0:=\mathbb P\{|b_j|\ge b_0\}>0\).
A matching edge survives when its four amplitudes all satisfy that event.
The \(r_N\) survival indicators are independent, each with probability \(p_0^4\), because the formal matching is disjoint and independent of the pairs.
If \(V\) is their sum, exponential Markov at parameter one gives
\[
 \mathbb P\{V\le p_0^4r_N/2\mid\mathcal F_i\}
 \le\exp[-p_0^4(1-e^{-1}-1/2)r_N].
 \tag{Fourth-matching-survival}
\]
Thus, outside \(e^{-c r_N}\), the actual polynomial of fair signs has at least a fixed multiple of \(r_N\) disjoint fourth coefficients with modulus at least
\[
 b_0^4\lambda_i\ge\frac{b_0^4\kappa a_i}{\sqrt2\,N^4}.
 \tag{Surviving-fourth-threshold}
\]
No upper amplitude bound, fourth moment, or small conditional load of the untruncated weighted polynomial is required.

<a id="pair-row-support-guard"></a>
### Uniformity over the entire paired sign support

For real scalars the identity \(|a_j|+|b_j|=\max\{|\xi_j'|,|\xi_j''|\}\) gives
\[
 \sup_\varepsilon\|x\|^2
 \le\frac1N\sum_j\max\{|\xi_j'|,|\xi_j''|\}^2
 \le\frac1N\sum_j((\xi_j')^2+(\xi_j'')^2).
\]
The weak law for the integrable variables \((\xi_j')^2+(\xi_j'')^2\), of mean two, gives \(e_N\to0\).
On the pair event complementary to the one defining \(e_N\), every allowed sign row lies in the radius-two ball in (Finite-variance-graph-hypotheses).
This pair event is measurable before the remaining signs are exposed and has conditional failure probability \(e_N\) given \(\mathcal F_i\).
It need not be independent of matching survival; adding their two failure probabilities suffices.
The actual second-input border is unchanged throughout.

<a id="distance-interval-and-average"></a>
### The finite interval and the full-matrix scale

Let \(d_i\) be the least singular value of the two physical columns after projection orthogonal to all other columns.
The [fixed exact graph identity](Yin_C_F0194_v13.md#graph-distance) gives on the invertible-minor branch
\[
 d_i=s_{\min}(\Gamma_i(x)^{-1/2}S_i(x)),\qquad
 S_i(x):=A_i-\mathcal B_i(x)^TZ_i\mathcal B_i(x),
\]
\[
 \{d_i\le r,\ \|L_N\|\le b_N\}
 \subseteq\{|p_i(x)|\le b_Nr\sqrt{\det\Gamma_i(x)}\}.
\]
Choose fixed \(c_1>0\) with \(2c_1<b_0^4\kappa/\sqrt2\), and put
\[
 r:=c_1b_N^{-1}N^{-4}.
\]
On good conditioning, pair support and matching survival, this distance event implies
\(|p_i(x)|\le c_1a_iN^{-4}\).
Its interval length is strictly smaller than (Surviving-fourth-threshold).
Apply the accepted [MNV16 Theorem 1.6](../audits/literature/MNV16/Yin_U001.md#matching-rank) to the real multilinear fair-sign polynomial scaled by that threshold, at degree four.
The actual matching rank is at least \(\lfloor p_0^4r_N/2\rfloor\), which is at least two eventually.
Absorbing that fixed rank factor and using eventual monotonicity of the source bound yields
\[
 \begin{split}
 &\mathbb P\{d_i\le c_1b_N^{-1}N^{-4},\ \|L_N\|\le b_N\}\\
 &\quad\le\mathbb P(\mathcal G_i^c)+e_N+e^{-c r_N}
              +C\sqrt{\log r_N}\,r_N^{-1/17}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{Finite-variance-integrated-distance}
\]
The parent norm event remains inside the probability being estimated; it is not included in the row conditioning.
Each index can use a separate two-copy probability space, since integrating its pairs and signs gives the original parent marginal law.
No simultaneous construction or union of pair guards over indices is needed.

The [fixed physical averaging proof](Yin_C_F0194_v13.md#block-averaging) requires only the present finite-second-moment first off-diagonal law, arbitrary independent diagonals, and an independent real symmetric second input.
Its rectangular step conditions on the entire second input and is uniform in its resulting shifts.
It supplies fixed \(a,\delta,c,C>0\) such that, for \(0\le u\le a/(4\sqrt N)\),
\[
 \begin{split}
 &\mathbb P\{\|L_N\|\le b_N,\ s_{\min}(L_N)\le u\}\\
 &\quad\le Ce^{-cN}+
 \frac2{\delta N}\sum_i
 \mathbb P\{d_i\le8b_NNu/a,\ \|L_N\|\le b_N\}.
 \end{split}
\]
It defines the distances without a minor invertibility assumption and includes full singular matrices of every corank.
Choose \(u:=c b_N^{-2}N^{-5}\), with \(c\le ac_1/8\).
Then \(8b_NNu/a\le c_1b_N^{-1}N^{-4}\), and the required upper bound on \(u\) holds eventually since \(b_N\ge1\).
Substituting (Finite-variance-integrated-distance) proves (Finite-variance-conditional-matrix-reduction).
The averaging pays each pair failure once, through its mean, and never multiplies \(e_N\) or the MNV error by \(N\).

<a id="selective-sign-energy-load"></a>
## Separate energy and load preservation by revealing large-amplitude signs


Let \(\xi\) be fixed, real, centered, with \(\mathbb E\xi^2=1\).
Condition on a sigma-field \(\mathcal F\) independent of the original fresh row, fixing a real polynomial \(p(x)\) of degree at most four in \(n\le N\) scaled coordinates.
In the application, \(\mathcal F\) contains the whole second input, the first-input principal minor and its exposed diagonal, and \(p\) is the actual Schur determinant.
Define its formal fourth sign layer and coefficient quantities by
\[
 \Pi_4 p(\varepsilon/\sqrt N)
 :=\sum_{|I|=4}c_I\varepsilon_I,\qquad
 S:=\sum_{|I|=4}c_I^2>0,\qquad
 \ell_j:=\sum_{I\ni j}c_I^2,\qquad
 \tau:=\max_j\ell_j/S.
\]
The coefficients \(c_I\) include the original \(N^{-2}\) factor.
Take independent copies \(\xi_j',\xi_j''\) and independent fair signs \(\varepsilon_j\), independent across coordinates and of \(\mathcal F\), and put
\[
 a_j:=(\xi_j'+\xi_j'')/2,\qquad
 b_j:=(\xi_j'-\xi_j'')/2,\qquad
 x_j:=(a_j+b_j\varepsilon_j)/\sqrt N.
\]
The vector \(x\) has exactly the original independent fresh-row law.
Since \(\mathbb E b_j^2=1/2\), choose a fixed \(T<\infty\) such that
\[
 \mu:=\mathbb E[b_j^2\mathbf1_{\{|b_j|\le T\}}]\ge1/4.
\]
Such a choice exists by monotone convergence and uses no fourth moment.

First condition on all pairs; then also reveal the signs with \(|b_j|>T\).
Equivalently, enlarge \(\mathcal F\) by the pairs and the variables \(\varepsilon_j\mathbf1_{\{|b_j|>T\}}\).
The set of unrevealed indices is fixed by the pairs, and its remaining signs are independent and fair under this conditioning.
Their fourth coefficients are exactly
\[
 \widehat c_I=c_I\prod_{j\in I}b_j
 \quad\text{for }I\subset\{j:|b_j|\le T\},\quad |I|=4.
 \tag{Selected-fourth-coefficients}
\]
A raw monomial of degree at most four with a repeated coordinate involves at most three distinct variables and cannot generate four distinct signs after the affine substitution.
A four-distinct monomial involving a revealed index has degree at most three in the remaining signs.
Thus there are no omitted fourth contributions in (Selected-fourth-coefficients).
All lower-degree coefficients, including those containing arbitrarily large revealed coordinates, are unrestricted in the MNV input.

Put \(\beta_j:=b_j^2\mathbf1_{\{|b_j|\le T\}}\).
The conditional fourth energy is
\[
 H:=\sum_{|I|=4}c_I^2\prod_{j\in I}\beta_j.
\]
Given \(\mathcal F\), the \(\beta_j\) are independent, bounded by \(T^2\), and have mean \(\mu\).
Disjoint products are independent, while the covariance for intersecting products is at most \(T^{16}\) in absolute value.
Consequently
\[
 \mathbb E[H\mid\mathcal F]=\mu^4S,\qquad
 \operatorname{Var}(H\mid\mathcal F)
 \le T^{16}\sum_{I\cap J\ne\varnothing}c_I^2c_J^2
 \le4T^{16}\tau S^2.
\]
Chebyshev and the deterministic upper amplitude bound give
\[
 \mathbb P\{H<\mu^4S/2\mid\mathcal F\}
 \le16T^{16}\mu^{-8}\tau,
\]
\[
 \widehat\sigma_4\ge\mu^2\sqrt S/\sqrt2,\qquad
 \widehat\tau_4\le2T^8\mu^{-4}\tau
 \quad\text{on }\{H\ge\mu^4S/2\}.
 \tag{Selected-energy-and-load}
\]
The number of remaining signs may be random; the original upper bound \(n\le N\) still bounds their number of fourth monomials.
This is a genuine finite-second-moment energy/load preservation statement, not a bound on untruncated random weighted loads.

<a id="finite-variance-second-input-corollary"></a>
## Concrete boundary and parent norm guards

Suppose additionally that the upper off-diagonal entries of \(B_N\) are independent copies of \(\eta/\sqrt N\), where \(\eta\) is a fixed real centered variance-one law, and that \(B_N\) remains independent of \(W_N\).
For each fixed physical index, its scaled border \(y_i\) satisfies \(\|y_i\|^2\to1\) in probability by the weak law.
Hence
\[
 \overline e_N^{(2)}:=\frac1N\sum_i\mathbb P\{\|y_i\|>2\}
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
If \(\|x\|,\|y_i\|\le2\), the actual two columns have orthogonal auxiliary supports, so
\[
 \|\mathcal B_i(x)\|
 =\max\{\|x+y_i\|,\|x+\alpha y_i\|\}
 \le B_0:=2(1+\max\{1,|\alpha|\}).
\]
Thus one can replace the graph-area assumption by the stronger minor condition
\[
 \sup_{R\in\mathbb R^{2n\times2}:\|R\|\le B_0}
          \sqrt{\det(I_2+R^TZ_i^2R)}\le a_i,
\]
retain the same formal energy/load hypotheses, and add only \(C\overline e_N^{(2)}\) to the matrix error.
Indeed, intersect each good event with \(\{\|y_i\|\le2\}\), which is \(\mathcal F_i\)-measurable, then apply the main theorem.
The second-row bound is required only for this concrete stronger interface, not for the arbitrary-\(B_N\) theorem.

If, in addition, the diagonal entries of input \(j\) have law \(\zeta_j/\sqrt N\) for fixed real laws with \(\mathbb E\zeta_j^2<\infty\), then
\[
 \mathbb E\|L_N\|_{\rm F}^2\le C N.
\]
This follows by expanding the two diagonal auxiliary blocks and the identity couplings, or by the two-term squared norm inequality and the input Frobenius expectations.
For every fixed \(\beta>1/2\), Markov therefore gives
\[
 \mathbb P\{\|L_N\|>N^\beta\}\le C N^{1-2\beta}.
\]
The main theorem at \(b_N=N^\beta\), with this additional norm error, has least-value scale \(cN^{-5-2\beta}\).
These finite-variance norm estimates do not prove that the formal good-event average tends to zero.

<a id="scope-and-provenance"></a>
## Remaining hypotheses and development provenance

The new theorem removes boundedness and the fair-sign restriction from the conditional row conversion.
It does not establish the required actual-minor fourth energy, coordinate spread, or graph-area comparison for every original finite-second-moment input model.
Nor does it supply intermediate logarithmic estimates or the full arbitrary-coefficient Task conclusion.
The separate energy/load lemma uses bounded selected amplitudes after revealing other signs; the main floor instead uses disjoint matching survival without an upper amplitude cutoff.
Both arguments retain every unrestricted lower coefficient and the original row marginal.

The argument was developed in [D10's finite-variance row calculation](../drafts/Yin_D_F0010.md#finite-variance-selective-sign-row), including an ordinary parallel check of the selected-amplitude formulas and the conditional support guard.
That draft supplies provenance only; the complete new reasoning is included here.
The quadratic matching source and [bounded fourth-weight proof](Yin_C_F0186_v17.md#bounded-two-copy-fourth-layer) are antecedents, not assertions that their old statements already had the present scope.
The exact MNV input retains its accepted external source-proof boundary.
The independent review records the corrected corollary normalization and its actual rechecked scope.
