# Two-original-row sixth-degree graph bridge and a three-channel probability floor

This theorem converts a complete formal sixth-layer coefficient bound into an actual-row probability estimate for the real three-channel path.
It retains both original independent borders, allows arbitrary independent exposed diagonal laws in the conditional bridge, and uses no bound on a retained inverse.
The fixed-model floor uses the adopted coefficient theorem C187v24.
The complete argument has received [independent whole-proof review](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_F0200_v16.md#sixth-original-row-whole-proof), adopted in the [proved decision](#decision-three-channel-sixth-bridge-reviewed).
No middle logarithmic estimate, determinant convergence or Task completion is asserted.

<a id="statement"></a>
## Exact conditional bridge

Let \(C_N,D_N\) be independent real symmetric matrices whose upper off-diagonal entries are independent copies of \(\xi_1/\sqrt N,\xi_2/\sqrt N\), respectively.
The two real laws are fixed, centered, and have variance one.
All their upper-triangular coordinates are independent within each size.
For this bridge, diagonal entries may have arbitrary real laws, independent of each other and of all off-diagonal coordinates; no diagonal moment or centering is required.
Coupling across dimensions is arbitrary.
Put
\[
 t=(0,1,\sqrt2),\qquad T=\operatorname{diag}(t),\qquad
 K=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\1&0&1\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix},
\]
and, for any deterministic real sequence \(\theta_N\), define
\[
 L_N^\theta=I_3\otimes C_N+T\otimes D_N+K\otimes I_N
                 +\theta_N I_{3N}.
\]
No size bound on \(\theta_N\) is needed for the conditional bridge.

For every physical index \(i\), let \(\mathcal F_i\) be generated by both retained principal minors \(C_{N,\{i\}},D_{N,\{i\}}\) and the two exposed diagonal entries \(C_{ii},D_{ii}\).
Let \(n=N-1\).
After putting the three exposed coordinates first, the actual block form is
\[
 L_N^\theta\simeq
 \begin{pmatrix}A_i&\mathcal B_i(x,y)^T\\
                 \mathcal B_i(x,y)&M_i\end{pmatrix},
\]
\[
 A_i=K+\operatorname{diag}(C_{ii}+t_aD_{ii})_{a=1}^3+\theta_NI_3,
 \qquad
 \mathcal B_i(x,y)e_a=e_a\otimes(x+t_ay).
\]
Here \(x,y\in\mathbb R^n\) are the two original scaled off-diagonal borders, independent of \(\mathcal F_i\), independent of one another, and with independent coordinates of their respective prescribed laws.
The real symmetric \(A_i,M_i\) are \(\mathcal F_i\)-measurable.

On the invertible-minor branch put
\[
 Z_i=M_i^{-1},\quad
 S_i(x,y)=A_i-\mathcal B_i(x,y)^TZ_i\mathcal B_i(x,y),
\]
\[
 p_i(x,y)=\det S_i(x,y),\qquad
 \Gamma_i(x,y)=I_3+\mathcal B_i(x,y)^TZ_i^2\mathcal B_i(x,y).
\]
The real polynomial \(p_i\) has degree at most six.
For formal independent signs indexed by \(\mathcal V_i=[n]\times\{1,2\}\), define the complete degree-six Walsh coefficients by
\[
 \Pi_6p_i(\epsilon^{(1)}/\sqrt N,\epsilon^{(2)}/\sqrt N)
     =\sum_{\substack{J\subset\mathcal V_i\\|J|=6}}c_{i,J}\epsilon_J,
 \qquad \sigma_{6,i}^2=\sum_{|J|=6}c_{i,J}^2.
\]
Both input variables at one physical row remain distinct.
For \(\sigma_{6,i}>0\), let
\[
 \tau_{6,i}
 =\frac{\displaystyle\max_{j\in[n]}
      \sum_{\substack{|J|=6\\J\cap(\{j\}\times\{1,2\})\ne\varnothing}}
                     c_{i,J}^2}{\sigma_{6,i}^2}.
\]
These formal coefficients already include \(N^{-3}\) normalization and do not replace the original entry laws by signs.

Fix \(\kappa>0\) and a deterministic \(N^{-1/2}\le t_N\to0\).
Suppose there are \(\mathcal F_i\)-measurable events \(\mathcal G_i\) and positive \(\mathcal F_i\)-measurable scales \(a_i\) such that, on \(\mathcal G_i\),
\[
 M_i\text{ is invertible},\qquad
 \sup_{\|x\|\le2,\ \|y\|\le2}\sqrt{\det\Gamma_i(x,y)}\le a_i,
\]
\[
 \sigma_{6,i}\ge\kappa a_i/N^3,\qquad
 \tau_{6,i}\le t_N.
 \tag{Sixth-actual-graph-inputs}
\]
Both row balls enter this hypothesis; neither border is held fixed.

For input \(\ell=1,2\), take independent copies \(\xi_{\ell j}',\xi_{\ell j}''\), and define
\[
 e_N^{(\ell)}=
 \mathbb P\left\{\frac1N\sum_{j=1}^{N-1}
       [(\xi_{\ell j}')^2+(\xi_{\ell j}'')^2]>4\right\},
 \qquad r_N=\left\lfloor(24t_N)^{-1}\right\rfloor.
\]
Then \(e_N^{(\ell)}\to0\) and \(r_N\ge2\) eventually.
There are fixed \(c,C>0\), depending only on \(\xi_1,\xi_2,\kappa\) and the fixed path coefficients, such that every deterministic \(b_N\ge1\) satisfies, for all sufficiently large \(N\),
\[
 \begin{split}
 &\mathbb P\{\|L_N^\theta\|\le b_N,\
             s_{\min}(L_N^\theta)\le c b_N^{-3}N^{-7}\}\\
 &\quad\le Ce^{-cN}+\frac C N\sum_{i=1}^N\mathbb P(\mathcal G_i^c)
       +C(e_N^{(1)}+e_N^{(2)})
       +Ce^{-cr_N}
       +C\sqrt{\log r_N}\,r_N^{-1/25}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{Sixth-conditional-matrix-bridge}
\]
The constants do not depend on diagonal distributions, exposed values, \(\theta_N\), any inverse norm, or \(b_N\).
The estimate includes full matrices of every corank.
All bad-minor probabilities are unconditional, and the row-pair failures are averaged rather than unioned over \(N\) indices.

## Fixed original-model floor

Now assume additionally that, for input \(\ell\), its diagonal entries are independent copies of \(\zeta_\ell/\sqrt N\), where \(\zeta_\ell\) is fixed, real, centered, and has finite second moment.
Keep arbitrary cross-size coupling and all independence within each size.
Write
\[
 L_N=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 C_N&I_N&0\\
 I_N&C_N+D_N&I_N\\
 0&I_N&C_N+\sqrt2D_N
 \end{pmatrix},
\]
\[
 P_N=(C_N+\sqrt2D_N)(C_N+D_N)C_N-2C_N-\sqrt2D_N.
\]
The conclusion is that a fixed \(a>0\), depending only on the prescribed input laws, satisfies
\[
 \mathbb P\{s_{\min}(L_N)<aN^{-17/2}
           \ \text{or}\ s_{\min}(P_N)<aN^{-17/2}\}\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{Three-channel-original-floor}
\]
This conclusion uses the exact all-scale coefficient assertion of C187v24.
That input and the new original-row bridge with its floor application are adopted as proved at their exact stated scopes.
The bridge (Sixth-conditional-matrix-bridge) does not use C187v24.

No summable failure rate or full-sequence almost-sure floor is asserted.
A polynomial least-value floor alone does not prove uniform integrability of the middle negative logarithmic range.
There is no logarithmic determinant limit, arbitrary matrix-coefficient or complex-input extension, or Brown-measure conclusion here.

## Complete proof

<a id="formal-sixth-matching"></a>
### Complete sixth-layer energy produces a measurable matching

Fix \(i\) and condition on \(\mathcal F_i\) on \(\mathcal G_i\).
Write \(S=\sigma_{6,i}^2>0\), and let \(\ell_v=\sum_{J\ni v}c_{i,J}^2\) be the individual-variable load.
It is at most the load of its physical group, hence \(\ell_v\le t_NS\).
There are at most \((2n)^6\) degree-six monomials.
Retain coefficients of modulus at least
\[
 \lambda_i=\frac{\sigma_{6,i}}{\sqrt2(2n)^3}
       \ge\frac{\kappa a_i}{8\sqrt2\,N^6}.
 \tag{Sixth-formal-threshold}
\]
The removed squared energy is at most \(S/2\).
Selecting a retained six-set and deleting every set intersecting it removes energy at most \(6t_NS\), by summing its six individual loads.
Thus greedy selection yields at least \(1/(12t_N)\) disjoint sets, with integer rounding handled by the smaller \(r_N\) above.
Take the first \(r_N\) sets in a fixed lexicographic greedy construction.
The matching and its threshold are measurable under \(\mathcal F_i\), before any pair or sign variables are exposed.
Disjointness here concerns the \(2n\) individual input variables, not their physical groups.

<a id="two-law-copy-thinning"></a>
### Two independent original laws give one fair-sign polynomial

For this index alone, enlarge the space with independent copies \(\xi_{\ell j}',\xi_{\ell j}''\) and fair signs \(\epsilon_{\ell j}\), independent for all \(\ell=1,2\) and \(j\le n\), and independent of \(\mathcal F_i\).
Put
\[
 m_{\ell j}=(\xi_{\ell j}'+\xi_{\ell j}'')/2,\qquad
 b_{\ell j}=(\xi_{\ell j}'-\xi_{\ell j}'')/2,
\]
\[
 x_j=(m_{1j}+b_{1j}\epsilon_{1j})/\sqrt N,\qquad
 y_j=(m_{2j}+b_{2j}\epsilon_{2j})/\sqrt N.
 \tag{Two-original-law-representation}
\]
Each coordinate selects one of its two independent copies equiprobably.
Consequently the resulting pair of borders has exactly its original conditional joint law.

For each law, \(\mathbb E b_{\ell j}^2=1/2\).
Choose fixed \(b_0,p_0>0\) such that
\[
 \mathbb P\{|b_{\ell j}|\ge b_0\}\ge p_0
 \qquad(\ell=1,2).
\]
Such common constants exist by taking the smaller of two positive thresholds and probabilities.
A retained matching edge survives if all six of its amplitudes have modulus at least \(b_0\).
The survival indicators are independent because the six-sets are disjoint in individual variables, and their success probabilities are at least \(p_0^6\).
Two variables sharing a physical row but coming from different inputs still have independent pairs.
If \(V\) counts surviving edges, exponential Markov gives
\[
 \mathbb P\{V\le p_0^6r_N/2\mid\mathcal F_i\}
 \le\exp[-p_0^6(1-e^{-1}-1/2)r_N].
 \tag{Sixth-matching-survival}
\]
No upper bound on pair amplitudes is imposed.

After all pairs are exposed and powers of signs are reduced, \(p_i(x,y)\) is a real multilinear fair-sign polynomial of degree at most six.
For each six-set \(J\), its degree-six coefficient is exactly
\[
 c_{i,J}\prod_{v\in J}b_v.
 \tag{Sixth-paired-coefficient}
\]
Indeed only an original degree-six monomial with six distinct individual variables can produce six distinct signs.
Every repeated-variable monomial has at most five distinct variables, and every lower degree also has fewer than six.
Translations by \(m_{\ell j}\) therefore do not alter the top distinct-sign coefficient, although all lower degrees may change.
On matching survival, at least \(\lfloor p_0^6r_N/2\rfloor\) disjoint sixth coefficients have modulus at least
\[
 b_0^6\lambda_i\ge
       \frac{b_0^6\kappa a_i}{8\sqrt2\,N^6}.
 \tag{Sixth-surviving-threshold}
\]

<a id="double-pair-support"></a>
### Both entire paired supports lie in fixed row balls

For real scalars,
\[
 |m_{\ell j}|+|b_{\ell j}|
   =\max(|\xi_{\ell j}'|,|\xi_{\ell j}''|).
\]
It follows that
\[
 \sup_{\epsilon^{(1)}}\|x\|^2
 \le N^{-1}\sum_j[(\xi_{1j}')^2+(\xi_{1j}'')^2],
\]
and the analogous inequality holds for \(y\).
The weak law applies to each fixed integrable squared-pair law, whose mean is two.
It gives \(e_N^{(1)}+e_N^{(2)}\to0\).
Outside this total pair failure, every remaining sign choice satisfies \(\|x\|,\|y\|\le2\), so the graph area is at most \(a_i\) on the entire sign support.
This pair guard is exposed before the fair signs; it is not inserted into the original minor conditioning.
It need not be independent of matching survival, since their failure probabilities are added.
There is no conditioning on a fixed second border and no requirement of small weighted loads after amplitude exposure.

<a id="three-column-graph"></a>
### Exact graph distance and the sixth-degree finite interval

For any real symmetric block matrix
\[
 L=\begin{pmatrix}A&B^T\\B&M\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad A\in M_3(\mathbb R),\quad M\text{ invertible},
\]
put \(Z=M^{-1}\), \(\Gamma=I_3+B^TZ^2B\), and
\[
 U=\binom{I_3}{-ZB}\Gamma^{-1/2}.
\]
Then \(U^TU=I_3\) and \(U^T\binom{B^T}{M}=0\).
The remaining columns have full rank because their lower block is \(M\).
Thus \(U\) is an orthonormal basis of their orthogonal complement, and the exposed three-column distance matrix is
\[
 D_i=U^T\binom{A}{B}=\Gamma^{-1/2}(A-B^TZB).
\]
In particular,
\[
 d_i=s_{\min}(D_i),\quad \|D_i\|\le\|L\|,\quad
 |\det(A-B^TZB)|=\sqrt{\det\Gamma}\,|\det D_i|.
\]
On \(\|L\|\le b_N\), its other two singular values are at most \(b_N\), so
\[
 |\det(A-B^TZB)|\le b_N^2d_i\sqrt{\det\Gamma}.
 \tag{Three-column-distance-determinant}
\]
This uses the exact graph area, not a bound on triangular elimination inverses.

For every physical index, even with singular retained minor, define \(d_i\) as the least singular value of its three columns projected orthogonally to all other columns.
The graph formula just proved is used only on \(\mathcal G_i\).
Choose \(c_1>0\) so small that
\[
 2c_1< b_0^6\kappa/(8\sqrt2),
\]
and put \(v_N=c_1b_N^{-2}N^{-6}\).
On good conditioning and the double-pair support guard,
\[
 \{d_i\le v_N,\ \|L_N^\theta\|\le b_N\}
 \subseteq\{|p_i(x,y)|\le c_1a_iN^{-6}\}.
\]
The interval length is strictly smaller than (Sixth-surviving-threshold).
Apply the accepted MNV16 theorem to the paired real fair-sign polynomial divided by that threshold, at degree \(d=6\).
Its matching rank is at least \(\lfloor p_0^6r_N/2\rfloor\), at least two eventually.
The exponent \(1/(4d+1)\) is \(1/25\).
Absorbing fixed factors and using eventual monotonicity of \(\sqrt{\log r}\,r^{-1/25}\) yields
\[
 \begin{split}
 &\mathbb P\{d_i\le c_1b_N^{-2}N^{-6},\
                    \|L_N^\theta\|\le b_N\}\\
 &\quad\le\mathbb P(\mathcal G_i^c)+e_N^{(1)}+e_N^{(2)}
       +Ce^{-cr_N}+C\sqrt{\log r_N}\,r_N^{-1/25}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{Sixth-integrated-distance}
\]
The parent norm guard stays inside the estimated event and is never added to the conditional row law.
For each index, the independent-copy construction is integrated back to the original marginal law before physical averaging.
No simultaneous copy realization over indices is required.

<a id="three-channel-physical-average"></a>
### Three rectangular blocks give physical averaging

Condition on the entire second input \(D_N\) for this separate rectangular estimate.
Apply C194v3's all-small-support rectangle theorem to \(C_N\) with each deterministic shift \(t_aD_N+\theta_NI_N\), \(a=1,2,3\).
The theorem is uniform over those shifts and requires no bound on their norms.
The three events share \(C_N\), but a union bound suffices and requires no independence between them.
It gives fixed \(\delta,a,c,C>0\) and an event \(\mathcal R_N\), of unconditional failure at most \(Ce^{-cN}\), such that for every
\[
 |J|=\lfloor\delta N\rfloor,\qquad I=[N]\setminus J,
\]
the three normalized rectangles have least singular value at least \(a/\sqrt N\).
The path coupling and scalar translation have zero entries across disjoint physical sets, and hence
\[
 L_N^\theta[I,J]=
 \operatorname{diag}\bigl(C_N[I,J],\
 (C_N+D_N)[I,J],\
 (C_N+\sqrt2D_N)[I,J]\bigr)
\]
in auxiliary-first order.
Its least singular value is at least \(a/\sqrt N\) on \(\mathcal R_N\).

Suppose \(\|L_N^\theta\|\le b_N\), \(\|w\|=1\), and
\(\|L_N^\theta w\|\le a/(4\sqrt N)\).
Write \(w=(w_i)_{i=1}^N\) in physical blocks \(w_i\in\mathbb R^3\), and put
\[
 h_N=a/(8b_NN).
\]
If at most \(\lfloor\delta N\rfloor\) blocks had norm greater than \(h_N\), choose \(J\) containing them.
Then
\[
 \|w_I\|\le\sqrt N h_N=\frac{a}{8b_N\sqrt N},
 \qquad \|w_J\|\ge\tfrac12
\]
eventually.
On \(\mathcal R_N\),
\[
 \|(L_N^\theta w)_I\|
 \ge \frac a{\sqrt N}\|w_J\|-b_N\|w_I\|
 \ge\frac{3a}{8\sqrt N},
\]
a contradiction.
The factor \(\sqrt N\) counts physical block norms and is unaffected by their dimension three.

Let \(Q_i\) project orthogonally to the span of all columns except physical block \(i\), and let \(C_i\) be those three columns.
Then \(d_i=s_{\min}(Q_iC_i)\) is defined without an invertibility or corank assumption.
The identity \(Q_iL_N^\theta w=Q_iC_iw_i\) implies
\(d_i\le\|L_N^\theta w\|/\|w_i\|\) whenever \(w_i\ne0\).
Apply the preceding block count to a real least singular vector.
For \(0\le u\le a/(4\sqrt N)\), it gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 &\mathbb P\{\|L_N^\theta\|\le b_N,\
                   s_{\min}(L_N^\theta)\le u\}\\
 &\quad\le Ce^{-cN}
 +\frac2{\delta N}\sum_i
  \mathbb P\{d_i\le8b_NNu/a,\ \|L_N^\theta\|\le b_N\}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{Three-column-distance-average}
\]
This follows by bounding the indicator of the parent event by the fraction of its at least \(\delta N/2\) small-distance indices on \(\mathcal R_N\).
It does not require independence of the distance events.
Choose \(u=c b_N^{-3}N^{-7}\), with \(c\le ac_1/8\).
Its threshold is at most \(c_1b_N^{-2}N^{-6}\), and \(u\le a/(4\sqrt N)\) eventually because \(b_N\ge1\).
Substituting (Sixth-integrated-distance) proves (Sixth-conditional-matrix-bridge).
The averaging pays each copy-support and MNV error only a fixed constant number of times.

<a id="original-model-good-events"></a>
### One shift and numerical minor-measurable events

We now assume the fixed-model laws in (Three-channel-original-floor) and use the C187v24 coefficient assertion at deletion bound one.
At each \(N\), the finitely many ordered eigenvalue laws of the unshifted full pencil and its single-deletion minors have only countably many atoms.
Choose one deterministic \(0<\theta_N<N^{-10}\) outside the union of negatives of those atoms.
Every corresponding shifted minor is invertible almost surely.
The countable intersection over dimensions preserves probability one for every allowed cross-size coupling.
This is the atom-avoidance mechanism of C187v20; it does not assert invertibility of an unshifted minor.

For an invertible \(M_i\), let \(q_{i1}\ge q_{i2}\ge q_{i3}\) be the three largest singular values of \(Z_i\).
Set
\[
 B_0=2(1+\sqrt2),\qquad
 a_i=\prod_{j=1}^3\sqrt{1+B_0^2q_{ij}^2}.
 \tag{Three-channel-measurable-area}
\]
This is a positive function of the actual retained minor.
Singular-value comparison gives, for every real \(3n\)-by-three matrix \(B\) with \(\|B\|\le B_0\),
\[
 \sqrt{\det(I_3+B^TZ_i^2B)}
 \le\prod_{j=1}^3\sqrt{1+B_0^2q_{ij}^2}=a_i.
\]
Equality is attained by taking \(B\)'s columns to be \(B_0\) times three orthonormal eigenvectors of \(Z_i^2\) at its largest eigenvalues.
Thus \(a_i\) is also the supremum over that matrix ball; measurability does not require choosing those eigenvectors.

For actual two-border support with \(\|x\|,\|y\|\le2\), the columns have orthogonal auxiliary supports, so
\[
 \|\mathcal B_i(x,y)\|
 =\max_a\|x+t_ay\|\le B_0.
\]
C187v24's bounded-boundary graph-area assertion therefore supplies a fixed \(\kappa>0\) such that, with probability tending to one, all the shifted single-deletion minors obey
\[
 \sigma_{6,i}\ge\kappa a_i/N^3.
\]
The top homogeneous term of \(p_i\) is \(-\det(\mathcal B_i^TZ_i\mathcal B_i)\), so its complete sixth energy and loads are exactly those of the coefficient input, irrespective of the exposed diagonal values.

Set \(\tau_{6,i}=1\) on the event that the sixth energy vanishes, and ignore the null singular-minor set.
C187v24's positive-energy and complete-load assertions imply
\(\max_i\tau_{6,i}\to0\) in probability.
There exists a deterministic sequence \(t_N\to0\), with \(t_N\ge N^{-1/2}\), such that
\(\mathbb P\{\max_i\tau_{6,i}>t_N\}\to0\).
For example choose successive dimension thresholds at which the probability of exceeding \(1/m\) is at most \(1/m\), and then take the larger of that piecewise level and \(N^{-1/2}\).
This construction uses only marginal laws and imposes no rate or cross-size independence.

Define \(\mathcal G_i\) by invertibility and the two numerical inequalities
\(\sigma_{6,i}\ge\kappa a_i/N^3\), \(\tau_{6,i}\le t_N\).
These quantities depend only on the retained original minors, and are therefore \(\mathcal F_i\)-measurable.
They satisfy every condition in (Sixth-actual-graph-inputs), and
\[
 \frac1N\sum_i\mathbb P(\mathcal G_i^c)
 \le\mathbb P\{\exists i:\mathcal G_i^c\}\longrightarrow0.
\]
The global event used to establish these numerical facts is not made part of the fresh-row conditioning.
No second-border guard or weighted-load estimate is needed.

<a id="finite-variance-parent-floor"></a>
### Finite second moments supply the parent norm and unshifted floor

For each fixed real \(t\), the off-diagonal coordinates of \(C_N+tD_N\) have the fixed square-integrable law \(\xi_1+t\xi_2\), of variance \(1+t^2\).
The weak law for its squared coordinates gives
\[
 \frac{\|C_N+tD_N\|_F^2}{N}\longrightarrow1+t^2
 \quad\text{in probability}.
\]
Indeed its off-diagonal contribution is
\(2N^{-2}\sum_{i<j}(\xi_{1,ij}+t\xi_{2,ij})^2\).
The diagonal contribution has expectation \(O(N^{-1})\) after division by \(N\), by its finite second moment, and vanishes in probability.
For the three fixed slopes \(0,1,\sqrt2\) and the four identity blocks of the path,
\[
 \|L_N\|_F^2/N\longrightarrow1+2+3+4=10.
\]
The translation changes the Frobenius norm by at most \(\sqrt{3N}\theta_N\).
Choose a fixed \(B>\sqrt{10}\).
Then
\[
 \mathbb P\{\|L_N^\theta\|>B\sqrt N\}\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{Three-parent-norm-guard}
\]
No fixed operator-norm bound on either original input has been used.

Apply (Sixth-conditional-matrix-bridge) with \(b_N=B\sqrt N\) and the numerical good events just constructed.
The averaged bad-minor probability, two pair weak-law errors, and matching-rank error all tend to zero.
Consequently, for some fixed \(c_*>0\),
\[
 \mathbb P\{s_{\min}(L_N^\theta)\le c_*N^{-17/2}\}
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
The exponent follows from \(b_N^{-3}N^{-7}=B^{-3}N^{-17/2}\).
Since
\[
 |s_{\min}(L_N^\theta)-s_{\min}(L_N)|
 \le\theta_N<N^{-10}=o(N^{-17/2}),
\]
the same floor holds for the original \(L_N\), after decreasing its constant.
This transfers the linear pencil before considering the cubic, rather than estimating a cubic perturbation involving unbounded original inputs.

<a id="cubic-inverse-block"></a>
### The cubic inverse is the upper-right block of the pencil inverse

For arbitrary equally sized real square matrices \(A,B,E\), put
\[
 L=\begin{pmatrix}A&I&0\\I&B&I\\0&I&E\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad P=EBA-E-A.
\]
Two swaps of block columns and block row subtractions give
\[
 L\longmapsto
 \begin{pmatrix}I&A&0\\B&I&I\\I&0&E\end{pmatrix}
 \longmapsto
 \begin{pmatrix}I&A&0\\0&I-BA&I\\0&-A&E\end{pmatrix}
 \longmapsto
 \begin{pmatrix}I&0&A\\0&I&I-BA\\0&E&-A\end{pmatrix}
 \longmapsto
 \begin{pmatrix}I&0&A\\0&I&I-BA\\0&0&EBA-E-A\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The two column-swap signs cancel, and all row factors have determinant one.
Thus \(\det L=\det P\), without inverting any input block.

When \(L\) is invertible, solve
\[
 L(u,v,w)^T=(0,0,f)^T.
\]
Its first two equations give \(v=-Au\) and \(w=(BA-I)u\); its third is \(Pu=f\).
Therefore
\[
 (L^{-1})_{13}=P^{-1},\qquad
 \|P^{-1}\|\le\|L^{-1}\|,\qquad
 s_{\min}(P)\ge s_{\min}(L).
 \tag{Three-cubic-inverse-block}
\]
If \(L\) is singular, \(\det P=\det L=0\), and both least singular values vanish, so the last inequality remains valid.
Substituting \(A=C_N\), \(B=C_N+D_N\), \(E=C_N+\sqrt2D_N\) gives exactly the stated ordered cubic \(P_N\).
There is no transpose, sign correction or input-norm factor in this transfer.
The already established unshifted \(L_N\) floor therefore implies the joint assertion (Three-channel-original-floor).
