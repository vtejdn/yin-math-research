# A fixed number of original input laws in the sixth-degree probability bridge

This theorem extends the probability interface to a fixed number of original real input laws, separately from the still developing physical coefficient application.
It was developed for Yin through ordinary reasoning and authoring self-checks and is adopted with [independent whole-proof review](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_F0200_v21.md#fixed-input-sixth-bridge-whole-proof).
The development in [D10](../drafts/Yin_D_F0010.md#fixed-input-count-sixth-probability-bridge) is extraction provenance, not a logical dependency.
It reuses the [accepted degree-six bridge](Yin_C_F0194_v16.md#formal-sixth-matching), the [positive-frame rectangle theorem](Yin_C_F0194_v17.md#statement) adopted at [C194v18](Yin_C_F0194_v18.md#decision-positive-frame-rectangles-reviewed), and the [accepted real matching theorem](../audits/literature/MNV16/Yin_U001.md#matching-rank).
The only new counting change in the row argument is from two independent original laws to a fixed finite number of them; the matrix averaging is supplied by the genuine positive frame instead of an identity noise coefficient.
No geometric coefficient hypothesis is silently supplied by this deduction.

<a id="statement"></a>
## Exact conditional statement

Fix real symmetric matrices \(A_0,\ldots,A_d\in M_3(\mathbb R)\), with fixed \(d\) and \(\chi_A>0\) in the complex-direction convention of C194v17.
For each input \(j\), fix a real centered variance-one off-diagonal law \(\xi_j\) and a real centered finite-second-moment diagonal law \(\zeta_j\).
The upper off-diagonal entries of \(W_j^{(N)}\) are independent copies of \(\xi_j/\sqrt N\), the diagonal entries are independent copies of \(\zeta_j/\sqrt N\), and the lower entries are their symmetric copies.
All coordinates and inputs are independent within each size, while cross-size coupling is arbitrary.
No higher moment, density or bounded support is assumed.
Let \(\theta_N\) be any deterministic real sequence.
Put \(L_N^\theta=(A_0+\theta_NI_3)\otimes I_N+\sum_{j=1}^dA_j\otimes W_j^{(N)}\).
At physical index \(i\), condition on the actual input minors and exposed diagonals, denoting this sigma-field by \(\mathcal F_i\).
With \(n=N-1\), the exact exposed matrix is
\[
 L_N^\theta\simeq
 \begin{pmatrix}H_i&R_i(x)^T\\R_i(x)&M_i\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 R_i(x)e_a=\sum_{j=1}^d(A_je_a)\otimes x_j,
\]
where the \(d\) original borders \(x_j\in\mathbb R^n\) remain independent and have coordinates \(\xi_j/\sqrt N\).
On the invertible-minor branch let
\[
 Z_i=M_i^{-1},\quad p_i(x)=\det(H_i-R_i(x)^TZ_iR_i(x)),\quad
 \Gamma_i(x)=I_3+R_i(x)^TZ_i^2R_i(x).
\]
The real polynomial \(p_i\) has total degree at most six.
For formal fair signs indexed by \(\mathcal V_i=[n]\times[d]\), write its complete sixth layer as
\[
 \Pi_6p_i(\epsilon/\sqrt N)=\sum_{|J|=6}c_{i,J}\epsilon_J,
 \quad \sigma_i^2=\sum_{|J|=6}c_{i,J}^2,
 \quad
 \tau_i=\frac{\max_{k\in[n]}\sum_{J\cap(\{k\}\times[d])\ne\varnothing}c_{i,J}^2}{\sigma_i^2}.
\]
All input variables at one physical position are distinct, while the load groups all of them.
The coefficients include the original \(N^{-3}\) scale.
Suppose \(\mathcal F_i\)-measurable events \(\mathcal G_i\) and positive scales \(a_i\) satisfy, on \(\mathcal G_i\),
\[
 M_i\text{ invertible},\qquad
 \sup_{\max_j\|x_j\|\le2}\sqrt{\det\Gamma_i(x)}\le a_i,
 \qquad \sigma_i\ge\kappa a_iN^{-3},\qquad \tau_i\le t_N,
 \tag{Fixed-d-sixth-good-minor}
\]
where \(\kappa>0\) is fixed and \(N^{-1/2}\le t_N\to0\) is deterministic.
For independent raw copies of each prescribed law define
\[
 e_{j,N}:=\mathbb P\!\left\{N^{-1}\sum_{k=1}^{N-1}
 [ (\xi'_{j,k})^2+(\xi''_{j,k})^2]>4\right\},
 \qquad r_N=\lfloor(24t_N)^{-1}\rfloor.
\]
Then \(e_{j,N}\to0\), and, for all sufficiently large \(N\), the following conditional-interface consequence holds for deterministic \(b_N\ge1\):
\[
 \begin{split}
 &\mathbb P\{\|L_N^\theta\|\le b_N,
          \ s_{\min}(L_N^\theta)\le c b_N^{-3}N^{-7}\}\\
 &\quad\le Ce^{-cN}+\frac C N\sum_i\mathbb P(\mathcal G_i^c)
       +C\sum_{j=1}^d e_{j,N}
       +Ce^{-cr_N}+C\sqrt{\log r_N}\,r_N^{-1/25}.
 \tag{Fixed-d-sixth-matrix-bridge}
 \end{split}
\]
The constants depend only on the fixed coefficients, laws and \(\kappa\), not on inverse norms, \(b_N\) or \(\theta_N\).
The next argument establishes this implication under its explicit coefficient and area hypotheses; it is not an unconditional floor for the coefficient class.

<a id="argument"></a>
## Complete candidate proof

<a id="fixed-law-matching"></a>
### Matching and exact original-law conversion

Fix a good conditioning and abbreviate \(\sigma_i^2=S\).
Each individual-variable load is at most its physical-group load, hence at most \(t_NS\).
There are at most \((dn)^6\) sixth-degree monomials.
Discarding coefficients smaller than
\[
 \lambda_i:=\frac{\sigma_i}{\sqrt2(dn)^3}
       \ge\frac{\kappa a_i}{\sqrt2d^3N^6}
\]
removes at most half the squared energy.
Each chosen six-set deletes intersecting energy at most \(6t_NS\), so a fixed lexicographic greedy rule supplies at least \(r_N\) disjoint retained six-sets.
This matching is measurable before fresh coordinates are exposed.

For every \((k,j)\), independently expose two raw copies and a fair sign, and set
\[
 m_{j,k}=\tfrac12(\xi'_{j,k}+\xi''_{j,k}),\quad
 b_{j,k}=\tfrac12(\xi'_{j,k}-\xi''_{j,k}),\quad
 (x_j)_k=(m_{j,k}+b_{j,k}\epsilon_{j,k})/\sqrt N.
\]
This selects one of two independent copies equiprobably, so its conditional joint law is exactly that of the original borders.
Because each \(\mathbb E b_{j,k}^2=1/2\) and \(d\) is fixed, choose common \(b_0,p_0>0\) with \(\mathbb P\{|b_{j,k}|\ge b_0\}\ge p_0\) for all \(j\).
The disjoint matching edges have independent survival indicators with probabilities at least \(p_0^6\).
Exponential Markov, exactly as in C194v16, leaves at least \(\lfloor p_0^6r_N/2\rfloor\) surviving edges outside probability \(Ce^{-cr_N}\).
Their paired sixth coefficients have modulus at least \(b_0^6\lambda_i\).
Indeed, only an original degree-six monomial with six distinct individual variables can yield six distinct signs; translations and sign-power reductions cannot create a sixth coefficient from a repeated-variable or lower-degree monomial.
The paired sixth coefficient is exactly \(c_{i,J}\prod_{v\in J}b_v\).

The scalar identity \(|m_{j,k}|+|b_{j,k}|=\max(|\xi'_{j,k}|,|\xi''_{j,k}|)\) implies
\[
 \sup_{\epsilon}\|x_j\|^2\le N^{-1}\sum_k[(\xi'_{j,k})^2+(\xi''_{j,k})^2].
\]
The fixed-law weak law gives the stated vanishing \(e_{j,N}\).
Outside probability \(\sum_j e_{j,N}\), all sign choices in the entire paired support satisfy \(\max_j\|x_j\|\le2\), hence their graph area is at most \(a_i\).
This guard is imposed on the pairs before conditioning on the signs; neither it nor a parent norm event is inserted into the original minor law.
No simultaneous copy construction across different physical indices is required.

<a id="graph-and-average"></a>
### Graph normalization and the positive-frame average

On a good minor the exact orthonormal graph basis is
\[
 U_i=\binom{I_3}{-Z_iR_i}\Gamma_i^{-1/2}.
\]
It spans the orthogonal complement of the other columns, since their retained square block is invertible.
Thus the exposed distance matrix is
\(F_i=\Gamma_i^{-1/2}(H_i-R_i^TZ_iR_i)\).
Let \(d_i=s_{\min}(F_i)\); at arbitrary, including singular, minors define \(d_i\) directly by projection of the three exposed columns onto the complement of the other columns.
On \(\|L_N^\theta\|\le b_N\), one has \(\|F_i\|\le b_N\) and
\[
 |p_i(x)|\le b_N^2 d_i\sqrt{\det\Gamma_i(x)}.
\]
Choose \(c_1>0\) with \(2c_1<b_0^6\kappa/(\sqrt2d^3)\).
On the good paired support,
\[
 \{d_i\le c_1b_N^{-2}N^{-6},\ \|L_N^\theta\|\le b_N\}
 \subseteq\{|p_i(x)|\le c_1a_iN^{-6}\}.
\]
The latter interval is shorter than every surviving matching coefficient threshold.
The accepted MNV16 real fair-sign theorem at degree six therefore bounds its conditional probability by \(C\sqrt{\log r_N}\,r_N^{-1/25}\), after the exponential survival exception.
Adding the pair and bad-minor failures and integrating gives the same bound for each original distance event, with the additional terms \(\mathbb P(\mathcal G_i^c)+\sum_j e_{j,N}+Ce^{-cr_N}\).
All lower-degree coefficients and the actual center remain unrestricted.

Apply the independently adopted C194v17 distance average to this actual positive-frame pencil.
Its shift is the deterministic \((A_0+\theta_NI_3)\otimes I_N\), and its distance threshold is \(8b_NNu_N/a\), where \(a>0\) is the rectangle constant.
Choose \(u_N=c b_N^{-3}N^{-7}\) with \(8c/a\le c_1\).
For all sufficiently large \(N\), this also satisfies the rectangle theorem's \(u_N\le a/(4\sqrt N)\).
Substitution proves (Fixed-d-sixth-matrix-bridge), including every full-parent corank and an average of unconditional minor failures.
The argument never conditions one original noise input on another or assumes an identity noise direction.

<a id="remaining-scope"></a>
## Remaining original-model application

To obtain a new original-model floor, the complete sixth coefficients and \(a_i\) in (Fixed-d-sixth-good-minor) must still be supplied on events whose averaged failure vanishes.
One must also justify a common atom-avoiding vanishing shift and return to the unshifted parent.
The [adopted spectral-point geometry](Yin_C_F0187_v26.md#decision-positive-frame-spectral-spread-reviewed) is relevant to the developing coefficient application, but is not used by the conditional proof above.
The free-gap high-energy branch and the original arbitrary-coefficient Task remain outside this conditional interface.
