# Inverse trace-norm approximation and conditional Schur control for real size-three pencils

This theorem extends the fixed-energy mechanism of [C194v18](Yin_C_F0194_v18.md#decision-low-inverse-energy-reviewed) to inverses approximable by a bounded normalized Frobenius-energy matrix with an error of nuclear norm \(o(N)\).
The approximation is measurable under the actual original input minors and need not commute with their inverse.
The full Schur numerator and its graph normalization are controlled without a physical-spread hypothesis or a rank bound.
A separate explicit mean approximation hypothesis then gives a parent least-value floor and the free-gap logarithmic application.
This is a project deduction developed for Yin through ordinary proof work and authoring self-checks, adopted by the [proved decision](#decision-inverse-trace-norm-approximation-reviewed) with [independent whole-proof review](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_F0200_v22.md#inverse-trace-norm-approximation-whole-proof).
Its [D10 development](../drafts/Yin_D_F0010.md#free-gap-finite-pole-resonance) is extraction provenance, not a mathematical dependency.
The adopted fixed-input sixth-degree bridge at [C194v19](Yin_C_F0194_v19.md#decision-fixed-input-sixth-bridge-reviewed) remains unchanged and is not needed in this proof.

## Original model and free-gap target

Fix \(d\ge1\) and real symmetric \(A_0,\ldots,A_d\in M_3(\mathbb R)\), independently of \(N\), with
\[
 \chi_A=\inf_{\|u\|=\|v\|=1}\sum_{j=1}^d|u^*A_jv|^2>0,
\]
where the unit vectors are complex.
Each \(W_j^{(N)}\) is real symmetric, its independent upper off-diagonal entries have the fixed real law \(\xi_j/\sqrt N\), and its independent diagonal entries have the fixed real law \(\zeta_j/\sqrt N\).
Assume \(\mathbb E\xi_j=\mathbb E\zeta_j=0\), \(\mathbb E\xi_j^2=1\), and \(\mathbb E\zeta_j^2<\infty\).
All upper coordinates and inputs are independent within each size; cross-size coupling is arbitrary.
No density, bounded-support assumption or moment above two is imposed.
Put
\[
 L_N=A_0\otimes I_N+\sum_jA_j\otimes W_j^{(N)},\qquad
 L=A_0\otimes1+\sum_jA_j\otimes s_j,\qquad
 \tau_3=\operatorname{tr}_3\otimes\tau,
\]
where the \(s_j\) are free centered variance-one semicircular elements in a finite von Neumann algebra with faithful normal trace \(\tau\).
Assume \(0\notin\sigma(L)\), fix \(0<g<\operatorname{dist}(0,\sigma(L))\), and define
\[
 v=\log\Delta_{\tau_3}(L)\in\mathbb R,\qquad
 X_N=\frac1{3N}\log|\det L_N|.
\]
A zero determinant means \(X_N=-\infty\).
The gap and boundedness of \(L\) give the finite free target; they impose no finite-matrix inverse-tail condition.
For the later target comparison put
\[
 D_N(g)=\frac1{3N}\sum_{|\lambda_a(L_N)|<g}
            \log\frac g{|\lambda_a(L_N)|},\qquad
 Y_N(g)=\frac1{3N}\sum_a\log\max(g,|\lambda_a(L_N)|).
\]
An exact zero contributes \(+\infty\) to \(D_N(g)\), and \(X_N=Y_N(g)-D_N(g)\).
The adopted [C186v4 logarithmic-growth controls](Yin_C_F0186_v4.md#log-statistics), applied to \(L_N^2\) and \(x\mapsto\frac12\log\max(g^2,x)\), give \(Y_N(g)\to v\) almost surely under every coupling.
Thus \(\limsup_NX_N\le v\) almost surely independently of the extra approximation premise below.
Consequently \(D_N(g)\to0\) in probability along a deterministic subsequence is itself a sufficient total-loss direction for the determinant limsup contract, by the [C190v1 marginal criterion](Yin_C_F0190_v1.md#coupling-criterion).

## Exact conditional statement

Use the original real model and the [three deletion levels of C194v18](Yin_C_F0194_v18.md#statement), with fixed deletion bound \(r\), one deterministic real sequence \(\theta_N\to0\), and the original \(N^{-1/2}\) normalization.
For deterministic \(T\subset[N]\), \(|T|\le r\), and \(i\notin T\), let the actual minor be \(M=M_{T,i}\) on \(J=[N]\setminus(T\cup\{i\})\), where \(n=|J|\).
Put \(A_N=A_0+\theta_NI_3\), \(\Lambda=\sum_j\|A_j\|^2\), and \(\mathcal S(H)=\sum_jA_jHA_j\).
The actual parent block decomposition is
\[
 L_{N,[N]\setminus T}^{\theta}
 \simeq\begin{pmatrix}A_N+D_i&R^T\\R&M\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Let \(\mathcal M_{T,i}\) be the sigma-field generated by the original input minors on \(J\), and let \(\mathcal F_{T,i}\) be generated by \(\mathcal M_{T,i}\) and the exposed original diagonal entries.
Thus \(D_i\) is \(\mathcal F_{T,i}\)-measurable, and the \(d\) original border vectors defining \(R\) remain independent of \(\mathcal F_{T,i}\), with their prescribed centered variance-one laws divided by \(\sqrt N\).
Choose any real symmetric \(3n\)-by-\(3n\) matrix \(E=E_{T,i,N}\) measurable under \(\mathcal M_{T,i}\).
Neither a spectral form nor commutation with \(M\) is assumed.
For fixed \(K\ge1\) and a deterministic sequence \(\delta_N\to0\), \(\delta_N\ge0\), define the minor-measurable branch
\[
 \mathcal B_{T,i,N}:=
 \left\{M\text{ invertible},\quad
       \frac{\|E\|_F^2}{N}\le K,\quad
       \frac{\|Z-E\|_1}{N}\le\delta_N\right\},
 \qquad Z=M^{-1}.
 \tag{Trace-approximation-branch}
\]
Here \(\|\cdot\|_1\) is nuclear norm, not the sum of absolute matrix entries.
On this branch put
\[
 H=Z-E,\quad g_E=\frac1N\sum_{k\in J}E_{kk},\quad
 B_E=A_N-\mathcal S(g_E),\quad
 h_K=\sqrt{3K},\quad c_K=(4h_K)^{-1}.
\]
The good conditioning event is
\[
 \mathcal G_{T,i,N}:=\mathcal B_{T,i,N}\cap
 \left\{\|B_Eg_E-I_3\|\le\frac12,
             \quad\|D_i\|\le\frac1{8h_K}\right\}.
 \tag{Trace-approximation-good-conditioning}
\]
The following deduction gives
\[
 \rho_N:=\sup_{\substack{|T|\le r\\ i\notin T}}
       \mathbb P(\mathcal B_{T,i,N}\setminus\mathcal G_{T,i,N})
       \longrightarrow0.
 \tag{Trace-approximation-relative-errors}
\]
The bound is uniform over the chosen measurable approximations with the same \(K,\delta_N\).
It concerns marginal probabilities, not the probability of a union over minors or a conditional law given \(\mathcal B_{T,i,N}\).
The event \(\mathcal G_{T,i,N}\) contains no condition on the fresh border.

There is a deterministic \(q_N(K)\to0\), supplied by the adopted finite-second-moment quadratic estimate below, such that, with
\[
 \epsilon_N=\min\{1,8h_K(q_N(K)+\Lambda\delta_N)\},\qquad
 V_N=3+\Lambda\bigl(\sqrt K+\sqrt N\,\delta_N\bigr)^2,
 \tag{Trace-approximation-errors}
\]
the complete Schur block and graph matrix
\[
 S=A_N+D_i-R^TZR,\qquad
 \Gamma=I_3+R^TZ^2R,\qquad F=\Gamma^{-1/2}S
\]
satisfy, on each good conditioning,
\[
 \mathbb P\{s_{\min}(S)<c_K\mid\mathcal F_{T,i}\}
 \le\epsilon_N,\qquad
 \mathbb P\{|\det S|<c_K^3\mid\mathcal F_{T,i}\}
 \le\epsilon_N,
 \tag{Trace-approximation-full-Schur}
\]
and, for every \(t,u>0\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathbb P\{s_{\min}(F)\le t\mid\mathcal F_{T,i}\}
   &\le\epsilon_N+c_K^{-2}V_Nt^2,\\
 \mathbb P\{|\det F|\le u\mid\mathcal F_{T,i}\}
   &\le\epsilon_N+\frac{V_N}{3c_K^2}u^{2/3}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{Trace-approximation-graph-tails}
\]
In particular both the \(N^{-1/2}\) distance threshold and the \(N^{-3/2}\) normalized-determinant threshold have vanishing conditional failure, since
\[
 \frac{V_N}{N}
 =\frac3N+\Lambda\left(\frac K N+
                \frac{2\sqrt K\,\delta_N}{\sqrt N}+\delta_N^2\right)
 \longrightarrow0.
 \tag{Trace-approximation-graph-scale}
\]
This conditional calculation itself does not use \(\chi_A>0\) or the free gap.
Those hypotheses enter the target and the subsequent parent-matrix application.
No arbitrary growing \(K_N\) is substituted for fixed \(K\) in these estimates.


### Conditional parent and logarithmic conclusions

For \(T=\varnothing\), additionally suppose that
\[
 \overline b_N:=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N
       \mathbb P(\mathcal B_{\varnothing,i,N}^{c})\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{Trace-approximation-parent-premise}
\]
This convergence may hold only on one deterministic infinite set of dimensions \(\mathcal N\); all probability conclusions in this paragraph are then restricted to \(N\in\mathcal N\).
For some fixed \(c>0\), the conclusion is
\[
 \mathbb P\{s_{\min}(L_N^\theta)\le cN^{-2}\}\longrightarrow0.
\]
If \(\theta_N=0\), or if the one common real shift satisfies \(0<\theta_N<N^{-3}\) and the same mean approximation premise holds for its actual shifted minors, then, after reducing \(c\),
\[
 \mathbb P\{s_{\min}(L_N)\le cN^{-2}\}\longrightarrow0,\qquad
 X_N\xrightarrow{\mathbb P}v
 \quad(N\to\infty,\ N\in\mathcal N).
\]
Consequently \(\limsup_{N\to\infty}X_N=v\) almost surely under every allowed cross-size coupling, with that limsup taken over all dimensions.
For \(\mathcal N\) containing all sufficiently large dimensions, the probability logarithmic conclusion holds along the full sequence.
These are implications from the explicit mean approximation premise, not assertions that the free gap supplies it.
The [finite-pole and inverse-tail discussion](#finite-poles-and-trace-tail-boundary) preserves the exact sufficient special cases and unresolved actual-model input.

## Proof

<a id="inverse-trace-norm-stability"></a>
### Noncommuting resolvent comparison and the actual-minor equation

For a real symmetric matrix \(X\), set \(f_\eta(X)=X(I-i\eta X)^{-1}\), where \(\eta>0\).
The exact identity, valid without commutation between \(Z\) and \(E\), is
\[
 f_\eta(Z)-f_\eta(E)
 =(I-i\eta Z)^{-1}(Z-E)(I-i\eta E)^{-1}.
 \tag{Trace-approximation-resolvent-identity}
\]
It follows by writing \(f_\eta(X)=((I-i\eta X)^{-1}-I)/(i\eta)\) and applying the inverse-difference identity in the displayed order.
Both outside factors are contractions, so
\[
 \|f_\eta(Z)-f_\eta(E)\|_1\le\|Z-E\|_1,
 \qquad
 \|f_\eta(E)-E\|_1\le\eta\|E\|_F^2.
\]
The second inequality follows by spectral calculus, since each real eigenvalue \(e\) contributes at most \(\eta e^2\).
Moreover \(f_\eta(Z)=(M-i\eta I)^{-1}\), and the partial-trace bound
\(\|\sum_k X_{kk}\|\le\|X\|_1\) holds for arbitrary complex \(X\) by trace duality.
Consequently, on \(\mathcal B_{T,i,N}\),
\[
 \|g_\eta-g_E\|\le u_N(\eta):=\eta K+\delta_N,\qquad
 \|g_E\|\le\frac{\sqrt{3n}\|E\|_F}{N}\le h_K,\qquad
 \|g_\eta\|\le h_K+u_N(\eta),
 \tag{Trace-approximation-resolvent-comparison}
\]
where \(g_\eta=N^{-1}\sum_k[(M-i\eta I)^{-1}]_{kk}\).

Use exactly the [fixed-\(\eta\) actual-minor identity of C194v18](Yin_C_F0194_v18.md#fixed-eta-identity):
\[
 B_\eta=A_N-i\eta I_3-\mathcal S(g_\eta),\qquad
 \sup_{T,i}\mathbb E\left\|B_\eta g_\eta-\frac nN I_3\right\|
 \longrightarrow0
 \quad\text{for every fixed }\eta>0.
 \tag{Trace-approximation-actual-minor-input}
\]
Its internal leave-one-out rows are original independent rows, and its internal minors are used only at the nonreal parameter.
The input is an unconditional expectation estimate for the actual pencil; the approximation \(E\) is not substituted for that pencil or its internal minors.
Fix \(a\) with \(\|A_N\|\le a\) eventually and write \(u=u_N(\eta)\).
Since \(\|\mathcal S\|\le\Lambda\),
\[
 \begin{split}
 \|B_Eg_E-B_\eta g_\eta\|
 &\le\|B_E\|\|g_E-g_\eta\|
       +\|B_E-B_\eta\|\|g_\eta\|\\
 &\le [a+\Lambda(2h_K+u)]u+\eta(h_K+u).
 \end{split}
 \tag{Trace-approximation-zero-defect}
\]
For every fixed tolerance, choose \(\eta>0\) sufficiently small at fixed \(K\), and then take \(N\) large so that \(\delta_N\) and \((r+1)/N\) are sufficiently small.
Markov's inequality applied to (Trace-approximation-actual-minor-input) yields
\[
 \sup_{T,i}\mathbb P\{\mathcal B_{T,i,N},
                         \ \|B_Eg_E-I_3\|>\varepsilon\}
 \longrightarrow0\qquad(\varepsilon>0).
\]
Together with \(\mathbb E\|D_i\|=O(N^{-1/2})\), this proves (Trace-approximation-relative-errors).
All constants and errors are uniform over the fixed deletion sizes; no union bound over \(T,i\) is taken.

<a id="inverse-trace-norm-schur-graph"></a>
### Original-row errors, complete numerator and graph area

The [quadratic estimate of C194v18](Yin_C_F0194_v18.md#quadratic-concentration) covers arbitrary deterministic coefficient matrices at a fixed Frobenius scale, including nonsymmetric matrices.
The coefficient maps of the \((a,b)\) entry are the same \(U_aEU_b^T\), with Frobenius norm at most \(\Lambda\|E\|_F\).
After conditioning on \(\mathcal F_{T,i}\), it therefore supplies a deterministic \(q_N(K)\to0\), uniform on the branch, with
\[
 \mathbb E\bigl[\|R^TER-\mathcal S(g_E)\|
                    \mid\mathcal F_{T,i}\bigr]\le q_N(K).
 \tag{Trace-approximation-residual-row-error}
\]
Only the prescribed second moments are used; neither this estimate nor the fixed-\(\eta\) input assumes a fourth moment.
For the signed selfadjoint remainder \(H=Z-E\), the deterministic order inequalities
\(-R^T|H|R\le R^THR\le R^T|H|R\) give
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathbb E[\|R^THR\|\mid\mathcal F_{T,i}]
 &\le\mathbb E[\operatorname{Tr}(R^T|H|R)\mid\mathcal F_{T,i}]\\
 &=\frac1N\operatorname{Tr}
       \left[\left(\sum_jA_j^2\otimes I_n\right)|H|\right]
 \le\frac{\Lambda\|H\|_1}{N}\le\Lambda\delta_N.
 \end{split}
 \tag{Trace-approximation-head-row-error}
\]
The middle trace is \(3n\)-dimensional, and the exact equality follows from the original row covariance and independence from the entire conditioning.
No independence between \(R^TER\) and \(R^THR\) is used.
Adding the two estimates gives
\[
 \mathbb E\bigl[\|R^TZR-\mathcal S(g_E)\|
                       \mid\mathcal F_{T,i}\bigr]
 \le q_N(K)+\Lambda\delta_N.
\]
On \(\mathcal G_{T,i,N}\), the product \(B_Eg_E\) is invertible and
\[
 B_E^{-1}=g_E(B_Eg_E)^{-1},\qquad
 \|B_E^{-1}\|\le2h_K.
\]
Except with conditional probability at most \(\epsilon_N\), the preceding quadratic error is at most \((8h_K)^{-1}\).
Combining it with the already conditioned diagonal bound gives
\(s_{\min}(S)\ge(2h_K)^{-1}-2(8h_K)^{-1}=c_K\).
This proves (Trace-approximation-full-Schur) for the complete numerator, including all lower degrees and repeated-coordinate terms.

For completeness, \(U=\binom{I_3}{-ZR}\Gamma^{-1/2}\) has orthonormal columns spanning the orthogonal complement of the other parent columns because \(M\) is invertible.
Thus \(F=U^T\binom{A_N+D_i}{R}\) is the actual physical block-distance matrix, and
\(|\det F|=|\det S|/\sqrt{\det\Gamma}\).
The nuclear-norm error permits more inverse energy than C194v18:
\[
 \frac{\|Z\|_F^2}{N}
 \le\frac{(\|E\|_F+\|H\|_1)^2}{N}
 \le(\sqrt K+\sqrt N\,\delta_N)^2.
\]
The exact graph trace and its bound are therefore
\[
 \mathbb E[\operatorname{Tr}\Gamma\mid\mathcal F_{T,i}]
 =3+\frac1N\operatorname{Tr}
       \left[\left(\sum_jA_j^2\otimes I_n\right)Z^2\right]
 \le V_N.
\]
On \(s_{\min}(S)\ge c_K\),
\[
 s_{\min}(F)\ge\frac{c_K}{\sqrt{\operatorname{Tr}\Gamma}},\qquad
 |\det F|\ge c_K^3\left(\frac3{\operatorname{Tr}\Gamma}\right)^{3/2}.
\]
Markov's inequality proves (Trace-approximation-graph-tails), including its endpoint choices in (Trace-approximation-graph-scale).
The graph area need not stay bounded, and the unnormalized determinant lower bound \(c_K^3\) is not misidentified with its normalized counterpart.

<a id="inverse-trace-norm-parent-interface"></a>
### The parent distance average and the determinant target

For the undeleted parent \(T=\varnothing\), suppose there exist the preceding \(K,\delta_N,E_{\varnothing,i,N}\) such that
\[
 \overline b_N:=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N
                  \mathbb P(\mathcal B_{\varnothing,i,N}^{c})
 \longrightarrow0.
 \tag{Trace-approximation-missing-probability-input}
\]
This is an additional actual-model probability hypothesis, not a consequence of the conditional proof.
All limits in this application may instead be restricted to any one deterministic subsequence of dimensions.
Let \(d_i=s_{\min}(Q_iC_i)\) be the physical column-block distance in the [adopted C194v17 statement](Yin_C_F0194_v17.md#statement).
On the invertible-minor branch it equals \(s_{\min}(F)\), while it remains defined without that branch.
Integrating the conditional estimate and paying for its complement gives
\[
 \frac1N\sum_i\mathbb P\{d_i\le N^{-1/2}\}
 \le\overline b_N+\rho_N+\epsilon_N+
                         c_K^{-2}\frac{V_N}{N}\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{Trace-approximation-mean-distance}
\]
This uses an average of probabilities, not an event on which all minors are simultaneously good.

The original finite-second-moment [Frobenius guard calculation](Yin_C_F0186_v22.md#frobenius-norm-guard) does not use its surrounding spectral-point hypothesis and gives here
\[
 \frac{\|L_N^\theta\|_F^2}{N}\xrightarrow{\mathbb P}
 Q_A:=\|A_0\|_F^2+\sum_j\|A_j\|_F^2.
\]
Choose fixed \(B>\max\{1,\sqrt{Q_A}\}\), so \(\mathbb P\{\|L_N^\theta\|>B\sqrt N\}\to0\).
Denote the constants of C194v17 by \(a_{\rm R},\delta_{\rm R},c,C\), to distinguish them from \(\delta_N\).
With \(b_N=B\sqrt N\) and \(u_N=a_{\rm R}N^{-2}/(8B)\), its exact distance threshold is
\[
 \frac{8b_NNu_N}{a_{\rm R}}=N^{-1/2}.
\]
The required inequality \(u_N\le a_{\rm R}/(4\sqrt N)\) holds eventually.
Hence the adopted rectangle and distance-average theorem yields
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathbb P\{s_{\min}(L_N^\theta)\le a_{\rm R}N^{-2}/(8B)\}
 &\le\mathbb P\{\|L_N^\theta\|>B\sqrt N\}+Ce^{-cN}\\
 &\quad+\frac2{\delta_{\rm R}}
       \left(\overline b_N+\rho_N+\epsilon_N+
                              c_K^{-2}\frac{V_N}{N}\right)
 \longrightarrow0.
 \end{split}
 \tag{Trace-approximation-conditional-parent-floor}
\]
It introduces no corank restriction or independence between distance events.

One may take \(\theta_N=0\) if the missing probability input includes actual minor invertibility.
Alternatively, the countable-atom argument at [C186v22's common-shift interface](Yin_C_F0186_v22.md#common-atom-avoiding-shift) chooses a single deterministic real \(0<\theta_N<N^{-3}\) avoiding the atoms of all the finitely many actual fixed-deletion minors at that dimension.
That argument is valid for the present selfadjoint matrices independently of a free spectral-point assumption and changes no original fresh-row law.
In this alternative, (Trace-approximation-missing-probability-input) must hold for the actual minors with that same chosen shift; atom avoidance does not supply the approximation.
The deterministic singular-value inequality then returns (Trace-approximation-conditional-parent-floor) to the original matrix with constant \(a_{\rm R}/(16B)\), since \(N^{-3}=o(N^{-2})\).
The shift is removed before taking logarithms.

### Original logarithms and every-coupling limsup

Let \(a_{1,N}\le\cdots\le a_{3N,N}\) be the original unshifted singular values and \(k_N=12\lceil N^{3/4}\rceil+2\).
The [adopted C187v3 middle theorem](Yin_C_F0187_v3.md#statement) gives \(a_{r,N}\ge c_1r/(3N)\) for \(r\ge k_N\), with summable failure.
Its middle negative-log calculation gives, for small \(t>0\), on that event,
\[
 \frac1{3N}\sum_{\substack{r\ge k_N\\a_{r,N}<t}}
          (-\log a_{r,N})\le Ct(1+|\log t|).
\]
On the original \(c'N^{-2}\) floor event,
\[
 \frac1{3N}\sum_{r<k_N}(-\log a_{r,N})_+
 \le\frac{k_N}{3N}(2\log N+|\log c'|)
 =O(N^{-1/4}\log N)\longrightarrow0.
\]
A union bound, first \(N\to\infty\) within \(\mathcal N\) and then \(t\downarrow0\), gives negative-logarithmic uniform integrability in probability on those dimensions.
Zero singular values contribute infinite loss and are excluded only on the original floor event.

The same Frobenius guard at zero shift gives, for each fixed \(D>Q_A\), on an event of probability tending to one,
\[
 \frac1{3N}\sum_{a_{r,N}>T}\log a_{r,N}
 \le\frac D3\sup_{x>T}\frac{\log x}{x^2}\longrightarrow0
 \qquad(T\to\infty).
\]
C186v4 gives almost-sure weak convergence of the original singular law to \(\nu_{|L|}\) under every coupling.
Integrate \(x\mapsto\log(\min(T,\max(t,x)))\), which is bounded and continuous for fixed \(0<t<1<T\), and remove the cutoffs by these two tail bounds.
The free law is bounded and has a gap at zero, so its logarithmic tails vanish.
This proves \(X_N\to v\) in probability for \(N\to\infty\) within \(\mathcal N\).

The almost-sure bound \(X_N\le Y_N(g)\to v\) in the model section gives \(\limsup_NX_N\le v\) along all dimensions.
Probability convergence on the deterministic infinite set \(\mathcal N\) supplies increasing deterministic \(N_\ell\in\mathcal N\) with
\[
 \mathbb P\{X_{N_\ell}<v-1/\ell\}\le2^{-\ell}.
\]
C190v1, equivalently first Borel--Cantelli for these complete prescribed marginals, gives the reverse limsup inequality for every coupling.
The subsequence depends only on the marginal model, not on cross-size dependence.
No full-sequence almost-sure logarithmic limit or expectation convergence of raw logarithms is asserted.
No assertion about the missing mean approximation premise or the unconditional free-gap contract has been obtained from this conditional assembly.

<a id="finite-poles-and-trace-tail-boundary"></a>
### Finite poles, growing pole counts and the remaining probability input

For an actual spectral decomposition
\[
 Z=E+H,\qquad H=\sum_{\ell=1}^{k_N}\lambda_\ell e_\ell e_\ell^T,
 \qquad Ee_\ell=0,
\]
with orthonormal inverse eigenmodes, \(\|H\|_1=\sum_\ell|\lambda_\ell|\).
Consequently fixed \(K\), fixed \(k\), and \(\sum_{\ell\le k}|\lambda_\ell|\le N\delta_N=o(N)\) do suffice for the conditional Schur and graph conclusions just proved.
There is no requirement that the head modes be physically spread.
As a comparison of allowed spectral scales, a single inverse pole of size \(N^{3/4}\) contributes \(N^{1/2}\) to normalized squared inverse energy but only \(N^{-1/4}\) to normalized nuclear error.
This comparison shows why the conditional hypothesis can extend the low-energy mechanism; it does not assert that such poles occur with positive probability in the actual model or that its low-energy event fails with positive probability.

The same proof permits any \(k_N\), provided the same total nuclear error is \(o(N)\); it does not need a separate condition \(k_N=o(N)\).
For comparison, the spectral deletion itself gives the alternative rank-only background estimate
\[
 \|g_\eta-g_E\|\le\eta K+\frac{k_N}{N\eta}.
 \tag{Trace-approximation-rank-background}
\]
The residual part contributes at most \(\eta\|E\|_F^2/N\), and each removed eigenmode contributes at most \(1/(N\eta)\).
Thus \(k_N=o(N)\) is enough to recover the background zero-defect comparison by first fixing \(\eta\) and then letting it decrease.
It does not make the head correction \(R^THR\) small by the present row estimate or control the present graph trace.
Already one pole of size comparable to \(N\) lies outside that small-error argument.
No counterexample to an actual-model theorem under rank control alone is claimed here; that stronger regime is unresolved.

In the free-gap application there is a canonical measurable choice, on the invertible branch,
\[
 E=M^{-1}\mathbf1_{\{|M|\ge g\}},\qquad
 H=M^{-1}\mathbf1_{\{|M|<g\}},\qquad
 \frac{\|E\|_F^2}{N}\le\frac3{g^2},
\]
with \(E\) defined by the bounded spectral function \(x\mapsto x^{-1}\mathbf1_{\{|x|\ge g\}}\) even on singular minors.
One may take \(K=\max\{1,3/g^2\}\).
The required nuclear error is exactly
\[
 \frac{\|H\|_1}{N}
 =\frac1N\sum_{|\lambda_a(M)|<g}\frac1{|\lambda_a(M)|}.
 \tag{Trace-approximation-gap-tail}
\]
Zero eigenvalues are assigned infinite error when this criterion is used without an atom-avoiding shift.
The free gap and weak convergence force the normalized number of such gap modes to vanish, but supply no bound for this inverse-first-moment tail.
The new actual-model input would be a deterministic \(\delta_N\to0\) for which the mean failure of this tail bound, including invertibility when needed, tends to zero along favorable deterministic dimensions.
The general trace-norm approximation allows other measurable selfadjoint approximants, including bounded spectral truncations of the inverse, without changing its proof.
At present that formulation supplies a rigorously checked conditional interface, not a proved probabilistic substitute for the remaining free-gap problem.

The trace-norm tail is only a sufficient direction and is not proposed as necessary for the determinant contract.
For the original parent itself, if
\(\delta_N^{\rm par}=N^{-1}\sum_{|\lambda_a(L_N)|<g}|\lambda_a(L_N)|^{-1}\to0\) in probability along a deterministic subsequence, then already
\[
 D_N(g)\le\frac g{3N}
       \sum_{|\lambda_a(L_N)|<g}|\lambda_a(L_N)|^{-1}
       =\frac g3\,\delta_N^{\rm par}
\]
by \(\log(g/|x|)\le g/|x|\), with both losses infinite at zero.
That parent-tail hypothesis bypasses the Schur and floor argument entirely, but is stronger than the sufficient total-log-loss target stated at the start.
The minor-average hypothesis above has a different role: it converts local conditional distance bounds into an original parent floor using the positive frame.
Neither of these actual inverse-tail hypotheses follows here from the free gap, and replacing the total-log-loss problem by either one does not by itself advance its probability proof.


<a id="remaining-scope"></a>
## Exact boundary and continuing question

This is a conditional extension of the low inverse-energy mechanism; the mean approximation premise for the actual free-gap model is not established.
In particular a free gap and \(o(N)\) gap-mode count do not supply the inverse-first-moment tail bound, which is only a sufficient condition and is not proposed as necessary for the determinant contract.
The proof permits growing pole counts with small total nuclear error, but rank control alone leaves strong poles untreated.
For one pole of size comparable to \(N\), the determinant resonance factor still involves two functions of the same original fresh rows.
That actual-law strong-resonance problem remains at the [continuing D10 investigation](../drafts/Yin_D_F0010.md#free-gap-finite-pole-resonance), and no estimate for it is assumed here.
The conditional result does not complete F13 or transfer automatically to all scalar-polynomial linearizations in F14.
