# Free-gap reservoirs and the high residual inverse-energy branch

This project deduction retains an entire exceptional spectral space in the real size-three positive-frame branch of F13.
It uses a minor-measurable coordinate reservoir to apply the existing complete sixth-degree coefficient argument to a compression of the actual inverse.
The coefficient scale still controls the graph denominator of the full inverse and the original fresh rows.
The resulting high-residual estimate leaves a bounded-energy remainder after two inverse modes, without assuming that one-mode residual energy is tight.
This complete statement is adopted after [independent whole-proof review](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_F0200_v29.md#free-gap-reservoir-whole-proof); its bounded-two-mode parent premise remains explicit and no general Task completion is asserted.
The [D10 reservoir development](../drafts/Yin_D_F0010.md#low-mode-flat-reservoir-probe) is extraction provenance, not a proof premise.

<a id="statement"></a>
## Exact original model and conclusions

Fix real symmetric matrices \(A_0,\ldots,A_d\in M_3(\mathbb R)\), with fixed \(d\), and assume
\[
 \chi_A=\inf_{\|a\|=\|b\|=1,\ a,b\in\mathbb C^3}
             \sum_j|a^*A_jb|^2>0.
\]
Use independent original real Wigner inputs with fixed centered variance-one off-diagonal laws and fixed centered finite-second-moment diagonal laws, normalized by \(N^{-1/2}\), with arbitrary cross-size coupling.
Put \(L_N^\theta=(A_0+\theta_NI_3)\otimes I_N+\sum_jA_j\otimes W_j^{(N)}\), where \(\theta_N\to0\) is deterministic and real.
Assume zero lies outside the spectrum of the free pencil \(L=A_0\otimes1+\sum_jA_j\otimes s_j\).
No bound on the original input norms or higher moments is imposed.

Fix a physical deletion bound \(r\).
For \(|D|\le r\) and \(i\notin D\), expose index \(i\) of the parent restricted outside \(D\), and let \(M\) be its principal block outside \(D\cup\{i\}\).
All retained coordinates keep the original normalization.
Condition on the original input minors and the diagonal coordinates at \(i\), denoting this sigma-field by \(\mathcal F\).
The remaining original border \(R\) is independent of \(\mathcal F\).
Write \(D_i=\sum_j A_j W_{j,ii}^{(N)}\) for the exposed diagonal block.
On the invertible-minor branch put
\[
 Z=M^{-1},\quad q_j=s_j(Z),\quad q_1\ge q_2\ge\cdots\ge0,
 \quad \mathcal E^{(2)}=\frac1N\sum_{j\ge3}q_j^2,
\]
\[
 S=A_0+\theta_NI_3+D_i-R^TZR,\qquad
 \Gamma=I_3+R^TZ^2R,\qquad F=\Gamma^{-1/2}S.
\]
There is a fixed \(c>0\) such that
\[
 \lim_{K\to\infty}\limsup_{N\to\infty}\sup_{D,i}
 \mathbb P\{M\text{ invertible},\ \mathcal E^{(2)}>K,
                     \ |\det F|\le cN^{-6}\}=0.
 \tag{Free-gap-high-residual-graph}
\]
This is a joint marginal estimate, not a uniform conditional assertion on every bare high-energy minor or a union over all indices.
It consequently survives their physical-index average without an extra factor of \(N\).
For any fixed \(B_0>0\), the corresponding projected physical column distance \(d_i\) satisfies the same double-limit estimate on
\[
 \{M\text{ invertible},\ \mathcal E^{(2)}>K,
 d_i\le cB_0^{-2}N^{-7},\ \|L_N^\theta\|\le B_0\sqrt N\}.
 \tag{Free-gap-high-residual-distance}
\]
Using the full-parent norm here also bounds each deleted parent.

The proof supplies an explicit reservoir for every small-rank near-kernel spectral space, quantitative physical spread for the complete compressed inverse and its first two residuals, and a restricted-variable original-law matching argument.
It does not establish a small-ball estimate on the complementary branch \(\mathcal E^{(2)}\le K\).
The [last section](#two-mode-parent-reduction) states precisely how that remaining branch would give a parent floor and the free-gap determinant conclusion.

## Complete candidate argument

<a id="whole-space-reservoir"></a>
### One reservoir for an entire exceptional space

Choose a fixed clipping width \(a>0\) below the free spectral gap and below the near-kernel tolerance in C194v25's natural-scale spread theorem.
For the actual minor put
\[
 V=\operatorname{Ran}\mathbf1_{(-a,a)}(M),\qquad
 E=Z\mathbf1_{\{|M|\ge a\}},\qquad H=Z-E.
\]
On the invertible branch, \(H\) is supported on \(V\), \(\|E\|\le a^{-1}=:C_E\), and \(\|E\|_F\le C_E\sqrt{3N}\).
C194v25's clipping argument supplies deterministic integers \(\ell_N=o(N)\) bounding \(\dim V\) outside a vanishing common exception, for every fixed physical deletion bound.
Its spread theorem supplies one common event of probability tending to one on which every real unit vector in \(V\) has at least \(pN\) blocks of norm at least \(c_-/\sqrt N\), with fixed \(p,c_->0\).
This simultaneous statement follows because every such vector has residual norm at most \(a\).

Set
\[
 \nu_N=\sqrt{\max\{1,\ell_N\}/N},\qquad
 T=\{k:\operatorname{tr}(P_kP_V)\le\nu_N\}.
\]
Here \(P_k\) is the rank-three physical block projection on the actual retained space.
Since \(\sum_k\operatorname{tr}(P_kP_V)=\dim V\), at most \(\ell_N/\nu_N=o(N)\) blocks lie outside \(T\).
Thus, eventually, simultaneously for all \(w\in V\),
\[
 \|P_Tw\|^2\ge\kappa\|w\|^2,\qquad
 \kappa:=pc_-^2/2>0.
 \tag{Reservoir-injection}
\]
The zero-dimensional space is harmless.
Both \(V\) and this choice of \(T\) depend only on the actual minor.
The intrinsic event asserting the rank bound and (Reservoir-injection) is also minor-measurable; the common original events only bound its unconditional failure probability.

Let \(U\) have orthonormal columns spanning \(V\), put \(J=P_TU\), \(G=J^TJ\), and let \(P\) project onto \(P_TV\) inside the retained coordinate space.
Then
\[
 \kappa I\preceq G\preceq I,\qquad
 P=JG^{-1}J^T,\qquad
 \max_{k\in T}\operatorname{tr}(P_kP)\le\alpha_N:=\nu_N/\kappa.
 \tag{Reservoir-leverage}
\]
The formula is interpreted as zero when \(V=\{0\}\).
For every real symmetric operator \(B=PBP\),
\[
 \max_k\|P_kB\|_F^2\le\alpha_N\|B\|_F^2,
 \qquad
 \sum_k\|P_kBP_k\|_F^2\le\alpha_N\|B\|_F^2.
 \tag{Reservoir-supported-spread}
\]
The first inequality uses \(\|P_kP\|^2\le\operatorname{tr}(P_kP)\).
For the second, bound each summand by \(\|P_kP\|^2\|BP_k\|_F^2\), then sum.

Write \(H=U\Lambda U^T\) and \(H_T=P_TH P_T=J\Lambda J^T\).
The polar decomposition of \(J\) identifies the nonzero singular values of \(H_T\) with those of \(G^{1/2}\Lambda G^{1/2}\).
Singular-value multiplication bounds in both directions give
\[
 \kappa s_j(H)\le s_j(H_T)\le s_j(H)
 \tag{Reservoir-signed-compression}
\]
for every nonzero index, with zeros appended.
No positivity of \(\Lambda\) is used.
In particular every squared singular tail is retained by a factor at least \(\kappa^2\).

<a id="compressed-high-residual"></a>
### The complete compressed inverse and its residual scales

Put \(W=P_TZP_T=H_T+E_T\), with \(E_T=P_TEP_T\).
For a matrix \(X\), write \(t_k(X)=(\sum_{j>k}s_j(X)^2)^{1/2}\).
The adopted Frobenius singular-value perturbation bound implies \(|t_k(X)-t_k(Y)|\le\|X-Y\|_F\).
For matrices of different sizes below, append zero singular values before comparing the tail vectors.
Consequently
\[
 t_2(W)\ge\kappa t_2(H)-\|E_T\|_F
 \ge\kappa t_2(Z)-C_E(1+\kappa)\sqrt{3N}.
\]
The operator-norm perturbation bound follows directly from the singular-value min-max formula and the triangle inequality.
It gives, for \(j\le3\),
\[
 s_j(W)\ge\kappa q_j-(1+\kappa)C_E.
\]
On \(\mathcal E^{(2)}>K\), one has \(q_3\ge\sqrt{K/3}\), since there are at most \(3N\) tail terms.
Choose a fixed \(K_0\) sufficiently large, depending only on the fixed constants.
For every \(K\ge K_0\), the displayed bounds imply
\[
 t_2(W)\ge(\kappa/2)\sqrt{KN},\qquad
 s_j(W)\ge(\kappa/2)q_j\quad(j=1,2,3).
 \tag{Compressed-tail-and-head}
\]
This includes all eigenvalue signs and multiplicities.

Write \(W=\sum_j\lambda'_je'_j(e'_j)^T\) with decreasing absolute eigenvalues \(q'_j\), and set
\(D_k=W-\sum_{j\le k}\lambda'_je'_j(e'_j)^T\), \(F_k=\|D_k\|_F\), for \(k=0,1,2\).
Because \((I-P)W=(I-P)E_T\),
\[
 \|(I-P)e'_j\|\le C_E/q'_j,
 \qquad
 \max_i\|P_ie'_j\|^2\le2\alpha_N+2C_E^2/(q'_j)^2
 \le C(\nu_N+K^{-1})\quad(j=1,2).
\]
Moreover
\[
 \|D_k-PD_kP\|_F\le C\sqrt N\quad(k=0,1,2).
 \tag{Compressed-residual-outside}
\]
Indeed \(W-PWP=E_T-PE_TP\) has Frobenius norm at most \(2C_E\sqrt{3N}\).
Each removed head contributes at most \(2|\lambda'_j|\|(I-P)e'_j\|\le2C_E\) to the difference from its compression.
Using (Reservoir-supported-spread) on \(PD_kP\), \(\|PD_kP\|_F\le F_k\), and \(F_k\ge F_2\ge(\kappa/2)\sqrt{KN}\), gives
\[
 \frac{\max_i\|P_iD_k\|_F^2+
                 \sum_i\|P_iD_kP_i\|_F^2}{F_k^2}
 \le C(\nu_N+K^{-1})\quad(k=0,1,2).
 \tag{Compressed-complete-spread}
\]
The constants are uniform in the minor, \(K\ge K_0\), the inverse magnitudes and all eigenvalue ratios.

<a id="restricted-complete-sixth"></a>
### Complete sixth coefficients on the reservoir

Freeze the original border coordinates outside \(T\), and retain all \(d\) input variables at each position in \(T\).
As a polynomial in these retained variables, the complete numerator \(\det S\) has degree at most six.
Its homogeneous degree-six part is exactly
\[
 -\det(R_T(x)^T W R_T(x)).
\]
The outside coordinates add terms of degree at most five, including the terms linear in retained coordinates in each Schur entry.
They change no complete sixth coefficient.

The local coefficient maps on \(T\) are precisely those in C187v27, with the same positive-frame constant and fixed map norms.
That proof's whole-space Gram, signed residual quadratic separation and local row/diagonal bounds apply to the real symmetric matrix \(W\); invertibility of \(W\) is not needed.
Its complete spectral allocation uses only its ordered singular values, residual Frobenius norms and those Gram estimates.
Every branch has a compatible tensor scale \(\mathcal W\ge q'_1q'_2q'_3\), a lower raw tensor bound \(c\mathcal W\), and repeated-physical-coordinate and group-incidence squared masses at most \(C(\nu_N+K^{-1})\mathcal W^2\) by the preceding estimates.
All allocation thresholds are fixed from the frame and map constants before the spectrum, so these constants do not vary with the branch.
Take \(K_0\) larger if necessary and then \(N\) large, so that the repeated-coordinate mass is at most half the squared lower bound.
The exact degree-six Walsh conversion from that proof, still with the original \(N^{-3}\) coefficient scale, yields
\[
 \sigma_{6,T}^2\ge cN^{-6}(q'_1q'_2q'_3)^2
              \ge c'N^{-6}(q_1q_2q_3)^2,
 \qquad \tau_T\le C(\nu_N+K^{-1}).
 \tag{Reservoir-complete-sixth}
\]
Here \(\sigma_{6,T}^2\) is the energy of the full sixth layer in retained individual variables, and \(\tau_T\) is its maximal physical-group load divided by that energy.
The energy lower bound already comes from six distinct physical positions; the load bound includes all individually distinct sixth coefficients.
No spectral-point assumption from C187v27 is imported: its geometric inputs have just been supplied for this compression.

The full graph denominator is bounded using the original inverse, not its compression.
For \(\max_j\|x_j\|\le2\), put \(B_*=2\sum_j\|A_j\|\).
Since \(q_3\ge\sqrt{K/3}\ge1\), singular-value compression gives
\[
 \sqrt{\det\Gamma(x)}\le
 \prod_{j=1}^3\sqrt{1+B_*^2q_j^2}
 \le a_Z:=(1+B_*^2)^{3/2}q_1q_2q_3.
 \tag{Full-graph-area}
\]
Thus \(\sigma_{6,T}\ge c a_ZN^{-3}\), with a fixed constant, even after the outside coordinates are frozen.

<a id="restricted-original-law-bridge"></a>
### Original-law matching with the same full denominator

There are at most \((dN)^6\) retained sixth monomials.
Discarding coefficients smaller than \(\sigma_{6,T}/[\sqrt2(dN)^3]\) loses at most half their energy.
The C194v19 greedy matching argument therefore gives at least
\[
 r_{N,K}=\left\lfloor\frac1{24C(\nu_N+K^{-1})}\right\rfloor
\]
disjoint retained six-sets, after increasing the fixed load constant.
For fixed sufficiently large \(K\), this number is eventually at least a fixed positive multiple of \(K\); it is not claimed to tend to infinity when \(K\) is fixed.
Every retained coefficient has modulus at least \(c a_ZN^{-6}\).
The matching and its reservoir are chosen before exposing any border coordinate.

Apply the exact two-copy representation from C194v19 to all original border coordinates, including those outside \(T\).
The matching survives on at least a fixed fraction of its edges outside probability \(Ce^{-cr_{N,K}}\), with a common fixed lower bound on each surviving pair difference.
Outside a probability \(e_N\to0\), the complete paired support satisfies \(\max_j\|x_j\|\le2\); this follows from the fixed-law weak law for the sum of squares of both copies over all retained-minor coordinates.
On that event (Full-graph-area) holds for every sign choice, including the outside signs.

Condition on all pairs and the outside signs, leaving the signs indexed by \(T\) independent and fair.
The degree-six coefficients on \(T\) equal their original coefficients times the six corresponding pair differences and do not depend on the frozen outside values.
All lower-degree terms remain unrestricted.
The accepted degree-six fair-sign theorem used in C194v19 bounds the probability of an interval shorter than a surviving coefficient by
\(C\sqrt{\log r_{N,K}}\,r_{N,K}^{-1/25}\).
Choose a fixed small \(c>0\) so that \(|\det F|\le cN^{-6}\) on the paired support implies \(|\det S|\le c a_ZN^{-6}\) in such an interval.
This gives, on the intrinsic good-minor event and \(\mathcal E^{(2)}>K\), a conditional bound
\[
 e_N+Ce^{-cr_{N,K}}+
                  C\sqrt{\log r_{N,K}}\,r_{N,K}^{-1/25}.
\]
The last expression has upper limit at most \(\omega(K)\to0\) as \(K\to\infty\), uniformly in the conditioning.
Adding the vanishing unconditional reservoir failure proves (Free-gap-high-residual-graph).
On the parent norm intersection, the exact graph identity gives \(\|F\|\le B_0\sqrt N\) and \(|\det F|\le B_0^2N d_i\), proving the distance assertion.
No conditional fresh-row law is claimed after conditioning on a full-parent good event.

<a id="two-mode-parent-reduction"></a>
### The exact remaining bounded-two-mode problem

For each \(N\), one may choose a deterministic real \(0<\theta_N<N^{-9}\) avoiding the negatives of all atoms of eigenvalue distributions of the finitely many required minors.
Each such distribution has at most countably many atoms, so a value outside their union exists in this interval.
This common choice makes every required shifted minor invertible almost surely and tends to zero fast enough for the transfer below.
It does not make the unshifted minors invertible or alter the original border laws.

For the whole parent, fix the constant \(B_0\) from the adopted original Frobenius guard and let
\[
 Q_N(K)=\frac1N\sum_i\mathbb P\{\mathcal E_{i,N}^{(2)}\le K,
 d_i\le cB_0^{-2}N^{-7},\ \|L_N^\theta\|\le B_0\sqrt N\}.
\]
C194v25's natural-scale distance average has fixed constants \(p,c_->0\) and threshold \(\sqrt N u/c_-\).
Choose \(c_*>0\) with \(c_*\le c c_-B_0^{-2}\), independently of \(K\), and set \(u=c_*N^{-15/2}\).
Partition each actual distance event by \(\mathcal E^{(2)}>K\) and its complement.
The preceding high-residual bound and the vanishing common norm and spread exceptions give
\[
 \limsup_N\mathbb P\{s_{\min}(L_N^\theta)\le c_*N^{-15/2}\}
 \le\frac1p\left(\limsup_N Q_N(K)+\omega(K)\right).
 \tag{Bounded-two-mode-parent-reduction}
\]
Thus the still unproved assertion \(Q_N(K)\to0\) for each fixed \(K\) would supply a polynomial parent floor without any one-mode residual-tightness hypothesis.
The same implication holds along an infinite deterministic set of dimensions, taking all limits along that set.
The shift transfers that floor to the original parent with constant \(c_*/2\).
The middle-range, logarithmic and arbitrary-coupling deductions are exactly those already checked in C194v25: the polynomial exponent changes the bottom contribution only by a fixed factor, still \(O(N^{-1/4}\log N)\).
Consequently the missing bounded-two-mode estimate would give the free-gap logarithmic limit in probability along those dimensions and the all-dimension almost-sure limsup for this coefficient class.
It supplies no full-sequence almost-sure logarithmic or general Brown conclusion.

The remaining branch is a complete Schur problem with at most two exceptional inverse modes and bounded residual energy.
C194v25 covers its bounded one-mode-residual subbranch, but no full two-mode theorem is inferred here.
An estimate for the discarded-coordinate conditional numerator alone would also be insufficient without the full graph denominator retained above.
