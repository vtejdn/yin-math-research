# High effective rank gives an original-law two-sided column-distance bound

This theorem combines the actual outer coefficient calculation with physical spread of the unmodified inverse.
It gives the averaged distance estimate on the high-effective-rank branch of the real-input model.
The complementary inverse profiles remain explicit; the preceding three-dominant-mode theorem retains its separate scope.
The complete high-rank branch theorem is adopted after [independent whole-proof review](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_F0200_v32.md#high-rank-original-distance-whole-proof).

<a id="statement"></a>
## Exact conditional theorem and averaged consequence

Use two independent real symmetric Wigner inputs with fixed centered variance-one off-diagonal laws of finite fourth moment and fixed centered diagonal laws of finite second moment.
All upper off-diagonal coordinates, diagonals and input colors are independent within each size, lower coordinates are symmetric copies, and coupling across sizes is arbitrary.
Retain the original normalization \(N^{-1/2}\) after every physical deletion.
Fix \(z\in\mathbb C\setminus\mathbb R\), \(K\ge1\), and a fixed prior deletion bound.
At a physical minor of dimension \(n=N-O(1)\), write
\[
 M=AB+2BA-zI,\quad G=M^{-1},\quad F=\|G\|_{\mathrm{HS}},\quad q=\|G\|,
 \qquad \mathcal T=\{G,BG,GB,BGB\}.
\]
The following conditions always refer to an invertible actual minor with \(\|A\|,\|B\|\le K\).
Set
\[
 D_G=\sum_{T\in\mathcal T}\sum_{k=1}^n|T_{kk}|^2,
 \qquad m_G=\max_k\sum_{T\in\mathcal T}
       (\|e_k^*T\|^2+\|Te_k\|^2),\qquad
 \vartheta_G=(D_G+m_G)/F^2.
 \tag{Physical-root-data}
\]
There are fixed \(C_0,R,c,C>0\), depending only on \(K,z\) and the laws, with the following property.
For any deterministic \(\alpha_N\downarrow0\) with \(\alpha_N\ge N^{-1}\), let \(\mathcal G_i\) be the actual-minor event
\[
 F\ge C_0\sqrt N,\qquad F^2\ge Rq^2,\qquad
 \vartheta_G\le\alpha_N,
 \tag{High-rank-good-minor}
\]
together with invertibility and the minor norm guard.
Condition on that original minor and the exposed original diagonal coordinates, with sigma-field \(\mathcal F_i\).
Let \(d_i\) denote the projection-defined physical three-column distance of the original companion \(\mathcal L_N(z)\) in C196.
For every \(B_*\ge1\), on \(\mathcal G_i\),
\[
 \mathbb P\{\|\mathcal L_N(z)\|\le B_*,\ d_i\le cB_*^{-2}N^{-6}
                       \mid\mathcal F_i\}
 \le e_N+Ce^{-cr_N}+C\sqrt{\log r_N}\,r_N^{-1/25}
       =:b_N\longrightarrow0,
 \tag{High-rank-original-distance}
\]
for all sufficiently large \(N\), where \(r_N=\lfloor c_1/\alpha_N\rfloor\to\infty\) for a sufficiently small fixed \(c_1>0\).
Here
\[
 e_N=\sum_{j=1}^2\mathbb P\left\{N^{-1}\sum_{k=1}^n
          [ (\xi'_{j,k})^2+(\xi''_{j,k})^2]>4\right\}\longrightarrow0
\]
uses independent raw copies of the two original laws.
The bound is uniform in the good minor, its inverse magnitudes and its exposed diagonal values.
The parent norm event is only an intersection inside the estimated probability.

There is an unconditional averaged consequence on the free spectral branch.
Put \(P=s_1s_2+2s_2s_1\), and now fix
\(z\in Z_0\cap\sigma(P)\setminus\mathbb R\), with the full-area finite-invertibility set \(Z_0\) from C197v1.
Let \(\Omega_{N,K}=\{\|A_N\|,\|B_N\|\le K\}\), and let \(\mathcal H_i\) require only actual root-minor invertibility and \(F_i^2\ge Rq_i^2\).
Then
\[
 \frac1N\sum_i\mathbb P\{\Omega_{N,K},\mathcal H_i,
                    d_i\le c_{K,z}N^{-6}\}\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{High-rank-averaged-distance}
\]
Consequently
\[
 \mathbb P\{s_{\min}(A_NB_N+2B_NA_N-zI)\le N^{-7}\}
 \le \mathbb P(\Omega_{N,K}^c)+o(1)
       +\frac C N\sum_i\mathbb P(\Omega_{N,K}\cap\mathcal H_i^c).
 \tag{High-rank-parent-reduction}
\]
The \(o(1)\) is independent of the physical index.
This is not a claim that high effective rank exhausts the inverse profiles.
The statement supplies no unrestricted floor, complex-input matching theorem or almost-sure Brown conclusion.

## Complete proof

<a id="physical-collisions"></a>
### Raw outer energy survives distinct physical positions

Use the exact C187v30 outer identity for the original Schur numerator:
\[
 \Phi_{4,2}(x,y)=4h_B(x,y),\qquad
 h_B=\det([Bx,x,y]^TG[Bx,x,y]).
\]
Under (High-rank-good-minor), its arithmetic-symmetric sixth tensor has norm at least \(cF^3\).
Increasing \(C_0\) accommodates the original \(N\) rather than minor \(n\) normalization.
The auxiliary correction has bidegree \((3,3)\), so it contributes no coefficient to this \((4,2)\) family.

Write \(V_1(x,y)=Bx\), \(V_2(x,y)=x\), \(V_3(x,y)=y\), and
\[
 K_{ab}=\tfrac12(V_a^TGV_b+V_b^TG^TV_a).
\]
These symmetric physical matrices represent every quadratic entry of the determinant.
Each is a fixed block embedding or fixed sum of block embeddings of members of \(\mathcal T\) and their transposes.
Let \(\pi_k\) retain the two row variables \((x_k,y_k)\).
Directly from those embeddings and the fixed norm guard,
\[
 \|K_{ab}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}\le C_KF,\quad
 \sum_k\|\pi_kK_{ab}\pi_k\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2\le C D_G,
\]
\[
 \|\pi_kK_{ab}\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2+
 \|K_{ab}\pi_k\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2
 \le C\sum_{T\in\mathcal T}(\|e_k^*T\|^2+\|Te_k\|^2).
 \tag{Quadratic-physical-controls}
\]
The transpose is essential here because \(G\) is not assumed symmetric.

Expand the determinant into its six products of three quadratic tensors and then symmetrize their six physical slots.
For one product \(K_1\otimes K_2\otimes K_3\), if two designated slots in the same quadratic factor share a physical position, the squared tensor mass is at most
\[
 C D_GF^4.
\]
Indeed the corresponding factor is restricted to its physical diagonal blocks, while the other two contribute their squared Hilbert--Schmidt norms.
If the two slots lie in different factors, their squared collision mass is at most
\[
 \sum_k\|\pi_kK_1\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2
          \|\pi_kK_2\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2\|K_3\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2
 \le C m_GF^4,
\]
with column versions of the same bound when those slots have the other orientation.
There are only fifteen slot pairs and a fixed number of permutations and determinant terms.
The triangle inequality and the fixed-sum Cauchy--Schwarz inequality therefore bound the norm of the tensor supported on repeated physical positions by
\[
 C F^2\sqrt{D_G+m_G}\le C\sqrt{\alpha_N}F^3.
 \tag{Physical-collision-cost}
\]
This argument estimates actual tensor coefficients; it does not remove coordinates from \(G\) or change its commutator identities.
For sufficiently large \(N\), subtraction from the raw lower bound leaves at least \(cF^3\) in the tensor supported on six distinct physical positions.

There is a separate incidence estimate.
The squared mass in which one specified physical position occurs in a specified slot of one product is bounded by its row or column load in that factor, times \(CF^4\) from the other factors.
Summing the fixed number of incidences gives
\[
 \text{squared tensor load at position }k\le
 C F^4\sum_{T\in\mathcal T}(\|e_k^*T\|^2+\|Te_k\|^2).
 \tag{Physical-sixth-load}
\]
Restriction to distinct physical positions only decreases these squared loads.

Let \(\sigma^2\) be the coefficient energy in the selected \((4,2)\) family after substituting \((x,y)=\varepsilon/\sqrt N\).
For six distinct individual variables, a polynomial coefficient is \(6!\) times its arithmetic-symmetric tensor entry, and the energy conversion has that same fixed factor after summing ordered entries.
Thus
\[
 \sigma\ge cF^3N^{-3},\qquad
 \max_k\sum_{J\ni k}|c_J|^2\le C m_GF^4N^{-6}
                                      \le C\alpha_N\sigma^2.
 \tag{Physical-high-rank-sixth}
\]
The last constant absorbs the energy lower bound.
Every selected coefficient is a coefficient of the complete degree-six Schur polynomial, not a surrogate.
Select its real or imaginary part, measurably from the minor, with at least half this energy; its loads increase by at most a fixed factor after normalization.

<a id="same-full-graph"></a>
### The complete graph has the same energy scale

For the original companion minor and complete original border,
\[
 Q^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix}I\\B\\A\end{bmatrix}
 G\begin{bmatrix}I&A&2B\end{bmatrix}+\operatorname{diag}(0,-I,-I),
\]
\[
 \Gamma=I_3+RQ^{-1}Q^{-*}R^*,\qquad
 F_i=\Gamma^{-1/2}(D_i-RQ^{-1}C).
\]
The rows of \(\Gamma^{-1/2}[I_3,-RQ^{-1}]\) are an orthonormal basis of the orthogonal complement of the outside columns, since their square retained block \(Q\) is invertible.
Consequently \(d_i=s_{\min}(F_i)\) and \(\|F_i\|\le\|\mathcal L_N(z)\|\).
The minor norm guard gives \(\|Q^{-1}\|\le C_Kq+1\le C_KF+1\).
For the full paired row support \(\|x\|,\|y\|\le2\), the actual border has bounded norm, whence
\[
 \sup_{\|x\|,\|y\|\le2}\sqrt{\det\Gamma}
 \le [1+C_K(F+1)^2]^{3/2}\le C_K'F^3=:a_i.
 \tag{High-rank-full-area}
\]
Here \(F\ge1\) follows by enlarging the fixed threshold.
This bound uses the full inverse and all fresh coordinates.
Equation (Physical-high-rank-sixth) therefore gives \(\sigma\ge c a_iN^{-3}\).

### Original-law matching and the distance event

There are at most \((2N)^6\) selected coefficients.
Discard those below \(\sigma/[\sqrt2(2N)^3]\) in modulus, losing at most half their energy.
A chosen monomial meets total energy at most six times the maximum physical load, so a deterministic greedy rule selects at least \(r_N=\lfloor c_1/\alpha_N\rfloor\) disjoint physical six-sets when \(c_1\) is sufficiently small.
Their real coefficient magnitudes are at least \(c a_iN^{-6}\).
The entire selection precedes exposure of the fresh border.

For every original raw coordinate use the exact two-copy representation
\[
 \xi=m+b\epsilon,\qquad m=(\xi'+\xi'')/2,\quad
 b=(\xi'-\xi'')/2,
\]
with an independent fair sign \(\epsilon\).
All copies and signs are independent across coordinates and colors.
This has exactly the original border law.
Each fixed variance-one law has constants \(b_0,p_0>0\) with \(\mathbb P(|b|\ge b_0)\ge p_0\); take common constants for the two laws.
The disjoint matching edges survive independently with probability at least \(p_0^6\), leaving a fixed positive fraction outside probability \(Ce^{-cr_N}\).
Conditional on the pairs, the signs remain independent and fair.
Total degree six implies that translations and sign-power reductions cannot change a coefficient on six distinct variables except for its exact multiplier \(\prod b\).
In particular excluded sixth terms and all lower terms cannot cancel a selected matching coefficient.

The bound \((|m|+|b|)^2\le(\xi')^2+(\xi'')^2\) puts every sign choice of both entire rows inside \(\|x\|,\|y\|\le2\) outside the pair-measurable exception \(e_N\).
Its probability tends to zero by the fixed-law weak law.
On that support and the parent norm intersection,
\[
 |\det(D_i-RQ^{-1}C)|
 =|\det F_i|\sqrt{\det\Gamma}\le B_*^2d_i a_i.
\]
Choose \(c\) in (High-rank-original-distance) small enough that its chosen real-component interval is shorter than the surviving coefficient threshold.
The exact degree-six fair-sign theorem accepted in C194v19 then gives
\(C\sqrt{\log r_N}\,r_N^{-1/25}\), uniformly in all lower coefficients and the interval center.
Adding the pair-norm and matching-survival exceptions proves (High-rank-original-distance).
The only external small-ball theorem used here is that already identified MNV16 Definition 1.5 and Theorem 1.6 interface; its original proof is not newly audited.

<a id="actual-average-proof"></a>
### Actual spectral minors and the parent remainder

At the stated fixed spectral parameter, C197v2 proves, uniformly over all guarded bounded-deletion minors,
\[
 F^2/N\longrightarrow\infty,\qquad \vartheta_G\longrightarrow0
\]
in its guarded almost-sure sense.
These conclusions apply to the unmodified matrices in (Physical-root-data).
They do not assume the high-rank condition.
There is therefore a deterministic sequence \(\alpha_N\downarrow0\), enlarged to at least \(N^{-1}\), for which the probability that some minor of a guarded parent has \(\vartheta_G>\alpha_N\) tends to zero.
For example choose successive deterministic size thresholds for the events with tolerances \(1/j\), using convergence in probability of the guarded maximum.
The analogous failure of \(F\ge C_0\sqrt N\) also tends to zero.
Finite invertibility holds almost surely at every required minor by the definition of \(Z_0\).

On \(\Omega_{N,K}\), the parent companion norm is bounded by \(B_{K,z}=|z|+2+3K\).
Integrating the conditional theorem and adding those uniform failures proves (High-rank-averaged-distance).
After adding the complement of \(\mathcal H_i\), its full distance average is at most
\[
 o(1)+\frac1N\sum_i\mathbb P(\Omega_{N,K}\cap\mathcal H_i^c).
\]
C196's actual companion average at least-value threshold \(N^{-7}\) requires distance threshold \(C\sqrt N N^{-7}=CN^{-13/2}\), smaller than \(c_{K,z}N^{-6}\) eventually.
A root near-kernel vector \(u\) lifts to \((u,B_Nu,A_Nu)\), whose norm is at least \(\|u\|\) and whose companion residual is \(((P_N-z)u,0,0)\).
Thus the root least-value event implies the companion one at the same threshold.
The average bound and the separate parent norm-guard complement prove (High-rank-parent-reduction).
No probability estimate for the complementary profiles or summability of the displayed errors has been supplied.
