# Exterior compatibility and entropy bounds for roots of a two-sided Wigner product

The [one-color graph equations](Yin_C_F0194_v31.md#one-color-root-reconstruction) fix \(g=Bh\) before exposing \(A\), with \(q=Ah\) in the actual two-sided product.
The following estimate uses the component of the outside equation perpendicular to \(\operatorname{ran}B_{JI}\), so it can be combined with a test on \(A_{II}\).
At a fixed nonreal shift, the phase-spread input gives a summable guarded exclusion whenever a positive fraction of the coordinates can be approximated by a sufficiently small alphabet.
The separate [conditional row and disjoint swap estimates](Yin_C_F0194_v32.md#statement) apply at their own scales, with their stated guard and shift conditions.

<a id="statement"></a>
## Conditional exterior estimate and guarded structured-root exclusion

Let \(A,B\) satisfy the [model of C194v31](Yin_C_F0194_v31.md#statement), write \(M=AB+2BA\), and fix \(K\ge1\), \(b>0\) and \(0<\tau<1/4\).
The strict upper entries of each color are independent and identically distributed with a fixed centered raw law of variance one.
The result allows atoms and needs only the second moments.
Fix a deletion fraction \(s_0>0\), a lower bound \(v>0\), and \(\ell\in\{1,2\}\).
For a unit complex root \(h\) that can depend measurably on \(B\), put \(g=Bh\).
For \(\ell=1\), require \(\|h_T\|^2\ge v\) for every \(|T^c|\le s_0 N\).
For \(\ell=2\), require for all such \(T\) that
\[
 \operatorname{Gram}\big((\operatorname{Re}h)_T,(\operatorname{Im}h)_T\big)\succeq v I_2 .
 \tag{Spread}
\]
The constants can depend on \(K,b,\tau,s_0,v\) and the entry laws.
There are \(\delta,c,C>0\) and a \(B\)-measurable event \(G_N\) of probability at least \(1-Ce^{-cN}\) with the following uniform conditional conclusion for all sufficiently large \(N\).
On \(G_N\cap\{\|B\|\le K\}\), fix any admissible \(h\), any \(B\)-measurable \(I\subset[N]\) of size at most \(\delta N\), and \(J=I^c\).
Let \(\Pi=\Pi(B,I)\) be the real orthogonal projection onto vectors supported on \(J\) whose restriction to \(J\) is perpendicular to \(\operatorname{ran} B_{JI}\), extended complex linearly.
For \(z\in\mathbb C\) and any fixed conditional target \(f\in\mathbb C^N\),
\[
 \mathbb P_A\left\{\|\Pi(M-zI_N)h-f\|\le b/\sqrt N\mid B\right\}
 \ \le\ N^{-(\ell/2-\tau)N}.
 \tag{Exterior}
\]
The same estimate holds upon additionally fixing the diagonal of \(A\).
For \(I=\varnothing\) the estimate applies to the full residual.
For nonempty \(I\) it controls the exterior equations without spending any randomness from \(A_{II}\).

Under finite fourth off-diagonal moments, fix \(z\notin\mathbb R\) and the full guard \(\Omega_K=\{\max(\|A\|,\|B\|)\le K\}\).
Fix \(a_0,b_0>0\).
For a finite \(B\)-measurable family \(\mathcal H_N(B)\) of unit vectors with cardinality at most \(N^{(1-\chi)N}\), \(0<\chi<1\), one has
\[
 \mathbb P\left\{\Omega_K,\ \exists h_*\in\mathbb C^N,\quad
 \|h_*\|=1,\quad \operatorname{dist}(h_*,\mathcal H_N(B))\le a_0/\sqrt N,\quad
 \|(M-zI_N)h_*\|\le b_0/\sqrt N\right\}
 \ \le\ Ce^{-cN}+N^{-cN}.
 \tag{Dictionary}
\]
There is also a deterministic class without a prescribed family.
Fix \(0<\gamma\le1\) and \(0\le\alpha<1\).
Let \(\mathcal V_N\) consist of the unit complex vectors \(h_*\) for which there are \(S\subset[N]\), \(|S|\ge\gamma N\), and \(u\in\mathbb C^S\) with at most \(\lfloor N^\alpha\rfloor\) distinct values and
\[
 \|(h_*)_S-u\|\le a_0/\sqrt N.
\]
Then for all sufficiently large \(N\),
\[
 \mathbb P\{\Omega_K,\ \exists h_*\in\mathcal V_N:
                     \|(M-zI_N)h_*\|\le b_0/\sqrt N\}
   \le Ce^{-cN}+N^{-cN}.
 \tag{Partial-alphabet}
\]
No restriction is made on coordinates outside \(S\).
The constants in these applications may depend on the fixed parameters and laws.
The probability bounds are summable for every cross-size coupling.
Control of the unrestricted norm-guard complement, an exhaustive covering of all remaining feasible roots and the summable planar defect remain separate.

## Complete argument

The external small-ball input is Koehler and Sohn, *Universality and sharp thresholds for ellipsoid fitting*, arXiv:2608.27372v1, Proposition 2.4 on printed and PDF page 14.
Its precise truncated-second-moment, short-column, covariance and radius assumptions are [recorded for this edition](../audits/literature/KS26/Yin_U001.md#ks26-u001).
The projected maps, their covariance spectrum and the entropy counts below are project deductions.

### A single event for every coordinate deletion

For any fixed \(\beta>0\), choose \(w>0\) so small that every real interval of length \(4w\) has semicircle mass below \(\beta/4\).
A fixed finite mesh covers all intervals of length \(2w\) intersecting \([-K,K]\) by intervals of length \(4w\).
For each mesh interval the expected empirical mass of \(B\) converges to its semicircle mass by [fixed-law weak convergence](Yin_C_F0186_v4.md#statement).
Changing the independent upper coordinates attached to one row index changes \(B\) by rank at most two, and the empirical interval mass by at most \(4/N\).
The [bounded-differences argument](Yin_C_F0186_v4.md#row-concentration) and a finite union bound give \(G_N\) on which every mesh interval contains at most \(\beta N\) eigenvalues.
If \(U\subset[N]\) has deletion size \(d=N-|U|\), interlacing at the two endpoints bounds the number of eigenvalues of \(B_0=B_{UU}\) in any such interval by \(\beta N+2d\).
Choose a small \(\alpha>0\), to be specified with \(\beta,\delta\) below, with \(\alpha<s_0\).
Set \(U=\{i: |h_i|+|g_i|\le L/\sqrt N\}\), where \(L\) is fixed sufficiently large to ensure \(d\le\alpha N\) on the guard, since \(\sum_i(|h_i|+|g_i|)^2\le2(1+K^2)\).
For \(\ell=2\), let \(x_1,x_2\) be the real and imaginary parts of \(h_U\), with \(y_1,y_2\) the same parts of \(g_U\).
For \(\ell=1\), choose the real or imaginary part with larger norm on \(U\) and use that same part for \(g_U\).
Then \(\operatorname{Gram}(x_a:1\le a\le\ell)\succeq (v/2)I_\ell\).

### Covariance calculation

For the moment retain all independent strict upper \(A\)-entries indexed by pairs in \(U\).
For \(i<j\) in \(U\), let \(F_{ij}=e_ie_j^T+e_je_i^T\) on \(\mathbb R^{|U|}\).
For the output restricted to \(U\), the column multiplying the raw \(A\)-variable in stacked real channels \(a=1,\ldots,\ell\) has blocks \(N^{-1/2}(F_{ij}y_a+2B_0F_{ij}x_a)\).
This follows by differentiating \(Ag+2BAh\) with \(B,h,g\) fixed.
Write \(L_U\) for the resulting normalized column map.
Formally add diagonal directions \(\sqrt2e_ie_i^T\) to the \(F_{ij}\).
The direct identity for this complete family is
\[
 \sum_F F u t^T F^T=\langle u,t\rangle I+t u^T .
\]
Expanding its four terms in the \(a,b\) covariance block shows
\[
 N L_U L_U^T= H+R-DD^T,
\qquad
 H_{ab}=\langle y_a,y_b\rangle I+
 2(\langle y_a,x_b\rangle+\langle x_a,y_b\rangle)B_0+
 4\langle x_a,x_b\rangle B_0^2.
\]
The four outer-product terms in each block give \(\operatorname{rank}R\le4\ell^2\), without any sign requirement.
The \(a\)-block of \(D\) is \(\sqrt2(\operatorname{diag}y_a+2B_0\operatorname{diag}x_a)\), so \(\|DD^T\|\le 2\ell(1+2K)^2L^2/N\).
The added diagonal directions are a covariance calculation only and their probability law plays no role.

Diagonalizing \(B_0\) decomposes \(H\) into the real \(\ell\)-by-\(\ell\) matrices
\[
 H(\lambda)=\operatorname{Gram}(y_a+2\lambda x_a:1\le a\le\ell),
 \qquad \lambda\in\operatorname{spec}(B_0).
\]
On \([-K,K]\), \(\|H(\lambda)\|\le C_K\) with an absolute choice depending also on \(\ell\le2\).
Its determinant is a real polynomial in \(\lambda\) of degree \(2\ell\) with leading coefficient \(4^\ell\det\operatorname{Gram}(x_a)\ge 4^\ell(v/2)^\ell\).
If \(\lambda_{\min}(H(\lambda))\le\kappa\), the absolute determinant is at most \(C_K^{\ell-1}\kappa\).
Factoring the polynomial shows that \(\lambda\) lies within \((C_K^{\ell-1}\kappa/[4^\ell(v/2)^\ell])^{1/(2\ell)}\) of some complex root, hence within that distance of its real part.
Take \(\kappa>0\) sufficiently small for this distance to be at most \(w\).
At most \(2\ell(\beta N+2d)\) eigenvalues of \(B_0\), with multiplicities, lie in this union of intervals, whose centers may depend on \(B,h\).
By restricting to good eigenvalue channels and intersecting with \(\ker R\), the min-max principle gives at least \(\ell|U|-2\ell^2(\beta N+2d)-4\ell^2\) eigenvalues of \(NL_UL_U^T\) of size at least \(\kappa/2\) for large \(N\).

### Exterior projection and original entries

Lifting these columns to the full physical output retains the indicated singular-value lower bounds since coordinate restriction is a contraction.
Applying \(\Pi\) in each real channel loses at most \(\ell\operatorname{codim}\operatorname{ran}\Pi\le 2\ell|I|\) further dimensions, by intersecting the good left singular subspace with the range of the projection.
Every column supported on a pair in \(I\cap U\) then vanishes, since \(Ag\) from that pair is supported on \(I\) and the exterior effect of \(BAh\) belongs to \(\operatorname{ran}B_{JI}\).
Consequently one can retain only the pairs in \(U\) outside \(I\times I\) with the same covariance.
Choose \(\alpha,\beta,\delta>0\) so that
\(\ell\alpha+2\ell^2(\beta+2\alpha)+2\ell\delta<\tau/2\).
There are at least \(m=\lfloor(\ell-\tau)N\rfloor\) left singular directions of the projected map with singular values at least \(a/\sqrt N\), where \(a=\sqrt{\kappa/2}\).
Since each pair has both endpoints in \(U\), its full column norm before the output projections is at most \(C_KL/N\).
In the \(m\)-dimensional good left singular space, multiply all projected columns by \(\sqrt N/a\).
Their sum of outer products dominates \(I_m\), and the largest norm is at most \(C_KL/(a\sqrt N)\).
The basis and all the projected coefficients can be fixed given \(B,h,I\), and each remaining raw \(A\)-entry is still independent with the original law.
Condition on the independent discarded entries and absorb their contribution in the target.
Apply [Koehler–Sohn, Proposition 2.4](../audits/literature/KS26/Yin_U001.md#ks26-u001), with \(t=C'/\sqrt N\).
Choose \(C'\) large enough for the short-column condition and for \(t\sqrt m\ge b/a\).
The bound after conditioning is \((CC'/\sqrt N)^m\le N^{-(\ell/2-\tau)N}\) for large \(N\), uniformly in the translation.
This proves (Exterior), including its diagonal-conditioned version.

### Conditioning and passage to a dictionary

Given \(B\), the event on the left of (Exterior) for a prescribed \(h,I,f\) is measurable in the original \(A\) outside \(I\times I\).
If an interior event has conditional probability at most \(p_N\) uniformly given that outside information, its intersection with the exterior event costs at most \(p_N N^{-(\ell/2-\tau)N}\).
The [interior reconstruction tests](Yin_C_F0194_v31.md#one-color-root-reconstruction) can be used under their own coefficient conditions and on their stated identification event.
The original \(B\)-exception for that identification must be charged at its original scope.
Conditional on \(G_N\cap\{\|B\|\le K\}\), summing (Exterior) over any eligible family of at most \(N^{(\ell/2-2\tau)N}\) vectors satisfying the corresponding spread condition costs at most \(N^{-\tau N}\).

For (Dictionary), use [deleted phase spread](Yin_C_F0196_v2.md#statement) for all guarded near-modes at the fixed nonreal \(z\).
Except on an event of probability \(Ce^{-cN}\), every guarded unit root \(h_*\) with residual at most \(b_0/\sqrt N\) satisfies (Spread) for constants \(s_0,d^2>0\) once \(N\) is sufficiently large.
For any unit approximant \(h\) at distance at most \(a_0/\sqrt N\), contraction under real phase projection and coordinate deletion shows it satisfies (Spread) with \(v=d^2/4\).
Indeed, each real phase of \(h\) retains norm at least \(d-a_0/\sqrt N\) after deletion.
Filter \(\mathcal H_N(B)\) by this deterministic spread condition.
This operation depends on \(B\) only through the eligible dictionary, so the surviving centers can use the conditional estimate even though the near-mode and its phase-spread event may depend on both inputs.
On the guard, the residual at the chosen center is at most \([b_0+(3K^2+|z|)a_0]/\sqrt N\).
Apply (Exterior) with \(I=\varnothing,\ell=2,f=0\) and \(0<\tau<\min(1/4,\chi/2)\).
The family size, the exceptional probability of \(G_N\) and the separately charged phase-spread exception give (Dictionary).

### Positive-fraction alphabet approximations

For each witness in (Partial-alphabet), reduce \(S\) to \(s=\lceil\gamma N\rceil\) coordinates, and put \(k=\lfloor N^\alpha\rfloor\), \(n=N-s\).
There are at most \(2^N\) choices of \(S\).
Each distinct value actually used by \(u\) has modulus at most \(1+a_0/\sqrt N\le2\) for large \(N\).
Round these values to a square complex mesh of spacing \(1/N\) on a fixed bounded box.
The ordered list of \(k\) centers costs at most \((CN^2)^k\), including padding shorter lists by zero.
The site labels cost at most \(k^s\), and center rounding costs at most \(C\sqrt{s}/N\) in Euclidean norm.
The tail \((h_*)_{S^c}\) is in the complex unit ball and has a Euclidean \(N^{-1/2}\)-net of size at most \((1+2\sqrt N)^{2n}\), including the value one for its cardinality if \(n=0\).
Join these two coordinate vectors and normalize every nonzero candidate to unit length.
Some candidate before normalization lies within \((a_0+C+1)/\sqrt N\) of the witness \(h_*\).
For large \(N\) that candidate is nonzero, and normalization changes the error by at most a factor two.
The resulting deterministic dictionary has cardinality at most
\[
 2^N(CN^2)^k k^s(1+2\sqrt N)^{2n}
     \le N^{[1-\gamma(1-\alpha)+o(1)]N}.
\]
Here \(k=o(N)\) since \(\alpha<1\), and the \(o(1)\) is deterministic at the fixed parameters.
Take any \(0<\chi<\gamma(1-\alpha)\) in (Dictionary) and absorb the fixed enlarged radius into its approximation constant.
This proves (Partial-alphabet).
