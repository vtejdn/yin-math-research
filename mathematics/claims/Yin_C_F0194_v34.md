# Joint concentration at the two reconstructed interior targets

This argument combines the [one-color exterior reconstruction](Yin_C_F0194_v31.md#one-color-root-reconstruction) with the real projection alphabet bound for the original inputs.
It estimates the probability of hitting both actual complex targets in many rows using the same real random entry in those two equations.

<a id="statement"></a>
## Original-law probabilities

Use the real symmetric Wigner model of [C196v3](Yin_C_F0196_v3.md#statement).
The two colors are independent within each size and have fixed centered variance-one raw strict upper laws with finite fourth moment, and fixed centered real diagonal laws of finite second moment.
Put \(M=AB+2BA\), fix \(z\notin\mathbb R\), and fix \(b>0\).
For an array of real vectors \(v_j\), write \(\rho_\xi(v_S,t)\) for the supremum over centers of the probability that the Euclidean norm of the centered-at-that-target sum \(\sum_{j\in S}\xi_j v_j\) is at most \(t\), using independent fresh copies of the fixed law \(\xi\).
Complex coefficient arrays are identified with their corresponding real vectors.

1. Fix \(a>0,\ 0<\theta\le1,\ 0<\eta<1\).
   Except on one original-law event of probability at most \(C N^{-3}+C\exp(-cN^{1/8})\), every unit \(h\) with \(\|(M-zI)h\|\le b/\sqrt N\) satisfies the following conclusions for every \(|S|\ge\theta N\).
   Put \(g=Bh,\ q=Ah\).
   The four-real-coordinate concentration of \((h_j,g_j)\) at radius \(a/N\) is at most \(N^{-4+\eta}\) for either fixed raw strict upper law.
   For the two-color triad the concentration at the same radius is at most \(N^{-6+\eta}\), where its fresh sum in \(\mathbb C^3\) is
   \[
     \left(\sum_{j\in S}\xi_{A,j}h_j,\ \sum_{j\in S}\xi_{B,j}h_j,\
              \sum_{j\in S}(\xi_{A,j}g_j+2\xi_{B,j}q_j)\right).
   \]
   These functions can be evaluated on the coefficients of a near-mode selected from both matrices.

2. There is \(\delta_0>0\) depending on the laws such that the following holds.
   Fix \(0<\delta\le\delta_0,\ 0<\nu<1\), take any deterministic \(I\) of size \(k=\lfloor\delta N\rfloor\), and put \(J=I^c\).
   Expose \(B\), all entries of \(A\) outside \(I\times I\), and the diagonal of \(A_{II}\), calling this information \(\mathcal F\).
   There is a \(B\)-measurable event \(G_B\) of probability at least \(1-C\exp(-cN^{1/8})\).
   There are constants \(a>0,\theta>0,\eta>0\), depending only on the laws and the fixed parameters, with the following property.
   On \(G_B\), for every \(\mathcal F\)-measurable unit candidate \(h\) whose pair \((h_j,(Bh)_j)\) has concentration at most \(N^{-4+\eta}\) under the raw \(A\) law at radius \(a/N\) on every suffix of \(I\) having at least \(\theta N\) elements in increasing index order,
   \[
      \mathbb P\{\|(M-zI)h\|\le b/N\mid\mathcal F\}\le N^{-(4-\nu)k}.
      \tag{Two-reconstructed-targets}
   \]
   For each \(\mathcal F\)-measurable enumerated family \(\mathcal H\) of unit candidates of cardinality at most \(N^{(4-2\nu)k}\), regardless of its concentrations, for all sufficiently large \(N\),
   \[
     \mathbb P\{\exists h\in\mathcal H:\ \|(M-zI)h\|\le b/N\}
       \le C N^{-3}+C\exp(-cN^{1/8})+N^{-\nu k}.
     \tag{Interior-family}
   \]
   No fixed operator-norm upper bound on the original inputs occurs in these estimates.
   All summable marginal exceptions may be discarded eventually under any cross-size coupling.

The last estimate concerns actual hits by the enumerated candidates at the indicated residual threshold.
Applying it to an approximant to an arbitrary near-mode requires a cover with controlled residual error at \(b/N\).
The unrestricted planar defect on the complement appearing in [C186v29](Yin_C_F0186_v29.md#statement) remains open.

## The four-dimensional Fourier estimate

The [planar scalar proof](../drafts/Yin_D_F0042.md#fourier-profile) motivates the following four-dimensional calculation.
In [C186v30](Yin_C_F0186_v30.md#statement), use the actual image \(g=Bh\).
For unit real \(e\in\mathbb R^4\), write \(s_j=e\cdot p_j\), where
\[
 p_j=(\operatorname{Re}h_j,\operatorname{Im}h_j,\operatorname{Re}g_j,\operatorname{Im}g_j).
\]
For each fixed \(\gamma>0,\ \alpha<1/2\), the input excludes, simultaneously for all \(e\) and actual near-modes, \(\gamma N\) of these real projection values within total Euclidean error \(2/\sqrt N\) of an alphabet of size at most \(\lfloor N^\alpha\rfloor\), with the stated summable original-law exception.
In particular any union of at most \(\lfloor N^\alpha\rfloor\) fixed-mesh intervals of length \(1/N\) contains fewer than \(\gamma N\) such projected coordinates.
Each interval has a real midpoint that can serve as an alphabet value, and approximating all coordinates so selected costs at most \(1/\sqrt N\).

For \(B\) use [C198v1](Yin_C_F0198_v1.md#sparse-truncation) at raw cutoff \(N^{7/16}\), with counting slack \(1/8\).
Write \(E\) for the endpoints of the marked \(B\) coordinates and \(B_0\) for the zero-truncated \(B\).
Outside an event of probability \(C\exp(-cN^{1/8})\), we have \(|E|\le2 N^{3/8}\), \(\|B_0\|\le K\) for a fixed \(K\).
Off \(E\) we have \(g_j=(B_0h)_j\), so \(\sum_{j\notin E}\|p_j\|^2\le1+K^2\).
For fixed \(|S|\ge\theta N\), discard \(E\) and all \(\|p_j\|>L/\sqrt N\), where \(L\) is a sufficiently large fixed number.
The remaining subset \(S_0\) has size at least \(3\theta N/4\) for large \(N\), simultaneously for all such \(S\).
One can bound the concentration after discarding the other summands, since for fixed coefficients a sum of independent variables has concentration no greater than that of any subsum.

Choose \(0<\varepsilon<\min(1/8,\eta/48)\), \(\Delta=N^{-\varepsilon}\), \(\gamma=\theta/4\) and \(\alpha=1/2-\varepsilon/2\).
For either raw law \(\xi\), there are \(0<l<H<\infty\) and \(p>0\) such that an independent copy \(\xi'\) satisfies \(\mathbb P(l\le|\xi-\xi'|\le H)\ge p\).
Let \(v=|\xi-\xi'|\).
For \(t\in\mathbb R^4\) and \(\phi(t)=\mathbb E\exp(2\pi i\,t\cdot\sum_{j\in S_0}p_j\xi_j)\), independence, \(1-\cos(2\pi u)\ge c\,\mathrm{dist}(u,\mathbb Z)^2\), and \(|\mathbb E e^{2\pi i s\xi}|^2=\mathbb E\cos(2\pi s(\xi-\xi'))\) imply
\[
 |\phi(t)|\le \exp\left(-c\,\mathbb E_v\sum_{j\in S_0}
                  \mathrm{dist}(v\,t\cdot p_j,\mathbb Z)^2\right).
 \tag{Symmetrized-characteristic-function}
\]
Linearity of expectation in the exponent permits the same symmetrizer \(v\) there for all \(j\).

For every real unit \(e\), the interval \([-N^{-1/2}\Delta,N^{-1/2}\Delta]\) meets at most \(O(1+\sqrt N\Delta)\) intervals of the standard real mesh of side \(1/N\).
Hence fewer than \(\gamma N\) of the projections \(s_j\) lie there and \(\sum_{j\in S_0}s_j^2\ge (\theta/2)\Delta^2\).
For \(\|t\|\le c_0\sqrt N\), choose \(c_0 H L<1/4\).
If \(v\in[l,H]\), every distance in (Symmetrized-characteristic-function) then equals \(|v\,t\cdot p_j|\).
Taking \(e=t/\|t\|\) away from zero yields \(|\phi(t)|\le \exp(-c\Delta^2\|t\|^2)\).

For \(c_0\sqrt N\le T=\|t\|\le N^{1-\varepsilon}\), \(e=t/T\) and \(v\in[l,H]\), the resonant part of \([-L/\sqrt N,L/\sqrt N]\) consists of projected values \(s\) such that \(\mathrm{dist}(vTs,\mathbb Z)\le\Delta\).
It meets at most
\[
 C(1+T/\sqrt N)(1+N\Delta/T)\le C' N^{1/2-\varepsilon}
\]
intervals of the mesh.
Indeed there are \(O(1+T/\sqrt N)\) relevant integers and each contributes an interval of length at most \(2\Delta/(lT)\).
The bound is below \(\lfloor N^\alpha\rfloor\) for large \(N\), uniformly in \(v,T,e\).
Thus at least \(\theta N/2\) coefficients in \(S_0\) have distance greater than \(\Delta\) in (Symmetrized-characteristic-function) for every such \(v\).
This gives \(|\phi(t)|\le\exp(-cN\Delta^2)\) on the intermediate frequency range.

For clarity, in dimension \(D=4\) take the product over coordinates of \((\sin(\pi u)/(\pi u))^2\), with value one at zero in each factor.
This nonnegative kernel is bounded below by an absolute positive constant near zero and is the inverse Fourier transform of \(\prod_{j=1}^D(1-|t_j|)_+\), as follows by integrating each triangular factor.
With \(T_0=N^{1-\varepsilon}/(2\sqrt D)\), its dilation \(x\mapsto K(T_0x)\) is bounded below on every centered radius-\(a/N\) ball after translation for large \(N\).
The Fourier integral and its bounds, uniform in the translation, give
\[
 \rho_\xi(p_S,a/N)
 \le C T_0^{-D}\int_{[-T_0,T_0]^D}|\phi(t)|\,dt
 \le C N^{-D+2D\varepsilon}+C \exp(-cN^{1-2\varepsilon}).
\]
Here the low-frequency Gaussian integral costs at most \(C\Delta^{-D}\).
The same proof with \(D=2\) and \(p_j=(\operatorname{Re}h_j,\operatorname{Im}h_j)\) gives the scalar bound \(C N^{-2+4\varepsilon}+C\exp(-cN^{1-2\varepsilon})\), since each direction in this plane extends to \((e,0,0)\) in the four-dimensional hypothesis.
To bound the triad sum, first fix the fresh \(B\) row.
Its scalar image \(\sum h_j\xi_{B,j}\) must hit the prescribed second complex target, while in the same necessary event the fresh \(A\) row must hit a prescribed pair of complex targets for its coefficients \(h_j,g_j\).
The shift from \(\sum 2q_j\xi_{B,j}\) is fixed in this conditioning.
The two bounds multiply by conditional expectation, and \(12\varepsilon<\eta\) absorbs their constants for large \(N\).
This proves item 1, with one exceptional event used for all reservoirs and both fixed laws.

## Reconstruction on clean rows

The \(B\)-exception in the proof above also permits a lower bound for \(s_{\min}(B_{JI})\) under the original law for fixed sufficiently small \(\delta\).
Fix the \(B\) mark indicators and values with at most \(2N^{3/8}\) marked endpoints.
Ignore all rows of \(J\) incident to a mark.
The remaining matrix \(C=B_{J\setminus E,I}\) has independent entries with the unmarked off-diagonal raw law conditional on \(|\xi_B|\le N^{7/16}\), and \(C=(B_0)_{J\setminus E,I}\).
All its entries are at off-diagonal unordered pairs because \(I,J\) are disjoint.
The conditional raw variance tends to one and the fourth moments are bounded uniformly.
For a fixed real unit vector on \(I\), subtract the means and standardize the entries, absorbing the row means into deterministic centers.
The [affine rectangular estimate](Yin_C_F0196_v1.md#affine-small-ball) applied to at least \(N/5\) independent rows gives \(\|Cu\|\ge d_1\) except with probability \(e^{-c_1N}\), for some fixed positive \(d_1,c_1\).
On the truncated norm guard, \(\|C\|\le K\), so a real sphere net of fixed mesh \(d_1/(2K+2)\) extends this to \(s_{\min}(C)\ge d_1/2\) with failure at most \(\exp(O(k)-c_1N)\) for those mark data, counting failure only on the guard.
Choose \(\delta_0\) small enough to absorb this net.
The same singular bound holds on complex vectors and after restoring the omitted rows.
Integrating the uniform exceptions over the mark data and paying the truncated guard and mark count gives \(G_B\), on which
\[
 |E|\le2N^{3/8},\qquad \|B_0\|\le K,\qquad s_{\min}(2B_{JI})\ge d_1 .
\]

Given \(\mathcal F\) and a candidate \(h\), \(g=Bh\) and \(q_J=A_{JJ}h_J+A_{JI}h_I\) are fixed.
Define
\[
 \widetilde b=zh_J-A_{JJ}g_J-A_{JI}g_I-2B_{JJ}q_J,\qquad
 q_I^*=(2B_{JI})^\dagger\widetilde b,\qquad q^*=(q_I^*,q_J).
\]
Write \(q=Ah,\ r=Mh-zh\).
Then \(2 B_{JI}q_I=\widetilde b+r_J\), so \(\|q-q^*\|\le d_1^{-1}\|r\|\) by full column rank on \(G_B\).
The first actual error \(Ah-q^*\) is \(q-q^*\), and the second \(Ag-zh+2Bq^*\) is \(r+2B(q^*-q)\).
On \(I\setminus E\), the rows of \(B\) and \(B_0\) agree.
The sum of squares of the two complex errors on those clean rows is consequently at most \(C_0^2\|r\|^2\), for a fixed \(C_0\).
This avoids an operator-norm restriction on the marked part of \(B\).

On \(\|r\|\le b/N\), the sum of squared raw two-target errors after multiplying these clean row equations by \(\sqrt N\) is at most \(C_0^2 b^2/N\).
Given \(0<\nu<1\), choose a fixed sufficiently small \(0<\lambda<\nu/32\), set \(\theta=\lambda\delta/2\), and choose \(a\) so large that \(C_0^2 b^2/a^2\le\lambda\delta/4\).
At most \(\lambda k\) clean rows can have raw Euclidean error above \(a/N\) for all sufficiently large \(N\).
The last \(\lceil\lambda k\rceil\) rows of \(I\) may be omitted so that each earlier tested row has at least \(\theta N\) fresh suffix coordinates.
The \(O(N^{3/8})\) dirty rows and all these omissions together leave at least \((1-3\lambda)k\) successful rows for large \(N\).

Now expose the still independent upper off-diagonal coordinates of \(A_{II}\) in increasing row order within \(I\).
The lower-triangular entries in row \(i\) were exposed earlier and its diagonal is already fixed.
Upon subtracting those contributions and the \(\mathcal F\)-measurable outside entries, the two reconstructed target centers in row \(i\) are predictable in this ordering.
The fresh term for both equations simultaneously is the single row sum
\[
       \sum_{j\in I,\ j>i}(h_j,g_j)\xi_{A,ij}.
\]
The suffix filter is \(\mathcal F\)-measurable and uses only the fresh-law concentration function of these fixed coefficients.
For every prescribed subset of successful rows among the eligible early clean rows, iterated conditional expectation therefore gives an upper bound \(N^{(-4+\eta)m}\), where \(m\) is the subset size.
Taking the union of at most \(2^k\) choices costs only \(\exp(O(k))\).
Choose \(0<\eta<\nu/4\).
Since \((4-\eta)(1-3\lambda)>4-\nu\), this proves (Two-reconstructed-targets) on \(G_B\).

Finally, for an enumerated family, filter out all vectors that fail one of the suffix tests before applying the conditional product.
Any unit vector with the claimed residual meets that filter outside the common exception from item 1, since \(b/N\le b/\sqrt N\).
No probability conditioned on that common exception is used in the row exposures.
Integrate the conditional union bound over the filtered family, then add the two original-law exceptions once.
This proves (Interior-family).
The ordered count uses the actual targets in the two necessities of the original residual equation even when their values depend on previously exposed rows.
The estimate alone does not supply a strict entropy surplus for an arbitrary fine cover of a complement kernel of complex dimension \(2k\).
