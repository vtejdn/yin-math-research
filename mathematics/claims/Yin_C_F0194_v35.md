# Planar loss for large products of interior row concentrations

<a id="statement"></a>
## Original-law conclusion

Use the real symmetric Wigner model of [C186v29](Yin_C_F0186_v29.md#statement) and its extreme logarithmic loss \(D_N\) with height \(L_N=\exp\sqrt{\log(N+1)}\).
Write \(D(z)=AB+2BA-zI\).
Fix \(R>0\), \(C_1>0\) and a sufficiently small constant \(\lambda>0\) allowed by [the exterior determinant estimate](Yin_C_F0196_v4.md#statement).
Let \(I\) be any deterministic ordered set of \(k=\lfloor\lambda N\rfloor\) coordinates, possibly varying with dimension.
For a complex unit \(h\) write \(a_j=(h_j,(Bh)_j)\in\mathbb C^2=\mathbb R^4\) in this order on \(I\).
With independent fresh copies of the fixed original raw off-diagonal \(A\) law, define
\[
 \rho_i(h,t)=\sup_{v\in\mathbb R^4}
   \Pr_{\rm fresh}\{\|\textstyle\sum_{j=i}^k a_j\xi_j-v\|\le t\},
 \qquad \pi(h,t)=\prod_{i=1}^{p}\rho_i(h,t),\qquad
 p=k-\lfloor k/10\rfloor.
\]
The last tenth supplies a macroscopic reservoir, with no permutation required.
For all sufficiently large fixed integers \(s\) depending on \(C_1\), let \(H_N(z)\) be existence of a unit \(h\) such that
\[
 \|D(z)h\|\le N^{-(s+10)},\qquad \pi(h,N^{-s})\ge N^{-C_1 k}.
 \tag{High-interior-product}
\]
For every \(v>0\) there are \(c,C>0\) such that eventually
\[
 \Pr\!\left\{\int_{|z|\le R}D_N(z)\mathbf1_{H_N(z)}\,dA(z)>v\right\}
 \le C N^{-3}+C e^{-cN^{1/8}} .
 \tag{High-product-planar}
\]
The same bound holds with the further restriction to \(F_N(z)^c\).
This covers all witnesses of (High-interior-product) under the original laws, with no prescribed family of full roots and no original input norm premise.
There is also an all-profile statement for a specified finite family \(\mathcal H_N(B)\) of unit roots measurable in \(B\).
Fix \(C_*>0,b_*>0\) and a uniform size bound \(\#\mathcal H_N(B)\le N^{C_*N}\).
One can first choose \(C_1\), then fix \(s\) as above, and finally choose \(a_*>s+10\) sufficiently large, so that (High-product-planar) also bounds the disk loss on parameters at which some unit \(h\) satisfies
\[
 \|D(z)h\|\le N^{-a_*},\qquad
 \operatorname{dist}(h,\mathcal H_N(B))\le b_*N^{-a_*}.
 \tag{Fine-family}
\]
This includes the low-product branch for that prescribed family, even though the macroscopic block used to classify the product need not be the block used to test its small concentration.
The cutoff \(s\) is selected after the concentration exponent \(C_1\).
At almost every parameter where \(D_N>0\), the minimum singular value is below \(e^{-L_N}<N^{-(s+10)}\) eventually.
The existence of such a mode supplies the residual inequality only.
The result leaves the parameters with positive loss and no mode satisfying the product inequality unresolved.

## Exterior data and uniform events

Fix a narrow strip \(|\operatorname{Im}z|<\delta\), to be chosen from \(R,v\) at the end.
Use the exterior sigma field consisting of the full \(B\), the \(A\)-entries outside \(I\times I\), and the diagonal of \(A\).
Write \(A^e\) for \(A\) with its entire \(I\times I\) block set to zero and put \(D^e=A^eB+2BA^e-zI\).
Write \(J=I^c\), \(H=\ker X\), \(Xh=(h_I,(Bh)_I)\), \(L=\Pi_H D\), and \(T(z)h=(Lh,Xh)\), as in [C196v4](Yin_C_F0196_v4.md#argument).
The operator \(L\) and the square map \(T\) are exterior-measurable whenever \(B_{JI}\) has full column rank.
Its failure of a fixed lower singular bound and the failure of \(\|A\|,\|B\|\le N^2\) have aggregate probability \(O(N^{-3})+Ce^{-cN^{1/8}}\) by that proof.
Define exterior tests using only the lower cross bound, \(\|B\|\le N^2\) and \(\|A^e\|\le2N^2\).
They pass whenever the preceding original events pass.
We impose the full norm restriction as an indicator when estimating the original small residual, without conditioning a fresh entry on that event.

By (Square-log-budget) in the same proof, with the same order of exceptional probability,
\[
 \int_{|z|\le R} W(z)\,dA(z)\le C_R N,\qquad
 W(z)=\sum_{i=1}^N\log^-s_i(T(z)).
\]
Thus the exterior-measurable set \(W(z)>N\sqrt{\log N}\) has area \(O_R(1/\sqrt{\log N})\).
At zeros of the determinant take \(W=+\infty\).
We shall estimate the probability at each remaining fixed \(z\), retaining the exterior restriction \(W(z)\le N\sqrt{\log N}\).

Put \(\epsilon=1/100\).
Apart from a single original event of probability \(O(N^{-3})+Ce^{-cN^{1/8}}\), each unit mode in (High-interior-product) on the compact off-axis set has more than \(\theta N\) sites in the final \(\lfloor k/10\rfloor\) coordinates with
\[
 |h_j|>1/N,\qquad \theta=\lambda/50 .
\]
Indeed, more than \(\lambda N/25\) coordinates of magnitude at most \(1/N\) would give a one-value approximation by zero with total error at most \(1/\sqrt N\) on those sites.
The [uniform off-axis step](Yin_C_F0186_v29.md#argument) applied with those fixed constants excludes the latter event.
For large \(N\) the last reservoir has size at least \(\lambda N/11\).
Use the weaker closed condition that at least \(\theta N\) coordinates there satisfy \(|h_j|\ge 1/N\).

This implies that every tail \(i\le p+1\) of these pairs has concentration at most \(C_\lambda N^{-1/2}\) at all radii at most \(N^{-2}\).
In fact, on at least half of the specified sites, one common real component of \(h_j\) has modulus at least \(1/(\sqrt2 N)\).
Represent each fresh scalar as a fair choice between two independent samples.
By independence and nondegeneracy of the fixed law, except with probability \(e^{-cN}\) over the sampled pairs, a positive fraction of the sites have a gap greater than a fixed positive constant.
After projection and conditioning on the unordered samples and other signs, the [real signed interval bound](Yin_C_F0194_v32.md#sign-interval) bounds the probability of every such ball by \(C_\lambda/\sqrt N\).
All these are concentration functions for fresh copies with prescribed coefficients.

## Encoding the observation on the same tails used in the row test

The following argument adapts the [one-multiplier joint profile count](Yin_C_F0194_v21.md#high-joint-product-count) to the interior block.
For the counting argument put \(m=k-\lceil k^{1-\epsilon}\rceil\) and \(\widehat\pi(h,t)=\prod_{i=1}^m\rho_i(h,t)\).
For large \(N\), \(p<m\).
The concentration functions are nondecreasing along the ordered tails.
Thus \(\rho_p(h,N^{-s})\ge \pi(h,N^{-s})^{1/p}\), which gives \(\widehat\pi(h,N^{-s})\ge N^{-2C_1 k}\) for each witness.
Choose an integer \(J_0>2C_1/\epsilon+1\) and \(s>20(J_0+1)+3\).
Put \(t_\ell=N^{-s+20\ell}\) for \(0\le\ell\le J_0\), so even the largest radius is at most \(N^{-2}\).
For every witness there is some \(\ell<J_0\) with
\[
 \widehat\pi(h,t_{\ell+1})\le N^{\epsilon k}\widehat\pi(h,t_\ell).
\]
Otherwise the successive ratios would raise the product above one.

Write \(t=t_\ell\).
On the exterior norm tests \(\sum_{j\in I}\|a_j\|^2\le 1+N^4\).
For each tail the real four-dimensional sum has second moment at most \(1+N^4\), so at least half its mass lies in a ball of radius \(C N^2\).
Covering that ball by \(t\)-balls gives a uniform polynomial lower bound \(N^{-D_s}\) on \(\rho_i(h,t)\) for some fixed \(D_s\).
Bin \(\rho_i(h,t)\), \(1\le i\le m\), into finitely many intervals \([r,N^\epsilon r]\) with consecutive endpoints, from \(N^{-D_s}\) up to one, recording the lengths \(m_r\) of their successive blocks.
The profiles are nondecreasing in \(i\) by convolution, so \(N^{O_s(1)}\) choices suffice.
Split a bin also at position \(p\) if it straddles this index, increasing the number of blocks by at most one.

For the deterministic coefficient count, apply Nguyen's real-vector inverse theorem, [arXiv:1105.5733v1, Condition 1 and Theorem 1.3, pp. 2–3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1105.5733v1#page=2), to the entire tail starting at the first index of each occupied bin.
Use exception number \(n'=\lfloor k^{1-2\epsilon}\rfloor\), admissible since every encoded tail has length at least \(k^{1-\epsilon}\).
In the source's polynomial lower bound one can enlarge its exponent by the fixed factor needed to compare \(N\) with this tail length.
For a nonzero tail, normalize by its Euclidean coefficient norm.
An identically zero tail has concentration one at every radius and is encoded by the singleton progression \(\{0\}\).
Visibility rules out a zero tail for \(i\le p\).
An enlarged radius \(2t\) includes the closed \(t\)-ball, so its concentration is at least \(r\) even under an open-ball convention.
The polynomial lower bound and the fixed difference-gap property meet the source hypotheses with fixed constants.
Take the source exception exponent \(1/2\) and a fixed polynomial concentration exponent large enough for all the tail lengths in use.
After scaling back, all but \(n'\) sites of that bin lie within \(2t\) of one proper symmetric progression of bounded rank and size at most
\[
 \max(1,C_s r^{-1}/\sqrt{n'}).
\]
For indices up to \(p\), visibility gives \(r\le C_\lambda N^{-1/2}\) and this maximum is at most \(C_{s,\lambda}r^{-1}N^{-1/2+\epsilon}\).
For all subsequent occupied blocks the maximum is at most \(C_s r^{-1}\).
There are only \(N^{O_s(1)}\) possible progression descriptions per bin.
Indeed each scaled generator coordinate is \(2t\,p_{ij}/p_0\), with polynomially bounded integers \(p_0,p_{ij}\), since rescaling cancels the normalizing norm, and that norm is \(O(N^2)\).
The side lengths of a proper progression fit within its polynomial cardinality.
These are exactly the [source clauses at the inspected edition](../audits/literature/Ngu12a/readme.md).

Round the exceptional sites and the last \(k-m\) unencoded sites to a grid of spacing \(t\) in \(\mathbb R^4\).
There are \(O_s(n'+k-m)=o(k)\) such sites, and their positions and polynomially bounded coordinates cost \(N^{o(k)}\) possibilities.
For fixed bin profile and fixed scale the number of codes of all \(k\) pairs is at most
\[
 N^{-(1/2-\epsilon)p+o(k)}\prod_{\rm blocks} r^{-m_r}.
 \tag{Observation-codes}
\]
Here an \(e^{O_s(k)}\) constant has been absorbed into \(N^{o(k)}\).
The proof never uses independence between \(h\) and \(Bh\) to count coefficients.

## Covering the fiber of one code

A full unit root is an exterior candidate when it has the declared scale and bin profile, visibility, a specified observation code with coordinate errors at most \(C t\), and \(\|Lh\|\le N^{-(s+10)}\).
This definition uses only the exterior data and the fresh test law.
It includes every witness with that code on the visibility event.
The difference \(v\) of two such candidates satisfies \(\|T v\|\le C\sqrt{k}\,t=r_*\), from both the observation differences and the exterior residuals.
For fixed exterior data with \(W(z)\le N\sqrt{\log N}\), \(T\) is nonsingular.
Every such fiber has an \(r_*\)-net in full root norm using actual members of the fiber as centers, of size at most
\[
 C^{2N}\prod_{j=1}^N\max(1,s_j(T)^{-1})^2
 \le \exp(O(N)+2N\sqrt{\log N})=N^{o(k)} .
\]
For the volume comparison, an \(r_*\)-separated subset gives disjoint real \(2N\)-dimensional balls of radius \(r_*/2\) around its points.
Relative to any one candidate, all centers lie in the ellipsoid with complex semiaxes \(r_*/s_j(T)\).
The union of the balls lies in the concentric ellipsoid with semiaxes \(2r_*\max(1,s_j(T)^{-1})\) by the triangle inequality in its rescaled norm.
Volume division gives the indicated estimate.
A maximal separated subset covers the fiber, including nonclosed fibers.
It suffices to choose it separately in each fixed exterior section: the event to be integrated below is measurable in the completion of the within-size law, and the uniform union bound for its section needs no jointly measurable choice of centers.

## Actual interior rows

For a fixed exterior candidate \(h'\), each target below is fixed before any off-diagonal entries of the interior are read.
Put \(U=A_{II}\), \(y=(h')_I\), \(w=(Bh')_I\), \(V=B_{JI}\).
The true residual decomposes as
\[
 (Dh')_J=(D^e h')_J+2VUy,\qquad
 (Dh')_I=(D^e h')_I+Uw+2B_{II}Uy .
\]
In particular define \(b_1=-\tfrac12 V^\dagger(D^e h')_J\), \(b_2=-(D^e h')_I-2B_{II}b_1\).
If a true witness in its fiber lies within \(r_*\) of \(h'\), then on the crude original norm bounds \(\|Dh'\|\le N^{-(s+10)}+(3N^4+R)r_*\).
The lower singular bound for \(V\) gives \(\|Uy-b_1\|+\|Uw-b_2\|\le C(1+N^2)\|Dh'\|\), using the exact two exterior and interior equations.
The raw two-complex-row tolerances, after multiplying by \(\sqrt N\), are therefore at most \(N^{9}t<t_{\ell+1}\) eventually, with room for fixed constants.
This implication uses the original matrices on their norm event.
The probability of the resulting row tests is bounded under the unconditioned interior law.

Expose interior entries in increasing upper row order.
At row \(i\) the sums in both channels use the same fresh raw scalar times the pair \(a'_j=(h'_j,(Bh')_j)\), \(j>i\).
All other entries in that row and both reconstructed targets are already fixed by the specified exposure.
Even for discrete laws the conditional probability of hitting the two targets is at most \(\rho_{i+1}(h',t_{\ell+1})\).
Iterated conditioning over the first \(m\) rows bounds the probability by the product of these factors.
It is at most \(\widehat\pi(h',t_{\ell+1})/\rho_1(h',t_{\ell+1})\), since \(\rho_{m+1}\le1\).
The denominator costs at most \(N^{O_s(1)}\) by the second-moment box bound.
Each chosen center belongs to the same scale and bin profile, so
\[
 \widehat\pi(h',t_{\ell+1})\le
 N^{\epsilon k}\widehat\pi(h',t)
 \le N^{2\epsilon k}\prod_{\rm blocks}r^{m_r}.
\]
After multiplication by (Observation-codes), the probability is bounded by \(N^{-k/3}\) for all sufficiently large \(N\), allowing for the \(N^{o(k)}\) ellipsoid cost, all choices of bin profiles and finitely many scales.
Indeed \((1/2-\epsilon)p/k-2\epsilon>1/3\) eventually.
This is a conditional bound on the existence of visible feasible witnesses on the crude norm guard, uniform at exterior sections satisfying the cross bound, exterior norm tests and the \(W(z)\) restriction.
No singular vector of the realized interior is fixed or supplied as a center.

## From the conditional count to the original integral

All events used in the parameter and exterior integration have universally measurable versions.
For example the probability of a closed ball is Borel in its coefficients and center by integration against the fixed product distribution, and the concentration supremum is Borel: use centers in a countable dense set with radius enlarged by \(1/n\), then decrease \(1/n\) to zero.
For fixed coefficients, tightness confines any sequence of centers achieving a positive limiting mass to a bounded set, proving equality with the closed-ball supremum.
The events asserting existence of a unit witness are projections of Borel sets in finite-dimensional real spaces, hence universally measurable.
Fubini on the completed product and sectionwise conditional bounds apply.
Equivalently, for the closed threshold in (High-interior-product) the functions \(\rho_i\) are upper semicontinuous jointly in the coefficients, by tightness of the fresh multipliers, boundedness of centers of positive mass on compact coefficient sets and the closed-ball inequality under convergence.
The existence event is closed after projecting the compact unit sphere.

By Fubini the expected off-axis area of the visible high-product event satisfying all the indicated restrictions is at most \(\pi R^2 N^{-k/3}\).
By Markov this area exceeds \(N^{-1}\) with probability at most \(\pi R^2 N^{1-k/3}\).
On the simultaneous good events the full parameter area where (High-interior-product) holds is at most the area of the real strip plus \(O_R(1/\sqrt{\log N})+N^{-1}\).
The [arbitrary-set envelope](Yin_C_F0186_v29.md#argument) bounds the integral of \(D_N\) on any set of area \(m\le\pi\) in this disk by
\[
 C_Rm+\tfrac12m(1+\log(\pi/m))
\]
outside an event of probability \(O_R(N^{-3})\).
For the prescribed threshold choose \(\delta>0\) so that the area \(4R\delta\) and the two vanishing terms eventually give a bound below \(v\).
The uniform visibility, cross-block, norm and integrated determinant exceptions together with the conditional area estimate give (High-product-planar).
First Borel--Cantelli applies under every coupling of the fixed marginal laws.

## Small products for a specified dictionary

Fix a partition of \([N]\) into \(M_1\) balanced deterministic sets \(Q_j\), with \(M_1\) a fixed large integer such that for all distinct pairs the union of two sets is small enough for C196v4's cross-block floor.
Put \(C_1>4M_1^2(C_*+1)\).
For a unit witness in (Fine-family) with \(\pi(h,N^{-s})<N^{-C_1 k}\), monotonicity gives \(\rho_1(h,N^{-s})<N^{-C_1}\) on \(I\), since \(\rho_1^p\le\pi\) and \(p\le k\).
Adding the independent coefficients from outside \(I\) only lowers concentration.
Take \(\widehat h\) from the prescribed dictionary with the asserted approximation.
For the original fresh multipliers on all \(N\) sites, the mean square of the error in the sum of pairs equals the squared coefficient distance and is at most \((1+N^4)b_*^2N^{-2a_*}\) on \(\|B\|\le N^2\).
By Chebyshev, if \(a_*>s+10+C_1\), the concentration of the full list \((\widehat h,B\widehat h)\) at radius \(N^{-s}/2\) is at most \(2N^{-C_1}\) for large \(N\).
This statement is about coefficients with independent test draws and is valid for each actual approximant of the witness.

For independent sums on the \(Q_j\), the product of the separate concentrations at radius \(N^{-s}/(2M_1)\) is at most the concentration of the complete sum at radius \(N^{-s}/2\), by adding the centers and using the triangle inequality.
Some \(Q_j\) therefore has concentration at most \((2N^{-C_1})^{1/M_1}\).
For every prescribed approximant this condition can be tested given \(B\), before sampling \(A\).
For each such \(Q_j\) choose another distinct block \(Q_l\), set \(I'=Q_j\cup Q_l\), and disclose the exterior of \(I'\) in \(A\).
Under the original crude norm event the approximant has residual at most \(N^{-a_*}+(3N^4+R)b_*N^{-a_*}\).
The preceding two-target reconstruction for \(I'\) implies corresponding raw row errors bounded by \(C_R(1+b_*)N^{7-a_*}<N^{-s}/(2M_1)\) on the cross-floor event.
Disclose the two within-block squares on \(Q_j,Q_l\).
The rows from \(Q_l\) to \(Q_j\) still use disjoint fresh entries, multiplying the two prescribed coefficients in each row with the same original scalar.
Their two targets are fixed by the exterior and within-block exposures.
The joint probability for each eligible approximant is at most \((2N^{-C_1})^{|Q_l|/M_1}\).
Charge the finitely many cross-floor exceptions once, uniformly over the dictionary.
Union over the at most \(N^{C_*N}\) approximants and the finitely many reservoir choices gives \(N^{-cN}\) for the low-product existence event at each fixed \(|z|\le R\) with the common good event imposed.
The conditional probability bound for the rows, as before, does not condition on the original \(A\) norm event.
Markov's inequality for its off-axis area and the arbitrary-set envelope give the required planar deviation when this branch is added to (High-product-planar).
The parameters for the dictionary assertion are chosen in the displayed order; there is no uniform choice over all \(C_*\) and accuracies \(a_*\).

For an arbitrary root, the low-product case has the opposite inequality at the initial scale \(N^{-s}\).
There the argument above cannot deduce a stable enlargement before the scale reaches \(N^{-2}\).
An unrestricted four-real-dimensional observation net would cost approximately \(N^{4sk}\) at that finest scale, while the defining low-product upper bound only guarantees \(N^{-C_1 k}\).
The required order of \(s,C_1\) prevents this direct union bound.
An additional supply of low-entropy candidates or an estimate adapted to fewer independent interior rows would be needed to exhaust that branch.
