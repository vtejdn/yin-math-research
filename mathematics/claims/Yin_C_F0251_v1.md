# Rectangular liberation cuts and negative diagonal forms in a correlated Haar pair

## Current assessment

[Selected decision](#decision-reviewed)

## Statement

Let \(0<r<1\), let \(U\) be Haar, and let \(V\) be a unitary free from \(U\) with law \(w_r\,dm\), where \(m\) is Haar probability on the unit circle and \(w_r(z)=(1-r^2)/|1-rz|^2\).
The [correlated pair](Yin_C_F0057_v1.md#3-correlated-haar-coordinates-retain-bounded-duals) \(X=(U,U^*V)\) has two Haar marginals and satisfies actual starred ACSY.
Let \(N=W^*(U)\), let \(\gamma\) denote its partial liberation against \(W^*(U^*V)\), with \(\gamma a=a\otimes1-1\otimes a\) on the base, and put \(\eta=\gamma^*(1\otimes1)\).
For spectral projections \(e,f\in N\) write \(t=\tau(e),\,s=\tau(f)\), and
\[
 G_{ef}=(\mathrm{id}\otimes\tau)I_{e\otimes f}\gamma,\qquad
 I_{e\otimes f}(a\otimes b)=ae\otimes fb .
\]
Inputs are in the actual represented starred polynomial core of \(X\).

1. The joint spectral measure of \(\eta\) for left and right multiplication by \(U\) is
   \[
      d\nu_\eta(z,w)=\frac{2r^2}{(1-r^2)^2}|z-w|^2\,dm(z)\,dm(w).
                                                                    \tag{1}
   \]
2. For \(n\ge1\) set \(p_n=V^n=(X_1X_2)^n\), \(P_{r,n}(z)=\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}r^{n-k}z^k\) and \(\lambda_f=\tau(fU)\).
   The two ordered cuts have values
   \[
    G_{ef}p_n=P_{r,n}(V)e(U\overline{\lambda_f}-s),\qquad
    G_{fe}p_n=P_{r,n}(V)f(U\overline{\lambda_e}-t).                  \tag{2}
   \]
   For example the squared norm of the first is \(\|P_{r,n}(V)\|_2^2\int_e|z\overline{\lambda_f}-s|^2\,dm(z)\).
   In particular
   \[
         \|G_{ef}p_n\|_2\le\sqrt s\,\|e\eta f\|_2,\qquad
         \|G_{fe}p_n\|_2\le\sqrt t\,\|f\eta e\|_2 .                    \tag{3}
   \]
   For fixed nonzero \(e,f\), the supremum over this unitary family of either output norm grows proportionally to \((1-r)^{-1}\) as \(r\uparrow1\).
3. For \(k=e,f\), put \(q_k(n)=\operatorname{Re}\langle p_n,G_{kk}p_n\rangle\) and \(S_n=\sum_{\ell=1}^n r^{2\ell}\).
   Then
   \[
        q_k(n)=-S_n\big(\tau(k)^2-|\tau(kU)|^2\big).                  \tag{4}
   \]
   Both forms can be arbitrarily negative on the same norm-one mixed word as the law varies, even for any two fixed nonzero disjoint arcs of the first Haar marginal.
   Nevertheless their combined contribution to the two-projection energy satisfies
   \[
     0\le -s q_e(n)-t q_f(n)
        \le \tfrac12(s+t)(1-r^2)\,\|e\eta f\|_2^2 .                   \tag{5}
   \]

The formulas allow overlapping projections as well.
For disjoint \(e,f\), (5) concerns the diagonal terms in the energy of the symmetric off-diagonal kernel \(e\otimes f+f\otimes e\).
All estimates on inputs in (2)–(5) concern the specified mixed words.

## Argument

### The liberation vacuum in free coordinates

The bounded dual construction for \(X\) and the [normal strong-to-ACSY implication](Yin_C_F0070_v1.md#statement) give actual ACSY on its represented starred polynomial core.
This core equals the algebra generated by the two free Laurent polynomial algebras of \(U,V\), since \(V=X_1X_2\) and \(X_2=U^*V\).
The Poisson moments are \(\tau(V^\ell)=r^{|\ell|}\) for all integers \(\ell\). [Free reference laws](Yin_C_F0057_v1.md#2-a-free-reference-pair-with-bounded-duals)

Let \(\gamma_0\) be liberation of \(N\) against the free \(V\) factor and let \(L\) be the outer derivation with \(L(U)=L(U^*)=0,\ L(V)=1\otimes V,\ L(V^*)=-V^*\otimes1\).
These prescriptions respect the Laurent inverses and define derivations on the reduced free product core.
On the marginal Laurent algebra, double tracing \(L(V^n)\) gives \(n r^{|n|}\) for every integer \(n\).
For \(n>0\) this uses the \(n\) cuts \(V^k\otimes V^{n-k}\), \(0\le k<n\).
For \(n<0\) the \(|n|\) negative cuts each have trace \(-r^{|n|}\).
Writing \(z=e^{i\theta}\), the same functional on \(p\) equals \(-i\int (d p/d\theta) w_r\,dm=i\int p (d w_r/d\theta)\,dm\).
With the Hilbert inner product linear in its second variable, its actual vacuum is the bounded centered function
\[
                         H=-i\,\frac{d w_r/d\theta}{w_r}(V).
\]
The free extension of \(L\) has this same vacuum and \(\gamma_0\) has vacuum zero.
Indeed on a centered reduced word of more than one block each differentiated block leaves another free factor on a trace leg, whose trace vanishes by centering and freeness.
The same reduced word pairs to zero with the centered \(H\) in the \(V\)-factor.
On a single block the assertions follow from the marginal calculations, including the zero double trace of the inner difference on \(N\).

We compute the norm of this vacuum.
The circle map \(\Phi(z)=(z-r)/(1-rz)\) has argument derivative \(w_r(z)>0\) and degree one, so \(\Phi(V)\) is Haar by change of variables.
Since \(\Phi(z)-\overline{\Phi(z)}=(1-r^2)(z-\bar z)/|1-rz|^2\), differentiation of \(w_r\) gives
\[
                   H=\frac{r}{1-r^2}\big(\Phi(V)-\Phi(V)^*\big),
             \qquad \|H\|_2^2=\frac{2r^2}{(1-r^2)^2}.                 \tag{6}
\]

Checking the starred generators gives on the full core
\[
           \gamma=\gamma_0+(I_{U\otimes U^*}-1)L .
\]
In particular \(\gamma V=U\otimes U^*V-1\otimes V\), since \(\gamma(U^*V)=0\).
Both sides then agree on \(V^*\) by inverse differentiation.
For any core \(p\), trace cyclicity and \(L(U)=L(U^*)=0\) identify the double trace of \(I_{U\otimes U^*}L(p)\) with that of \(L(UpU^*)\).
Thus \(\eta=U^*HU-H\).
For \(a,b\in N\), freeness and \(\tau(H)=0\) give \(\|aHb\|_2^2=\|H\|_2^2\|a\|_2^2\|b\|_2^2\).
The polarized identity in both factors identifies the closed span of these words with the coarse \(N\)-bimodule scaled by \(\|H\|_2\).
In these coordinates \(\eta\) has coefficient \(\bar z w-1\), whose modulus is \(|z-w|\).
This proves (1).

### Two ordered outputs on powers of the product

Apply the Leibniz rule to the formula for \(\gamma V\).
In the cut with left prefix \(V^k\), \(0\le k<n\), the two trace factors after insertion are \(\tau(fU^*V^{n-k})=\overline{\lambda_f}r^{n-k}\) and \(\tau(fV^{n-k})=s r^{n-k}\), by freeness.
The terms in the first leg have right factors \(Ue\) and \(e\), respectively.
This proves (2), including the reversed expression by exchange of \(e,f\).
For \(d=e(U\overline{\lambda_f}-s)\) one has \(\|P_{r,n}(V)d\|_2^2=\|P_{r,n}(V)\|_2^2\|d\|_2^2\) by trace cyclicity and freeness.
Writing \(P_{r,n}(V)=V^n\sum_{\ell=1}^n r^\ell V^{-\ell}\) gives
\[
    \|P_{r,n}(V)\|_2^2
       =\sum_{\ell,m=1}^n r^{\ell+m+|\ell-m|}
       =\sum_{j=1}^n(2j-1)r^{2j}
       \uparrow\frac{r^2(1+r^2)}{(1-r^2)^2}\quad(n\to\infty).
                                                                    \tag{7}
\]
Since \(z\overline{\lambda_f}-s=\int_f(z\bar w-1)\,dm(w)\), Cauchy–Schwarz gives \(\|d\|_2^2\le s\int_{e\times f}|z-w|^2\,dm(z)dm(w)\).
Comparison of (6) and (7) proves (3).
For a nonzero spectral projection \(f\) one has \(|\lambda_f|<s\), since equality would concentrate its Haar restriction on one point.
Thus for fixed nonzero \(e,f\) the factor \(\|d\|_2\) is positive and independent of \(r\), and (7) gives the asserted growth.

### The signed diagonal contribution

Taking \(e=f=k\) in (2), traciality and freeness split the diagonal pairing into
\(\tau(V^{-n}P_{r,n}(V))\,\tau(k(U\overline{\lambda_k}-\tau(k)))\).
The two factors are \(S_n\) and \(|\lambda_k|^2-\tau(k)^2\).
This proves (4).
For each nonzero \(k\) the latter factor is strictly negative as just shown.
Taking \(n\) large at each \(r\uparrow1\) makes \(S_n\) arbitrarily large, while both generator marginals remain Haar.

For nonzero \(e,f\) set \(\alpha=\lambda_e/t\) and \(\beta=\lambda_f/s\).
One has \(1-|\alpha|^2\le2(1-\operatorname{Re}(\alpha\bar\beta))\), since the difference is \(1-|\beta|^2+|\alpha-\beta|^2\ge0\), and likewise after exchanging the two means.
The integral \(J=\int_{e\times f}|z-w|^2\,dm(z)dm(w)\) equals \(2ts(1-\operatorname{Re}(\alpha\bar\beta))\).
Hence (4) gives \(-s q_e(n)-t q_f(n)\le (s+t) S_n J\).
Use \(S_n\le r^2/(1-r^2)\) and \(\|e\eta f\|_2^2=2r^2 J/(1-r^2)^2\) from (1) to obtain (5).
If either projection is zero both sides vanish.
For disjoint projections the composition square of \(e\otimes f+f\otimes e\) is \(s e\otimes e+t f\otimes f\), whose signed contribution in the [finite-kernel energy identities](Yin_C_F0077_v1.md#uniform-row-l2-bounds-close-the-kernel-composition-hierarchy) is \(-s q_e-t q_f\).
Thus negative diagonal forms of unbounded magnitude occur in this actual system across its parameter family, with their size accounted for by the cross compression on the stated words.
