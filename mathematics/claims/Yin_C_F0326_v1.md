# Hilbert-space kernel bounds from a dominated liberation gradient

## Current assessment
[Selected decision](#decision-reviewed)

## Statement

Use a bounded normal coordinate \(Z\), its marginal law \(\mu\), and a weakly dense represented starred algebra \(\mathcal A\) generated by the marginal polynomials and a unital omitted star algebra in a finite von Neumann algebra with faithful normal probability trace \(\tau\).
Partial liberation \(\gamma\) takes a marginal polynomial \(a\) to \(a\otimes1-1\otimes a\) and kills the omitted algebra.
Assume it exists on \(\mathcal A\) with an actual vacuum \(\zeta=(i\gamma)^*(1\otimes1)\).
Suppose the joint spectral measure of \(\zeta\) for left and right multiplication by \(Z\) satisfies
\[
                         \nu_\zeta\le m^2\mu^2,\qquad m<\infty,\quad m\ge0 .
                                                                         \tag{1}
\]
For a real symmetric \(q\in L^2(\mu^2)\) put \(E_q=(\mathrm{id}\otimes\tau)iI_q\gamma\) and \(F_q(u)=\operatorname{Re}\langle u,-iE_qu\rangle\), with inner insertion \(I_{a\otimes b}(v\otimes w)=va\otimes bw\).
For every \(u=h\pm i\sqrt{1-h^2}\) with \(h\in\mathcal A\) a strict selfadjoint contraction,
\[
 \begin{split}
 |F_q(u)|\le\|E_qu\|_2&\le(2m+3)\|q\|_{L^2(\mu^2)},\\
 \|E_qp\|_2&\le 2(2m+3)\|q\|_{L^2(\mu^2)}\|p\|\qquad(p\in\mathcal A).
 \end{split}                                                             \tag{2}
\]
The original slice closure and its actual adjoint have domains containing all bounded vectors \(M\Omega\).
For each \(u\), the functional \(F_q(u)\) on bounded real symmetric kernels has a real symmetric density of \(L^2(\mu^2)\) norm at most \(2m+3\).
Its negative part as a signed measure has the same norm bound.

There is also a signed estimate on two disjoint blocks.
For orthogonal marginal spectral projections \(e,f\) with \(t=\tau(e)>0,\ s=\tau(f)>0\), put \(k=e/t-f/s\).
Then
\[
 F_{e\otimes f+f\otimes e}(u)\ \ge\
 -ts\left(\frac{\|e\zeta e\|_2^2}{t^3}+
          \frac{\|f\zeta f\|_2^2}{s^3}+
          \frac{\|k\zeta k\|_2^2}{1/t+1/s}\right).                       \tag{3}
\]
This block estimate also holds without (1).

For an actual normal tuple, let \(g(z,v)=\int\kappa(z,w,v)^2d\mu(w)\) be the marginal kernel of squared Menger curvature at \(Z\).
Under (1) both oriented original coordinate slices are bounded on polynomial contractions if
\[
  g=\sum_k\lambda_k 1_{B_k}\otimes1_{B_k}+b\quad(\mu^2\text{-a.e.}),\qquad
  b\in L^2(\mu^2),\quad
  L=\sum_k\frac{\lambda_k}{t_k}\|e_k\zeta e_k\|_2^2<\infty,              \tag{4}
\]
where \(\lambda_k\ge0,\ t_k=\mu(B_k)>0,\ e_k=1_{B_k}(Z)\).
In fact the matched curvature form obeys \(F_g(u)\ge-L-(2m+3)\|b\|_2\).
Consequently a deficient exchange pair satisfying the spectral domination in [the restricted normal comparison](Yin_C_F0324_v1.md#statement) has \(g\notin L^2(\mu^2)\), and its \(g\) admits no decomposition (4) at a deficient coordinate.
Whether every pair in that class has finite original slice cost remains open.

## Argument

### Iteration in the Hilbert norm

By the [marginal adjoint formula](Yin_C_F0077_v1.md#the-marginal-restriction-of-the-actual-liberation-adjoint), for real symmetric \(q\in L^2(\mu^2)\cap L^2(\nu_\zeta)\) the real derivation \(iI_q\gamma\) is well-defined on the original core with actual vacuum \(\eta_q=S_q\zeta\), where \(S_q\) is the joint spectral action on the vacuum.
The row and column corrections cancel by symmetry.
Hence (1) gives \(\|\eta_q\|_2\le m\|q\|_{L^2(\mu^2)}\) for every real symmetric Hilbert kernel.
Each fixed \(h\in\mathcal A\) has a finite liberation expansion, so insertion on it involves bounded outer factors multiplying \(q\) in the coarse Hilbert space.
The differentiated binomial series, with derivative factor \(j\|h\|^{j-1}\) on \(h^j\), then gives \(\|E_qu\|_2\le C_u\|q\|_{L^2(\mu^2)}\) at each fixed test \(u\).
Kernel norms below refer to \(\mu^2\).

First take a nonzero finite bounded real symmetric tensor \(q\).
By the [finite-kernel energy inequality (B6)](Yin_C_F0077_v1.md#uniform-row-l2-bounds-close-the-kernel-composition-hierarchy),
\[
       \|E_bu\|_2^2\le2(\|\eta_b\|_2+\|b\|_2)\|E_bu\|_2+
                                  \|E_{b\circ b}u\|_2
\]
for each finite bounded real symmetric \(b\), where \(\circ\) is integral operator composition.
This identity's underlying estimates apply on the bounded marginal extension and to either analytic sign by the first-derivative series.
The powers \(q^{\circ k}\) remain finite bounded real symmetric tensors.
The Hilbert--Schmidt ideal inequality gives \(\|q^{\circ k}\|_2\le\|q\|_2^k\), since the integral operator norm is at most the Hilbert--Schmidt norm.
For \(b=q^{\circ 2^j}\) apply (1) at every stage, including \(j=0\).
The quantities \(w_j=\|E_{q^{\circ 2^j}}u\|_2/\|q\|_2^{2^j}\) obey
\[
          0\le w_j\le C_u,\qquad w_j^2\le 2(m+1)w_j+w_{j+1}.
\]
Taking the finite supremum gives \((\sup w_j)^2\le(2m+3)\sup w_j\).
In particular \(w_0\le2m+3\).
The zero kernel has zero value.
Finite bounded symmetric kernels are dense in the real symmetric \(L^2(\mu^2)\) subspace by finite product partitions and symmetrization.
The same approximants converge in \(L^2(\nu_\zeta)\) by (1).
Their values at \(u\) converge by the fixed-test estimate, which proves the first line of (2), since \(\|u\|_2=1\).
Averaging the two analytic signs bounds the slice of \(h\), scalar dilation includes all selfadjoint contractions, and taking real and imaginary parts proves the second line.

The actual core adjoint identity \(E_q^*p=p\eta_q-E_qp\) gives the core adjoint bound \((5m+6)\|q\|_2\|p\|\).
On bounded core approximants tending strongly to \(x\in M\) the inputs converge in trace Hilbert norm and both sets of outputs are Hilbert-bounded.
Weakly convergent subnets and weak closure of the two closed linear graphs give the asserted original domain inclusions.
The real Hilbert-space representation theorem on symmetric kernels gives the density for \(F_q(u)\), and its positive and negative parts are again real symmetric functions.

### Off-diagonal blocks

For a nonzero bounded real centered marginal function \(k\), the kernel \(a=k\otimes k\) has row zero and \(a\circ a=\|k\|_2^2a\).
In (B0c) of the same finite-kernel calculation only the composition term survives.
It equals \(\|k\|_2^2F_a(u)\), since \(\operatorname{Im}\langle u,E_a u\rangle=\operatorname{Re}\langle u,-iE_a u\rangle\).
Completing the square in (B0e) therefore gives
\[
       F_a(u)=\frac{\|\eta_a\|_2^2-\|E_au-u\eta_a\|_2^2}{\|k\|_2^2}
                     \le\frac{\|k\zeta k\|_2^2}{\|k\|_2^2}.
\]
For \(k=e/t-f/s\), use the tensor identity
\[
   e\otimes f+f\otimes e
       =ts\big((e/t)^{\otimes 2}+(f/s)^{\otimes 2}-k^{\otimes 2}\big).
\]
The [projection square identity](Yin_C_F0242_v1.md#the-cost-of-a-measurable-spectral-block) gives \(F_{e\otimes e}(u)\ge-\|e\zeta e\|_2^2/t\), and likewise for \(f\).
Combining these lower bounds with the centered upper bound and \(\|k\|_2^2=1/t+1/s\) proves (3).

### Curvature modulo positive diagonal blocks

Let \(q_N\) be the first \(N\) positive blocks in (4).
The same projection square identity gives \(F_{q_N}(u)\ge-L\).
By (2), \(F_{q_N+b}(u)\ge-L-(2m+3)\|b\|_2\).
The kernel \(b\) is real symmetric by (4).
In the [matched signed curvature calculation](Yin_C_F0292_v1.md#the-signed-estimate-on-analytic-unitaries), the actual marginal has finite total squared curvature, and Jensen with \(|z-v|\kappa(z,w,v)\le2\) gives \((z-v)g\in L^2(\mu^2)\).
The compact support gives \((z-v)b\in L^2(\mu^2)\).
Since \(0\le q_N\le g+|b|\), dominated convergence yields \((z-v)(q_N+b)\to(z-v)g\) in \(L^2(\mu^2)\), also with conjugate gaps.
These are the two nonzero generator tensors of the matched weighted liberation, up to its fixed scalar normalization.
Finite Leibniz expansions and the differentiated binomial series pass the bound to \(F_g(u)\).

The same signed curvature calculation gives \(V^2\le2\|\xi\|_2V+(L+(2m+3)\|b\|_2)/2\), where \(V^2\) is the sum of the squared slices of the two real coordinate components on \(u\) and \(\xi\) is the coordinate vacuum.
Averaging the two analytic signs and splitting general core inputs into real and imaginary parts gives finite cost on core contractions in both orientations.
In particular the empty block sum covers \(g\in L^2(\mu^2)\).
Equivalently, on an exchange pair with the same bounds at both coordinates, the [sum of matched forms](Yin_C_F0314_v1.md#statement) and the bound \(H(u)\le4\|\xi\|_2^2\) bound the negative part of the difference \(D(u)\).
This proves the stated obstruction for any deficient pair of the specified type.
