# A selfadjoint pair with maximal Delta and no liberation dual system

## Current assessment

[Selected decision](#decision-direct-sum-counterexample)

## Statement

Let \(S_1,S_2\) be a standard free semicircular pair generating a von Neumann algebra \(N\) with a faithful normal tracial state \(\tau_N\).
Give \(M=N\oplus N\) the state \(\tau(a\oplus b)=(\tau_N(a)+\tau_N(b))/2\), and set \(X_j=S_j\oplus(S_j+5\,1)\) for \(j=1,2\).
Then \(W^*(X_1,X_2)=M\), both coordinates have regular scalar shifts, and \(\Delta(X_1,X_2)=2\).
There is no liberation dual system in the [generator-domain formulation](Yin_C_F0079_v4.md#liberation-dual-system), with \(M\Omega\subseteq\operatorname{Dom}L_i\cap\operatorname{Dom}L_i^*\).

## Argument

### The common spectral projection

The spectra in the two summands are contained in \([-2,2]\) and \([3,7]\).
Choose a continuous function on \([-2,7]\) equal to \(1\) on the first interval and \(0\) on the second.
Polynomial approximation gives real polynomials \(p_n\) converging to this function uniformly, so \(p_n(X_j)\to e=1\oplus0\) in operator norm for both \(j\).
In particular \(e\in C^*(1,X_1)\cap C^*(1,X_2)\).
The algebra generated by \(X_1,X_2\) as a von Neumann algebra then contains \(S_j\oplus0=eX_j\) and \(0\oplus S_j=(1-e)X_j-5(1-e)\) for both \(j\), proving generation.
The coordinate laws are mixtures of two translates of nonatomic semicircular laws.
Faithfulness of the trace gives zero kernel for all real scalar shifts, and nonreal shifts are invertible because the coordinates are selfadjoint.

### Maximal Delta

The right creation operators on the semicircular Fock space satisfy \([S_j,r_i]=\delta_{ij}P\), with \(P\) the vacuum projection for \(N\), by the basic commutators in [Voi98, §5.13](../audits/literature/Voi98/Yin_U005.md#semicircular-model-and-sign-correction-513) in variance-one normalization.
The [bounded-dual implication in MSY23, Proposition 6.2](../audits/literature/MSY23/Yin_U005.md#definition-61-and-proposition-62-the-dual-system-route) gives \(\Delta(S_1,S_2)=2\).

The [full linear matrix criterion, MSY23, Theorem 3.1](../audits/literature/MSY23/Yin_U002.md#msy23-u002) equates maximal Delta of a tracial tuple with injectivity of the evaluations of all full square linear matrices.
A full matrix \(A(x_1,x_2)\) remains full under \(x_j\mapsto x_j+5\,1\) because a factorization through a smaller size would pull back under the inverse substitution.
Translation also preserves affine linearity.
Thus the two blocks \(A(S_1,S_2)\) and \(A(S_1+5\,1,S_2+5\,1)\) are injective by the criterion for the semicircular pair.
Their direct sum is \(A(X_1,X_2)\) on the two tracial Hilbert-space summands and is injective.
This holds for every full square linear matrix, so the same criterion gives \(\Delta(X_1,X_2)=2\).

### The obstruction to a liberation dual

In \(H=L^2(M,\tau)\) let \(\Omega=1\), with \(P_\Omega\) the projection onto \(\mathbb C\Omega\).
The first coordinate of a liberation dual would satisfy \([X_1,L_1-P_\Omega]=[X_2,L_1]=0\) on \(M\Omega\).
For \((Z,B)=(X_1,L_1-P_\Omega)\) or \((X_2,L_1)\), commutation with \(Z\) propagates to \(p_n(Z)\) on \(M\Omega\), since \(Z\) preserves that subspace.
The two domain inclusions hold also for \(L_1-P_\Omega\) because \(P_\Omega\) is bounded.
For every \(\xi\in M\Omega\),
\[
 \langle \xi,p_n(Z)B\Omega\rangle
 =\langle B^*\xi,p_n(Z)\Omega\rangle
 \longrightarrow \langle B^*\xi,e\Omega\rangle
 =\langle \xi,Be\Omega\rangle .
\]
The inner product is linear in the second variable.
The operator norm limit on the left and density give \(eB\Omega=Be\Omega\) for each choice of \(B\).
Subtracting the two identities would give \([e,P_\Omega]\Omega=0\).
In fact \([e,P_\Omega]\Omega=(e-\tau(e)1)\Omega\) has norm \(1/2\) because \(e\) is a projection of trace \(1/2\).
No \(L_1\) with the required relations and domains can exist.
