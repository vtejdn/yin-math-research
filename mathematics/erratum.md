# Mathematical issues

## Yi Han, Brown measure convergence for the spectrum of polynomials in Ginibre matrices

Yin's [direct audit of arXiv:2606.01664v1](audits/literature/Han26/Yin_U001.md#han26-u001) identifies limitations in Yi Han's printed proof.
In Lemma 6.11 (p. 40, proof on pp. 47–48), \(C_p\) is valid for shifts in the bounded disk of (2.5), and \(C_{p,z}\) suffices for each fixed shift.
The [quadratic test](audits/literature/Han26/Yin_U001.md#shift-dependence) rules out uniformity on the entire plane for (2.2)–(2.3).
On p. 26 the identity for a restricted adjoint needs orthogonal compression, (3.17) on p. 21 needs its good events or their error probabilities, and the mass bound following (6.9) on p. 40 needs an expectation on the left.
The [individual checks](audits/literature/Han26/Yin_U001.md#local-corrections) give the corrected forms, the inner-product convention on p. 18 and the full covariance required in the p. 40 footnote.
These corrections suffice for Theorems 1.2 and 1.4 in the stated iid holomorphic models.
For any use of a uniform resolvent comparison, specify the shift range and the chosen linearization.
The [GUE Task](tasks/Yin_T_F0012_v1.md#contract) uses the Gaussian theorem only for holomorphic polynomials in independent paired inputs.
