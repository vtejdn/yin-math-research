# Atiyah strategy assessment: seven routes and the surviving arithmetic gap

Date: 2026-11-11 (Asia/Hong_Kong).
This requested assessment concerns all seven current strategies for [Yin_T_A0001](../tasks/Yin_T_A0001_v1.md#contract).
The main agent synthesized the responsible materials with three delegated route assessments and a bounded agent-S allocation assessment.
This is a strategic synthesis of retained results and their limitations, not a new theorem, independent proof review, exhaustive literature search or Task completion.
The recommendations below do not themselves change the rolling strategies' dispositions or initiate another research round.

## Overall assessment

The Task still asks for integer unnormalized kernel dimension for every finite complex group-ring matrix over every torsion-free group.
No retained result proves that full statement, supplies a valid counterexample, or establishes a new positive group class beyond the compared known mechanisms.
There is substantial completed work on actual affiliated interfaces, explicit sufficient classes, exact atom formulas and useful failure tests.
These achievements must be distinguished from reducing the central arithmetic difficulty in an uncontrolled group.

The seven routes are not seven independently advancing proofs.
Routes 1--3 are permanence or approximation approaches whose first missing input is a concrete additional group structure or model.
Routes 4 and 6 provide the most developed actual-operator tests, but their general continuations pass the unresolved arithmetic to transformed coefficients or projection intersections.
Route 5 has genuinely additional conditional-independence information for an actual amalgam.
Route 7 retains joint group-word information, but its proposed transfer to integer boundary exponents remains missing.

The most useful common question is what independently checkable restriction survives from a finite group-ring input after elimination, conditional expectation, formation of kernel projections or a boundary limit.
This is an assessment of shared proof obligations, not a theorem identifying all those problems or claiming one has been reduced to another.

## Finite extensions, infinite edges and approximation

For route 1, the [complete index-two criterion](Yin_L_A0003_v1.md#complete-norm-criterion-and-the-half-rank-witness) works in the actual ring
\[
D\oplus Dt,\qquad td=\alpha(d)t,\qquad t^2=h,
\]
and identifies its division property with absence of a solution of \(\alpha(b)b=h\) in the independently known actual coefficient division ring \(D\).
An actual solution already has a route to a finite group-ring matrix of kernel dimension \(1/2\).
The [actual embedding and closure comparison](Yin_L_A0003_v1.md#division-and-rational-closure-comparison) and that extraction are substantive completed interfaces, with the [whole-argument review](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_A0005_v1.md#whole-argument-finding).

The selected surface-group extension is solved for every rational candidate by a different cyclic quotient and leading-coefficient descent.
Both groups are already in Linnell's class \(\mathcal C\), so this is a successful calibration rather than a new permanence theorem.
The [Promislow comparison](Yin_L_A0003_v1.md#retained-candidate-beyond-the-ambient-cyclic-quotient-test) can test a mechanism without an ambient integer character, but its virtually abelian Atiyah coverage is already known.
A new-coverage allocation therefore needs a concrete pair outside applicable existing transfer results.
A counterexample allocation needs an actual torsion-free extension and an admissible rational solution, not an abstract split algebra or a formal norm equation.

For route 2, the [infinite-edge probe](Yin_L_A0001_v1.md#infinite-edge-probe) has not established either the proposed Tor condition or its needed actual analytic comparison.
Exact Tor vanishing and zero von Neumann dimension of Tor are different possible targets.
A proposed cyclic-edge hypothesis must say which conclusion it yields and why that conclusion suffices.
Even a successful permanence theorem would concern its demonstrated graph-of-groups class, without a reduction of arbitrary torsion-free groups to that class.

For route 3, convergence of dimensions in the fixed finite set \(\{0,\ldots,n\}\) immediately preserves integrality.
The hard missing object is an approximating sequence with those integer ranks.
The [JZ19 audit](../audits/literature/JZ19/Yin_U001.md#rank-and-approximation-definitions) already supplies the identified sofic rank-approximation interface; the [approximation probe](Yin_L_A0001_v1.md#approximation-probe) retains its exact use and failure modes.
Finite quotients with growing denominator lattices do not provide integer-valued approximants, and weak spectral convergence does not preserve a zero atom.
Additional small-spectrum estimates are justified only after selecting a family not already covered by an available rank-convergence theorem.

## Rational closure and scalar elimination

Route 4 has an exact all-size interface with the original Task through the [linear and bordered equivalences](Yin_L_A0004_v1.md#setting-and-exact-criteria).
It has also established specific independently checkable sufficient classes rather than only restating those equivalences.
The [two-term whole-row criterion](Yin_L_A0009_v1.md#a-concrete-sufficient-condition-closed-under-elimination), positive-real-part closure, [preserved cyclic gauges](Yin_L_A0010_v1.md#an-explicitly-preserved-coefficient-class), and [two conditional noncyclic classes](Yin_L_A0011_v1.md) provide actual pivot certificates and exact closed-kernel accounting.
The last two noncyclic mechanisms are positive imaginary compression on specified zero-real-part coordinates and inverse-difference arrays in one normalized coefficient component over an independently known division ring.

Their hypotheses are genuine extra structure.
In particular the [rational conjugate collision](Yin_L_A0011_v1.md#an-actual-torsion-free-collision-after-one-step) shows why original group-element separation cannot be reused after elimination without testing the new difference.
The [relative-path comparison](Yin_L_A0011_v1.md#individual-diffuse-path-laws-do-not-suffice) also shows why individual Haar laws and abstract torsion-freeness do not replace the canonical joint trace.
The most recent conditional induction is independently covered by [R_A0013](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_A0013_v1.md#whole-artifact-finding).

The single-cyclic-denominator family
\[
r=(1-f)-(1-h)(1-g)^{-1}(1-k),\qquad g\ne e,
\]
still has only its general admissible denominator and exact rank reduction, together with applicable special-case certificates.
Its arbitrary torsion-free zero-or-unit assertion remains unproved in this investigation.
The [completed indicable two-generator families](Yin_L_A0008_v1.md#a-singleton-extreme-support-term-and-the-earlier-examples) are related calibrations, not a classification of this four-parameter family.

The [degree-one stabilization](Yin_L_A0008_v1.md#terminating-induction-for-word-polynomials) and [three-term whole-row splitting](Yin_L_A0010_v1.md#an-elementary-row-splitting-step) do not produce easier universal base cases.
The latter preserves kernel dimension for arbitrary group-ring matrices while adding certified identity pivots.
Thus an unrestricted three-term integrality theorem still contains the whole Task.
Further refinement is valuable only if it supplies a preserved structure or an independent rank constraint beyond this reformulation.

## Amalgamated atom transport

Route 5 has a real source of extra information: the vertex algebras of an actual amalgam are conditionally free over its edge algebra.
The [BBL21 interface](../audits/literature/BBL21/Yin_U001.md#general-base-algebra-and-the-a0001-strategy-interface) gives, when the expected kernel has a bounded inverse, the unnormalized identity
\[
\nu_n(Y_1-b_1)+\nu_n(Y_2-b_2)=n+\nu_n(Y-b).
\]
Here \(\nu_n\) is unnormalized kernel dimension, and the \(b_j\) are actual subordination boundary values in the matrix edge von Neumann algebra.
This is stronger input than rewriting a kernel dimension as an atom mass.

Its [three unresolved diffuse-base interfaces](Yin_L_A0013_v1.md#three-missing-interfaces-for-a-diffuse-cyclic-base) remain distinct: expected-kernel degeneration, total support-cut trace corrections, and applicability of known vertex ranks to the actual boundary shifts.
The [completed pilot](Yin_L_A0014_v1.md) proves that full support need not give a bounded inverse, that two support-only cuts can both fail in one realization, and that the whole expected kernel need not lie in the cyclic rational field.
It also supplies a valid affiliated repair and an affine realization with integer corrections, so the negative examples do not refute all normalization methods.
Its complete mathematical scope has the [independent review](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_A0014_v1.md#whole-artifact-finding).

In the positive zero-atom calibration the actual boundary shifts vanish.
More positive examples of that kind cannot test the unresolved boundary arithmetic.
The next useful pilot must have an indefinite decomposition or a non-bottom atom, independently certified nonzero kernel and boundedly invertible expected kernel, and summands that do not separately annihilate the chosen kernel.
Opposite controlled base shifts of the old example do not supply a different test.
All completed examples are in the central cyclic amalgam \(\mathbb Z\times F_2\); no noncentral cyclic-edge permanence result has been obtained.

## Geometric index and actual projection origin

Route 6's most useful recent advance is an exact description of where geometric counting stops and coefficient arithmetic begins.
The [signed-edge calculation](../claims/Yin_C_A0004_v3.md#statement) proves that the original incidence index does not persist under the tested nonzero signed perturbations.
The [infinite commuting-orbit theorem](../claims/Yin_C_A0005_v2.md#statement) gives
\[
\nu_G(H_p)=\tau(p)+\sum_{\ell\ {\rm odd}}\tau(e_\ell),
\]
with a complete path decomposition, no missed half-infinite component, and an explicit truncation error.
This is a genuine exact formula, but the weights \(\tau(e_\ell)\) need independent arithmetic control.

The [Boolean lower-bound criterion](../claims/Yin_C_A0005_v2.md#a-trace-lower-bound-forces-a-finite-boolean-orbit) and [abelian augmentation rigidity](../claims/Yin_C_A0005_v2.md#augmentation-rigidity-for-abelian-group-ring-matrix-projections) provide such control at their stated actual group-ring projection scope.
A kernel projection derived from a group-ring matrix need only belong to the von Neumann algebra.
It cannot be treated as an actual group-ring projection without a separate proof.
Individual integer projection ranks do not establish a uniform lower bound for all nonzero Boolean intersections.

Two retained tests prevent an overstated conclusion.
The [coupled residual](../claims/Yin_C_A0005_v1.md#coupled-residual-and-energy) contains every selfadjoint scalar coefficient-group kernel even with the fixed rank-one height extremes.
The [torsion-free Bernoulli realization](../claims/Yin_C_A0005_v2.md#torsion-free-realization-in-the-von-neumann-algebra) has nullity \(2\theta/(1+\theta)\), but its matrix is not over the group ring.
Actual finite-lamp group-ring realizations instead have torsion.
Neither is an Atiyah counterexample.

The strongest next test therefore starts with explicit finite group-ring middle coefficients and derives their actual kernel/support projections and transport.
It should have an uncontrolled nonzero overlap defect or another feature outside the finished positive and abelian-projection cases.
Then seek an independently certified intersection constraint or exhibit a precise failure of the proposed invariant.
The current complete statements have [R_A0017_v3](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_A0017_v3.md#whole-artifact-finding) and [R_A0018_v2](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_A0018_v2.md#finding-infinite-orbit) at their exact source and foundational boundaries.

## Return words and boundary exponents

Route 7 uses torsion-freeness at a real combinatorial step: a repeated identity-labelled closed word has an identity-labelled primitive root.
The [multivariate return product](Yin_L_A0015_v1.md#torsion-free-cycle-arithmetic-and-its-obstruction) retains coefficient dependence and is not merely a change of notation for one scalar moment.

The required analytic-arithmetic implication remains missing.
The [stronger language counterexample](Yin_L_A0016_v1.md#even-cyclic-root-closure-and-positive-moments-do-not-suffice) has cyclic root closure, nonnegative primitive counts and a positive aggregate law, yet a half atom.
It is not a genuine group identity language.
The [positive-definite word-kernel test](Yin_L_A0017_v1.md#positive-definite-word-kernels) restores actual torsion-free quotient-group data when all its hypotheses hold, but currently yields no independent boundary arithmetic.
Arbitrary complex specialization and passage to the relevant boundary remain separate obligations.

A new generating-function computation deserves investment only if it tests an additional joint compatibility condition or a boundary estimate absent from that counterexample.
The central-winding formula is a legitimate selected scalar calibration, not an all-matrix quantization theorem.
The [regularity obstructions](Yin_L_A0017_v1.md#regularity-obstructions) exclude universal D-finiteness and positive-power bounds for the small positive spectral tail.
The lattice correction also excludes the proposed finite algebraic-factor form for the entire transform; it does not exclude a separate atomic index formula.
These source-use conclusions retain the inspected passages and excluded uses recorded in L_A0017, rather than asserting a new full proof audit.

## Shared bottlenecks and auxiliary methods

There are three common obligations that the report does not count as solved by an equivalent formulation.
First, a route must preserve the actual finite group-ring input rather than enlarge freely to arbitrary affiliated elements, diffuse-base coefficients, measurable projections or positive formal languages.
Second, it must certify the arithmetic of the resulting kernel trace independently of the desired Atiyah conclusion.
Third, a sufficient structural subclass needs a propagation or permanence argument before it can address the universal group and matrix quantifiers.

The [amenable extraction](../claims/Yin_C_A0001_v1.md#statement) is a completed conditional tool, not a proof of amenability or scalar analytic injectivity from torsion-freeness.
The [JZ19 tensor completion](../claims/Yin_C_A0002_v1.md#statement) supplies a domain-correct lower bound; a matching upper bound or discrete constraint remains needed at zero.
Neither calls for more generic cutoff work without a selected application.

Cauchy residues, reciprocal slopes, free-heat and entropy observables in [L_A0015](Yin_L_A0015_v1.md) make equivalent targets and possible measurements precise.
They currently supply no extra arithmetic condition and do not justify an eighth independently funded route.
Likewise, an exact finite correction matrix, a formal determinant identity, or a finite set of moments does not establish its kernel integrality.

The completed negative examples concern proposed sufficient mechanisms or enlarged input categories.
They must not be reported as evidence of a counterexample satisfying the Task's complete hypotheses.

## Proof direction, refutation direction and next decision

The inspected material supports retaining proof-oriented work on a bounded structural test, rather than expanding routine calibrations.
It does not yet support making a broad counterexample search the main activity.
The index-two norm criterion and the geometric fractional models provide precise counterexample interfaces, but neither currently has the necessary actual admissible candidate.
This is an allocation judgment from available evidence, not evidence that the conjecture is true.

The recommended next discriminator joins S6's actual-coefficient-origin question with S4's independently certified transformations.
Specify the group, finite matrix coefficients and all actual evaluation data first.
Derive the projections or rational separations from those data, then check whether the existing path or elimination formula really applies.
A successful new constraint should cover at least one case not already settled by the retained cyclic, graded, energy, finite-index or abelian-projection mechanisms, or explain a missing interface without claiming new group coverage.
Do not assume every useful intermediate formula must be a universal theorem.

Three outcomes would change the next allocation.
A preserved actual-input restriction gives a concrete proof mechanism to extend.
A genuine finite complex group-ring matrix over a verified torsion-free group with certified nonintegral kernel dimension triggers full counterexample verification.
A return only to arbitrary measurable projections, previously solved stationary or positive classes, or an unknown scalar kernel trace lowers the branch to a conditional tool and stops further routine spectral expansion.

Keep one bounded S5 pilot as a distinct source of conditional-independence information.
Use S1 only after its candidate screen; keep S2 and S3 as reserves and S7 as support until their specific missing input is supplied.
No route has been declared refuted merely because its current sufficient construction is limited.
The recorded Task contract and current lifecycle remain unchanged.
