## The decisive mechanisms

For C_F0050, the coordinate spectral cutoffs preserve the relevant original local graphs.
Their projection meet gives a common dense left module.
Density in Hilbert norm alone does not make this module a core for the original closed dual.
The review isolates a sufficient additional bridge: uniform boundedness of the original dual values on the chosen vacuum cutoff sequence would permit convex graph approximants.
That uniform bound remains unproved and is not part of C_F0050's accepted conclusion.

For C_F0051, the radial reference measure supplies an actual adjoint vacuum and bounded-function domains.
The transfer identity uses only the forward L2 norm comparison from the reference measure to the new measure.
Polynomial approximation and weak closedness recover the original operators, rather than substitute closed extensions.
Strict positivity is used to identify null sets and essential sup norms; its removal is treated separately in the next paragraph.

The independently reviewed nonnegative-density continuation C_F0052 allows density zero sets and removes the unused angular normalization, retaining only bounded nonnegative density and total mass one.
Zero extension first recovers the intrinsic norm estimate through the same original closed graph.
The actual vacuum and adjoint are established before this graph argument, so the construction is not circular.
It does not identify the null sets or essential sup norms of the two measures globally.
