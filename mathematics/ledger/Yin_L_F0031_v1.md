## 5. What Changed and What Did Not

This formulation removes the constrained Hilbert--Schmidt domain, retains just one freely chosen positive matrix, and makes the MS24 reduction a single scalar scaling limit.
The [ordered form](../claims/Yin_C_F0164_v1.md#3-an-ordered-formula-without-an-inverse-linear-map) additionally removes the inverse linear map.
These are useful changes in presentation and feasibility, not an independent proof of the determinant identity: the global comparison still comes from Yin_C_F0124.

For scalar variance \(\eta(q)=\sigma q\), the [positive coordinate](../claims/Yin_C_F0164_v1.md#2-a-bijection-with-all-positive-trial-matrices) gives \(b(q)^2=\sigma+q^{-1}\), exactly parametrizing the strict scalar domain \(b^2>\sigma\).
If the noise is zero, \(b(q)=q^{-1/2}\), so no artificial restriction remains.
The root self-check verified these limits, bijectivity, normalization and quadratic completion; a separate review is required before treating this record as an independently reviewed reusable result.
