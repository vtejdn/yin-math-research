# Weighted liberation in the Haar-Poisson model: exact failed counterexample families

Human author: Yin.
These tests were developed and self-checked by the previously assigned review agent at root's explicit request.
They are mathematical development, not independent review; that agent cannot independently review a proof incorporating these contributions.
The general law-independent arbitrary-weight estimate is refuted by [C_F0146](../claims/Yin_C_F0146_v1.md#statement).
The tests below retain their narrower realized Haar-Poisson scope, actual vacua and analytic all-degree bounds; that atomic counterexample does not replace these failed tests or settle the normal ACSY comparison.

## Historical test question and fixed model scope

Let (M,tau) be finite tracial, let N be an abelian marginal algebra, and let A=alg(N_core,B) be a weakly dense represented star algebra.
Suppose the represented partial liberation gamma has gamma(n)=n tensor 1-1 tensor n and gamma(B)=0, and the real derivation beta=i gamma has its actual L2 vacuum zeta.
Let g be a nonnegative real symmetric marginal kernel, let h=sqrt(g), and assume h belongs to the actual marginal beta* domain with eta=beta*h.
The proposed estimate is

Re <u,(id tensor tau) I_g gamma(u)> >= -C ||eta||2 squared

for all operator-norm-one analytic-core unitaries u, with a numerical constant independent of the law, kernel and u.
All appearances of I_g gamma(u) must have actual Hilbert values or a specified actual graph limit.
The first tests below restrict to finite bounded marginal kernels, where the products and all relevant first-derivative domains are unambiguous.
A counterexample in that subclass would refute the proposed general estimate.

Only realized tracial algebras with the actual represented liberation derivation and actual vacuum count as counterexamples.
Abstract spectral weights and finite numerical matrices without these properties are diagnostics only.

## Exact test in the correlated Haar/Poisson model

The following is a development calculation, checked by its author but not independently reviewed.
Retain the actual C_F0057 model with U Haar and V free from U, of Poisson parameter 0<r<1, and target algebras N=W*(U), B=alg(U*V,(U*V)*).
The [fixed Poisson score calculation](../ledger/Yin_L_F0055_v1.md#haar-poisson-score-and-vacuum) supplies w_r, f_r, the moments (T1) and the actual scalar conjugate vector xi_V=V(1+i f_r(V)); use that notation here.

The partial liberation of N against B satisfies

\[
\gamma U=U\otimes1-1\otimes U,\qquad
\gamma V=U\otimes U^*V-1\otimes V.
\]

Decompose it as the free-reference liberation of U against V, whose vacuum is zero, plus the V difference quotient with right coefficient \(U\otimes U^*V-1\otimes V\).
The actual polynomial tensor-adjoint formula applies because these are bounded reference-core coefficients.
Its vacuum is

\[
\gamma^*1=U^*\xi_V V^*U-\xi_V V^*,\qquad
\zeta=(i\gamma)^*1=U^*f_r(V)U-f_r(V).
\tag{T2}
\]

In particular this is a realized liberation vacuum, not an independently specified spectral weight.

Write \(\sigma_r^2=\|f_r(V)\|_2^2=2r^2/(1-r^2)^2\), as proved in the fixed score calculation, equation (T3).

Freeness and the centering of f_r(V) make the map

\[
a\otimes b\longmapsto a(U)f_r(V)b(U)
\]

an isometry up to the scalar factor sigma_r from L2(m squared) into H.
Under this map (T2) is the kernel conjugate(z) v minus 1.
Consequently its actual left/right spectral measure is

\[
d\nu_\zeta(z,v)=\sigma_r^2|z-v|^2\,dm(z)dm(v).
\tag{T4}
\]

For every bounded nonnegative real symmetric marginal kernel g, put \(J_g=\int g(z,v)|z-v|^2dm(z)dm(v)\).
The square root is bounded, real and symmetric.
The actual marginal graph theorem of C_F0077 applies, so its actual vacuum satisfies

\[
\eta_g=\beta^*\sqrt g=S_{\sqrt g}\zeta,\qquad
\|\eta_g\|_2^2=\sigma_r^2J_g.
\tag{T5}
\]

This uses the actual spectral graph and does not infer a vacuum from a formal pairing alone.

## Exact form reduction for every test in the V algebra

Let \(C_g=(\mathrm{id}\otimes\tau)I_g\gamma\).
For every Laurent polynomial p in V, direct expansion of gamma(p) and freeness show

\[
\langle p,C_gp\rangle=\frac{J_g}{2}\langle p,C_1p\rangle.
\tag{T6}
\]

Here is the coefficient check.
Each positive-power summand of gamma(V to the power n) is

\[
V^kU\otimes U^*V^{n-k}-V^k\otimes V^{n-k},
\quad0\le k<n.
\]

After inserting a separated coefficient a(U) tensor b(U) and pairing the contraction against p(V), freeness makes its coefficient factor \(\tau(Ua(U))\tau(b(U)U^*)-\tau(a(U))\tau(b(U))\).
This is the integral of a(z)b(v)(z conjugate(v)-1).
For a negative power the same factor occurs with the sign supplied by inverse differentiation.
Thus the entire form factors through \(\int g(z,v)(z\bar v-1)dm^2\).
Real symmetry of g makes this integral real and equal to -J_g/2.
For g=1 it equals -1, proving (T6).
Finite separated tensors are dense in coarse L2; for fixed p the pairing is continuous in that norm because gamma(p) is a finite bounded tensor expansion.
Thus the calculation extends to every bounded kernel g used here.

The same identity holds for the specified analytic-core unitaries \(u=a(V)+i\sqrt{1-a(V)^2}\), where a is a real Laurent polynomial and its operator norm is less than one.
The differentiated binomial series converges for both gamma and its bounded inner insertion by g; bounded g defines a bounded inner operator on the coarse correspondence.
Norm and first-derivative limits pass both sides of (T6).
The same holds for u*.

The actual unweighted lower bound from [D_F0002, (R1)](../drafts/Yin_D_F0002.md#continuing-lower-form-routes-after-the-curvature-obstruction), or directly [C_F0063's exact real liberation energy identity](../claims/Yin_C_F0063_v1.md#appendix-a-automatic-actual-liberation-contractions), gives \(\operatorname{Re}\langle u,C_1u\rangle\ge-\|\zeta\|_2^2=-2\sigma_r^2\).
Combining it with (T5)-(T6) proves the proposed estimate with C=1 for this entire test class:

\[
\operatorname{Re}\langle u,C_gu\rangle\ge-\|\eta_g\|_2^2.
\tag{T7}
\]

The previously known core unitaries V to the power n have the sharper exact value

\[
\operatorname{Re}\langle V^n,C_gV^n\rangle
=-\frac{J_g}{2}\sum_{l=1}^n r^{2l}.
\tag{T8}
\]

When J_g>0, the ratio of its negative part to the squared square-root-vacuum norm is

\[
\frac{(1-r^2)(1-r^{2n})}{4}\le\frac14.
\tag{T9}
\]

If J_g=0 the bounded nonnegative kernel vanishes off the null diagonal and both sides are zero.
Formula (T8) tests core unitaries, whereas (T7) explicitly addresses the narrower analytic-unitary family in the question.
No claim that V to the power n is itself of the positive-square-root analytic form is needed.

## Extension to actual mixed-word kernels

For a finite unitary word u in U,V and their inverses, write the actual represented derivative as gamma(u)=sum_t e_t a_t tensor b_t, with signs e_t.
Let E_N be the trace-preserving conditional expectation onto N=W*(U).
Inserting a finite separated kernel and using the trace gives the actual quadratic kernel

\[
K_u(z,v)=\sum_t e_t E_N(u^*a_t)(z)E_N(b_t)(v).
\tag{T10}
\]

For real symmetric g the relevant density is q_u(z,v)=one-half Re(K_u(z,v)+K_u(v,z)).
This follows first on separated kernels and then on bounded marginal kernels by Hilbert norm approximation of the kernel and the fixed finite bounded tensor expansion.
It is not a formal finite-matrix model.

The Haar rotation U mapped to exp(it)U fixes the law and V and commutes with E_N.
Hence for any mixed word w of total U exponent k,

\[
E_N(w)=d(w)U^k,\qquad d(w)=\tau(U^{-k}w).
\tag{T11}
\]

Every term of gamma(u) preserves the total U exponent, so (T10) depends only on the angle difference.
The constants d(w) are actual free-product moments.
For this real Poisson parameter they are real polynomials in r, computed if needed by repeatedly centering alternating U/V blocks: the trace of a centered alternating product is zero, and removing blocks expands the desired trace into shorter words.
All scalar U moments are zero except degree zero, and scalar V moments are r to the absolute exponent.
This gives an exact terminating recurrence, not an approximation of the joint law.

For the family W=U^a V with integer a, the conditional expectation is E_N(W^l)=r^l U^(al) for every positive l.
If a is nonzero, expansion of each V into r plus its centered part leaves only the all-scalar term: any nonempty centered V subword has intervening nonzero same-sign powers of the Haar unitary, so its conditional expectation is zero by freeness.
At a=0 the claim is the Poisson moment formula.
Since W=U^(a+1)(U*V), liberation gives

\[
\gamma(W^n)=\sum_{l=1}^n
\bigl(W^{n-l}U^{a+1}\otimes(U^*V)W^{l-1}
       -W^{n-l}\otimes W^l\bigr).
\]

Conditional expectation is N-bimodular.
Using (T11) in this expansion proves the exact finite real kernel, with theta=arg(v)-arg(z),

\[
q_{(U^aV)^n}(\theta)=\sum_{l=1}^n r^{2l}
 [\cos((a(l-1)-1)\theta)-\cos(al\theta)].
\tag{T12}
\]

This includes the zero kernel for a=-1, since then W is the annihilated generator U*V, and the earlier V^n kernel for a=0.
It gives q_(U^a V)=r squared times (cos(theta)-cos(a theta)).
Likewise direct cancellation of the initial U block gives, for positive b,

\[
q_{U^aV^b}(\theta)=\sum_{l=1}^b r^{2l}(\cos\theta-1)
                       +r^{2b}(1-\cos(a\theta)).
\tag{T13}
\]

In particular this latter family is no worse pointwise than the V^b test.
Formula (T12) is a genuinely mixed repeated-word test and does not reduce to the single J_g scaling of the V-only algebra.

## Uniform bounds for two mixed-word families

Formula (T12) in fact rules out degree-growth failure in this entire family.
Put t=r squared, phi=a theta, x squared=1-cos(theta), and

\[
A_n(\phi)=t\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}t^k e^{ik\phi},\qquad
a_n=A_n(0)=\frac{t(1-t^n)}{1-t}.
\]

Then q_n=Re[(exp(-i theta)-exp(i phi))A_n(phi)].
Its value with theta replaced by zero while phi is kept fixed is

\[
q_n^0=(1-t)\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}t^k(1-\cos(k\phi))
                  +t^n(1-\cos(n\phi))\ge0.
\]

Cauchy--Schwarz with weights (1-t)t to the power k gives

\[
|A_n(\phi)-a_n|^2\le\frac{2t^3}{(1-t)^2}q_n^0.
\]

Indeed A_n-a_n is t/(1-t) times the weighted sum of exp(ik phi)-1; the total weight is at most t, and its weighted squared-difference sum is at most 2q_n^0.
Therefore completion of the scalar square yields

\[
q_n\ge q_n^0-a_nx^2
 -\sqrt2 x\sqrt{2t^3q_n^0/(1-t)^2}
\ge-\left(a_n+\frac{t^3}{(1-t)^2}\right)x^2
\ge-\frac14\sigma_r^2|z-v|^2.
\tag{T14}
\]

The last step uses sigma_r squared=2t/(1-t) squared and 1-t+t squared<=1.
This is pointwise for every theta, every integer a and every n>=1.
Integrating against any bounded nonnegative symmetric g and using the ACTUAL vacuum identity (T5) proves the proposed lower estimate with C=1/4 on this full mixed-word family.
No growth in n can give a counterexample here.
These are unitary core-word tests; this conclusion is not promoted to all analytic functions of those words.

A second exact family is the commutator u_a=U^a V U^(-a)V^(-1), for nonzero integer a.
Directly differentiating gives six terms.
The last two produce 1-cos(theta); the two middle terms produce t(1-cos(a theta)); the initial two terms produce -t squared(1-cos((a+1)theta)).
Thus

\[
q_{u_a}(\theta)=1+t-t^2-\cos\theta-t\cos(a\theta)
                                      +t^2\cos((a+1)\theta).
\tag{T15}
\]

For the conditional expectation check, E_N(VU^a V*)=tU^a for a nonzero, since U^a is centered and V is free from N.
This verifies the six-term computation by actual trace pairings.
The a=0 word is 1 and is excluded from (T15).

Write y squared=1-cos(a theta).
The triangle inequality for unit-circle chords gives 1-cos((a+1)theta)<=(x+y) squared.
Completing the square gives

\[
q_{u_a}\ge (1-t^2)x^2+t(1-t)y^2-2t^2xy
\ge-\frac{t^3}{1-t}x^2
\ge-\frac1{27}\sigma_r^2|z-v|^2,
\tag{T16}
\]

since t squared(1-t)/4<=1/27 on 0<t<1.
Thus this entire high-U-degree family also cannot refute the proposed estimate.

Positivity itself does fail.
At a=1, (T15) becomes \((1-\cos\theta)[1+t-2t^2(1+\cos\theta)]\), which is negative near theta=0 when 4t squared>1+t.
Another short example is u=(UV)^3, whose (T12) density is \(2t^2(1-\cos^2\theta)(1+2t\cos\theta)\).
For t=3/4 and cos(theta)=-5/6 its normalized negative ratio is exactly 1/1024.
Thus these are genuine negative mixed forms, but their negative ratios remain controlled by the actual square-root-vacuum cost.
