This ordinary continuation for Yin on 2026-11-06 tests the near-corank-one part of the high-matching concentration route for the [finite-variance Brown Task](../tasks/Yin_T_F0012_v3.md#contract).
The formulas and applications received ordinary authoring self-checks and the separate [independent whole-scope review R200v17](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_F0200_v17.md#near-corank-normalization-scope).
It gives an explicit normalization of the already retained isolated-row calculation and identifies a probability consequence of an existing accepted theorem.
It is not a new independent review, an improvement for every high-matching minor, or a Brown convergence conclusion.
The decisive reused input is [C194v1, restricted probability statement](../claims/Yin_C_F0194_v1.md#statement), whose isolation conditions do not require low matching.
Its [isolated-row calculation](../claims/Yin_C_F0194_v1.md#isolated-row-reduction) already keeps the full center and combines two linear forms by a union bound.

## Model, conditioning and the probability target

Use the [actual three-real-input model](Yin_L_F0066_v9.md#actual-model-and-target), with fixed centered variance-one real off-diagonal laws and real diagonal laws of finite second moment, independent Wigner factors within each size and arbitrary coupling across sizes.
The centered diagonal laws of the Task are included.
Put \(A:=W_1\), \(B:=W_2\), \(C:=W_3\), fix \(z\ne0\), and set \(M:=A-zC^{-1}B^{-1}\) on the invertibility event for \(B,C\).
The selected pilot has nonreal \(z\); the calculation and accepted inputs here only require \(z\ne0\).
For each index \(i\), let \(D_i:=M_{\widehat i,\widehat i}\), \(T_i:=\operatorname{adj}D_i\), \(Q_i:=(T_i+T_i^T)/2\), and use
\[
 \mathcal F_i:=\sigma((W_1)_{\widehat i,\widehat i},(W_1)_{ii},B,C).
\]
On \(T_i\ne0\), define
\[
 Z_i:=\det M/\|T_i\|_F,\qquad
 \kappa_i(\tau):=\mathbb P\{|Z_i|\le\tau\mid\mathcal F_i\}.
\]
The event \(\mathcal H_i(h,r)\) requires \(T_i\ne0\) and a matching of at least \(r\) edges in the graph with edges \(j\ne k\) satisfying \(|(Q_i)_{jk}|\ge h\|T_i\|_F\).
The target is an \(O(N^{-B_*})\) bound on the mean probability of backgrounds in
\[
 \Lambda_N\cap\mathcal H_i(N^{-H},\lfloor\sqrt N\rfloor),
 \qquad \kappa_i(N^{-\Theta})>N^{-B_*},
\]
for a desired fixed \(B_*>1\), a sufficiently fine fixed \(\Theta>0\), and the previously chosen fixed graph exponent \(H\).
Here \(\Lambda_N\) is the \(B,C\)-measurable polynomial inverse guard; its precise role and the exponent order are specified below.
The result below controls the isolated-minor portion of this target and reduces the remaining portion to polynomially invertible \(D_i\).

## Complete numerator and exact singular-value expansion

Fix one index, suppress its subscript, and retain the exact conditioning
\[
 \mathcal F:=\sigma((W_1)_{\widehat i,\widehat i},(W_1)_{ii},B,C).
\]
Use the [complete block and normalization](../ledger/Yin_L_F0066_v9.md#actual-model-and-target)
\[
 M=\begin{pmatrix}\mu&(x+f)^T\\x+g&D\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad x:=\xi/\sqrt N,\qquad T:=\operatorname{adj}D,
\]
\[
 Z(\xi):=\frac{\det M}{\|T\|_F}
  =\frac{\mu\det D-(x+f)^TT(x+g)}{\|T\|_F}.
 \tag{Near-corank-complete-numerator}
\]
All shifts and \(D\) are \(\mathcal F\)-measurable, while the coordinates of \(\xi\) retain their independent original real law.
No condition on a norm of the full \(M\), which contains the fresh border, is imposed through this conditioning.

Let \(m:=N-1\ge2\), and write the singular values in increasing order as
\[
 0\le t_1<t_2\le\cdots\le t_m.
\]
This section assumes \(t_2>0\), so that \(D\) is invertible or has corank one.
Choose a singular-value decomposition
\[
 D=U\operatorname{diag}(t_1,\ldots,t_m)V^*,
 \qquad U=(u_1,\ldots,u_m),\quad V=(v_1,\ldots,v_m),
\]
measurably from \(D\), with deterministic choices of bases and phases.
The separation \(t_1<t_2\) makes the smallest singular subspaces one-dimensional; all choices below depend only on the conditioned background.
Put
\[
 \omega:=\det U\,\overline{\det V},\quad
 \rho^2:=\sum_{j=2}^m(t_1/t_j)^2,\quad
 a:=(1+\rho^2)^{-1/2},\quad r:=t_1/t_2.
\]
The adjugate multiplication identity, or the usual inverse formula when \(t_1>0\), gives
\[
 \frac{T}{\|T\|_F}
 =\omega a\left(v_1u_1^*
            +\sum_{j=2}^m\frac{t_1}{t_j}v_ju_j^*\right),
 \qquad
 \frac{\det D}{\|T\|_F}=\omega a t_1.
 \tag{Near-corank-exact-adjugate}
\]
Indeed \(\|T\|_F=(\prod_{j=2}^m t_j)\sqrt{1+\rho^2}\), and the displayed formula remains valid at \(t_1=0\).
Define the actual affine forms
\[
 \ell_j(x):=(x+f)^Tv_j,\qquad
 h_j(x):=u_j^*(x+g),\qquad
 \ell:=\ell_1,\quad h:=h_1.
\]
Then the exact identity is
\[
 Z=\omega a\left[
       \mu t_1-\ell(x)h(x)
       -\sum_{j=2}^m\frac{t_1}{t_j}\ell_j(x)h_j(x)
                  \right].
 \tag{Near-corank-exact-product}
\]
In particular the term \(\mu\det D\) has become the explicit center \(\omega a\mu t_1\), not zero by assumption.
The residual matrix in parentheses has operator norm \(r\), giving
\[
 \left|Z-\omega a\{\mu t_1-\ell(x)h(x)\}\right|
 \le a r\|x+f\|\,\|x+g\|.
 \tag{Near-corank-centered-error}
\]
The corresponding Frobenius residual is \(a\rho\le a\sqrt{m-1}\,r\), but the border error above uses the operator norm and loses no extra square root of the dimension.
Also
\[
 (1+(m-1)r^2)^{-1/2}\le a\le1.
\]
For \(r\le N^{-1/2}\), one therefore has \(a\ge1/\sqrt2\).
For exact corank one, \(a=1\), \(t_1=r=0\), and this is precisely the full-numerator factorization in L66v9.

## Original fresh-row balls, polynomial guards and the shared randomness

For the original finite-variance law, \(\mathbb E\|x\|^2=m/N\le1\).
Thus for every fixed \(q>0\),
\[
 \mathbb P\{\|x\|>N^q\mid\mathcal F\}\le N^{-2q},
 \tag{Near-corank-original-row-tail}
\]
uniformly in the background.
This is an event bound, not conditioning on a cutoff or replacement by a truncated law.
For a bounded off-diagonal law \(|\xi|\le K_\xi\), the ball \(\|x\|\le K_\xi\) instead holds surely.

On the \(B,C\)-measurable guard \(\Lambda_N\), with \(\|B^{-1}\|,\|C^{-1}\|\le N^L\), the actual shift gives
\[
 \|f\|,\|g\|\le |z|N^{2L},\qquad
 |\mu|\le |(W_1)_{ii}|+|z|N^{2L}.
\]
If the additional background-measurable event \(|(W_1)_{ii}|\le N^d\) is used, choose
\[
 K_0>\max\{q,d,2L\}+1.
\]
For sufficiently large \(N\), the fresh-row ball then implies
\[
 |\mu|,\ \|x+f\|,\ \|x+g\|\le N^{K_0}.
\]
The probability of the displayed diagonal-guard failure is at most
\(\mathbb E|\zeta_1|^2 N^{-2d-1}\), since \((W_1)_{ii}=\zeta_1/\sqrt N\).
This diagonal guard is used only in the following deterministic error comparison, not added as a hypothesis to the probability theorem below.

Suppose in addition that
\[
 t_2\ge N^{-L_2},\qquad t_1\le N^{-J}.
\]
The center and residual together satisfy, on that ball,
\[
 \left|Z+\omega a\,\ell(x)h(x)\right|
 \le a\left(N^{-J+K_0}+N^{-J+L_2+2K_0}\right).
 \tag{Near-corank-zero-center-error}
\]
Given a desired exponent \(\Theta>0\), a choice
\[
 J>\Theta+L_2+2K_0+2
\]
makes this error at most \(N^{-\Theta}\), and also gives \(a\ge1/\sqrt2\), for all sufficiently large \(N\).
More generally, without discarding the center, (Near-corank-centered-error) implies
\[
 \begin{split}
 \kappa(N^{-\Theta})
 \le{}&N^{-2q}
 +\mathbb P_\xi\left\{
 |\ell(x)h(x)-\mu t_1|
 \le a^{-1}N^{-\Theta}+rN^{2K_0}
 \ \middle|\ \mathcal F\right\}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{Near-corank-centered-concentration}
\]
The probability on the right is for the unchanged original row law; dropping its intersection with the ball only enlarges it.
When the stronger displayed choice of \(J\) applies, the event \(|Z|\le N^{-\Theta}\) on the ball is contained in
\[
 \{|\ell(x)|\le2N^{-\Theta/2}\}
 \ \cup\
 \{|h(x)|\le2N^{-\Theta/2}\}.
 \tag{Near-corank-linear-union}
\]
The two forms share the same \(\xi\) and are not conditionally independent.
As forms in the normalized coordinates \(x\), their coefficient vectors have unit norm; as forms in the raw variables \(\xi\), the coefficient norms are \(N^{-1/2}\).
Also \(u_1^*(x+g)=\bar u_1^{\,T}(x+g)\), so both are genuine complex-coefficient linear forms in the original real entries.
Multiplying either form by \(\sqrt N\) makes its raw coefficient norm one, multiplies its affine offset by \(\sqrt N\), and turns the radius \(2N^{-\Theta/2}\) into \(2N^{(1-\Theta)/2}\).
Their approximate kernel relations are
\[
 Dv_1=t_1u_1,\qquad D^T\bar u_1=t_1\bar v_1.
\]
These are the exact right/transpose-left interfaces used by the accepted isolated-row argument.
No multiplication of the two probabilities is justified, and no new least-common-denominator hypothesis is being adopted.
Outside sufficiently strong absolute isolation, the center \(\mu t_1\) or the residual can exceed the requested window; the zero-product union then has no justification from a relative gap alone.

## The accepted isolated-minor theorem already controls this background upper tail

The probability input in [C194v1](../claims/Yin_C_F0194_v1.md#statement) concerns a raw \(N\)-by-\(N\) real Wigner matrix plus an arbitrary deterministic complex shift bounded entrywise by \(N^\gamma\).
For every prescribed failure exponent \(b>0\), it supplies fixed exponents \(A_0,L_{\rm iso}>0\) and an \(O(N^{-b})\) bound for the union over all principal indices of the tiny-ratio event together with either exact corank one or its absolute and relative isolation conditions.
Its independent-random-shift extension is uniform on the shift guard.
No low-matching premise, external minor-norm premise, or full-\(M\)-norm premise appears in that statement.
Its proof's raw-entry cutoffs and diagonal conditioning are internal to the proved probability bound.
They do not change the original conditional law in \(\kappa_i\).

Fix the desired \(B_*>1\), retain the existing \(B,C\)-measurable inverse guard \(\Lambda_N\), with \(\mathbb P(\Lambda_N^c)=O(N^{-B_*})\), and fix its exponent \(L\) before the following choices.
Conditioning on the full \(B,C\), the raw matrix \(\sqrt N M\) has its original first-input Wigner law and shift \(-\sqrt N z C^{-1}B^{-1}\), bounded entrywise by \(N^{2L+2}\) for sufficiently large \(N\).
Choose the raw shift exponent \(2L+2\) and failure exponent \(b:=2B_*+2\) in C194v1.
Let \(A_0,L_{\rm iso}\) be the resulting fixed exponents.
Define the background event
\[
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal I_i:=\{T_i\ne0\}\cap\bigl(
 &\{\operatorname{corank}D_i=1\}\\
 {}\cup{}&
 \{D_i\text{ invertible},\
 t_1(D_i)\le N^{-L_{\rm iso}-1/2},\
 t_1(D_i)/t_2(D_i)\le N^{-L_{\rm iso}}\}
 \bigr).
 \end{split}
 \tag{Near-corank-normalized-isolation}
\]
Both \(\mathcal I_i\) and \(\Lambda_N\cap\mathcal H_i(N^{-H},\lfloor\sqrt N\rfloor)\) are measurable in the exact \(\mathcal F_i\).
They do not involve the remaining first border or the norm of the full \(M\).
The actual diagonal is already part of \(\mathcal F_i\); no further diagonal condition is needed here.

For any fixed \(\Theta\ge A_0+1\), the two normalization identities are
\[
 t_j(\sqrt N D_i)=\sqrt N\,t_j(D_i),\qquad
 \frac{|\det(\sqrt N M)|}
 {\|\operatorname{adj}(\sqrt N D_i)\|_F}
 =N|Z_i|.
 \tag{Near-corank-raw-transfer}
\]
Thus \(\mathcal I_i\) is exactly sufficient for the raw isolation event, and \(|Z_i|\le N^{-\Theta}\) implies the required raw ratio at most \(N^{-A_0}\).
Conditioning on \(B,C\) and integrating the uniform theorem yields
\[
 \mathbb P\left\{\Lambda_N\cap
       \bigcup_{i=1}^N
       \bigl(\mathcal I_i\cap\{|Z_i|\le N^{-\Theta}\}\bigr)\right\}
 \le C N^{-b}.
 \tag{Near-corank-existing-joint-bound}
\]
There is no union loss beyond the union already included in the source theorem.
For any \(\mathcal F_i\)-measurable event \(\mathcal A_i\subseteq\{T_i\ne0\}\), the defining conditional probability gives
\[
 \mathbb E[\mathbf1_{\mathcal A_i}\kappa_i(N^{-\Theta})]
 =\mathbb P(\mathcal A_i\cap\{|Z_i|\le N^{-\Theta}\}).
\]
Apply this with
\(\mathcal A_i:=\Lambda_N\cap\mathcal I_i\cap
 \mathcal H_i(N^{-H},\lfloor\sqrt N\rfloor)\).
Markov's inequality and (Near-corank-existing-joint-bound) then give
\[
 \frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N
 \mathbb P\{\mathcal A_i,\
       \kappa_i(N^{-\Theta})>N^{-B_*}\}
 \le N^{B_*}\frac1N\sum_i
       \mathbb E[\mathbf1_{\mathcal A_i}\kappa_i(N^{-\Theta})]
 \le C N^{-B_*-2}.
 \tag{Near-corank-existing-upper-tail}
\]
The high-matching restriction can be included or omitted throughout this deduction.
It is therefore unnecessary to prove a new joint arithmetic estimate for this sufficiently isolated subbranch.
The conditional atom in [L66v9](../ledger/Yin_L_F0066_v9.md#fixed-laws-and-background-probability) is compatible with this bound because its background itself is sufficiently rare.
Neither result asserts a uniform conditional probability bound over every background.

## What remains after using the existing second-value floor

Use [C194v5, second-smallest floor](../claims/Yin_C_F0194_v5.md#second-smallest-floor) at failure exponent \(b+2\), with the actual \(N-1\) minor dimension and original \(\sqrt N\) normalization as in its [full assembly](../claims/Yin_C_F0194_v5.md#in-probability-floor).
The full inverse-product shift is independent of the first-input principal block after conditioning on \(B,C\).
There is a fixed \(L_2\ge1\) for which, on \(\Lambda_N\), the simultaneous failure probability of \(t_2(D_i)\ge N^{-L_2}\) is \(O(N^{-b-1})\), after the index union and harmless exponent margins.
This use is the arbitrary-power second-value assertion, not the nonsummable least-value conclusion.
The [normalization theorem's guards](../claims/Yin_C_F0194_v15.md#statement) likewise retain a second-value floor but do not replace it by a least-value floor.

Choose
\[
 J>L_{\rm iso}+L_2+1.
\]
On the simultaneous second-value event, every minor with \(t_1(D_i)\le N^{-J}\) and \(T_i\ne0\) lies in \(\mathcal I_i\).
More explicitly, with the per-index event \(\mathcal G_i:=\{t_2(D_i)\ge N^{-L_2}\}\in\mathcal F_i\), the resulting estimate is
\[
 \begin{split}
 \frac1N\sum_i\mathbb P\{&
 \Lambda_N\cap\mathcal H_i(N^{-H},\lfloor\sqrt N\rfloor),\
 t_1(D_i)\le N^{-J},\
 \kappa_i(N^{-\Theta})>N^{-B_*}\}\\
 &\le C N^{-B_*-2}+C N^{-b-1}.
 \end{split}
 \tag{Near-corank-smallest-value-branch}
\]
Indeed on \(\mathcal G_i\) the event is included in the already bounded isolated branch, and its complement contributes at most the simultaneous second-value failure probability.
The simultaneous guard is used only for this probability comparison; no conditioning on other indices' minors is inserted into \(\kappa_i\).
This conclusion requires only the source isolation thresholds; the stronger \(J\) used for the illustrative fresh-row error bound is optional and can be imposed without changing the deduction.

The remaining high-matching target can consequently be restricted, outside the stated arbitrarily small polynomial failures, to invertible actual minors with
\[
 t_1(D_i)>N^{-J}.
 \tag{Near-corank-remaining-minors}
\]
These minors have a polynomial inverse bound, but that fact alone provides no summable concentration estimate for their complete quadratic numerator.
This is a restriction of the remaining branch, not an estimate for all high matching.
The [full-corank tail](../claims/Yin_C_F0194_v2.md#product-statement) remains a separate input to the global many-index assembly and is not needed for the conditional upper-tail deduction just given.
The actual first-row center, lattice structure, and typical background distribution are still unresolved in (Near-corank-remaining-minors).
This is direct application of the accepted isolated-minor and second-value theorems with the displayed normalization and conditional-probability bookkeeping.
It neither repeats their inverse-concentration proofs nor claims a new external theorem.
No aggregate-energy argument, general-polynomial extension or planar assembly is supplied, and the full F0012 contract remains unresolved.
