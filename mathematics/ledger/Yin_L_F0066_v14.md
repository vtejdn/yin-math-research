# A legal multirow product loses its gain after conditioning on the companion statistic

This ordinary research for Yin on 2026-11-06 tests one concrete reservoir scheme for the [L66v12 coupled event](Yin_L_F0066_v12.md#coupled-near-kernel-interface) and the [L66v13 entropy gap](Yin_L_F0066_v13.md#entropy-and-average-boundary).
The actual model is the three independent normalized real Wigner factors and fixed nonreal z of [L66v12](Yin_L_F0066_v12.md#actual-complete-quadratic).
For \(n=N-1\), retain
\[
 X=(W_1)_{\widehat i,\widehat i},\qquad
 F=-z(C^{-1}B^{-1})_{\widehat i,\widehat i},\qquad
 S=X+\operatorname{sym}F,\qquad K=\operatorname{skew}F.
\]
In particular F is the principal block of the full inverse product, not a product of inverses of principal blocks.
The complete numerator, its actual center and linear terms, and its passage to a structured candidate and companion remain exactly those in L66v12.
This record neither repeats nor strengthens that preceding inverse-concentration reduction.
Simultaneous exposure preserves an independent rectangular block and gives a legitimate product of conditional row probabilities.
The product involves a conditional concentration profile that can equal one for every candidate, even when the ordinary row concentration tends to zero.
The obstruction below therefore rules out a specified conditioning-and-supremum argument, rather than the actual coupled background estimate or F0012.
The retained result is a useful method failure with ordinary authoring self-checks; it is not an independently reviewed result or a Claim argument.
All external inputs are reused at their cited existing inspection and acceptance scope.

Fix a deterministic candidate \(u\in\mathbb C^n\), \(1/2\le\|u\|\le2\), independently of X conditional on \(B,C\).
Use the polynomial shift, S-inverse and norm guards of [L66v12](Yin_L_F0066_v12.md#polynomial-row-combination-floor), with \(\|K\|\le N^{k_0}\), \(\|S\|\le N^{k_0}\) and \(\|S^{-1}\|\le N^L\).
The failure exponent q is chosen larger than \(B_*+1\) for the desired background exponent \(B_*>1\).
The S norm guard is an event being bounded, not part of the exposure conditioning below.
Set \(Q_0:=2k_0+L\) as in [L66v13](Yin_L_F0066_v13.md#fixed-candidate-estimate).
The minor-inverse exponent \(L_0\) below is chosen for the same q before choosing the precision a.

## Simultaneous exposure preserves the original rectangular variables

Fix a deterministic set \(I\subset[n]\), \(|I|=r=o(n)\), before looking at X, and put \(J:=[n]\setminus I\), \(p:=n-r\).
The special test below has \(r=O(\log N)\), with \(n=N-1\).
After ordering I first, write
\[
 S=\begin{pmatrix}H&Y^T\\Y&S_0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 K=\begin{pmatrix}K_I&k^T\\-k&K_0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 u=\binom{\alpha}{w},\qquad v=\binom t{v_J}.
 \tag{MR-actual-blocks}
\]
Here \(K_I^T=-K_I\), \(K_0^T=-K_0\), and \(k\) has size \(p\)-by-r.
Condition on the actual full \(B,C\), all original X coordinates within I and within J, and the original diagonal coordinates.
This fixes \(H,S_0,K_I,K_0,k\).
The remaining cross block is
\[
 Y=N^{-1/2}V+Y_0,
\]
where the pr entries of V are independent copies of the first input's original real law and \(Y_0\) is deterministic under this conditioning.
The other cross block is the same \(Y^T\); it is not a second independently sampled matrix.
This follows directly from independence of the distinct original upper-triangular coordinates across the disjoint cut.

For the fixed set I, the accepted [C188v2 symmetric-shift theorem](../claims/Yin_C_F0188_v2.md#statement) supplies an arbitrary-power failure bound for the needed polynomial inverse guard on \(S_0\).
Its size is p and its normalization is still \(N^{-1/2}\); writing its Wigner part as \(\sqrt{p/N}\,W_p\) gives the source's exact scale.
Because \(p/N\to1\), the fixed polynomial exponents absorb this change.
No simultaneous guard over all possible growing reservoirs is inferred.
The restriction that I is fixed is retained throughout this test.
Write the minor guard as \(\|S_0^{-1}\|\le N^{L_0}\).
The event \(\|S\|\le N^{k_0}\) also gives \(\|Y\|,\|S_0\|\le N^{k_0}\), while the deterministic K bound gives \(\|K_0\|,\|k\|\le N^{k_0}\).

On \(S_0\) invertible put
\[
 G:=S_0^{-1},\qquad b:=G(-k\alpha+K_0w),\qquad
 A:=K_0G,\qquad c:=S_0w-K_0b.
\]
The exact equation \(Sv=Ku\) yields
\[
 v_J=b-GYt,
\]
\[
 (H-Y^TGY)t=K_I\alpha+k^Tw-Y^Tb.
 \tag{MR-exact-Schur-relation}
\]
If S is invertible, the Schur matrix in this equation is invertible and fixes the actual parameter t.
The retained and exposed parts of the other coupled equation are
\[
 (Su-Kv)_J=Y\alpha+A\,Yt+c+kt,
 \tag{MR-retained-system}
\]
\[
 (Su-Kv)_I=H\alpha-k^Tb+Y^Tw-K_It+k^TGYt.
 \tag{MR-exposed-system}
\]
Thus t is the actual r-dimensional component of \(S^{-1}Ku\), not an independent center.
These identities are valid for the actual inverse-product F and its prescribed Wigner randomness.

The polynomial companion bound in L66v12 gives \(\|t\|\le R_N:=2N^{L+k_0}\).
On the same norm and minor-inverse guards, replacing t by a sufficiently fine deterministic r-dimensional complex grid changes (MR-retained-system) by at most its prescribed tolerance.
Indeed its derivative in t is \(AY+k\), and
\[
 \|AY+k\|\le N^{2k_0+L_0}+N^{k_0}\le2N^{2k_0+L_0}.
\]
Choose integers \(C>2k_0+L_0+3\) and \(a>Q_0+L_0+k_0+3\).
A complex coordinate mesh \(N^{-a-C}\) has rounding error at most \(\sqrt{2r}N^{-a-C}\) in Euclidean norm.
The displayed operator bound makes the retained-equation error at most \(\delta_N:=N^{-a+Q_0}\) for large N.
The number of grid points is at most \(N^{O((a+1)r)}\), which is \(\exp(O_a((\log N)^2))\) when \(r=O(\log N)\).
This is a finite-dimensional parameter covering derived from the exact Schur system, not a net for an arbitrary n-dimensional companion.
The full S norm is used only in the intersection being bounded for the rounding error; it is not added to the cross block's conditioning.

## The legitimate product uses a conditional profile

Test the enlarged retained-system event after rounding t to one fixed grid point.
This upper bound may omit (MR-exact-Schur-relation) and (MR-exposed-system); their formulas are retained because losing them may be consequential.
Fix an omitted retained set \(E\subset J\), \(|E|\le C_0\).
All quantities except V are deterministic at this stage.

For an exact and finite conditional calculation, specialize only the first off-diagonal law to the original Rademacher law, which is an allowed fixed-law pilot.
Let \(\varepsilon\in\{-1,1\}^r\) be a fair sign vector, and define the scalar pair
\[
 W:=\alpha^T\varepsilon,\qquad Q:=t^T\varepsilon.
\]
For each attainable q and \(s\ge0\), put
\[
 \rho_{\alpha\mid t}(q;s)
 :=\sup_{z\in\mathbb C}
       \mathbb P\{|W-z|\le s\mid Q=q\},
 \qquad
 \overline\rho_{\alpha\mid t}(s)
 :=\mathbb E_Q[\rho_{\alpha\mid t}(Q;s)].
 \tag{MR-conditional-profile}
\]
These are finite-distribution quantities, so every conditional probability is taken at a positive-probability atom and the suprema are measurable.
This profile is different from the [ordinary linear concentration function](../audits/literature/Ngu12a/Yin_U001.md#concentration-conventions).

For the p independent rows of V, write \((W_j,Q_j)\) for the corresponding independent copies of \((W,Q)\).
After multiplying the retained equations by \(\sqrt N\) and absorbing \(Y_0,c,kt\), their random part is
\[
 W+A Q+d(t),
 \tag{MR-row-pair-form}
\]
where \(W,Q\in\mathbb C^p\) and \(d(t)\) is deterministic under the current conditioning.
Given the whole vector Q, the variables \(W_j\) remain conditionally independent, but each has the conditional law given \(Q_j\), not its original marginal law.
The center \(-(AQ)_j-d_j(t)\) can depend on all components of Q.
Taking the row concentration supremum only after Q is fixed gives the valid inequality
\[
 \begin{split}
 &\mathbb P\{|W_j+(AQ)_j+d_j(t)|\le s
                         \text{ for all }j\notin E\mid Q\}\\
 &\qquad\le\prod_{j\notin E}\rho_{\alpha\mid t}(Q_j;s).
 \end{split}
\]
The coordinates \(Q_j\) are independent before that conditioning.
Integrating therefore gives the genuine product bound
\[
 \mathbb P\{|W_j+(AQ)_j+d_j(t)|\le s
                         \text{ for all }j\notin E\}
 \le \overline\rho_{\alpha\mid t}(s)^{p-|E|}.
 \tag{MR-legal-product}
\]
No multiplication of mutually conditioned whole-matrix probabilities is used here.
For a coordinate tolerance \(\delta_N=N^{-a+Q_0}\), fixed-parameter rounding is absorbed by choosing \(s_N:=2\sqrt N\,\delta_N\).

After summing over the at most \(N^{C_0}\) omitted sets, a candidate family \(\mathcal U_N\) and the parameter grid \(\mathcal T_N\), this particular argument gives the conditional bound
\[
 N^{C_0}
 \sum_{u\in\mathcal U_N}\ \sum_{t\in\mathcal T_N}
 \overline\rho_{u_I\mid t}(s_N)^{p-C_0}.
 \tag{MR-weighted-count}
\]
The fixed-I guard failures are paid separately once, at their chosen arbitrary polynomial power.
For example, the part of this sum with
\(\overline\rho_{u_I\mid t}(s_N)\le N^{-C_*-\eta}\), \(\eta>0\), would overcome the available \(N^{C_*N+o(N)}\) candidate count when \(r=O(\log N)\).
The issue is whether all remaining pairs can be disposed of.
The following exact calculation shows that the unrestricted sum in (MR-weighted-count) cannot itself tend to zero.

<a id="coding-parameter-obstruction"></a>
## A permitted logarithmic parameter can encode the entire original sign row

Let
\[
 r_N:=2\left\lfloor\frac{\log_2N}{4}\right\rfloor,\qquad
 t_j:=2^{j-1}\quad(1\le j\le r_N).
 \tag{MR-coding-parameter}
\]
Then
\[
 \|t\|^2=\frac{4^{r_N}-1}{3}<4^{r_N},\qquad
 \|t\|<2^{r_N}\le\sqrt N.
\]
Hence this parameter lies well inside the actual polynomial disk supplied by the companion bound.
Its coordinates are integers, so it also belongs to the usual complex lattice net of any integer-power mesh \(N^{-a-C}\).
This is not a change of the original entry law, which remains the same Rademacher law at every N.

The identity
\[
 t^T\varepsilon
 =2\sum_{j=1}^{r_N}2^{j-1}
             \frac{\varepsilon_j+1}{2}-(2^{r_N}-1)
\]
shows that the \(2^{r_N}\) sign vectors give \(2^{r_N}\) distinct values.
Thus Q determines the whole vector \(\varepsilon\).
Consequently, for every deterministic \(\alpha\in\mathbb C^{r_N}\) and every \(s\ge0\),
\[
 \rho_{\alpha\mid t}(Q;s)=1
 \quad\text{almost surely},\qquad
 \overline\rho_{\alpha\mid t}(s)=1.
 \tag{MR-conditional-profile-collapse}
\]
The conditional scalar or coordinate laws are point masses and fail the fixed nondegeneracy condition of the ordinary inverse Littlewood--Offord input.
Independence across the p conditioned rows survives; a useful conditional concentration bound within each row does not.

This loss is not explained only by sparse u.
Let \(m_N:=\lceil\sqrt n\rceil\) and choose the dense rational candidate
\[
 u:=m_N^{-1}\mathbf1_n.
\]
Its norm lies between \(1/2\) and one for large n, all coordinates lie in a fixed-rank progression of size three, and its rational height is polynomial.
For the retained I, all coordinates of \(\alpha=u_I\) equal \(1/m_N\).
At every radius \(0\le s<1/m_N\), the ordinary, unconditioned concentration is
\[
 \sup_z\mathbb P\{|\alpha^T\varepsilon-z|\le s\}
 =2^{-r_N}\binom{r_N}{r_N/2}
 \asymp r_N^{-1/2}.
 \tag{MR-marginal-conditional-gap}
\]
The binomial asymptotic reuses the [already inspected Stirling application](Yin_L_F0066_v11.md#complete-atom-and-matching), with r_N in place of its even group size.
The sufficiently fine windows required by the preceding reduction satisfy \(s_N<1/m_N\).
Nevertheless the conditional profile in (MR-conditional-profile-collapse) is exactly one.
Thus ordinary linear anti-concentration cannot be substituted into (MR-legal-product).

The distinct values of the normalized companion statistic \(Yt\) are separated, along their affine real line, by \(2/\sqrt N\).
Any conditioning bin of diameter strictly below this separation also identifies the entire sign row.
The failure therefore persists with sufficiently fine finite-precision conditioning, not merely exact-value conditioning.
For a uniform bound based on the available polynomial estimate for \(\|A\|\), keeping the uncertainty in \(AQ\) below the very small retained-equation window requires bins substantially finer than this separation.
A different coarser or structured conditioning may be useful, but the present guard-based replacement does not establish it.

## What this trial rules out and what remains necessary

The coding t is in the relaxed finite-dimensional parameter set for every u.
Its contribution to (MR-weighted-count) is one before summing over candidates, even if only the displayed dense candidate is retained.
Therefore the scheme
\[
 \text{condition on }Yt
 \ \longrightarrow\ \text{take each row's center supremum}
 \ \longrightarrow\ \text{sum over the full parameter grid}
\]
has a nonvanishing upper-bound term.
It cannot produce the desired \(O(N^{-B_*})\) averaged background tail by replacing the conditional profile with ordinary Littlewood--Offord concentration.
This is a concrete failure of that proposed probability multiplication and counting scheme, not merely the earlier coarse \(e^{-cN}\) versus \(N^{CN}\) comparison.

No assertion has been made that the coding parameter solves (MR-exact-Schur-relation) for the actual observed cross block.
The example tests the uniform conditional-profile estimate needed after that relation has been dropped and the parameter has been enlarged to a full net.
Nor does a conditional center attaining its supremum have to be the center supplied by \(AQ+d(t)\).
Those two losses are precisely why (MR-conditional-profile-collapse) does not constitute an actual coupled-background counterexample.
The full inverse-product model, the mean probability target and F0012 remain unrefuted.

A reopening of this particular scheme must retain additional information that the failed estimate discarded: restrictions from the actual Schur parameter relation, the actual centers, or a joint row-pair probability estimate before the companion statistic determines the row.
An unrestricted weighted census using only (MR-conditional-profile) cannot be the next sufficient lemma, because the coding point makes that census nonvanishing.
The independent \(B,C\) distribution was used only to condition on its actual deterministic inverse-product shift and guards.
No further gain from averaging that distribution has been proved or excluded.
The existing [fixed-candidate exponential bound](Yin_L_F0066_v13.md#fixed-candidate-estimate) remains valid at its exact scope, and no full structured-family or mean-background estimate is added here.
