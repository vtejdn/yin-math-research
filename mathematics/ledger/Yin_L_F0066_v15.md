## What the accepted profile count actually needs

The [C194v1 count](../claims/Yin_C_F0194_v1.md#structured-vector-counting), equations (26)--(32), exposes the original symmetric matrix in a triangular order.
The coefficients of each unexposed row are the corresponding long tail of the same deterministic candidate u.
Its probability product and inverse-theorem compression therefore involve the same almost-n coordinates.
At a profile value \(\rho\), the inverse theorem gives a progression of size at most
\[
 C\rho^{-1}N^{-1/2+\varepsilon}
\]
for nearly all coordinates in the corresponding long block.
The \(\rho^{-1}\) factor in the count cancels its row-probability factor, leaving a negative multiple of \(N\log N\).
The nondegenerate reservoir in that proof has size \(N^{1-\varepsilon}\), not \(O(\log N)\).
The count already permits nonsymmetric deterministic offsets, but it requires the actual original-row near-kernel constraints; L66v12 identifies why those constraints are unavailable for its coupled system.

For a fixed logarithmic reservoir I, any ordinary or joint concentration profile of
\[
 (\alpha^T\epsilon,t^T\epsilon),\qquad \alpha=u_I,
\]
initially sees only the r coefficient pairs \((\alpha_\ell,t_\ell)\).
It does not see the \(p=n-r\) coordinates \(w=u_J\) that supply the leading \(N^{C_*N}\) candidate entropy.
Merely stratifying the short pairs and retaining the old coarse count for w therefore does not reproduce the cancellation in C194v1.
The next calculation keeps w in its actual center rather than replacing that center by a supremum.

## The complete D floor controls all but at most 2r center directions

Keep the [L66v14 block notation](Yin_L_F0066_v14.md#simultaneous-original-exposure):
\[
 S=\begin{pmatrix}H&Y^T\\Y&S_0\end{pmatrix},\quad
 K=\begin{pmatrix}K_I&k^T\\-k&K_0\end{pmatrix},\quad
 u=\binom\alpha w,\quad v=\binom t{v_J},
 \quad G=S_0^{-1},\quad A=K_0G.
\]
Here \(n=N-1\), \(r=|I|=O(\log N)\), \(p=n-r\), and
\[
 D=S+K
 =\begin{pmatrix}H+K_I&Y^T+k^T\\Y-k&D_0\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad D_0:=S_0+K_0.
\]
The original conditioning \(\mathcal H_I\) fixes the actual full B,C and all original X coordinates internal to I and J.
It leaves \(Y=N^{-1/2}V+Y_0\), with the pr entries of V independent copies of the original first-input real law.
In particular \(D_0,S_0,K_0,k,H\) are \(\mathcal H_I\)-measurable; Y is not.

Use the existing minor and norm guards
\[
 \|S_0\|\le N^{k_0},\quad \|G\|\le N^{L_0},\quad
 \|K\|\le N^{k_0},\quad \|S\|\le N^{k_0}.
\]
The full event
\[
 s_{\min}(D)>N^{-J}=:\delta
 \tag{CG-full-D-event}
\]
is intersected with the event to be estimated.
It is never inserted into \(\mathcal H_I\); doing so would alter the original cross-block law.

For every \(x\in\ker(Y^T+k^T)\),
\[
 \|D_0x\|
 =\left\|D\binom0x\right\|
 \ge\delta\|x\|
\]
on (CG-full-D-event).
This kernel has dimension at least \(p-r\).
By the singular-value variational characterization, \(D_0\) has at most r singular values below \(\delta\).
This is a necessary condition on the conditioned background whenever (CG-full-D-event) occurs, even though the displayed witnessing subspace can depend on Y.
If the condition fails for the fixed \(D_0\), the entire full-D event is empty for that conditioning.
No separate probability estimate for \(s_{\min}(D_0)\), and no invertibility of \(D_0\), is asserted.

Define the actual center operator
\[
 T_0:=S_0-K_0G K_0
      =D_0G D_0^T.
 \tag{CG-center-operator}
\]
The equality follows by expanding
\((S_0+K_0)G(S_0-K_0)\), using \(S_0^T=S_0\) and \(K_0^T=-K_0\).
The singular values of \(D_0^T\) and \(D_0\) agree.
Choose a right singular subspace for \(D_0^T\) of dimension at least \(p-r\) on which its lower bound is \(\delta\).
Requiring \(GD_0^Tx\) to lie in a good right singular subspace for \(D_0\) imposes at most another r linear conditions.
On their intersection,
\[
 \|T_0x\|
 \ge\delta\|GD_0^Tx\|
 \ge\delta N^{-k_0}\|D_0^Tx\|
 \ge\tau\|x\|,\qquad
 \tau:=N^{-2J-k_0}.
 \tag{CG-center-floor}
\]
Consequently at least \(p-2r\) singular values of \(T_0\) are at least \(\tau\).
The selected singular subspaces of \(D_0,D_0^T,T_0\) depend only on the conditioned background; they do not use Y.
Also
\[
 \|T_0\|\le \|S_0\|+\|K_0\|^2\|G\|
 \le2N^{2k_0+L_0}.
\]
Thus the complete D floor gives a polynomially controlled high-dimensional part of the actual center map, without falsely giving a floor for every principal minor.

Fix a retained omission set \(E_J\subset J\), \(e:=|E_J|\le C_0\), and let \(\Pi:J\to J\setminus E_J\) denote coordinate restriction.
Put \(B_E:=\Pi T_0\).
Intersect a good domain of \(T_0\) with \(\ker(\Pi_{E_J}T_0)\); this loses at most e dimensions.
It follows that \(B_E\) has at least \(p-2r-e\) singular values at least \(\tau\).
Let \(R_E\) be its pseudoinverse truncated to singular values at least \(\tau\), and put
\[
 P_E:=R_EB_E,\qquad L_E:=\ker P_E.
\]
Then \(P_E\) is an orthogonal projection, with
\[
 \|R_E\|\le N^{2J+k_0},\qquad \dim L_E\le2r+e.
 \tag{CG-measurable-inverse}
\]
Here \(R_E,P_E,L_E\) are all \(\mathcal H_I\)-measurable after the deterministic omission set is fixed.
One can choose their singular bases measurably; no random cross-block candidate is fixed by this choice.
The implication from (CG-full-D-event) to these bounds allows an upper estimate to discard any conditioned background on which they fail.

## Exact centers produce a graph of the original independent rectangle

The exact companion relation remains
\[
 v_J=G(-k\alpha+K_0w)-GYt,
\]
\[
 \mathcal R_Y(\alpha,t,w)
 :=(H-Y^TGY)t-K_I\alpha-k^Tw
       +Y^TG(-k\alpha+K_0w)=0.
 \tag{CG-actual-Schur}
\]
The retained equations are exactly
\[
 (Su-Kv)_J
 =T_0w+Y\alpha+A\,Yt+A k\alpha+kt.
 \tag{CG-actual-center}
\]
No term in this expression is an arbitrary independently chosen center.
The exposed equations are
\[
 \mathcal E_Y(\alpha,t,w)
 :=H\alpha-k^TG(-k\alpha+K_0w)
       +Y^Tw-K_It+k^TGYt.
 \tag{CG-actual-exposed}
\]
They must be small on all but the original bounded number of omitted I rows.
The Schur equation is exact on every I row.

Suppose the retained coordinate residuals outside \(E_J\) have modulus at most
\(\delta_N:=N^{-a+Q_0}\), where \(Q_0=2k_0+L\) is fixed before a.
Write their vector as \(\eta\), so \(\|\eta\|\le\sqrt p\,\delta_N\).
Applying \(R_E\) to (CG-actual-center) gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 w&=w_*(Y;\alpha,t,\ell)+e_w,\\
 w_*(Y;\alpha,t,\ell)
 &:=-R_E\Pi(Y\alpha+A\,Yt+A k\alpha+kt)+\ell,\\
 \ell&:=(I-P_E)w\in L_E,\qquad
 e_w:=R_E\eta.
 \end{split}
 \tag{CG-random-graph}
\]
For \(1/2\le\|u\|\le2\), \(\|\alpha\|,\|\ell\|\le2\), and the existing companion bound gives \(\|t\|\le2N^{L+k_0}\).
The residual obeys
\[
 \|e_w\|\le N^{-a+Q_0+2J+k_0+1/2}.
 \tag{CG-graph-error}
\]
The parameter space \((\alpha,t,\ell)\) has complex dimension at most \(4r+e\).
Its linear operators and the basis for \(L_E\) are fixed by \(\mathcal H_I\), but its graph value \(w_*\) depends on V.

For fixed parameters this dependence is explicit:
\[
 w_*
 =h_{\alpha,t,\ell}
 -N^{-1/2}\sum_{\nu=1}^r
       R_E\Pi(\alpha_\nu I+t_\nu A)V_{\cdot\nu},
 \tag{CG-original-column-image}
\]
where h includes the actual \(Y_0,A k\alpha,kt,\ell\) terms.
The r columns of V are independent original-law vectors.
This representation does not make v independent or condition away any of those vectors.

On the displayed guards, the derivatives of \(w_*\) in \(\alpha,t\) have norm at most \(2N^{2J+3k_0+L_0}\).
A sufficiently fine complex parameter grid of mesh \(N^{-a-C}\), with fixed C chosen after these exponents, changes \(w_*\) by at most \(N^{-a}\).
Its cardinality is
\[
 N^{O((a+1)(r+C_0))}
 =\exp(O_a((\log N)^2)).
 \tag{CG-parameter-count}
\]
This is a subexponential family of random affine graphs, not a subexponential family of deterministic vectors.
Applying [L66v13's fixed-candidate estimate](Yin_L_F0066_v13.md#fixed-candidate-estimate) to the output of one of these graphs would reverse its required independence order.

The derivatives of both \(\mathcal R_Y\) and \(\mathcal E_Y\) in \(\alpha,t,w\) have polynomial bounds \(O(N^{2k_0+L_0})\) on the same guards.
Thus substituting the graph and rounding parameters gives necessary approximate forms of (CG-actual-Schur) and (CG-actual-exposed), with tolerance \(N^{-a+C_{\rm rel}}\) for a fixed exponent \(C_{\rm rel}\).
For example one may take
\[
 C_{\rm graph}:=Q_0+2J+k_0+2,\qquad
 C_{\rm rel}:=Q_0+2J+3k_0+L_0+5.
\]
The graph is within \(N^{-a+C_{\rm graph}}\) of w, and the original exposed residual \(N^{-a+Q_0}\) is absorbed by the latter tolerance.
These necessary conditions retain the actual centers and the actual Schur relation; they are not replaced by center suprema.

## What the original product law supplies, and the still missing tube estimate

The column image has substantial rank in a nondegenerate reservoir.
If \(|\alpha_\nu|\ge(2\sqrt n)^{-1}\) for some \(\nu\), the [L66v13 half-rank calculation](Yin_L_F0066_v13.md#uniform-half-rank-bound), with scalar parameter \(t_\nu\), gives at least \(\lfloor(p+1)/2\rfloor\) singular values of \(\alpha_\nu I+t_\nu A\) at least
\[
 |\alpha_\nu|N^{-L_0-k_0}.
\]
The map \(R_E\Pi\) loses at most \(2r+e\) directions.
Every one of its nonzero singular values is at least \(\|B_E\|^{-1}\ge\frac12N^{-2k_0-L_0}\).
Hence the \(\nu\)-th column coefficient in (CG-original-column-image) has at least
\(\lfloor(p+1)/2\rfloor-2r-e\) singular values at least
\[
 \frac{|\alpha_\nu|}{2\sqrt N}N^{-2L_0-3k_0}.
 \tag{CG-graph-rank}
\]
To justify the loss, project \(\alpha_\nu I+t_\nu A\) away from the kernel of \(R_E\Pi\); the removed operator has rank at most \(2r+e\), then use the lower bound on the remaining singular values of \(R_E\Pi\).
This uses the original independent column, not an independent companion.

A largest coordinate can be included by choosing among a predetermined family of at most n reservoirs, each of size r and containing its designated index.
The symmetric-minor guard follows from C188v2 at failure power \(q+2\), with the same \(p/N\) normalization adjustment as L66v14, and is paid once after this polynomial union.
No union over all \(\binom nr\) reservoirs is required or claimed.
The full D floor and norm guards remain intersections, not new conditioning.

The rank calculation supports the existing type of exponential bound for an individual target or suitable fixed low-dimensional affine target.
It does not bound membership in \(Q^p\) for a progression Q of polynomial size.
Even an independent sign vector under the identity map lies surely in \(\{-1,0,1\}^p\), despite full rank.
This elementary observation only identifies why rank and the original product law alone do not imply progression-tube avoidance.
It is not an example with the actual \(R_E\), actual centers and Schur equations, and supplies no counterexample to the present probability goal.

## A short row-pair profile does not yet supply that estimate

The information limitation can be checked without conditioning on either member of the pair.
Take the original Rademacher pilot and
\[
 r=2\left\lfloor\frac{\log_2N}{4}\right\rfloor.
\]
For every deterministic pair of complex coefficient vectors \((\alpha,t)\), the joint variable
\((\alpha^T\epsilon,t^T\epsilon)\in\mathbb C^2\) has at most \(2^r\) atoms.
Its largest atom is therefore at least
\[
 2^{-r}\ge N^{-1/2}.
 \tag{CG-short-pair-atom}
\]
In particular a joint concentration lower bound \(N^{-B_*}\), with \(B_*>1\), imposes no restriction at all on such pairs.
This is a support-size statement for the original fixed sign law.
It neither says that the full event has that probability nor shows that any chosen t solves the actual Schur relation.

The accepted [Ngu12a Theorem 1.3 interface](../audits/literature/Ngu12a/Yin_U001.md#real-scalar-geometric-inverse) fixes its exponent B and ambient dimension before the coefficient-list length tends to infinity.
For the joint pair its ambient real dimension is four, but its list length is r.
For a nonzero pair array, divide the coefficients by \((\sum_\nu|\alpha_\nu|^2+|t_\nu|^2)^{1/2}\) and rescale the radius by the same factor to meet the source normalization; its concentration probability is unchanged.
Converting a threshold \(N^{-B_*}\) to \(r^{-B_{\rm src}}\) gives
\[
 B_{\rm src}=\frac{B_*\log N}{\log r}\longrightarrow\infty.
\]
Thus this application does not retain the source's fixed rank, fixed exceptional-fraction constants or the polynomial descriptor count required by the long-tail argument.
The existing accepted interface supplies no uniform bounds in this diverging parameter.
At a genuinely polynomial-in-r profile threshold the source can give structure for the r coefficient pairs, but those pairs are already covered by the subexponential parameter grid.
Such structure alone does not force the \(p\) graph coordinates into fewer choices.

There is a separate scale issue even if one optimistically restores an ordinary reservoir anti-concentration factor.
For equal nonzero coefficients and even r, at a radius smaller than the modulus of their common coefficient the ordinary sign-sum concentration equals
\[
 2^{-r}\binom r{r/2}\asymp r^{-1/2}.
\]
This reuses only the [already inspected Stirling application](Yin_L_F0066_v11.md#complete-atom-and-matching).
A valid product of p such factors, if available for the actual event, would have size
\[
 \exp\{-\tfrac12p\log r+O(p)\}
 =\exp\{-\Theta(N\log\log N)\}
\]
for logarithmic r.
It does not offset the old \(N^{C_*N}\) candidate count.
This is an exact marginal benchmark and an entropy comparison, not a multiplication claim for the coupled system and not a lower bound on its true probability.
A successful profile stratification must also compress the remaining graph-coordinate possibilities or exploit the actual Schur and center constraints; an improved marginal bound alone leaves the leading count untouched.

The useful new interface is therefore (CG-random-graph), with its measurable operators, controlled bad-space dimension and exact original-column law.
The first unproved step is the feasible progression-tube estimate (CG-feasible-graph-tube), beyond fixed-target rank concentration.
The accepted original-row profile count does not already supply that estimate, and this test proves no full structured-family bound or actual-background counterexample.
The complete mean-background target and the full F0012 contract remain open.
