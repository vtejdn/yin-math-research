# A repeated interior determinant with an exterior pseudospectrum on the low-product branch

The [conditional determinant in D42](../drafts/Yin_D_F0042.md#repeated-interior) factors the characteristic polynomial into an exterior determinant, a cross-block Jacobian and a repeated-interior determinant.
On 2026-11-13 an agent for Yin checked the following support example in ordinary exploration.
On a fixed nonreal disk the repeated determinant has a pointwise exponential floor for every real symmetric interior, yet a set of interiors of conditional probability tending to one has an order-one original defect at parameters in the [unstructured complement](../claims/Yin_C_F0186_v29.md#statement) and outside the [high-product event](../claims/Yin_C_F0194_v35.md#statement).
The example tests uniform conditioning on exterior data and retains the dependence of the near-mode on the sampled interior.

Fix the structured-class constants \(a,b,\gamma>0,\ 0\le\alpha<1,\ \gamma\le1\), and the high-product constants \(C_1>0\) and sufficiently large \(s\).
Use independent standard real Gaussian raw entries on and above the diagonal of each color, with the usual normalization \(1/\sqrt N\).
Choose a small constant \(\lambda>0\) as specified below, \(k=\lfloor\lambda N\rfloor\), \(n=N-2k\), and use coordinates \(\mathbb R^n\oplus\mathbb R^k\oplus\mathbb R^k\).
Take the middle block as the ordered set \(I\) and require \(2\lambda<\gamma/2\).
Choose orthonormal real \(d_1,d_2\) supported on the last \(\lfloor k/10\rfloor\) sites of \(I\).
Write \(S\) for the upper shift of size \(n\), \(R\) for the reversal matrix of size \(n\), and \(L=e_1d_1^T+e_2d_2^T\).
Consider the exterior with matrices
\[
 B=\begin{pmatrix}R&0&0\\0&0&2I_k\\0&2I_k&0\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 A=\begin{pmatrix}
 SR&L&-RL\\
 L^T&U&0\\
 -L^TR&0&0
 \end{pmatrix},
 \qquad U=A_{II}.
\]
The final physical matrices will be obtained by a common deterministic orthogonal rotation in the first \(n\) coordinates.
All the displayed matrices are real symmetric since \(S R=R S^T\).
The Gaussian product measure has full support on the prescribed exterior entries, both before and after this rotation.
No bound independent of \(N\) is asserted for the raw values of the prescribed exterior coordinates.

<a id="interior-floor"></a>
## The exterior chain and the repeated determinant

Before rotation, direct multiplication gives
\[
 M=AB+2BA=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 S+2S^T&0&0\\
 -3L^TR&0&2U\\
 3L^T&4U&0
 \end{pmatrix}.
\]
In the notation of D42, \(B_{I^cI}\) has all \(k\) singular values equal to two, \(H=\ker X\) is the chain space, and \(C_H=S+2S^T\).
Both \(C_H\) and the interior operator \(M_s=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&2U\\4U&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)\) are similar to real symmetric matrices by diagonal similarities.
For \(M_s\) one such similarity has condition number \(\sqrt2\), so
\(\|(M_s-z)^{-1}\|\le\sqrt2/|\operatorname{Im}z|\) for every real symmetric \(U\).
For a nonreal \(z\), the homogeneous chain equation has only the zero solution.
On the two spectator blocks \(X(y,v)=(y,2v)\) and the map \(Z\) of D42 sends \((f,g)\) to \((g,4f)\).
The exterior \(W\) and its repeated determinant therefore satisfy
\[
 W(z)=
 \begin{pmatrix}0&zI_k/8\\zI_k&0\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 \det(\operatorname{diag}(U,U)-W(z))
   =\prod_{j=1}^k(\mu_j(U)^2-z^2/8).
\]
Each factor has modulus at least \(|\operatorname{Im}z|^2/8\), by factoring over \(\sqrt8\,\mu_j(U)\) and its negative.
In particular the conditional lower tail below \((\min_{z\in\Omega}|\operatorname{Im}z|^2/8)^k\) is empty for every \(z\) on the closed disk \(\Omega=\{|z-i/2|\le1/20\}\).

For the chain let \(x_n(z)=1\), \(x_{n-1}(z)=z/2\), and define the preceding coordinates backwards by \(x_{i-1}=(zx_i-x_{i+1})/2\).
The chain residual \((C_H-z)x\) vanishes in rows \(2,\ldots,n\).
Its first entry is bounded in modulus by \(Cq^n\) for a fixed \(q<1\), uniformly for \(z\in\Omega\).
Indeed the scalar generating function of \(x_{n-j}\) is \(1/(1-(z/2)t+t^2/2)\).
On the larger disk \(|z|\le 2/3\), choose \(T>1\) with \(T/3+T^2/2<1\).
The power series on \(|t|=T\) has bounded modulus, so its coefficients are bounded by \(CT^{-j}\).
The same series bound and differentiation in \(z\) give a uniform bound for \(\|x\|+\|\partial_zx\|\) on \(\Omega\).
The last two coordinates give a uniform lower bound on the least singular value of the real two-column matrix \([\operatorname{Re}x,\operatorname{Im}x]\), since their two-by-two minor has determinant of magnitude \(|\operatorname{Im}z|/2\).

Write \(Kx=(-3L^TRx,3L^Tx)\) for the coupling to the spectator blocks.
The full vector
\[
 h_0(z,U)=(x(z),-(M_s-z)^{-1}Kx(z))
\]
has norm between one and a constant and has full residual at most \(Cq^n\), uniformly on \(\Omega\) for all \(U\).
The spectrum of \(M\) is real by its triangular form, so the finite-eigenvalue convention in [the definition of \(D_N\)](../claims/Yin_C_F0186_v27.md#clipping-definition) is inactive.
Its least singular value gives
\[
 D_N(z)\ \ge\ \frac{n\log(1/q)-\log C-L_N}{N}\ \ge\ c>0
 \qquad (z\in\Omega)
\]
for large \(N\), since \(L_N=\exp\sqrt{\log(N+1)}=o(N)\).

The same vector describes every bounded-residual direction quantitatively.
For \(h=(v,w)\) of unit norm with \(\|(M-z)h\|\le u\), put \(c_0=v_n\).
On deleting row one and column \(n\), the chain matrix \(C_H-z\) gives \(2I-zS_{n-1}+S_{n-1}^2\), which has least singular value at least \(1-|z|\).
It follows from the last \(n-1\) rows that \(\|v-c_0x\|\le C u\).
The spectator resolvent bound then gives \(\|h-c_0h_0\|\le C'u\), uniformly in \(U\) and \(z\).
For \(u=o(1)\), these estimates give \(c_*\le |c_0|\le1\) with a fixed \(c_*>0\).

## Removing all structured and high-product modes

Choose a deterministic orthogonal \(O\) in the chain coordinates as follows.
For every \(z\in\Omega\), every set of \(\ell=\lceil\gamma N/2\rceil\) chain sites and every partition of these sites into at most \(m=\lfloor N^\alpha\rfloor\) classes, require the restriction of \(Ox(z)\) to have Euclidean distance greater than \(K_0/\sqrt N\) from the space of complex arrays constant on the classes.
Here \(K_0\) is a fixed number larger than \((a+2C'b)/c_*+2\), allowing the same exclusion up to residual \(2b/\sqrt N\).

To see existence, use a polynomial-size \(N^{-1}\)-mesh in \(\Omega\), and initially require the larger distance \(2K_0/\sqrt N\) on the mesh.
The Lipschitz bound for \(x\) transfers the desired inequality to the whole disk.
At fixed \(z\) write the real two-column matrix of \(x(z)\) as \(V_z D_z\), where \(V_z\) has orthonormal columns and both singular values of \(D_z\) are bounded above and below by fixed positive numbers.
For Haar \(O\), the distribution of \(O V_z\) is that of the orthonormal frame \(G(G^TG)^{-1/2}\) for an \(n\)-by-two matrix \(G\) of independent standard real Gaussians, by orthogonal invariance.
With probability \(1-e^{-cN}\), \(\|G\|\le C\sqrt N\), by an exponential moment for its squared Frobenius norm.
A partition failing the required distance on this event has \(\|P G_T\|_{\rm HS}\le C' K_0\), where \(T\) denotes the selected sites and \(P\) projects orthogonally off the arrays constant on the classes.
For a specified partition of \(T\) into \(m'\le m\) nonempty classes, the squared norm has the chi-square law on \(2(\ell-m')\) degrees of freedom, by Gaussian orthogonal invariance.
Its lower-tail probability is at most \((C_{K_0}/(\ell-m))^{\ell-m}\) for large \(N\), by bounding the standard Gaussian density by its supremum on the corresponding fixed-radius ball and using its even-dimensional volume.
There are at most \(2^n\) site subsets and at most \(m^\ell\) labelled partitions for each subset.
The union bound tends to zero at rate \(\exp(-[(1-\alpha)\ell-o(N)]\log N)\), in addition to the one norm exception per mesh point.
This proves existence of one \(O\) for all \(z\) simultaneously.

Conjugate both colors by \(\operatorname{diag}(O,I_k,I_k)\), which preserves the computed \(W\), the bound for \(D_N\) and the interior coordinate arrays.
For a candidate residual at most \(b/\sqrt N\), its chain coordinates are within \(C'b/\sqrt N\) of \(c_0 Ox(z)\).
A structured witness on \(\lceil\gamma N\rceil\) physical sites would supply a partition on at least \(\ell\) chain sites and approximate \(Ox(z)\) there within \((a+C'b)/(c_*\sqrt N)\).
The chosen rotation excludes every such witness uniformly for \(z\in\Omega\) and every real symmetric \(U\).

It remains to check the low-product condition for all fine near-modes.
When \(U=0\), the \(I\) coordinates of \(h_0\) are
\[
 -3z^{-1}L^TRx=-3z^{-1}(d_1+(z/2)d_2).
\]
The two real columns of this array on the reservoir supporting \(d_1,d_2\) have their least singular value bounded below by a fixed constant on \(\Omega\), since \(z\) is nonreal.
The spectator resolvent identity gives \(\|h_0(z,U)-h_0(z,0)\|\le C_\Omega\|U\|\), with the same bound after chain rotation.
Choose \(\eta>0\) sufficiently small that the reservoir singular bound persists for \(\|U\|\le\eta\), also after multiplication by any \(c_0\) with \(|c_0|\ge c_*\) and addition of an \(o(1)\) error.
Every unit \(h\) with residual at most \(2N^{-(s+10)}\) has this conclusion by the near-line estimate.
For the independent standard Gaussian test variables in C194v35, the complex first component \(\sum_{j=i}^k\xi_j h_j\) has a two-dimensional real Gaussian density bounded above by a fixed constant on every tail \(i\le p=k-\lfloor k/10\rfloor\), since the tail contains this reservoir.
Its concentration at radius \(t\) is at most \(C t^2\), which bounds the joint concentration of the two complex channels at the same radius.
Thus every fine near-mode satisfies \(\pi(h,N^{-s})\le(CN^{-2s})^p<N^{-C_1 k}\) when \(2s(9/10)>C_1\) and \(N\) is large.
This proves the complement of the actual high-product existence event.

Finally take \(\lambda\) sufficiently small depending on \(\eta,\gamma\) and the original block fraction permitted in C194v35.
For a fixed real unit vector \(v\), the conditional variable \(v^TUv\) is real Gaussian with variance at most \(2/N\), using the triangular independent entries including the diagonal.
A real \(1/4\)-net has size at most \(9^k\) by comparison of ball volumes and controls \(\|U\|\) by twice the maximum absolute quadratic form on the net.
Thus the conditional probability of \(\|U\|>\eta\) is at most \(2\cdot9^k\exp(-N\eta^2/16)\le2e^{-cN}\).
The interior is the same sample in both diagonal blocks throughout.

## Scope of the conditional failure

At the prescribed exterior, with conditional probability at least \(1-2e^{-cN}\), the integral of \(D_N\) over \(\Omega\cap F_N^c\cap H_N^c\) is at least \(c|\Omega|\).
The conclusion also occurs on exterior neighborhoods of positive probability at each sufficiently large fixed \(N\).
Indeed the least singular value of the full matrix on the whole disk is small uniformly on the compact set of \((z,U)\) with \(z\in\Omega,\ \|U\|\le\eta\).
Choose the perturbation for this \(N\) sufficiently small to preserve an exponential upper bound on it and the uniform residual comparisons for the old matrices, using thresholds \(2b/\sqrt N\) and \(2N^{-(s+10)}\).
The strict partial-alphabet and covariance margins established above then preserve the two exclusion events, with the new \(h\)-coordinate test using only its first complex component.
Only a finite set of eigenvalue parameters may need to be omitted from the integral.
The pole-free exterior compression, the cross rank and the determinant floor on this compact \((z,U)\) set also persist with a factor \(1/2\) in the determinant floor by continuity in fixed dimension.
For this last assertion one can choose continuous orthonormal bases for the moving kernel and its complement near the fixed full-rank observation map by projecting the original bases and orthonormalizing.
The Gaussian exterior law charges each such open neighborhood.

This is a support test for a conditional estimate uniform in exterior data, including exterior positions with a well-conditioned cross block and an entire affine \(W(z)\).
For the stated Gaussian law these neighborhoods can be chosen with probability at most \(e^{-cN}\), since even a single prescribed entry of \(B\) between spectator blocks has normalized value two.
No lower estimate on the neighborhood probabilities across dimensions is obtained here.
The [summable planar target for the Wigner ensemble](../claims/Yin_C_F0186_v29.md#statement) averages over the exterior as well as over \(U\).
It remains possible for typical exterior data or an independent exterior estimate to rule out this mechanism.
The construction and the earlier [fixed-color support covering obstruction](Yin_L_F0066_v22.md#fixed-color-low-product) have different conclusions, since here the same exterior is fixed for a high-conditional-probability family of interiors on a nonreal disk.
