\title[On conjugate variables and dual systems]{On conjugate variables and dual systems}
\date{}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
For a finite tuple of bounded normal generators of a finite tracial von Neumann algebra, we prove that actual conjugate variables on the represented star algebra are equivalent to strong dual systems whose operators and adjoints are defined on $\M\Omega$.
Both slices of each coordinate derivation satisfy a universal operator-norm-to-$L^2$ estimate, which gives these domains for the original canonical closures.
For general bounded generators, the conjugate-variable and full-domain dual hypotheses are incomparable, although either implies maximal $\Delta$.
\end{abstract}
