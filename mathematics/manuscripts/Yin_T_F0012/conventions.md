# Writing Conventions

This English article follows Yin's [author-wide choices](../../../profiles/Yin/writing/readme.md) and [free-probability choices](../../../profiles/Yin/writing/free_probability.md), including the default audience and MSY23 as the primary style model.
The [MSY23 observations](../../audits/literature/MSY23/readme.md#presentation-observations) guide statement placement and proof transitions.
The paper uses its own prose.

The model uses the unnormalized trace `\Tr` and normalized trace `\tr_N`.
Hermitization uses squared singular values, so the potential carries a factor of one half in front of the logarithmic integral.
The [citation map](citation.md) identifies the corresponding source conventions.
