# Paper Organization

1. **Introduction.** Set the normalization, define affine and monic pencils, and state the Brown-measure, monic-pencil and affine determinant results.
2. **Distance to singular pencils.** Compress an approximate kernel of an affine pencil and lift the repaired equation by Hermitian perturbations.
3. **Gaussian tubes and the least singular value.** Average the uniform-ball tube bound in Gaussian coordinates and prove the summable monic estimate.
4. **Normalized determinants of affine pencils.** Use rank regularity, a moving norm cutoff and Gaussian concentration to prove the affine limits, including convergence in expectation.
5. **From the pencil estimate to Brown measure.** Identify the fixed-shift potential through linearization and scalar logarithmic truncation, then use Fubini and local \(L^1\) convergence to obtain the almost-sure weak limit.
