# Paper Organization

1. **Introduction.** Set the normalization, recall Brown measure, state the convergence theorem and the least-singular-value estimate, and explain their connection.
2. **Distance to singular pencils.** Compress an approximate kernel and its residual, apply a polynomial distance inequality, and lift the resulting exact equation by Hermitian perturbations.
3. **Gaussian tubes and the least singular value.** Extend the uniform-ball bound to the determinant zero set, pass to Gaussian coordinates, and prove the pencil estimate.
4. **From the pencil estimate to Brown measure.** Use linearization to obtain the lower bound for each fixed shift, control the logarithmic tails using singular-value counts, and pass from potentials to weak convergence.
