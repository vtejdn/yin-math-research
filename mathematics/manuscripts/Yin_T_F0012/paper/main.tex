\title[Brown measure convergence for GUE polynomials]{Brown measure convergence for polynomials in independent GUE matrices}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
We prove that the empirical eigenvalue distribution of any fixed scalar polynomial in independent normalized GUE matrices converges weakly almost surely to the Brown measure of the same polynomial in free semicircular elements.
The result holds for every coupling across matrix sizes.
For affine matrix pencils in the GUE inputs, we also prove almost-sure convergence of normalized logarithmic determinants, with convergence in $L^1$ when the limiting logarithm is finite.
\end{abstract}
