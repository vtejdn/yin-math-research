## Mechanism and obstruction

The [F0001 strategy comparison](../ledger/Yin_L_A0069_v1.md#what-transfers-from-the-f-tasks) selects a direct use of the normal-generator rationality criterion on a cyclic edge and a complementary [unitary deformation of the original regular operator](Yin_S_A0012.md#route) for overlapping coefficient groups.

For [Yin's inverse layers](../audits/literature/Yin20/Yin_U002.md#61-definition-iv55-and-all-parts-of-remark-iv56), write \(P_j(G)\) for scalar injectivity on all nonzero members of layer \(D_j(G)\).
