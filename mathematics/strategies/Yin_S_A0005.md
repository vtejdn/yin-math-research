## Mechanism and exact relationship to A0001

The [atom reformulation](../ledger/Yin_L_A0012_v1.md#matrix-atoms-an-exact-formulation-without-rational-inverses) converts the Task into all-size spectral atom mass quantization.
Its equivalent scalar rational formulation alone would reproduce route four's zero-or-unit problem.
This route instead introduces actual conditional independence: for \(G=G_1*_H G_2\), the vertex algebras are free over \(B:=\mathcal N(H)\) for the canonical expectation.

The first intended class has \(H\cong\mathbb Z\) and vertex groups whose relevant matrix ranks are independently known.
For a selfadjoint group-ring pencil \(Y=Y_1+Y_2\), use the operator-valued subordination limits to transfer its kernel trace to vertex kernels.
The [BBL21 interface](../audits/literature/BBL21/Yin_U001.md#general-base-algebra-and-the-a0001-strategy-interface) already supplies an exact trace identity for general base algebras under bounded invertibility of the expected kernel.
The [strategy feasibility record](../ledger/Yin_L_A0013_v1.md#source-of-independent-information) fixes the matrix normalization, conditional implication and source scope.

This is an analytic alternative to [S_A0002's](Yin_S_A0002.md) Tor and universal-localization approach, with possible shared test groups.
It does not supersede that route.
It differs from [S_A0004](Yin_S_A0004.md) by using atom-transfer identities instead of independently certified scalar pivots, and from [S_A0003](Yin_S_A0003.md) by not requiring integer-rank approximating groups.
A successful result would cover the demonstrated amalgamation class; no reduction of every torsion-free group to that class is assumed.
The [Cauchy-transform synthesis](../ledger/Yin_L_A0015_v1.md) supplies exact boundary observables and compares the distinct geometric-index and return-word routes without changing this route's amalgamated transfer mechanism.

## First calibration and its effect

The [completed first tests](../ledger/Yin_L_A0014_v1.md) use the actual central cyclic amalgam \(\mathbb Z^2*_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Z^2\cong\mathbb Z\times F_2\).
A two-vertex polynomial matrix has both expected-kernel supports equal to the identity but no bounded inverse, so both support cuts are ineffective.
Its [cyclic affiliated normalization](../ledger/Yin_L_A0014_v1.md#an-exact-affiliated-normalization-and-its-limits) repairs the gap, while its [affine realization](../ledger/Yin_L_A0014_v1.md#affine-realization-changes-the-support-behavior) admits two integer support corrections.
The failure is therefore sensitive to the chosen realization and does not exclude all normalization methods.

An [additive two-vertex positive model](../ledger/Yin_L_A0014_v1.md#an-additive-two-vertex-model-with-an-invertible-expected-kernel) has kernel dimension one and expected kernel bounded below by \(I_3/114\).
Its expected kernel [leaves the cyclic rational field](../ledger/Yin_L_A0014_v1.md#the-expected-kernel-leaves-the-cyclic-rational-field), so rationality of the whole conditional expectation is not an admissible universal target.
Nevertheless, the [actual subordination boundary values vanish](../ledger/Yin_L_A0014_v1.md#the-actual-subordination-boundary-values-are-nevertheless-zero).
Positivity at the zero atom forces this whenever the expected kernel is invertible, so further positive zero-atom examples cannot test nontrivial boundary-coefficient behavior.
These are exact calibrations, not new group coverage or a general trace-transfer theorem.

## Nonzero boundaries and finite kernel witnesses

The [indefinite four-path construction](../claims/Yin_C_A0007_v1.md#an-indefinite-four-path-model) has a simple non-bottom zero atom, uniformly invertible expected kernel and nonzero nonscalar actual boundary values \(b_1=B_0\), \(b_2=-B_0\).
Its unnormalized nullities satisfy \(2+3=4+1\); a finite-tree gauge and cyclic vertex blocks independently explain the example, which supplies no new group class.
The [finite polynomial kernel certificate](../claims/Yin_C_A0007_v3.md#recovery-from-polynomial-kernel-columns) takes \(YW=0\), \(C=E(WW^*)\), and \(N_j=E(Y_jWW^*)\), with no full-kernel spanning assumption.
If \(C\ge\varepsilon I\), then the actual expected kernel is boundedly invertible and the boundary values satisfy \(b_j=N_jC^{-1}\in M_n(\mathbb C(H))\).
Alternatively, bounded invertibility of the expected kernel and full support of \(C\) suffice separately, even when \(C^{-1}\) is unbounded.
The [cyclic polynomial normalization](../claims/Yin_C_A0007_v3.md#polynomial-normalization-over-the-cyclic-edge) now needs only full support of \(C\).
Decomposing the finite witness into coefficients over distinct cosets \(Hu_s\) gives a full-row-rank Laurent matrix; its column-module basis yields \(W=DW_0\) with \(D\) a finite cyclic matrix and independently certified affiliated unit.
The normalized expected Gram has a polynomial right-inverse certificate and a strict positive lower bound, while \(Y'=D^*YD\) remains a finite additive vertex matrix and preserves nullity.
The [vertex-rank reduction](../claims/Yin_C_A0007_v3.md#conditional-reduction-to-the-known-vertex-ranks) proves integer total nullity whenever both vertex groups already satisfy complex Atiyah, including a noncentral cyclic edge, without separately assuming bounded invertibility of the original expected kernel.
This does not assert that the original subordination boundary values exist with the normalized values' properties.
The [rank-deficient corner](../claims/Yin_C_A0007_v3.md#polynomial-normalization-over-the-cyclic-edge) and [two-variable witness obstruction](../claims/Yin_C_A0007_v3.md#why-the-same-polynomial-repair-fails-for-a-two-variable-edge) delimit the repair precisely.
The selected [independent whole-artifact review](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_A0020_v3.md#whole-candidate-finding) verifies the complete analytic interface, original and normalized certificates, all rank conventions and source limits.

The [identity-expectation witness](../claims/Yin_C_A0007_v3.md#an-identity-expectation-kernel-matrix-and-finite-boundary-values) strengthens the normalized recovery.
The polynomial right inverse produces \(X_0\in M_n(\mathbb CG)\) with \(Y'X_0=0\) and \(E(X_0)=I_n\), giving \(b_j'=E(Y_j'X_0)\in M_n(\mathbb CH)\).
Thus the normalized vertex operators are finite group-ring matrices; independently certified integer nullities of those two specific matrices suffice without invoking their entire vertex division closures.
This removes a rational-coefficient verification step while leaving finite-witness existence and full expected support unresolved.

## General-edge normalization and deficient support

The [rectangular normalization](../claims/Yin_C_A0007_v4.md#statement) now removes the cyclic restriction when a displayed finite witness already has full-support expected Gram.
All coset coefficients form a finite edge matrix \(D:n\times N\), and \(Y'=D^*YD\) has the exact correction \(\nu(Y')=\nu(Y)+N-n\).
The universal stacked-coset witness has expected Gram \(I_N\), so the new actual boundary values are finite edge matrices and the two corrected vertex operators remain finite.
No principal-ideal or coefficient-division assumption is used; two independently certified integer vertex nullities suffice.
The [independent whole-candidate finding](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_A0020_v4.md#whole-candidate-finding) checks the general-base analytic interface, rectangular domains, all size corrections and the prior obstruction comparisons.
The earlier same-size cyclic construction remains a smaller realization, while the higher-rank abelian local obstruction does not prevent this genuinely rectangular enlargement.

The [noncentral finite-kernel example](../ledger/Yin_L_A0023_v1.md) has analytic nullity two but polynomial-kernel span of dimension one, and every finite polynomial witness has expected Gram of rank at most three in four coordinates.
Its general stable-shear theorem proves the same support deficit for every finite number of adjoined zero coordinates within the specified additive-preserving triangular family.
The [whole-artifact finding](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_A0027_v1.md#whole-artifact-finding) checks those universal quantifiers and the exact support-cut corrections.
The concrete corner compression succeeds only with a proved extra kernel dimension; it is not a nullity-preserving deletion and is already explained by a certified boundary row.
Thus more kernel columns or zero stabilization within that shear family cannot automatically supply the full-support premise.
