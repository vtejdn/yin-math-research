## Mechanism and exact relationship to A0001

The [atom reformulation](../ledger/Yin_L_A0012_v1.md#matrix-atoms-an-exact-formulation-without-rational-inverses) converts the Task into all-size spectral atom mass quantization.
Its equivalent scalar rational formulation alone would reproduce route four's zero-or-unit problem.
This route instead introduces actual conditional independence: for \(G=G_1*_H G_2\), the vertex algebras are free over \(B:=\mathcal N(H)\) for the canonical expectation.

The first intended class has \(H\cong\mathbb Z\) and vertex groups whose relevant matrix ranks are independently known.
For a selfadjoint group-ring pencil \(Y=Y_1+Y_2\), use the operator-valued subordination limits to transfer its kernel trace to vertex kernels.
The [BBL21 interface](../audits/literature/BBL21/Yin_U001.md#general-base-algebra-and-the-a0001-strategy-interface) already supplies an exact trace identity for general base algebras under bounded invertibility of the expected kernel.
The [strategy feasibility record](../ledger/Yin_L_A0013_v1.md#source-of-independent-information) fixes the matrix normalization, conditional implication and source scope.

This is an analytic alternative to [S_A0002's](Yin_S_A0002.md) Tor and universal-localization approach, with possible shared test groups.
It does not supersede that route.
It differs from [S_A0004](Yin_S_A0004.md) by using atom-transfer identities instead of independently certified scalar pivots, and from [S_A0003](Yin_S_A0003.md) by not requiring integer-rank approximating groups.
A successful result would cover the demonstrated amalgamation class; no reduction of every torsion-free group to that class is assumed.
The [Cauchy-transform synthesis](../ledger/Yin_L_A0015_v1.md) supplies exact boundary observables and compares the distinct geometric-index and return-word routes without changing this route's amalgamated transfer mechanism.

## Inputs and unresolved bridges

Reuse [C_F0181](../claims/Yin_C_F0181_v1.md#graded-subordination-proof) for its proved scalar-free mechanism and precise finite-matrix support bookkeeping.
Its finite-dimensional base is a real restriction of that application.
The diffuse cyclic base requires separate control of expected-kernel degeneration, total support-cut trace corrections, and the algebraic scope of the actual subordination boundary values.

The [explicit \(\mathbb Z^2\) matrix](../ledger/Yin_L_A0013_v1.md#full-support-does-not-supply-a-bounded-inverse-even-for-a-group-ring-matrix) has a full-support expected kernel with no bounded inverse.
It rules out a universal one-step support repair, not every two-sided repair.
Arbitrary base projections also rule out extending integer rank to unrestricted \(B\)-coefficients.
Keep the actual group-ring origin of the pencil and the subordination constraints on its boundary values.

One sufficient implementation would obtain integer total correction and reduce each resulting vertex kernel trace to an independently controlled vertex rational rank.
Constant almost-everywhere fiber ranks and boundary membership in controlled rational rings are possible stronger certificates, not established or necessary hypotheses.
Exact cancellation or a weaker kernel-trace reduction is equally admissible.

## First calibration and its effect

The [completed first tests](../ledger/Yin_L_A0014_v1.md) use the actual central cyclic amalgam \(\mathbb Z^2*_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb Z^2\cong\mathbb Z\times F_2\).
A two-vertex polynomial matrix has both expected-kernel supports equal to the identity but no bounded inverse, so both support cuts are ineffective.
Its [cyclic affiliated normalization](../ledger/Yin_L_A0014_v1.md#an-exact-affiliated-normalization-and-its-limits) repairs the gap, while its [affine realization](../ledger/Yin_L_A0014_v1.md#affine-realization-changes-the-support-behavior) admits two integer support corrections.
The failure is therefore sensitive to the chosen realization and does not exclude all normalization methods.

An [additive two-vertex positive model](../ledger/Yin_L_A0014_v1.md#an-additive-two-vertex-model-with-an-invertible-expected-kernel) has kernel dimension one and expected kernel bounded below by \(I_3/114\).
Its expected kernel [leaves the cyclic rational field](../ledger/Yin_L_A0014_v1.md#the-expected-kernel-leaves-the-cyclic-rational-field), so rationality of the whole conditional expectation is not an admissible universal target.
Nevertheless, the [actual subordination boundary values vanish](../ledger/Yin_L_A0014_v1.md#the-actual-subordination-boundary-values-are-nevertheless-zero).
Positivity at the zero atom forces this whenever the expected kernel is invertible, so further positive zero-atom examples cannot test nontrivial boundary-coefficient behavior.
These are exact calibrations, not new group coverage or a general trace-transfer theorem.

Keep the distinction between central-base calibration and noncentral cyclic-edge coverage.
An actual noncentral test must verify the embeddings and the needed conditional distributions, rather than import the central Fourier calculation.

Do not round small trace errors to integers without an independent discrete constraint.
