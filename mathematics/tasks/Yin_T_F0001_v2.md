## Main Question and Exact Starting Assumptions

Let \((M,\tau)\) be a finite von Neumann algebra with faithful normal probability trace, \(H=L^2(M,\tau)\), \(\Omega=1\), and \(P_\Omega\) the vacuum projection.
Let \(X=(X_1,\ldots,X_d)\) be a finite bounded normal tuple generating \(M\).
No mutual commutativity or free independence is assumed.
Distinguish

\[
\mathcal P_X=\mathbb C\langle X,X^*\rangle,
\qquad A_X=\mathbb C\langle X\rangle,
\qquad F_X=\operatorname{Div}_{L^0(M)}A_X.
\]

The starred algebra is the analytic test domain; generator adjoints are not automatically adjoined to the algebra whose scalar affiliated division closure is being characterized.
Use the left multiplication representation and ordinary finite rank.

Retain exactly the starting assumptions of [C_F0025](../claims/Yin_C_F0025_v1.md#yin_c_f0025).
Complex-linear \(D_{i,0}\) have domain \(\mathcal P_X\Omega\), the vacuum belongs to their actual Hilbert-adjoint domains, and

\[
[D_{i,0},X_j]=\delta_{ij}P_\Omega
\quad\text{on }\mathcal P_X\Omega,
\qquad
[D_{i,0},X_j^*]\text{ has a compact bounded extension.}
\]

C_F0025 derives closability and the Hilbert-Schmidt starred-commutator interface at this scope.
Write \(D_i=\overline{D_{i,0}}\).
Neither an all-\(M\) domain nor an initial left-\(M\)-module domain is an added hypothesis.
The exact ACSY24 derivation formulation remains a source interface to compare at its recorded scope.

**Primary objective:** establish a complete finite-rank regular-commutator characterization of

\[
u\in F_X\qquad(u\in L^0(M))
\]

from these original polynomial-domain data, including genuinely unbounded \(u\).

For arbitrary bounded left/right fractions

\[
u=s^{-1}a=bt^{-1},\qquad s,a,b,t\in M,
\quad s,t\text{ regular},
\]

the existing construction is formally \(sD_i b-aD_i t\).
Defining its legitimate small-domain realization, test spaces and any bounded finite-rank extension is part of the objective.
The formal identity \(s[D_i,u]t\) supplies no unproved composition or domain inclusion.
Preserve the target of arbitrary bounded fraction choices and independence of the finite-rank condition from those choices; the regular commutator itself may depend on the fraction pair.
Use the adopted [regular-commutator convention](../claims/Yin_C_F0013_v1.md#1-statement-and-exact-scope).

## Companion Question and Sufficient Routes

The bounded weak-form question remains explicitly assigned here.
For \(f\in M\) and \(p,q\in\mathcal P_X\Omega\), put

\[
\beta_i^f(p,q)=\langle D_{i,0}^*p,fq\rangle
              -\langle f^*p,D_{i,0}q\rangle,
\]

with inner product linear in the second variable.
Determine the converse to C_F0025: whether every \(f\in F_X\cap M\) has bounded finite-rank representatives of these forms.
C_F0025 proves the other implication.
This is a separately tracked companion question, not the primary unbounded target and not a presumed necessary intermediate theorem.
A review of the main target must report this companion's actual disposition separately.

[C_F0027](../claims/Yin_C_F0027_v1.md#yin_c_f0027) supplies an all-\(M\) extension route from uniform core estimates.
Graph-core identification, local modules, and direct small-domain regular-commutator constructions are alternative methods.
A valid full unbounded criterion need not first prove a particular all-\(M\) inclusion or a chosen graph-core bridge.
Any modification of the intended criterion must be stated explicitly and compared with the original assumptions and full affiliated scope.

The primary route is [Yin_S_F0011](../strategies/Yin_S_F0011.md#s_f0011).
