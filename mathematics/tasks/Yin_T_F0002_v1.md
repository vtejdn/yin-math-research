## Research Outcomes and Continuation Boundary

The density and adjoint directions have different outcomes.

For density, [Yin_C_F0084](../claims/Yin_C_F0084_v1.md#yin_c_f0084) shows that a proper test module of arbitrarily large trace support cannot replace Hilbert-space density in the global rationality criterion.
The counterexample uses actual globally bounded operators and their adjoints, an infinite-dimensional test module, and an omitted positive-trace component; it does not manufacture an adjoint for a nondense restriction.
This is an obstruction to the specified weakening, not a classification of all possible replacement hypotheses.

For the adjoint premise, [Yin_C_F0085](../claims/Yin_C_F0085_v1.md#yin_c_f0085) proves that selfadjoint generating tuples need only closable dual restrictions on an arbitrary dense left M-module E.
It constructs a dense left module in each actual adjoint domain by gluing local L1 form densities in measure and checking the adjoint pairing against all of E.
[Yin_C_F0086](../claims/Yin_C_F0086_v1.md#yin_c_f0086) extends that conclusion to mixed selfadjoint/unitary coordinates and their nondegenerate affine images, so normal coordinates with spectra on individual lines or circles are covered.
Its common right-invariant submodule is proved to be a graph core of the original dual restriction; the conclusion remains on the original E.
Both results recover the full Yin_C_F0029 affiliated criterion with arbitrary regular bounded fractions.

The complementary normal results remain useful:
[Yin_C_F0036](../claims/Yin_C_F0036_v1.md#yin_c_f0036) handles arbitrary normal spectra when a bounded reference dual exists;
[Yin_C_F0037](../claims/Yin_C_F0037_v1.md#yin_c_f0037) derives Hilbert-Schmidt starred commutators automatically;
[Yin_C_F0039](../claims/Yin_C_F0039_v1.md#yin_c_f0039) constructs adjoint modules from a summable conjugate spectral phase on the all-M domain, including analytic embedded spectral arcs.
Yin_C_F0038 is the separately preserved all-M selfadjoint proof with a global L1 density, which is not asserted for arbitrary E in Yin_C_F0085.

Each responsible Claim decision selects its exact proof and independent review evidence; the [Claim index](../claims/index.md) lists those materials.
The [contract comparison](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_F0083_v1.md#yin_t_f0002-both-requested-domain-directions) checks both original directions and the full criterion.
No premise has been weakened merely by renaming closability.

Remaining questions are arbitrary normal spectra on small dense modules without a reference dual, convergence of infinite phase corrections on such modules, factor-specific substitutes for density, and compatible nondense tests with dense aggregate information.
[Yin_S_F0012](../strategies/Yin_S_F0012.md#s_f0012) preserves these directions.
