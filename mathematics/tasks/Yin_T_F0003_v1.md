## Target and Conventions

For a finite tracial von Neumann algebra M generated by a bounded tuple X, put A_X=C<X>, without automatically adjoining generator adjoints.
Seek general, independently checkable sufficient conditions for

\[
\operatorname{Div}_{L^0(\mathcal M)}\mathcal A_X\cap\mathcal M
=\operatorname{Div}_{\mathcal M}\mathcal A_X.
\]

State normality, selfadjointness, freeness, dual-system and model assumptions in each proposed result; do not impose an unrequested universal claim on all tuples.
The Yin_C_F0012 counterexample already excludes a universal implication from existence of bounded duals.
Distinguish this equality from the stronger-in-a-different-direction property that every rational affiliated L2 element is bounded.
A bounded final value does not guarantee a bounded inverse linear pencil, as the existing MSY23/Yin20 rechecks establish.

Investigate structural conditions and stable operations that produce a substantial class: rational coordinate changes, finite residual reconstruction, controllability/observability with analytic bounds, and the existing q-Gaussian, holomorphic R-diagonal and analytic-transport models.
Conditions merely restating the desired equality do not settle the goal.

## Reviewed Outcomes and Continuation Boundary

The first research round produced three independently reviewed sufficient mechanisms, all for actual scalar bounded division closure:

- [Yin_C_F0087](../claims/Yin_C_F0087_v1.md#yin_c_f0087): principal numerator-denominator ideals permit cancellation and a Bezout reconstruction of a bounded inverse.
  This proves the equality for every single bounded operator, without normality.
- [Yin_C_F0089](../claims/Yin_C_F0089_v1.md#yin_c_f0089): finite-index transfer from a subgroup whose affiliated closure is a division ring and satisfies the equality.
  Together with the accepted free-group input, this includes finitely generated virtually free groups and free families with uniform finite atomic laws.
- [Yin_C_F0090](../claims/Yin_C_F0090_v1.md#yin_c_f0090): an adjoint-closed Noetherian normal domain of dimension at most one, continuously represented on a compact full-support probability space, admits bounded descent by one positive denominator.
  The proof explicitly distinguishes abstract local fractions from admissible affiliated denominators.

[Yin_C_F0088](../claims/Yin_C_F0088_v1.md#yin_c_f0088) gives a singular-curve obstruction even for an element in the generated C*-algebra.
[Yin_C_F0091](../claims/Yin_C_F0091_v1.md#yin_c_f0091) shows failure for a regular two-dimensional polynomial ring, so ring normality alone does not suffice.
These results do not require a dual system and do not replace Yin_C_F0012's distinct counterexample under bounded duals.

The [independent contract comparison](../audits/reviews/Yin_R_F0087_v1.md#yin_t_f0003-general-conditions-and-substantial-families) checks the original acceptance criteria and the independent proof evidence.
Broader diffuse free-product stability, nonuniform atomic families, multivariable q-Gaussians and general analytic transport remain open.
[Yin_S_F0013](../strategies/Yin_S_F0013.md#s_f0013) records the continuation handoff; the related results of the separate rational-L2 investigation do not settle these bounded scalar reconstruction questions.
