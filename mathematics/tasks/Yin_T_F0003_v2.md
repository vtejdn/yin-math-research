## Research contract

<a id="contract"></a>
<a id="yin_t_f0003"></a>

Let M be a finite von Neumann algebra with faithful normal probability trace, and let X be a finite bounded generating tuple.
Write A_X=C<X>, without automatically adjoining adjoints when generators are not selfadjoint.
Seek general, independently checkable sufficient conditions for

\[
\operatorname{Rat}_{L^0(M)}(A_X)\cap M
=\operatorname{Rat}_M(A_X).
\]

Here Rat denotes matrix rational closure in the indicated ambient algebra.
The [original scalar division-closure question](Yin_T_F0003_v1.md#target-and-conventions) remains part of the Task.
State which closure is treated in every theorem, and prove the needed scalar-to-matrix comparison instead of silently identifying them.
For each result state normality, selfadjointness, freeness, dual, spectral and algebraic hypotheses explicitly.
Distinguish bounded rational descent from the assertion that every rational L2 element is bounded.
A condition that merely restates the desired equality is insufficient.

Retain the verified principal-ideal, finite-index and normal-curve mechanisms and the cusp and planar obstructions recorded in [v1](Yin_T_F0003_v1.md#reviewed-outcomes-2026-09-11).
Continue to test structural conditions, rational coordinate changes, finite residual reconstruction and independently verifiable analytic bounds, without superseding the separate model-specific Tasks.

### Selfadjoint bounded-dual test branch

Search for a counterexample simpler than [the sandwich pair](../claims/Yin_C_F0012_v1.md#statement), keeping every generator selfadjoint and a bounded dual system on the whole standard H=L2(M,tau):

\[
[X_j,D_i]=\delta_{ij}P_\Omega,\qquad D_i\in B(H).
\]

The example must exhibit a bounded f in the affiliated matrix rational closure but outside the matrix rational closure in M, and prove W*(X)=M.
Compare the simplification concretely: lower polynomial degree in specified elementary free inputs, fewer nontrivial factors, or a substantially simpler complete bounded-dual construction.
A renaming, a selected unbounded realization, or nonmembership proved only for one expression is not enough.
The [nonnormal pair (h,bh)](../claims/Yin_C_F0012_v2.md#statement) does not meet this branch's selfadjoint hypothesis.
A failed candidate supplies a scoped obstruction, not a universal nonexistence theorem.
Use the branch to identify hypotheses genuinely missing from the equality; do not let examples replace the main sufficient-condition objective.
