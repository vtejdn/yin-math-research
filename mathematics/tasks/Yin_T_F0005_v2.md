## Setting and Independent Role

Work in a finite tracial von Neumann algebra \((M,\tau)\) with faithful normal probability trace, \(H=L^2(M,\tau)\), vacuum \(\Omega\), and a finite bounded normal tuple \(X\) generating \(M\).
Keep \(A_X=\mathbb C\langle X\rangle\) separate from the starred polynomial test algebra.
Rational closures use \(A_X\) without adjoining extra adjoints.
Dense left modules below mean Hilbert-dense complex-linear left \(M\)-modules, which need not contain the vacuum or be polynomial domains.

## Q1: Analytic Spectral Arcs on Small Modules

Extend [C_F0039](../claims/Yin_C_F0039_v1.md#yin_c_f0039)'s automatic adjoint-module conclusion from \(E=M\) to arbitrary dense left \(M\)-modules \(E\) for closable duals with the prescribed normalized generator commutators.
A concrete test remains

\[
X_j=S_j+i\epsilon_j S_j^2,\qquad S_j=S_j^*,\quad
\epsilon_j\in\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}.
\]

No bounded reference dual is assumed.
State and justify the actual domains and convergence of any infinite paired-operator correction.
Do not interpret normalization as a requirement that an arbitrary module contain \(1\).

Inherited progress:

- [C_F0102](../claims/Yin_C_F0102_v1.md#yin_c_f0102) provides weighted local-orbit sufficient estimates and an obstruction to arbitrary representation-dependent series continuity.
- [C_F0104](../claims/Yin_C_F0104_v1.md#yin_c_f0104) supplies two sufficient right-domain conditions: a bounded right-coordinate commutator or a dense smooth right submodule.
  Neither is automatic from arbitrary \(E\).
- [C_F0114](../claims/Yin_C_F0114_v1.md#normal-dual-affiliated-comparison) identifies the difference of two existing dual choices with unique right-affiliated multiplication on a common original graph core, and transfers dense actual-adjoint-module existence.
  Constructing one good reference from the bare hypotheses remains unresolved for a general noncommuting analytic-arc tuple.

The primary remaining Q1 obligation is existence, not another comparison between already available good references.

## Q2: General Spectral Phases

Determine whether the projectively summable conjugate-phase assumption can be removed, initially even on \(E=M\).
The relevant normal divided-difference kernel is

\[
\frac{\overline z-\overline w}{z-w}
\]

off the null diagonal.
Identify a replacement mechanism or an exact counterexample satisfying the actual remaining dual assumptions, including normal spectra beyond lines and circles.
Pointwise boundedness and Hilbert-Schmidt kernel control do not by themselves supply the required projective expansion or legitimate unbounded compositions.

The existing line/circle and summable-phase conclusions remain sufficient cases.
C_F0114's comparison does not construct a reference or remove phase regularity.
[C_F0054](../claims/Yin_C_F0054_v1.md#normal-phase-realification-obstruction) is relevant route-obstruction evidence, not a counterexample to this original question.

## Q4: Replacements for Density

Find useful separation and compatibility hypotheses for nondense test spaces, including factor-specific tests or jointly total compatible families with uniform finite-rank control.
[C_F0084](../claims/Yin_C_F0084_v1.md#yin_c_f0084) rules out simply omitting an arbitrarily small positive-trace component.
Do not assign a single-valued Hilbert adjoint to a nondensely defined operator without an explicit valid construction.

[C_F0110](../claims/Yin_C_F0110_v1.md#yin_c_f0110) establishes the compatible-family alternative: individual windows may be nondense, cross-adjoint pairings assemble actual dual and adjoint modules, and mixed test matrices have a uniform finite-rank criterion.
Aggregate density remains a premise.
Individual window ranks alone do not suffice.
The remaining question concerns meaningful non-total or factor-specific substitutes for that aggregate separation.
