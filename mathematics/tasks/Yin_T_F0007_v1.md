## Yin_T_F0007: Free-Product Stability of Bounded Division Closure

Origin and authorization: user-assigned, 2026-09-11, original question 7.
Current state: active.

## Exact Question

Let (M_i,tau_i), i=1,2, be finite tracial von Neumann algebras with faithful normal probability traces, and let A_i be finitely generated unital complex subalgebras generating M_i as von Neumann algebras.
In the reduced tracial free product (M,tau)=(M_1,tau_1)*(M_2,tau_2), let A be the unital algebra generated by their canonical copies.
Suppose each input satisfies

    Div_{L0(M_i)} A_i intersect M_i = Div_{M_i} A_i.

Must the combined coordinates satisfy

    Div_{L0(M)} A intersect M = Div_M A ?

The ambient algebra and permitted inverses are part of each expression.
Do not silently assume the affiliated closures are division rings or that arbitrary coordinate algebras are closed under adjoints.
State any such extra assumptions when testing restricted permanence results.

## Selected Tests and Boundaries

Prioritize freely independent bounded selfadjoint variables with compactly supported nonatomic marginal laws; Yin_C_F0087 supplies each single-coordinate input.
Also test nonuniform finite atomic marginals, keeping their projection/rank phenomena visible.
Uniform finite-spectrum cases already covered by Yin_C_F0089 are baselines.
The free pair (h,b) underlying Yin_C_F0012 is a concrete diffuse test; the changed coordinates (h,hbh) are not freely independent and do not refute this question.

This asks for bounded rational reconstruction, not rational L2 boundedness for arbitrary marginal laws, which can fail already in one variable.
Abstract rank-preserving rational-closure identifications require additional analysis before they can transfer bounded parts.
The newly authorized Atiyah group route is related but does not itself prove this analytic permanence statement.

## Completion Criteria

A proof of the full permanence statement or an exact counterexample satisfying both input equalities and the prescribed free-product realization, independently reviewed.
Restricted sufficient conditions and explicit obstructions to individual methods are progress, not replacement completion criteria.
