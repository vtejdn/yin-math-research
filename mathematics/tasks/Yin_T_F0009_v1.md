## Inherited Questions and Conventions

This Task inherits the R-diagonal, analytic-free-transport and related structural sufficient-condition investigation from [the earlier T_F0001 expansion](Yin_T_F0001_v1.md#expanded-affiliated-and-boundedness-contract).
Work in specified finite von Neumann algebras with faithful normal probability traces, using actual affiliated operators and their actual evaluation domains.
For the prescribed bounded generators \(X\), write

\[
A_X=\mathbb C\langle X\rangle,\qquad
F_X=\operatorname{Div}_{L^0(M)}A_X.
\]

Do not adjoin generator adjoints to the chosen polynomial algebra automatically.
Distinguish scalar inverse closure from matrix rational closure, formal series from evaluated operators, and square integrability from boundedness.
For nonnormal R-diagonal coordinates, applicability of a normal-tuple theorem requires its own justified generating model.

Investigate the following two properties separately:

\[
F_X\cap L^2(M)\subseteq M,
\qquad
F_X\cap M=\operatorname{Div}_M A_X.
\]

The first is rational square-integrable boundedness; the second is finite scalar reconstruction using inverses that are bounded in \(M\).
Neither a bounded scalar affiliated output, membership in \(C^*(X)\), a norm limit of polynomials, nor existence of bounded duals supplies the second conclusion by itself.
