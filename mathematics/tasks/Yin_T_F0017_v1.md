## Research contract

<a id="contract"></a>

Find a concrete counterexample simpler than the pair in [C_F0012 v1](../claims/Yin_C_F0012_v1.md#statement), while retaining all the following conditions.
Let M be a finite von Neumann algebra with faithful normal tracial probability state, H=L2(M,tau), and Omega=1.
The finite tuple X consists of bounded selfadjoint generators of M.
There are bounded operators D_i on all of H with [X_j,D_i]=delta_ij P_Omega.
For A=C<X>, the witness f belongs to Rat_{L0(M)}(A) intersect M but not to Rat_M(A), with matrix rational closure in both ambients.

The baseline is X=(h,hbh), h having law 4t^3 dt on (0,1), and b a standard semicircular element free from h.
The [one-sided pair in C_F0012 v2](../claims/Yin_C_F0012_v2.md#statement) is excluded because its second generator is not normal.
All generators being selfadjoint, the polynomial algebra is already closed under adjoints.

Seek a demonstrable reduction in the construction or proof: for example, lower polynomial degree in explicitly specified elementary free input variables, fewer nontrivial factors, or a substantially simpler bounded-dual construction.
Keep the comparison explicit; a mere renaming or an unproved formal inverse does not establish a simplification.
Two generators are preferred, without treating an unproved universal minimality claim as a premise.
No requirement of independence among the output generators is added.

Completion requires the complete example, whole-space boundedness and every dual relation, generation of the actual tracial algebra, and nonmembership excluding every bounded matrix rational representation.
A failed natural candidate is useful partial progress but does not complete the search.
Record an independent inspection of the completed example and its actual comparison with this contract.
Do not weaken selfadjointness, boundedness of the duals, or the closure distinction to close the task.
